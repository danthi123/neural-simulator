# Pre-registration: the day-14 milestone weekly at 1.5B, six seeds (AWS)

Written 2026-11-15 before any run. Capability: Sleep consolidation (milestone day 14, `brain/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4:
sleep inside the live brain on every restart; high-use facts never wrong; a lesion visibly changes replies; cortex size
chosen). Board #279/#280/#281.

**Record brief.**
- The cortex size was decided on 11-03: 1.5B, six seeds, sleep recall 8.83 vs 4.0 at 0.4B (commit 1aabdc42a).
- The live package at 1.5B is gain 0.5 (`results/2026-11-13-live-gain-1.5b.md`, GO) and key S0
  (`2026-11-14-key-default-1.5b.md`).
- The last weekly (2026-11-13) ran the 0.1B cortex.
- The only 1.5B weekly is the 11-03 preview, run before the gain refit. There, and at 0.1B, the hippocampus-off arm
  beat the intact arm after the night: 5 vs 2 (preview) and 4 vs 2 (0.1B) correct of 6 keyed questions. The
  reinstatement hurt the slept cortex. The gain refit was the fix for that (live gain GO: told 8.00 vs 4.83).
- Known open defect, not tested here: false recall after sleep (`results/2026-11-14-near-miss-guard-1.5b.md`).
- `weekly.py` gets `--seed` (sleep seed and torch/numpy). The default (unset) leaves behaviour unchanged.

**Prerequisites.** Everything is in place: restart = night, weekly `--shared-sleep`, 1.5B sleep defaults, and gain
auto. Host decision: none.

**Method.** `python -m brain.eval.weekly --model <1.5B pth> --device cuda --shared-sleep --seed S`, six seeds
42/43/44/100/101/102, plus one run with no `--seed`. The unseeded run is the weekly record:
`brain/eval/transcripts/2026-11-15.md`. One AWS batch (g6.xlarge L4), `jobs/2026-11-15-aws-weekly-1.5b.txt`. Scoring
is the weekly's own `mark_for` (the strict, correct-perspective scorer). Each seed's transcript is kept as
`transcripts/2026-11-15-1.5b-s<seed>.md`.

**Criteria** (session 2, after the restart; the six registered seeds):
- **W0 instrument:** every run slept once (header says "one shared night"; the runner asserts `sleeps >= 1`) and
  scored all 6 keyed questions in both arms.
- **W1 high-use facts (#280 proxy):** the intact arm answers both "Do you remember my name?" (Dan) and "What's your
  name?" (Nova) correctly on 6/6 seeds. This is a proxy: the real #280 test (strength grows with use) stays separate.
- **W2 a lesion visibly changes replies (#281):** on >= 5/6 seeds, at least 3 of the 4 told-fact questions (cat,
  sister, car, work) get a different reply with the hippocampus off.
- **W3 sleep plus hippocampus do not hurt:** intact told-fact correct (of 4), six-seed mean >= the lesion arm's
  mean, and intact keyed total (of 6) mean >= 3. The 11-13 0.1B intact arm scored 2.

**Decision.**
- GO = W0-W3. Flip the weekly default to the 1.5B cortex with one shared night (`--model 1.5b --shared-sleep`
  default; tests stay pinned at 0.1B). Then land-result.
- Otherwise the transcript still lands as this week's record, the default stays, and the unmet milestone items are
  reported (§4: "if not met, stop and report").

**Reported, not criteria:** lesion-arm keyed totals, false or invented content in replies to the story and feeling
turns, and the unseeded transcript.

**Addendum (2026-11-15, before any run):** report-only seeds 200-205 run on pool40/41/42 on CPU (fp32;
`jobs/2026-11-15-pool-weekly-1.5b.sh`, two seeds per node). They are reported next to the registered six and are not
