# Pre-registration: own-reply gain (speaking-induced suppression) against in-context collapse, 1.5B

Written 2026-11-16 before any run with the switch. Research gate fired (two levers on in-context collapse failed:
habituation, NO-GO; reply monitor, pool report-only shows the echo replaced by a stored-line loop on 1121, AWS
1126-1131 pending). Research round (workflow wf_83f0ad09-15d: ML, neuroscience and record reports, opus synthesis)
ranked this method first.

**Record brief.** Known: the collapse is a sampled onset the conversation state then reinforces (echo-onset probe
1/12; Xu et al. 2022, self-reinforcement: the more a sentence is in context the likelier it repeats). Both failed
levers left the state untouched. New: act on the state write. Biology: own speech is suppressed in auditory cortex
relative to playback (Houde et al. 2002; Eliades & Wang 2008), weaker in conditions with a weak self/other tag
(echolalia). Old track: corollary-discharge authorship tag, 6-seed GO (`research/findings/
2026-08-01-agency-authorship-tag-corollary-discharge-source-monitor-6seed-GO.md`), never ported. Prerequisites: a
recurrent state that integrates heard and own text (met); the onset is sampled (met). Expected to fail if the stored
line is pushed by the consolidated synapses or reinstatement on every turn rather than by the context.

**Method.** `Brain(own_gain=g)` (switch, default OFF = None). `hear()` commits the exchange by reading the heard turn
at full gain, then the reply, and scales the reply's change to the matrix memory (wkv slots) by g; token-shift slots
as read. g = 1.0 through the split read is the control. Host decision: none (one gain; no text rule).

**Stage 1, report-only probe (`brain/experiments/own_gain_probe.py`, pool).** Force the recorded onset (the first two
day-2 replies of a collapsed conversation), then let the brain speak freely, 6 sampling seeds per gain, gains
none / 1 / 0.5 / 0:
- E: echo onset, cortex alone (hippocampus and night off), script 1121, echo-onset probe conversation 4.
- B: stored-line onset ("I keep bees in my garden."), intact (hippocampus and night on), script 1121, the reply-monitor
  home and transcript.
Counts on the 14 free turns: pronoun-normalised echoes, repeats of the onset reply.
Kill criterion (named by the research round): if g = 0 still echoes on > 10 of 14 free turns on average in E, the
own-reply write is not the amplifier and the method is dead. Lead: mean echoes (E) or onset repeats (B) at g = 0 or
0.5 at most half of g = 1. A lead goes to stage 2.

**Stage 2 (only on a lead), written as an addendum before it runs:** six fresh AWS seeds, default vs the chosen g, the
bars of `2026-11-16-reply-monitor-1.5b.md` (collapse, recall, false recall, paired blind reading >= 5/6, answer
rate), plus the risk the research named: own-statement consistency (does the brain contradict what it said).
