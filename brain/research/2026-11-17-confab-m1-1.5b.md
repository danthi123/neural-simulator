## Method
`brain/experiments/confab_m1.py`, unchanged read-outs and criteria from the 0.1B pre-registration (F, C_mean / C_max /
C_cmean / C_cmin, A_lp, A_bin, AND; DEV for the fit, fresh v2+v3 for the verdict). New for 1.5B:
- **Home** (`--build`): there are no slept 1.5B abstain-v2 homes. Each seed builds the 11-13 confidence-gate home: the
  12 FACTS are told to a live Brain, and the restart is one night (480 steps, lr 5e-4, KL 3, rank 8). Brain defaults are
  as at this commit. There is no lesson episode, so nothing is excluded from retrieval. Read-outs reinstate at the hippocampus gain 4.0 (`KeyedHippocampus` default, = the 11-13 f arm), not the live
  0.5; forced top-1 (`best()`), so the S_all bar plays no part.
- **Scale and host:** 1.5B weights `rwkv7-g1l-1.5b-20261031-ctx34304.pth`, cuda, one AWS GPU. Seeds 42/43/44/100/101/102.
  Each seed is one `both` job: dev, then fresh, on the same home.
- Commands:
  1. `python -m brain.experiments.confab_m1 both --build --seed S --model <1.5B> --device cuda --work <w> --out D`
  2. `... fit --dir D --out D/fit.json`. This is written and committed before any fresh number is read.
  3. `... report --dir D --bars D/fit.json --out D/report.json`

  D = `brain/experiments/results/2026-11-17-confab-m1-1.5b/`.

## Criteria (identical to the 0.1B pre-registration)
- **P1:** the fresh told-vs-never AUROC of the chosen read-out is at least 0.85 on 6/6 seeds.
- **P2:** at least 75% of the untold-attribute subset is gated (below the seed's LOSO DEV bar) on 6/6 seeds.
- **PASS** (a lead, not a GO) = P1 and P2. Only then wire it as a gate and rerun a reply-level test.
- **FALSIFIED** if, on at least 5/6 seeds, the fresh AUROC is below 0.75 or is not at least 0.08 above that seed's F.
- **(ii) dead** if A_bin is true on never-told questions as often as on told ones.
- Neither PASS nor FALSIFIED = inconclusive.

## Report-only read-outs (added for this run; they cannot pass or fail this pre-registration)
The research round asked for these. They are computed on every row and reported in `report.json` `extras`:
- **Coff_mean, Coff_cmean:** C with the night's adapters off in both states, so the night's prior cannot answer for
  the memory.
- **C2off_mean:** the best episode's Coff_mean minus the runner-up's (recall-to-reject contrast).
- **G:** grounding of the cortical answer. The mean log-prob gain of C's own greedy tokens under the reinstated episode,
  adapters off. Was the answer carried by the memory?
- **margin:** F minus the runner-up's score.
- **jaccard:** lexical baseline.

Each is reported three ways:
- told vs never;
- told-answered-right vs never ("right" = either route names the key, an instrument read-out);
- told vs untold-attribute.

An extra that reaches P1's bar (fresh at least 0.85 on 6/6 seeds and at least 0.08 above F) and beats jaccard by 0.10
is a candidate for a NEW pre-registration, on a NEW fresh set. It is never a pass of this one.
