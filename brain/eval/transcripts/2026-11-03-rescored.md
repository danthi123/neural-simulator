## Three most telling exchanges

1. Intact, "What do I do for work?" -> "I drive a red Volvo." (recalled: "User: I drive a red Volvo."). The recalled episode was the wrong fact, and the cortex read it out as if it were the answer; the same Volvo episode also answered "What do I do in my free time?", and the saxophone episode answered "What instrument does my mother play?". Retrieval collapses onto one episode and cannot discriminate between the stored facts.
2. Sleep + hippocampus off, "What is my sister's name?" -> "My sister's name is Pixel." and "Where was I born?" -> "I was born in Denver." Sleep put the facts into the weights, but cat/sister and uncle/birthplace blur (false recall, the known defect).
3. Sleep + hippocampus off, "What do I do for work?" -> "I work as a system administrator." and "What do I do in my free time?" -> "I play chess." / "What is my favourite food?" -> "I love ramen." Genuinely correct and not an echo (strict agrees), with the hippocampus off, versus hippocampus off without sleep where every answer is a confabulation ("I was born in a castle", "I play the piano"). This is the real signal in the run.

## Did the arms behave as expected?

- Hippocampus off 0/12 (0/12 strict): as expected. Pure confabulation, no leakage.
- Intact: partly. Retrieval works for the name/cat/sister/car, but only 3/12 are real answers; the six drawn facts mostly return the told sentence verbatim (lenient credits it) or the wrong episode. In session 1 the brain's replies to six consecutive tellings were the identical "That's a good choice." (hippocampus-off equally stuck on "That's a bit of a name."): the conversation is weak.
- Sleep: better than intact on strict (5 vs 3) and answers in a different, fluent voice. Anomaly: its session 1 (same base cortex, hippocampus on, identical to intact's session 1) produced fluent multi-sentence replies where intact's were terse repeats. By the code both use the same cortex, so the difference is unexplained (candidates: sampling, or state left on the shared cortex by earlier arms or by PlasticCortex construction); not checkable without a rerun.

The `brain/eval/weekly.py` at 7cb3223e2 marks with the strict correct-perspective scorer, so the weekly headline will drop when that branch is merged on top.
