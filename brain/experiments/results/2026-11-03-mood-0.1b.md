# Mood shapes the reply to the same question, through the modulation only: 0.1B, six seeds (2026-11-03)

**Verdict (CORRECTED 11-03 after the skeptic review): NOT a pre-registered PASS.** Criteria 1 and 3 hold 6/6, but
criterion 4 (primary-content echo) FAILS on 2/6 seeds (s101, s102) under the echo instrument registered before the
run, and criterion 2 (lesion byte-identical) holds by construction, so it is not evidence. What survives is a lead:
on 6/6 seeds an induced mood moves the tone of the reply (lexical CI above 0, embedding CI above 0), mostly through
the NEGATIVE mood; positive appraisal is near 0 on s100/s101. 0.1B only, not wired into the live `Brain`.
Iteration 2 (pre-registered fix): design doc section 9.

## Corrections (skeptic, 11-03)
1. **Criterion 4 fails on 2/6 seeds under the registered instrument.** The registered echo instrument (commit
   28089180a: any reply word longer than 3 letters that occurs in a primary) gives mood-arm mean minus lesion-arm
   mean: s42 +0.042, s43 +0.042, s44 -0.028, s100 +0.042, **s101 +0.111, s102 +0.111** (bar: at most +0.10).
   (Per arm, s101: pos 0.667 / neg 0.778 / lesion 0.611; s102: pos 0.583 / neg 0.583 / lesion 0.472.) The table
   below shows a REVISED echo instrument (sensory/bodily content words only) that was changed after the run had
   started (the JSONs hold the old instrument for s42/43/100/101 and the new one for s44/102); under it every seed
   passes, but a post-hoc instrument cannot rescue a pre-registered criterion. Recomputed from the
   saved replies: `python -m brain.experiments.mood_table <dir>` (column "echo mood-off REGISTERED").
2. **The appraisal events were not held out.** The appraisal check scored all 8 events per valence, and the 6 that
   induced the mood were drawn from the same 8. "Held-out" is withdrawn.
3. **Criterion 2 holds by construction.** `Affect.modulate` returns the state untouched when the switch is off, so
   pos and neg lesion replies are byte-identical whatever the mood; that is a code identity, not a lesion result.
   The informative lesion contrast is mood-on vs mood-off for the SAME mood, on each valence.
4. **The on-vs-off contrast is one-sided.** Negative mood vs off lowers lexical sentiment on 6/6 seeds; positive mood
   vs off is ~0 or negative on s100 (+0.00; m_pos -0.005) and s101 (-1.49; m_pos -0.092), because positive events
   appraise ~0 or negative there. The pos-neg contrast is carried mainly by the negative arm.

## What ran
`brain/affect.py` (default OFF): innate bodily + social primaries (seed draws 8 of 13 per sign) define a valence axis
in the cortex's fast memory; appraisal = projection of an event's trace on that axis; mood = slow leaky integrator
(TAU 4 events); modulation = `16 * mood * axis` added to the matrix memory before the question is read.
Per seed, 6 positive (praise, gain, reunion) or 6 negative (threat, loss, insult) events are appraised; then 12 neutral
test questions x 3 samples are answered from the SAME birth state in every arm (the events never enter the answering
state; the only path is the mood scalar). Common random numbers across arms. Instruments are outside the brain
(`brain/experiments/sentiment_instrument.py`): lexical sentiment per 100 words (primary; disjoint from all primary
words), MiniLM-L6 embedding sentiment (secondary), primary-content echo, NLL of the reply under the unmodulated cortex.

    python -m brain.experiments.mood_probe --seed <s> --threads 3 --out mood_0.1b_s<s>.json   # s in 42 43 44 100 101 102
    python -m brain.experiments.mood_table brain/experiments/results/2026-11-03-mood-0.1b

Run on the workstation under a 2.5 GB systemd memory scope, 2 lanes, ~20 min per seed (box heavily loaded).
GAIN 16 was chosen on 6 dev questions (not the test questions), seed 42, before this run.

## What it shows
- Criteria (1) lexical CI above 0 on 6/6, embedding CI above 0 on 6/6, and (3) random direction smaller than mood
  on 6/6 hold. (2) holds by construction (see Corrections). (4) FAILS on s101 and s102 under the registered echo
  instrument (+0.111 each); it passes under the revised instrument, which does not count. Fluency (NLL) roughly
  unchanged (s101 negative arm 1.16 vs 0.95 is the worst).
- The effect is specific to the valence axis on the embedding instrument (random ~0), and the cortex speaks it in
  its own words; no affect word is ever added by host code.

## What it does not show / caveats
- **Positive appraisal is weak:** positive events (not held out) read positive on 0.88 of events on 4 seeds, 0.50 and 0.12
  on s100/s101, where the "positive" mood came out ~0 or negative. The neg arm drives most of the contrast
  (neg-off negative 6/6; pos-off positive 4/6). Negative events read negative 1.00 on every seed.
- Margin over the random direction is thin on s101 (3.52 vs 3.36 lexical); the random direction also lowers
  positivity a little on 5/6 seeds (perturbation size alone has a small negative effect).
- Negative mood partly works through primary imagery ("dark", "cold"): echo is measured, but content intrusion and
  "tone" are not cleanly separable at this gain.
- The echo instrument was revised mid-run (it counted function words; ~0.5 echo even with the modulation off). The
  table uses the revised instrument on the saved replies; that is post hoc, and the registered instrument fails
  criterion 4 on 2/6 seeds (Corrections). Lexical and embedding instruments were not changed.
- Not wired into `Brain.hear` (the answering state here lacks the conversation); arousal is read out but does not
  act; the self-report is numbers only. 0.1B only (PREREQUISITES: check a second size before attributing tone).
