What this says: at 1.5B the lived don't-know exchange makes the forced gate answer "I don't know" on ~97% of never-told
questions with the source fix intact (no recitation, no "Nova"), versus ~3% by the keyword instrument (about 23% once refusals are counted) with nothing reinstated. v1's topic leak,
visible at 0.1B (57/360), is nearly gone at 1.5B (5/360), so v2's advantage shrinks to a few leaks; the gain is
scale, not the method.

Judged against scale (`brain/PREREQUISITES.md`): this is a pass at the target size, so the 0.1B shortfall (69% DEV) was
a scale limit, not a wall. Limits that stay: no sleep, the gate is forced by the host (not the brain's own learned
gate, M5), lived_self's DK is about 96% a verbatim copy of the guided sentence, and the gated sets hold only
never-told questions, so answering TOLD facts under the gate is untested (that test is required before any default
flip), one lesson question and one guided sentence, keyword instruments, 36+24 questions per seed. This is a LEAD,
not a GO; `abstain` stays OFF and `live_self_belief` an experiment-only scaffold. Next: decide v1 vs v2 on cost of the
remaining leaks only after the learned-gate (M5) test; the lesson question set and the strict scorer would be the first
robustness checks.
