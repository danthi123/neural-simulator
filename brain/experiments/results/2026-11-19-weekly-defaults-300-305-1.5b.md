## Defects, ranked by how much they hurt a conversation

1. **After the night the hippocampus makes answers worse than the lesion (31 vs 35 of 36).** Every intact error
   comes from reinstating the wrong episode: "What's your name?" fetches the Marguerite telling 6/6 seeds (answers
   "My name is Dan." / "My name is Marguerite."), "What do I do for work?" fetches the Volvo telling 6/6 (s302:
   "You drive a red Volvo."), and a fetched telling flips the speaker on s301 ("My cat is called Pixel."). Same cost as
   the 11-13 weekly and the 11-13 confidence/mixed-day results; S_all + slept bar fixes the selection (11-18) but was
   not flipped (S3/S4).
2. **Slept user facts intrude into open generation, in both arms.** The fox in the story is named "Pixel" in 12/12
   arm-runs; the user's job enters the story (s301 both arms, s302 lesion: Marguerite the owl fixes a backup job);
   "How are you feeling?" -> "I'm feeling great! I drive a red Volvo..." (s303 intact, s304 lesion). This is the
   false-recall family (11-15 weak-night control: false recall is not a night-strength trade-off; 11-17/18 KILL of
   self-teacher levers at 1.5B). It comes from the slept cortex, not from recall (the lesion arm shows it too).
3. Curiosity false fires are harmless here: the zorbit lesson is fetched as context on the backup-job / Marguerite turns
   (4/6 seeds), and replies stay ordinary (s301 asks follow-ups: "What kind of backup job was it?").

No collapse on these seeds (the seed-42 "Pixel." loop of 11-19 does not recur). Session 1 is fluent and warm on 6/6.

## What it means for the next milestone
The 12-14 milestone is a multi-week life with a night after each day. Both defects above are per-night costs and are
expected to compound with more nights; the design must measure them per night from the first run (a baseline arm at
current defaults) before any new method.
