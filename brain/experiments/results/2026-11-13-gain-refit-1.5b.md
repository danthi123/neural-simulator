## Skeptics (three, fresh context, sonnet): all NARROWED, none REFUTED

- Instrument: hand-graded test at 0.5 is told 7/12 and para **5/12**. The scorer over-credited "My name is Pixel." and
  a generic peanut-allergy warning. It also rejects first-person echoes ("My cat is called Pixel."), so these counts
  are lower bounds. Gain 4 is worse by hand than the scorer says. R2 and R3 still hold.
- Power: fact bootstrap (10k). Dev 0.5 - 4 = +42 (95% CI 27..56), 0.5 - 1 = +23 (12..33). Test told 0.5 - 4 = +6
  (1..10). Test para 0.5 - 4 = +4 (-1..9): the paraphrase advantage over gain 4 is not established on 12 facts. The
  curve is not monotone (6 > 4).
- Scope: this is the oracle path (the right episode, forced) on an unslept cortex with no conversation history. Live,
  routing is S_all with the size bar (5-7 of 12 fire), and on 11-13 reinstatement hurt after sleep.

## Report-only follow-ups (run after the verdict, `followup/`)

- Wrong-memory control (the episode of fact i+1, test): at 0.5, 1/24 right (a prior guess, "allergic to peanuts")
  and the wrong fact appeared in 0/24 replies. At 1, 0/24 and 1/24. The answers come from the right episode, and a
  wrong episode at 0.5 does not inject its fact. The cortex still confabulates without the right memory ("My cat is

- Finer grid: dev 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 = 26 / 58 / 52 of 108; test told 6 / 7 / 4, para 4 / 7 / 4. The peak is about
