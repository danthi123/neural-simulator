What it shows:
- **Recall: holds on all six seeds.** True replay 3-4 of 12 against 0-1 for shuffled and 0 for no sleep. The
  in-context ceiling of the 0.1B cortex is 6 of 12, so sleep reaches about two-thirds of what this cortex can do
  even with the facts in front of it.
- **Paraphrase: not shown.** 2.2 against 1.3 for shuffled. The shuffled arm trains the same name words, so some
  paraphrase hits are names leaking, not facts.
- **False recall: a defect.** Any sleep (true or shuffled) raises it from 0 to about 2 of 4: the cortex starts
  answering unrelated questions with the learned names. True does not beat shuffled here (equal mean; above it on
  seed 100), so the HANDOFF "false recall not above shuffled" bar fails on seed 100 and the absolute rate is the
  real problem.
- **Drift: small** (KL 0.016-0.038 on ordinary text).
