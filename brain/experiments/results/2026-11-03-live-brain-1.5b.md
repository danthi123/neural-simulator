Question: does the LIVE brain (Brain object: hear, save, restart = night, fresh Brain per question) consolidate the 12 told
facts at the chosen cortex size (1.5B, decided 11-03 in `2026-11-03-cortex-size/2026-11-03-cortex-size.md` section 2b),
the way the bare `sleep_probe` showed (sleep_true recall 8.83/12, six seeds)? The 0.1B run of this probe

## Primary scorer
`strict_scorer.correct_answer(reply, key, question, told)`: key as a word, not in the question, not a tautology, not a
wrong fact-bank subject, not a first-person restatement of the told sentence (told = the fact's day-1 sentence, also for
PARA questions and for arm e). False recall stays as in the probe: any of the 12 keys as a word in a never-told reply.
The old strict `score` counts are reported beside it for comparison with the 0.1B table (which used `score`).

## Criteria (recall / paraphrase = correct_answer counts; table: `python -m brain.experiments.live_brain_table <dir>
## --glob 'live15_s*.json' --scorer correct --p1-mean 4`)
- P1 (sleep alone answers): recall c > d AND c > e on every one of the six seeds, and mean c >= 4/12 (above 0.4B's
  six-seed sleep_probe mean 4.0; 0.1B's bar was 2).
- P2 (sleep adds over the intact control): recall a >= b on at least 5/6 seeds and mean(a) - mean(b) >= 1.
- P3 (false recall): mean false recall a <= mean b + 0.5, and mean c <= mean e.
- P4 (paraphrase, reported, not required): mean paraphrase c > mean e + 1.
- Verdict: P1 and P2 and P3 = LEAD (one probe is never a GO). Any of them failing = reported as that criterion failing.
- Also reported, not criteria: per-seed sleep seconds, `cuda_peak_bytes` (must be < 24 GiB or the run is invalid for that
  seed), echo counts, lenient counts, c vs the bare sleep_probe recall on the same seed (8.83 mean), and the 0.1B table.
- Invalid run (not a negative): a seed that crashes, OOMs or is cut by the dead-man has no result; the missing seeds are
  rerun with the same command, never replaced by other seeds. No setting is changed after the first seed is seen.

### Criteria (as pre-registered, bars unchanged)
- P1 PASS: c > d and c > e on all six seeds (lowest margin s44: 7 vs 2 and 0); mean c 9.33 >= 4. c is near the bare
  `sleep_probe` 1.5B recall (8.83 mean).

- P3 FAIL: mean false recall a 4.00 vs b 3.00 + 0.5 = 3.5 (fails; sleep adds exactly 1 of 4); second clause mean c 3.50 <= mean e 4.00 holds.

- Verdict: P1 and P2 hold, P3 fails, so this is NOT a LEAD under the written rule. The sleep mechanism works in the live

### Judged against scale
The P1/P2 passes are the scale-responsive part: 0.1B had c 5.50 and a - b = +0.17 (P2 failed); 1.5B has c 9.33 and +1.67.
The P3 failure is not explained by scale: false recall was ~2 of 4 at every size in the offline probe and was not reduced by
size, KL guard or steps (memo, `PREREQUISITES.md` lines 48, 119-123). The shuffled arm e confabulates 4 of 4 and d (adapters
off) 0 of 4, so the learned names, not generic drift, supply it. The missing prerequisite is an abstention signal (both
familiarity gates failed to generalise, 11-03). Expected failure; a method verdict on sleep-without-abstention, not on sleep.
Caveat on P2: b is low at 1.5B (2.83, vs 6.33 at 0.1B), so the hippocampus-on path itself answers poorly here (a 4.50 is
also far below c 9.33); the +1.67 partly reflects a weak intact path, not only a strong sleep. One sampled reply per question.

### Caveats added after skeptic review (2026-11-03)
1. Hippocampal retrieval in arms a/b fetches the right day-1 episode for only 6 of 12 told questions, identically on
   every seed (e.g. "What is my cat called?" recalls the Tomasz best-friend episode). Arm b is therefore capped near 6,
   and KEEP shows that sleep beats a misrouting hippocampus, not a working one.
2. Arm a (hippocampus on + sleep, 4.50) is below arm c (hippocampus off, 9.33) by about 5/12 on every seed: at 1.5B the
   hippocampal route currently HURTS recall. This is the main next problem (fix retrieval/routing at 1.5B).
3. The "shuffled = 0" clause is near-guaranteed by design: deranged answers are scored against the true keys.
4. The sleep timing range should include the shuffled nights: 626-648 s per night overall (632-648 s was the main nights only).
