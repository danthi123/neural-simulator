# Mood iteration 2: positive appraisal fixed; the lesion moves NEGATIVE tone on 6/6 but positive tone on only 3/6 (0.1B, six seeds, 2026-11-03)

**Verdict: NOT PASS on the pre-registered criteria (design doc section 9, frozen in section 10 before the run).**
6 of 7 criteria met; criterion 3 (positive mood vs modulation off, lexical CI above 0) holds on 3/6 seeds (bar 5/6).
What improved over iteration 1: positive events now appraise positive on a HELD-OUT set (5/6 seeds at >= 0.75; s101
0.62), the positive mood is positive on 6/6 (iteration 1: ~0 or negative on s100/s101), and the effect is specific
to the valence pattern against a matched-norm random direction (5/6, paired CI). The tone effect is still one-sided:
negative mood darkens replies on 6/6; positive mood brightens them reliably on 3/6. A lead, not a GO. 0.1B only;
not wired into the live `Brain`; modulation switch default OFF.

## What changed (iteration 2, all frozen before the run)
- Innate primaries balanced (8 bodily + 8 social per sign; seed draws 4 + 4). Iteration 1 drew 8 of 13 from lists
  with 3 social-negative and 6 social-positive items.
- Appraisal = two opponent poles (positive and negative primary centroids), each centred on 6 neutral BODY states,
  read from the deeper half of the layers (6-11). Selected on the DEV pool by a rule written before selection; round 1
  (6 candidates incl. balancing alone) found nothing; round 2 (64 cells, layer subsets) was a declared second fork
  (section 10). Held-out events (8 + 8 + 4, new scenarios) were never used for induction, selection or GAIN.
- Lesion on both valences: one `off` arm (modulation off; identical for any mood by construction, not evidence);
  pos vs off and neg vs off. Random direction with each layer's norm matched, driven by the same mood scalar;
  specificity = paired (pos - neg) - (rand_pos - rand_neg).
- Unchanged: GAIN 16, TAU 4, the 12 test questions x 3 samples, lexical + embedding instruments (lexical lists
  hash-frozen by a test), echo = the revised instrument registered in section 9 and applied to every seed.

## What it shows
- The appraisal fix holds out of sample: held-out positive sign accuracy 1.00 on 5/6 seeds (s101 0.62), negative
  0.88-1.00. Iteration 1's positive accuracy (on non-held-out events) was 0.12-0.88.
- Negative mood through the modulation alone lowers reply sentiment vs off on 6/6 (CI below 0), the embedding
  instrument agrees on both valences 6/6, and the effect is specific to the valence pattern on 5/6 (s100's paired
  specificity CI includes 0). Echo of primary content stays at or below off (+0.03 at most).
- Positive mood: lexical CI above 0 vs off on s42/s43/s44 only; s101 positive but CI includes 0; s100 and s102
  slightly below off. The embedding instrument is above 0 on 6/6 but small (+0.017 to +0.111).

## Why positive is still weak (read from the numbers; not tested)
- Ceiling: the birth register is friendly, so off replies are already positive (lexical 2.5-6.1). The two seeds
  where positive fails have the most positive off baselines (s100 6.13, s102 5.91).
- Perturbation cost: a random pattern of the same norm lowers lexical sentiment on 5/6 seeds for the positive mood
  and 6/6 for the negative mood (mean -1.29 / -2.22). The positive modulation must overcome that cost; relative to
  the random arm it is positive on 6/6 (pos - rand_pos +0.04 to +5.06), but s102 is +0.04.
- Iteration 1's ~0 positive effect on s100/s101 was an appraisal failure (mood ~0); that is fixed. What remains is an
  ACTION asymmetry: one additive pattern at GAIN 16 darkens more easily than it brightens.

## Caveats
- The appraisal method was chosen among 70 cells on 20 dev events; the held-out set is the only fair test of it,
  and it is small (8 + 8 events per seed, same 16 on every seed).
- 0.1B only; tone control is partly an LM property (PREREQUISITES): check 0.4B before attributing.
- Not wired into `Brain.hear`; arousal is read out and does not act; self-report numbers only.
- The smoke test before the run (s42, dev questions, 20 tokens) read the held-out appraisal once; nothing was changed
  after it.

## Reproducibility (note added in integration-final, 2026-11-03)
- The numbers above are read from the six committed JSONs; the run was not repeated from a clean checkout. The runs used
  `--threads 2` on CPU and the weights file recorded in each JSON (`model`, 0.1B g1d); a different thread count or
  weights file can change float rounding and so sampled replies, so a re-run is expected to match in direction, not
  necessarily byte for byte.
- The probe's usage line suggested `--gain 4`; every JSON here was produced at GAIN 16 (the `Affect.GAIN` default and the
  value stored in each JSON). The usage line is corrected to 16.
- The appraisal read-out was selected among 70 dev cells (round 1 + a declared second round); the selection itself is
  recorded in the design doc, not re-derived by `mood_probe.py`, which takes the selected `twopole_deep` read-out as default.

## Claim limits
Only this table is claimed: the modulation lowers sentiment on 6/6 seeds for negative mood; it raises it on 3/6 for
positive mood; a random direction of matched norm also lowers sentiment. The "ceiling" explanation for the weak positive
arm is a reading of the baselines, not a tested cause.
