# 2026-11-03: M1, a CA1-style comparator for confabulation (signal only, 0.1B CPU, six seeds)

Method M1 of `brain/research/2026-11-03-confabulation-next-methods.md` (ranked first): verify the retrieved episode
against the question (retrieve, then check). Capability: Metacognition (`brain/PREREQUISITES.md`: PARTIAL).
Biology: CA1 match/mismatch (Hasselmo & Schnell 1994; Lisman & Grace 2005; Kumaran & Maguire 2007) and strategic
retrieval monitoring (Moscovitch & Melo 1997; Gilboa et al. 2006). Branch `research/confab-m1-1103` off
`research/landing-1103`. Code: `brain/experiments/confab_m1.py`; test `tests/test_brain_confab_m1.py`.

## Pre-registration (committed before any dev, fit or fresh run of this experiment)

**Cheapest decisive test, as the plan specifies: signal only, no new sleep.** The six slept 0.1B brains of abstain-v2
(`_scratch/brainv2/abstain-v2-1103/homes/s<seed>`: 12 facts + the W3 lesson, one night, 240 steps, lr 5e-4, KL 1) are
copied (originals untouched) and read; nothing is stored, nothing slept. Hippocampus on, S_all key, the lesson episode
excluded from retrieval (as abstain-v2), every question read from the same birth state.

**Read-outs (all inside the brain's own cortex and hippocampus; no host text, no reply strings).**
- `F`: S_all familiarity (abstain-v2's gate signal; the baseline M1 must beat).
- `C` (i) cue completion: reinstate the best S_all episode (forced top-1); along "User: <question>", gain_t = log
  p(question token t | reinstated) - log p(t | not reinstated), consolidated synapses ON in both. Does the memory
  predict what is ASKED (attribute included)? Four variants, chosen on DEV only: `C_mean`, `C_max` (all question
  tokens), `C_cmean`, `C_cmin` (the "content" half: tokens whose not-reinstated surprisal is at or above the question's
  median; no word list). Choice: largest pooled DEV AUROC, ties -> `C_cmean`.
- `A_lp` (ii) route agreement, the CA1 comparison of two routes: H = greedy 12-token answer of the reinstated cortex
  with consolidated synapses OFF (episodic route); `A_lp` = mean over H's tokens of log p_C - log p_P, C = consolidated
  ON with nothing reinstated (cortical route), P = pretrained with nothing reinstated. Label-free, continuous.
- `A_bin`: the plan's literal "both name the same learned token": the H and C greedy answers share a learned name.
  Uses the instrument's name list, so it is an analysis read-out for the falsifier, not a wireable signal.
- `AND`: min of the leave-one-seed-out DEV percentiles of the chosen C variant and `A_lp`.
The chosen read-out = largest pooled DEV AUROC among {chosen C, A_lp, AND} (ties: that order). Frozen in `fit.json`
before any fresh read-out is computed.

**Sets.** DEV = abstain-v2's 72 (36 told, 36 never-told), fitting only. FRESH = abstain-v2's fresh v2 (12 told, 24
never incl. 8 untold attributes, 8 general; seen once by these brains in v2's eval, never by any M1 read-out) + fresh
v3 written now (`confab_m1.py` FRESH_TOLD3 / FRESH_NEVER3: 12 told rewordings, 16 untold topics with lexical traps such
as "What's my goldfish called?", "Where does my father work?", 8 untold attributes such as "What instrument does my
sister play?"; disjointness asserted at import). Per seed: 24 told vs 48 never-told (16 untold attributes).
Bars: DEV only, leave-one-seed-out, abstain v1/v2 rule (answer iff signal >= t, max kept-told - kept-never s.t.
kept-told >= 85%).

**Criteria (six seeds 42/43/44/100/101/102, fresh v2+v3 per seed).**
- P1: fresh told-vs-never AUROC of the chosen read-out >= 0.85 on 6/6 seeds.
- P2: untold-attribute subset (16 per seed) gated (below the seed's LOSO bar) >= 75% on 6/6 seeds.
- PASS (a lead, not a GO) = P1 and P2. Only then: rerun abstain-v2 FR+L C1-C3 with M1 as the gate signal.
- FALSIFIED if on >= 5/6 seeds fresh AUROC < 0.75 or not above the same seed's S_all `F` fresh AUROC by >= 0.08.
- (ii) DEAD if `A_bin` is true on never-told questions as often as on told ones (DEV pooled or fresh pooled): the two
  routes share the "my X -> name" schema and are not independent.
- Neither PASS nor FALSIFIED = inconclusive. v2 and v3 AUROCs are also reported separately; general questions are
  reported (gated count) only.

**Scale.** The plan asks for the DEV signal at 0.1B and 1.5B. 1.5B needs the GPU, which is busy (not to be used by
this run): 1.5B is NOT run here. A 0.1B failure is then a verdict at 0.1B only (in-context ceiling 6/12), not on the
method (`brain/PREREQUISITES.md`); the 1.5B run is the next step either way.

Commands (each under `tools/memcap.sh 6 --` once `tools/mem_ok.sh 6` allows, at most 2 at once):
`python -m brain.experiments.confab_m1 dev --seed S --out D/dev_sS.json`; `... fit --dir D --out D/fit.json`;
`... fresh --seed S --out D/fresh_sS.json`; `... report --dir D --bars D/fit.json --out D/report.json`,
D = `brain/experiments/results/2026-11-03-confab-m1/`.
