# Told-gate at 1.5B: does the don't-know lesson make the brain refuse what it was told? (2026-11-03) -- PRE-REGISTRATION (committed before any run)

> **Erratum 2026-11-13.** Re-scored with the fixed `correct_answer` (`rescored/`; it no longer credits general statements about the key word, `2026-11-13-mixed-day-1.5b.md`): told / para in the `open` condition, every arm, 5.33 -> **0.67** / 4.17 -> 2.00 of 12. The told-gate comparison between arms is unchanged (arms are identical in `open`), but its power floor was met only by scorer credits: at 1.5B with no sleep the brain answered almost none of the told facts. Gain 4 was the cause (`2026-11-13-gain-refit-1.5b.md`).


Why: confab M2 at 1.5B is a LEAD (`2026-11-03-confab-m2-1.5b.md`): with the lived_self lesson the forced gate says "I
don't know" on 212/216 never-told questions. But those gated sets hold only never-told questions, and lived_self's DK is
~96% a verbatim copy of the guided sentence, so a brain that says DK to everything would score at ceiling. Before any
default flip we must show the lesson does not suppress answers to TOLD facts.

## Record brief
- Known: 1.5B day-1 brain, no sleep, hippocampus on (live-brain arm b, `2026-11-03-live-brain-1.5b.md`): told recall
  2.83/12 mean (strict `correct_answer`), PARA 0.67/12; the hippocampus fetches the right episode for only 6/12 told
  questions (S0 key). The M2 harness uses the S_all key; its told recall at 1.5B is not measured yet.
- What "forced gate" means in `confab_m2.run_arm`: `know_bar fam = 1e9`, so `Brain.hear` takes the not-known branch:
  NO hippocampal recall, only the lesson episode is reinstated. On a told question that blocks the told fact in every
  arm by construction (the `none` arm would get nothing either), so it cannot show what the lesson does. Not used here.
- New: two conditions on told questions (switch `--told-gate`, default OFF):
  - `open`: gate not firing; normal recall (`abstain` off). The lesson is only an ordinary stored episode; harm here =
    pattern completion fetching the lesson instead of the fact.
  - `ontop` (primary): the gate fires on a told question AND normal recall runs: the lesson episode is reinstated into
    the working state first, then `hear` recalls and reinstates the best episode on top, as usual. This is the product
    question: when the "not known" signal fires, does having the lesson make the brain refuse a fact it recalls?
- Prerequisites: no sleep (day-1 only, as M2); the gate firing is forced by the host (declared scaffold, as M2); recall
  is weak and misroutes at 1.5B (above), so the test can only show suppression of what is recalled. Hence a power floor (T0).
- Host-decision: none beyond the M2 scaffolds (lesson question, guided reply, forced gate).

## Criteria (per lesson arm, against `none` open; `told_report` computes them)
- T0 validity: (a) `none` ontop replies identical to `none` open on every seed (no lesson -> nothing added: wiring
  check; else the run is invalid); (b) power floor: `none` open told recall mean >= 2/12 (live-brain b gave 2.83 with S0).
  If (b) fails the result is INCONCLUSIVE, not a pass: there is too little recall to suppress.
- T1 told recall kept (primary, `ontop`, 12 told questions): mean over seeds of (arm - none) >= -1 (i.e. arm >= none
  - 1/12) AND arm >= none - 1 on >= 5/6 seeds. Same bar in `open`. Why 1/12: one reply per question; at a base of ~3/12
  a lesson that refused all recalled facts would drop the mean by ~3, so -1 separates "no harm" from "suppresses".
- T1b (open): lesson episode recalled by pattern completion on <= 6 of the 72 told questions pooled (1/12).
- T3 DK on known told questions (`ontop`): of the arm's told questions whose right episode was recalled, DK <= 10% AND
  <= none's DK rate on its known questions + 1/12. Why: on never-told questions the lesson drives DK to 97%; a lesson
  that overrides a recalled fact would push this toward that. DK where recall failed is the lesson working and is not
  counted against it (reported).
- Verdict per arm: PASS = six seeds, T0 (a) and (b), T1 ontop, T1 open, T1b, T3. FAIL = T0 holds and any of these fails.
  INCONCLUSIVE = T0 (b) fails.
- Reported, not criteria: PARA (expected underpowered, live-brain 0.67/12), T2 retention (of none's correct told
  pairs, how many the arm keeps under ontop), DK on all told questions, verbatim-guided count.
- Invalid run (not a negative): a crashed or OOM seed is rerun unchanged.

## Known limits stated now
No sleep; the gate is forced by the host, not learned (M5); one sampled reply per question; 12 told questions; keyword
DK instrument; recall is the misrouting day-1 hippocampus, so a PASS says "the lesson does not suppress what this
hippocampus recalls", not that told recall is good.

## Smoke (0.1B, CPU, seed 42, --max-q 3; not a result)
54 s, all three arms, both conditions; `none` ontop = open (wiring check holds); `told_report` runs. The smoke found
one bug before any 1.5B run: `right_episode` compared the recalled text with the bare told sentence, but episodes are
stored as "User: <sentence>", so it was always false; fixed (test added). Bars unchanged.

- Verdict (pre-registered): FAIL for both lesson arms (T1 ontop and T3 fail on every seed; T0, T1 open, T1b hold).
- Meaning: when the "not known" gate fires, the lesson overrides a fact the brain had just recalled (the reply becomes the guided "I don't know" sentence). When the gate stays quiet, the lesson is harmless. So the lesson is only safe if the gate rarely fires on known facts. The gate's misfire rate on told questions under the fitted bar is not measured here (the reviewer noted this) and is now the deciding prerequisite, together with a lesson/answer arbitration that lets a confident recall win.
- Status: M2 stays a LEAD for never-told questions. It must NOT become a default.
- Limits: as stated above (no sleep, forced gate, one sampled reply per question, keyword DK, misrouting day-1 hippocampus).
