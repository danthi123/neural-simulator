## What it shows
- The ceiling reading of iteration 2 is supported at 0.1B: with a mid-scale off baseline (+0.61), the same frozen
  modulation brightens replies on 6/6 against both the null and OFF (iteration 2, friendly register: 3/6 vs OFF).
- The "any perturbation lowers sentiment" reading does not generalise: the null's effect vs OFF is ~0 at 0.1B on this
  register and POSITIVE at 0.4B (+1.7 both signs). At 0.4B the null moves replies the way the positive mood does, so
  positive mood is indistinguishable from a random push of the same size; negative mood is still specific (6/6).
- The asymmetry is therefore size-dependent, not a fixed property of an additive pattern.

## Caveats (read before citing)
- **Negative tone is partly a refusal mode.** Replies containing "I'm sorry" / "can't help": 0.1B neg 35/144 vs off
  9/144, pos 0/144, random 3-19/144; 0.4B neg 48/144 vs off 8/144. "sorry" is a NEG instrument word. Exploratory,
  NOT pre-registered: dropping the word "sorry" leaves neg-null at -0.8..-4.6 (0.1B) and -0.9..-2.6 (0.4B), all
  negative, about half the registered size. A darker reply and a refusal are both counted as "darker".
- Echo (C6) on this register: the off arm itself contains a primary content word in 0.12-0.50 of replies ("help",
  "alone", "cold" are in the revised echo list and natural for "a day alone at home", "winter"); with 24 rows one reply
  moves the rate by 0.04. The 0.1B failures (+0.104, +0.125) are mostly "help"/"cold"/"pain"; under the registered
  instrument they count. Not revised.
- 24 paired rows per seed (iteration 2: 36), 40 tokens; one register; held-out appraisal set is 8 + 8 events.
- At 0.4B the held-out POSITIVE appraisal drops (0.62 on s44/s101) with the deep half = layers 12-23 by the same rule;
  the appraisal method was selected at 0.1B only.
- One GAIN (16) at both sizes; the 0.4B dev showed GAIN 32 gives a much larger effect at an NLL cost (ratio 1.66).
