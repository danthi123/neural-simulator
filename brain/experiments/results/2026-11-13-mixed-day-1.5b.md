## The scorer credited general statements about the key word (instrument skeptic)

Reading the replies, many of f's "correct" answers do not answer the question: "Oslo is the capital of Norway.",
"Biscuit is a type of cookie, not a name.", "Marguerite is a French name.", "Lyon is a city in France.".
`strict_scorer.correct_answer` only needed the key word, so it credited them. The scorer now also rejects a reply
that opens with the key as the subject of is/are/was and addresses no one (`is_generic`, tested in
`brain/tests/test_strict_scorer.py`). Arm c is never affected (0 credits removed in any run). It is strict on a few
borderline replies ("Pixel is a cat.", "Pixel is a male cat."); counting every generic reply that names the asked
subject as right changes no conclusion below.

Re-scored (same replies, `table_rescored.txt`):

**M1 fails (f - c on new facts 0.67 < 1.5): VOID by the registration.** The hippocampus did fire on 28 of 36 new

## The same defect in the earlier 1.5B runs (re-scored, same replies)

- 11-13 confidence gate (`results/2026-11-13-confidence-gate-1.5b/table_rescored.txt`): f 7.67 -> **5.67**; c 9.33
  and h 9.17 unchanged. G0-G4 still hold. The cost of reinstating into a slept cortex is larger than reported.
- 11-05 routing fix (`results/2026-11-03-hippo-routing-1.5b/live/table_rescored.txt`): a 4.50 -> 2.83,
  b 2.83 -> **0.50**, f 7.67 -> 5.67, **g (fix, no sleep) 4.83 -> 0.50**. F5 (g - b >= 2) now **fails** (0.50 vs
  0.50). With no sleep, the 1.5B brain answers almost none of the 12 facts told that day, whether the hippocampus
  routes right (g) or wrong (b). Of g's 72 told replies: 3 right, 26 general statements about the right key, 25 other
  wrong replies after a recall, 18 nothing recalled.

So at 1.5B every told-fact answer in these runs comes from sleep (c 9.33). Reinstatement makes the right word
available, but the cortex then talks about the word instead of answering with it.

## Exploratory, not registered (scope skeptic)

`cue` (cortical cue familiarity) separates slept from new told rows (AUC 0.98) but is at chance for c-right vs
c-wrong within the slept set (0.49): it detects which half was slept, and its 11-13 bar does not transfer (told 5.50
fixed vs 7.0 refit here). `mean_logp` does carry within-slept information (AUC 0.78). None of this is a lead while
the hippocampus cannot answer new facts.

## Verdict and next method

Registered: NOT A LEAD. With the fixed scorer: VOID (M1). Brain defaults unchanged. Skeptics: instrument REFUTED
(the scorer), power NARROWED (h = f within noise; seed bootstrap of h - f about [-0.67, +0.33]), scope NARROWED
(cue is a novelty detector).

The capability gap is upstream of the gate: the hippocampus must make the cortex ANSWER with a fact told today.
The reinstatement gain (4) and the S_all size bar were both fitted with the old scorer (`hippo_gain_probe.py`,
`2026-11-03-sall-live-bar-refit.md`), so they were partly tuned to produce general statements. Next: refit the
reinstatement gain on unslept 1.5B reads with the fixed scorer (dev bank, held-out facts), pre-registered against
b and g re-scored here (0.50). Other results scored with `correct_answer` (0.1B live-brain, weekly, told-gate) are
not re-scored yet.
