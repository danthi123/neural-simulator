## What it shows
- **Scale did not move it.** Fresh C_mean is 0.66 at 0.1B and 0.62 at 1.5B. Plain familiarity F is 0.70 at both.
  The 0.1B result blamed the 0.1B reading limit. That explanation is now refuted: the episodic route reads better at
  1.5B, and the comparator is no better.
- **The failure is the untold attribute of a told topic.**
  - Every read-out scores told vs untold attribute at 0.40-0.59.
  - "Where did my brother learn the cello?" retrieves the cello memory, which predicts the question's words well:
    C_mean averages 0.39 on untold-attribute rows against 0.37 on told rows. G is only partly raised (mean 0.16
    against 0.30 told and -0.52 for other never-told rows). This row's G is -0.78.
  - The episodic route answers "My brother plays the cello." The cortical route answers "Your brother learned the cello
    at the Novacello Academy" (seed 101, `fresh_s101.json`, `m1_s101.log.txt`).
  - Nothing in these read-outs asks whether the memory contains the asked-for attribute. The 1.5B cortex completes
    "brother + cello" as a good match.
- **The contrast against the runner-up (C2off) is worse than the raw score.** Told topics have no close second memory,
  so the contrast carries topic distinctness, not answer content.
- **Retrieval misses.** On 22/144 fresh told rows (15%) the reinstated episode is not the told fact's. On the
  right-episode rows only: F 0.77, G 0.76, C_mean 0.62. Neither reaches 0.85, and C_mean does not rise. For 27/96
  untold-attribute rows the reinstated episode is not the topic's own, so the cello mechanism covers about 72% of them.
- **The scale comparison is not like for like.** 0.1B had the W3 lesson, a 240-step KL 1 night, and twice the DEV
  rows. Fresh F and C_mean are still the same order at both sizes.
- A verdict on M1 as specified (C_mean / A_lp / AND), at 0.1B and 1.5B. F and G are at the same level (about 0.70)
  and neither is a lead. The capability stays open. Metacognition stays PARTIAL.
