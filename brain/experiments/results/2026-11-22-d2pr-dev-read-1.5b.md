**Verdict on the method:** loss-prioritised replay lowers the stock self-reply leak at the reply level (PR-l, robust
over nights), but the loss weighting concentrates replay on a few high-loss episodes, which the registered engagement
bar forbids. Neither arm goes to TEST. Next method (its own prereg, DEV): PR-l with a per-episode share cap so replay
cannot concentrate more than the strength-only weighting does, the day-1-2 window named, fp32 for every arm.
