  read positive events as positive. Positive mood is barely produced on 4/6 seeds, so the positive arm tests a

  every seed and its push is still too weak to beat the null on half of them. The dev rule capped GAIN by fluency

- Decision (section 15): C3 and C4 both fail, so this is a NO-GO for the additive matrix-memory bias at 1.5B. That
  is a verdict on the method, and on the positive side it is confounded by the appraisal fault.
