## What the transcripts show (two compounding mechanisms, both measured)

1. **A hub episode forms by use-strengthening on wrong fetches (D1 compounds).** Recall adds USE_BOOST 0.5 to any
   fetched episode (`hippocampus.py:153-154`), right or wrong. In s311 "My dog is called Bramble." (told L4) was
   fetched on most lived turns; after L7 it has uses 10, strength 6.0, the strongest non-permanent episode by 2.4x
   (store dump of the trunk home), and it is fetched 86 times in the life's transcript, for name, cat, job, car and
   open prompts. s312 has its own hubs (barber Rashid 46, the late bus 41, Marguerite 41). Even with no sleep at all
   (L-nosleep, store 51) the intact brain gets 0/6 H on the final morning, wrong fetch 6/8: the hippocampus alone
   cannot pick the right episode out of 51 under S0 + strength weighting.
2. **The slept adapters grow without bound and speech degrades (D2 and language compound).** The adapter norm rises
   about 50 per night, linearly (7x by night 7; the design's per-seed kill rule is 3x). By L7 the slept brain's
   lived replies loop and drift ("I think autumn is my favourite season." on three unrelated turns, "That sounds like a
   good way to get fresh bread." to a mail-relay incident), it answers H questions in the first person ("My cat is
   called Pixel.", "I drive a red Volvo."), and "What is my sister's name?" -> "My name is Meridian.". The never-slept
   brain on the same L7 stays fluent (a full fox story, a correct chlorophyll answer) but has no memory of the user.

So in this life, sleep buys the H facts (hippocampus-off still 2-5/6 where no-sleep is 0/6) and costs fluency. The cost
grows each night. Recall gets worse each day because it keeps strengthening whatever it fetched.

## Next (method, after all Stage-0 seeds are read)
Two candidates, each on dev seeds first, each with its own record check:
- M-use: strengthen a fetched episode only when the fetch is selective (its score clears the runner-up by a margin) or
  the reply uses it. Biology: reconsolidation strengthens a trace that was retrieved and confirmed, not any trace that
  was active. Kills the rich-get-richer loop.
- M-scale: nightly synaptic downscaling of the adapters (SHY), i.e. a norm homeostat that keeps the total adapter
  norm bounded, so each night adds new learning without the old drift piling up. The old-track downscaling (sim/,
  r2 NO-GO) was a different substrate and goal (fading weak facts); not evidence here.
