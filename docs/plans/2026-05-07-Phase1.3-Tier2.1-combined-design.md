# Phase 1.3 + Tier 2.1 combined: hippocampus consolidation at synonym scale

**Status:** DESIGN
**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Goal:** Validate that McClelland 1995 / Buzsaki 2013 complementary
learning systems theory holds for the Tier 2.1 8-word synonym vocab,
not just the Tier 1 4-word primary vocab.

## Background

Two validated single-axis results:

1. **Phase 1.3 (3/3 PASS, mean 96% hippo-OFF retention, 2026-05-07).**
   Tier 1 architecture, 4-word vocab. Hippocampus consolidates memory
   into cortex via SWR sleep replay. After consolidation, hippocampus
   can be lesioned and cortex still recalls the vocab.

2. **Tier 2.1 BREAKTHROUGH (W→A 5/6, A→W 6/6 aligned, A→W mean 63.7%,
   2026-05-06).** Tier 2.1 v4 scale-up architecture, 8-word synonym
   vocab ({north,up}, {east,right}, {south,down}, {west,left}). Each
   motor pool develops sub-population structure where different
   synonyms activate different sub-populations.

Open question: **does sleep replay consolidate synonym structure into
cortex, or does the more complex 8-word binding pattern require
hippocampus permanently?**

Biology prediction (CLS theory): consolidation should work across
vocab sizes; the mechanism is content-agnostic.

## Hypothesis

H1 (CLS theory): Sleep replay consolidates Tier 2.1 synonym vocab
into cortex with similar retention to Tier 1. Hippo-OFF retention
should be >= 80% for both primary AND synonym words.

H0 (null): Synonym structure is too complex for cortex-alone recall;
hippo-OFF retention drops below 50% for synonym words specifically.

H_alt (partial): Primary words consolidate well (matches Phase 1.3
4-word result); synonym words show lower retention because they share
motor sub-populations with primaries.

## Architecture

Reuse Phase 1.3 SWR replay machinery + Tier 2.1 v4 scale-up:
- n_lang_input = 4096 (Tier 2.1 scale)
- n_motor_per_action = 1000 (Tier 2.1 scale)
- n_motor_fs_per_action = 120 (Tier 2.1 scale)
- All 8 synonym words trained via embodied Hebbian co-firing
- Hippocampus enabled with SWR replay every N awake events
- Plasticity gates: `set_awake_gates`/`set_sleep_gates` from
  text_minimal_isolation

Consolidation schedule:
- Awake: 400 events/word × 8 words = 3200 events (matches Tier 2.1
  validated config)
- Consolidation interval: every 4 events/word (= 100 sleep cycles
  total)
- Sleep SWR events: 200 per cycle (matches Phase 1.3 default)

Wall-clock estimate: ~30 min single seed on RTX 3090
(Tier 2.1 ~10 min + 100 sleep cycles × ~12s = ~30 min).

## Eval protocol

After consolidation training:
1. Lesion hippocampus (`freeze_all_gates`, then disable hippo region)
2. Test all 8 synonym words via baseline-vs-driven delta
3. Report:
   - Per-word accuracy
   - Per-action accuracy (does motor_N still get hit by both
     "north" and "up"?)
   - Primary-vs-synonym split
   - Retention ratio (post-lesion / pre-lesion)

Baseline (no consolidation): same training but without sleep cycles.
Hippo-OFF retention should be NEAR ZERO if consolidation is doing
real work.

## Decision criteria

GO (publish as biology validation):
- 3/3 seeds at >= 80% retention for primary words
- 3/3 seeds at >= 60% retention for synonym words
- Both > control (no-consolidation baseline) by >= 30pp

PARTIAL (interesting but caveated):
- Primary retention >= 80% but synonym retention < 60%
- Indicates content-dependent consolidation; biology research
  question

NO-GO (architectural insight):
- Both primary and synonym retention < 50%
- Indicates Tier 2.1 sub-population binding doesn't survive
  consolidation; need a different replay strategy

## Risks

1. **Tier 2.1 sub-population structure may not replay cleanly.** Phase
   1.3's SWR replay drives random subsets of cortex; if motor sub-pops
   are sparse, the replay may not target them well. Mitigation: tune
   `swr_drive_pA` for the bigger motor pools (Phase 1.3 default
   100pA may be too weak for 1000-neuron motor pools).

2. **Consolidation may average synonyms together.** If sleep replay
   re-activates language_input randomly, both "north" and "up" features
   may consolidate to a unified sub-pop, losing the synonym
   discrimination. Mitigation: track per-action-AND-per-synonym
   accuracy separately to detect this.

3. **30 min wall clock may be too long for tight iteration.** Mitigation:
   add `--smoke` flag with reduced events for fast (~5 min) sanity check
   before full runs.

## Out of scope

- 12-word vocab (Tier 2.1 also validated 12-word, but adds complexity)
- Cross-trial interference (Phase 1.4 territory)
- Two-word phrases (Tier 2.3 territory, currently architecture-limited)

## Why this matters

If H1 confirms, the project demonstrates that:
- Biology-grounded continual learning (Phase 1.4) +
- Biology-grounded consolidation (Phase 1.3) +
- Biology-grounded synonym binding (Tier 2.1) all compose

That's the scaffolding for "vocabulary growth + memory persistence
without backprop, biology-grounded all the way down" — the foundational
Path F premise that makes Project Nord scale-up worth pursuing.
