# Structural-Mechanism Map — the eight faculties, by role in the whole brain

This is the low-level structural map of the sim-brain, faculty by faculty. Each faculty is described by its ROLE IN THE WHOLE LOOP (what the rest of the brain needs from it), not by a narrow benchmark; then the biological mechanism(s) that realize that role, with references preserved verbatim; then what the repository actually has (with findings cited), where the current implementation is a template/proxy rather than a grounded/emergent mechanism, and the concrete cheapest-first grounded next build.

It is a companion to the PROJECT CHARTER (`docs/plans/2026-08-02-PROJECT-CHARTER-grounded-emergence-realignment.md`) and to `HANDOFF.md` — the charter states the realigned mission and the P1-P7 principles those documents reference; this map is the per-faculty structural detail an incoming agent needs to act. The audit-level scaffold ledger (global shortcut backlog) lives with the charter/handoff; the per-faculty burn-down targets are given here under each faculty's "Scaffolds in use".

The eight faculties: (1) grounded language + speech-as-action; (2) world-model + perception; (3) episodic + semantic memory + consolidation; (4) affect + emotion; (5) motivation + curiosity + drive; (6) self-model + metacognition + honesty; (7) learning / plasticity / credit assignment / growth; (8) the embodied integration loop + action selection. Read the Cross-cutting section last — it names the threads that recur across all eight (the missing-companion-process pattern, and the deep-credit boundary that several faculties deliberately route around).

## 1. grounded language + speech-as-action (comprehension + generation)

### Role in the whole

Language is the brain's channel for turning an INTERNAL STATE into a world-changing ACTION and turning others' speech back into a model update — it is not a benchmark to pass. To serve its role in the whole loop it must do five concrete things. (1) INTENT-IN: take a preverbal message that arises INSIDE the brain — a communicative goal composed from affect (mood/surprise), curiosity (an uncertainty it wants to resolve), the world-model, the self-model, and a model of what the interlocutor needs to know — and be CAUSED BY that state, not by a query string or a fixed input cue. (2) GROUND: every content word must be internal reference — the same neural assembly that fires when the brain perceives/imagines/acts-on the referent (Pulvermüller: 'dog'-the-word shares cells with
dog-the-percept and dog-the-action), so meaning is the brain's own representation, not a corpus co-occurrence code. (3) REALIZE-AS-ACTION: emit the utterance as a motor sequence (articulation) whose FORM (word choice, order, grammar) is produced by the brain, with a consequence in the world/social partner. (4) COMPREHEND: map heard speech onto those SAME grounded representations plus the conversation/interlocutor model, producing a world/self update and a prediction error where expectation and input mismatch. (5) CLOSE THE LOOP + REPORT HONESTLY: the speech act's consequence ('was I understood? did the world/partner change as I intended?') must return as reward/surprise that reshapes BOTH message-formation and realization — i.e. language is learned FROM communicating
with a contingent partner — and when the familiarity/grounding monitor reads low the faculty must express that uncertainty or ask, rather than fabricate (emergent from the self-model, not a bolted gate).

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Hickok-Poeppel dual-stream speech architecture** — Two parallel cortical pathways from auditory cortex: a DORSAL sensorimotor stream (pSTG -> arcuate fasciculus -> IFG/Broca) mapping sound to articulation (damage -> conduction/repetition aphasia), and a VENTRAL semantic stream (mid/inferior temporal -> anterior temporal lobe) mapping sound to MEANING (damage -> semantic aphasia). Comprehension needs ventral; speaking needs dorsal. In-repo the dorsal sound->motor idea exists but the ventral sound->grounded-meaning stream is essentially absent (embeddings are direction-keyed Gaussian, not grounded). — *Ref:* Hickok G & Poeppel D (2007), 'The cortical organization of speech processing', Nat Rev Neurosci 8(5):393-402; Saur D et al. (2008), 'Ventral and dorsal pathways
for language', PNAS 105(46):18035-18040
- **Pulvermuller embodied action-word ensembles** — Word meaning is carried by distributed cell assemblies spanning Wernicke's, Broca's and motor/somatosensory cortex; the SAME neurons fire for perceiving the word and executing the action, bound by Hebbian co-activation during embodied acquisition. Action verbs map somatotopically onto motor cortex ('kick'->leg, 'grasp'->hand). This is the mechanistic definition of grounding = internal reference, and the target the project's separate 10-neuron motor pools do NOT yet meet (needs overlapping ensembles). — *Ref:* Pulvermuller F (2005), 'Brain mechanisms linking language and action', Nat Rev Neurosci 6(7):576-582; Hauk O, Johnsrude I, Pulvermuller F (2004), 'Somatotopic representation of action words in human motor and
premotor cortex', Neuron 41(2):301-307
- **Levelt speaking model (intention -> conceptualizer -> formulator -> articulator)** — Production is a pipeline from a communicative INTENTION: the conceptualizer does macroplanning (select + order the message, language-independent) and microplanning, emitting a PREVERBAL MESSAGE; the formulator grammatically and phonologically encodes it; the articulator speaks. The synthesis (what to combine/contrast/foreground) happens in the MESSAGE, before words. The project's current design skips the message and asks a word-generator (or a query parser) to invent the plan — the precise inversion this faculty must correct. — *Ref:* Levelt WJM (1989), 'Speaking: From Intention to Articulation', MIT Press; Indefrey P & Levelt WJM (2004), 'The spatial and temporal signatures of word
production components', Cognition 92(1-2):101-144
- **Hagoort Memory-Unification-Control (MUC)** — Three components active in normal language use: MEMORY (posterior temporal MTG/STG lexical store of form-meaning pairs), UNIFICATION (inferior frontal gyrus/Broca combining lexical entries into syntactic/semantic structure), and CONTROL (dlPFC selecting among alternatives, holding task set). Compositional grammar lives in Unification — largely MISSING in-repo (EMERGE-23 uses fixed NOUN-VERB-NOUN frames; connectives are host templates). — *Ref:* Hagoort P (2014), 'Nodes and networks in the neural architecture for language: Broca's region and beyond', Curr Opin Neurobiol 28:136-141
- **Mirror-system / motor grounding of language (gesture-to-speech)** — Broca's region overlaps the mirror-neuron system; language plausibly evolved from a manual/orofacial action-recognition system, so speech comprehension and production share a sensorimotor substrate that grounds words in action understanding. Supports realizing speech AS action and grounding content in the brain's own motor/perceptual codes rather than in text statistics. — *Ref:* Rizzolatti G & Arbib MA (1998), 'Language within our grasp', Trends Neurosci 21(5):188-194
- **Kuhl social-gating / contingent-interaction language learning** — Infants learn phonetic and word structure ONLY from contingent SOCIAL interaction, not from non-social audio/video of the same input (the 'social gating' hypothesis). Word-meaning binding requires shared reference/joint attention (Tomasello), not passive corpus exposure. This is the biological warrant for training language from a contingent teacher-as-caregiver and against the isolated corpus-predictor — and it is exactly what the repo's arcA contingent-teacher atom tests. — *Ref:* Kuhl PK (2007), 'Is speech learning gated by the social brain?', Dev Sci 10(1):110-120; Kuhl PK (2004), 'Early language acquisition: cracking the speech code', Nat Rev Neurosci 5(11):831-843; Tomasello M (2003),
'Constructing a Language', Harvard
- **Prediction-in-language / production as active inference** — Comprehension and production are unified by forward models: speakers and listeners predict upcoming input and each other's utterances, and mismatch (prediction error) drives both processing and learning. Casts speech as an ACTION taken to reduce expected surprise / achieve a communicative goal — the predict-and-act-in-a-world objective (P1) rather than next-token corpus mimicry. — *Ref:* Pickering MJ & Garrod S (2013), 'An integrated theory of language production and comprehension', Behav Brain Sci 36(4):329-347; Friston K & Frith C (2015), 'Active inference, communication and hermeneutics', Cortex 68:129-143
- **Songbird vocal sensorimotor learning (tutor + auditory-feedback error)** — Birdsong is the cleanest animal model of speech-as-a-learned-motor-action: a juvenile hears a tutor template, then shapes its own vocal motor sequence by minimizing the error between its auditory feedback and the memorized template, via a basal-ganglia (Area X) reinforcement pathway. A concrete biological blueprint for generation as a feedback-shaped motor sequence learned from a social tutor — directly parallel to the e-prop-on-spikes production learning the repo has begun. — *Ref:* Doupe AJ & Kuhl PK (1999), 'Birdsong and human speech: common themes and mechanisms', Annu Rev Neurosci 22:567-631

### Where it's a template vs grounded

The faculty grounds CONTENT but not INTENT or MEANING, and it grounds it in hand-built codes — so it is still fundamentally the isolated-corpus-predictor / cue->word associator the charter names as the wrong turn. Four concrete gaps. (1) INTENT is external, not internal: the trigger to speak is a host-parsed query string ('what does dog eat?') or a given input cue (the subject/verb are handed to the frame renderer); nothing generates speech from the brain's OWN state (affect, curiosity, world/self-model). EMERGE-23 and full-frame speech both go cue->utterance; the grounded integration goes query->gated-fact->render. WHAT to say and WHY are supplied from outside the language faculty — that is the template signature. (2) MEANING/comprehension is thin: content words map to
concept POOLS, but those pools are largely hand-allocated sparse codes (EMERGE-23's 3-block POS/content/family codes; direction-keyed Gaussian embeddings), and Pulvermuller's 'same neurons for word AND action' is only partially wired (separate 10-neuron motor pools, not overlapping ensembles). There is no ventral sound->grounded-meaning stream (catalog G.24 MISSING) — comprehension does not update a world-model. (3) The FLUENCY layer (Qwen2.5-0.5B / WKV / TinyStories 21M) is a transformer trained by BPTT to predict a text corpus divorced from the brain's grounding — the exact trap — bolted on and gated/verified from OUTSIDE, not emergent from the brain expressing a state. The WKV de-risk shows only the task-format ADAPTER is reservoir-shaped; the pretrained core is
still BPTT-corpus. (4) GRAMMAR and HONESTY are templates: grammar is a fixed NOUN-VERB-NOUN frame or a host discourse-plan (connectives/aggregation authored in host code, FRAME_LEXICON), not emergent Unification; the no-confab moat is a host VERIFY re-parse-and-reject + a gate, not an emergent self/familiarity-model property. NET: content-grounding is genuinely validated and brain-native (this is the real asset); intent-grounding, meaning/comprehension-grounding, learned-from-communicating, and emergent grammar are the grounded-emergent gaps.

## 2. world-model + perception (grounded, predictive)

### Role in the whole

This faculty must supply the INTERNAL REFERENTS that language and thought point at — not "recognize objects to pass a decode test," but hand the rest of the brain codes with the right structure to bind to. Concretely it owes the whole loop four things: (1) a ventral "what" code whose SIMILARITY GEOMETRY matches real stimulus similarity (same-category objects near, different far), stable enough under nuisance variation that word↔concept binding, category inheritance, and the graded/no-confab moat can ride it reliably — this is the substrate that makes a word MEAN something internal rather than co-occur with a token; (2) a dorsal "where"/orienting signal that drives attention and action selection; (3) a PREDICTIVE / forward signal (s,a→s′) so the brain can anticipate,
generate prediction-error for curiosity, and later imagine/simulate — the perceptual driver of the affect and curiosity faculties; (4) all of it SELF-STABILIZED (sparse, selective, gain-controlled) so downstream readouts inherit clean codes instead of having to re-normalize. In the short-timescale (roadmap Stage 1) the load-bearing deliverable is (1)+(4): unsupervised category discovery from real perception that is stable/selective enough to (a) bind to first words, (b) carry category similarity for inheritance, and (c) receive a core-affect valence tag from reward context.

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Oriented simple/complex cells + sparse coding (V1 front end)** — Hubel-Wiesel oriented receptive fields, Gabor-shaped, phase-pooled into position/phase-invariant complex cells; the RF bank can EMERGE unsupervised from a sparse-coding objective over natural images rather than being hand-set — the grounded (vs template) version of our host Gabor formula. — *Ref:* Hubel & Wiesel 1962 (J Physiol, cat striate receptive fields); Jones & Palmer 1987 (J Neurophysiol, 2D Gabor fit); Olshausen & Field 1996 (Nature 381:607, 'Emergence of simple-cell receptive field properties by learning a sparse code for natural images')
- **Ventral-stream untangling toward invariant object identity** — Up the V1→V2→V4→IT hierarchy, object-identity manifolds are progressively 'untangled' into position/scale-tolerant, linearly-separable codes (Tanaka IT columns). This is the target our deployed cortex_v2→IT STDP path FAILS to reach (found inert). — *Ref:* DiCarlo, Zoccolan & Rust 2012 (Neuron 73:415, 'How does the brain solve visual object recognition?'); Tanaka 1996 (Annu Rev Neurosci 19:109, 'Inferotemporal cortex and object vision')
- **Learning invariance from temporal continuity (trace rule)** — Features that co-occur across a short time window (an object moving/transforming) bind into one invariant unit via a slow post-synaptic trace. This is the record-validated route to invariant codes in-repo (EMERGE-50 GO), unlike the inert feedforward STDP hierarchy. — *Ref:* Földiák 1991 (Neural Computation 3:194, 'Learning invariance from transformation sequences')
- **Divisive normalization (the missing companion process #1)** — Cortical neurons divide their drive by the pooled activity of their neighbours — a canonical gain-control that keeps codes selective across contrast/clutter. Absent from the deployed ventral path, which is why it jumps dead→saturated with no selective operating point. — *Ref:* Carandini & Heeger 2012 (Nat Rev Neurosci 13:51, 'Normalization as a canonical neural computation'); Heeger 1992 (Vis Neurosci)
- **Homeostatic synaptic scaling / intrinsic plasticity (missing companion #2)** — Neurons multiplicatively rescale synaptic strengths and adjust intrinsic excitability to hold a target firing rate — the slow process that would pin V1→IT at a stable sparse regime instead of dead-or-saturated. Primitive exists in sim but is off for this path. — *Ref:* Turrigiano 2008 (Cell 135:422, 'The self-tuning neuron: synaptic scaling of excitatory synapses'); Desai, Rutherford & Turrigiano 1999 (Nat Neurosci)
- **Predictive coding (hierarchical error minimization)** — Higher areas predict lower-area activity; only the residual error propagates up. Provides both a representation-learning objective and the prediction-error signal curiosity/affect consume. In-repo the local update provably tracks the backprop gradient (cos 0.995) but PC-as-a-learner is unresolved at cheap config. — *Ref:* Rao & Ballard 1999 (Nat Neurosci 2:79, 'Predictive coding in the visual cortex'); Whittington & Bogacz 2017 (Neural Computation, PC local update ≈ backprop gradient)
- **Predictive cognitive map (spatial world-model)** — Hippocampal/entorhinal place+grid codes form a predictive successor-representation map of space/state — the 'where' half of a grounded world-model and the bridge from perception to a navigable, imaginable model of the environment. — *Ref:* O'Keefe & Dostrovsky 1971 (Brain Res, place cells); Stachenfeld, Botvinick & Gershman 2017 (Nat Neurosci 20:1643, 'The hippocampus as a predictive map'); Whittington et al. 2020 (Cell, TEM)

### Where it's a template vs grounded

The CATEGORY-DISCOVERY + grounding path is genuinely grounded (codes track real Gabor/V1 visual similarity, RSA 0.99, and per-image scramble collapses inheritance — the structure IS visual, not injected). That is the strong, honest part. The gaps: (1) V1 RF structure is a host-designed Gabor FORMULA (`apply_v1_gabor_weights` overwrites the pathway), not developmentally self-organized — a template of the innate RF prior, not emergent (B1 self-org validated in numpy only). (2) INVARIANCE is not achieved by the substrate: the validated 'what' codes are a competitive pool over RETINOTOPIC V1 features, so they carry category similarity but NOT position/scale/rotation invariance; the deployed V1→V2→IT hierarchy meant to untangle them is inert. So the ventral 'what' stream is
a category-similarity template, not a DiCarlo untangled manifold. (3) The 'world-model' is today almost entirely the SEMANTIC/relational cortex plus a toy 6-way transition table — there is little PERCEPTUAL, predictive world-model (no online s,a→s′ driven by real perception feeding curiosity/imagination). (4) Missing-companion-process signature (the project's own diagnosis): the deployed ventral path replaced divisive normalization AND homeostatic scaling — two processes real cortex runs ALONGSIDE feedforward drive — with a single static drive constant, which is exactly why it is dead-or-saturated. Notably both primitives EXIST in `sim/regions.py` (Carandini-Heeger `input_divisive_norm`; Turrigiano homeostasis) but are guarded no-ops NOT wired into the visual path. The
deployed invariance path is thus a narrow feedforward-STDP template missing the two stabilizing companion processes.

## 3. Episodic + semantic memory + consolidation (reconstructive, associative, emotionally-weighted memory + wake/sleep replay to cortex)

### Role in the whole

For the whole brain-loop this faculty must (1) bind a moment of experience ONE-SHOT — the co-active perceptual/affective/motor/context codes the rest of the brain is already representing — into a single retrievable trace, so an experience can later change behavior; (2) COMPLETE that trace from a partial, degraded, or associative cue (you smell coffee → the whole morning returns), i.e. be reconstructive and content-addressable, not exact-key lookup; (3) be emotionally/salience-weighted at encode (amygdala/neuromodulator gain) so what mattered is preferentially stored and replayed; (4) hand experience to cortex offline via replay so that repeated episodes GENERALIZE into schema/semantic knowledge and stop depending on the hippocampus (systems consolidation), while
protecting old schemas from catastrophic overwrite; (5) support UPDATE-in-place on reactivation (reconsolidation) so a corrected fact revises rather than duplicates; and (6) feed all of the above back into perception/affect/self-model/language as grounded content the agent can talk ABOUT and reason FROM. Concretely for the near-term conversational loop: it must be the ONE store the whole loop writes to and reads from, holding grounded (not word-token) fillers, returning graded/lossy completions the language and self-model faculties consume — not a side database queried by exact key.

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity (BTSP) — one-shot plateau-gated encoding** — A single dendritic Ca2+ plateau (seconds-wide eligibility) potentiates all inputs co-active within a ~1-2s window, forming a place field / episodic assembly in ONE trial without repetition — the biological basis of one-shot episodic binding. Instructive signal is the plateau, not rate coincidence. — *Ref:* Bittner, Milstein, Grienberger, Romani & Magee 2017, 'Behavioral time scale synaptic plasticity underlies CA1 place fields', Science 357:1033-1038 (mechanism: Milstein et al. 2021, eLife 10:e73046)
- **CA3 recurrent-collateral attractor pattern completion** — Dense CA3 recurrent synapses form an autoassociative attractor: a partial cue drives the network to the nearest stored assembly, reconstructing the whole episode. Requires CA3 NMDA-dependent plasticity; this is the content-addressable, reconstructive core that distinguishes memory from a database. — *Ref:* Nakazawa et al. 2002, 'Requirement for hippocampal CA3 NMDA receptors in associative memory recall', Science 297:211-218 (theory: Marr 1971, Phil Trans R Soc B 262:23)
- **Sharp-wave ripple replay + systems consolidation (CLS)** — During NREM/quiet wake, CA3 SWRs replay waking sequences (time-compressed, forward and reverse), driving cortical plasticity; over time the memory becomes hippocampus-independent and interleaved replay lets cortex extract overlapping structure into schema/semantic knowledge without catastrophic forgetting. — *Ref:* Wilson & McNaughton 1994, Science 265:676-679 (replay); Foster & Wilson 2006, Nature 440:680-683 (reverse replay); McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995, Psychol Rev 102:419-457 (complementary learning systems)
- **Schema-accelerated consolidation** — New facts consistent with an existing cortical schema consolidate within ~24h instead of weeks, and are protected against interference — the mechanism by which semantic structure both speeds and shields new episodic content. — *Ref:* Tse, Langston, Kakeyama, Bethus, Spooner, Wood, Witter & Morris 2007, 'Schemas and memory consolidation', Science 316:76-82
- **Prediction-error-gated reconsolidation** — A reactivated memory becomes labile and is UPDATED IN PLACE — but only when retrieval carries a prediction error; a fully-predicted re-statement re-stabilizes unchanged. Mismatch-driven labilization is a distinct process from Hebbian strengthening (updating vs. new trace). — *Ref:* Nader, Schafe & LeDoux 2000, Nature 406:722-726; Sevenster, Beckers & Kindt 2013, Science 339:830-833 (PE necessity); Osan, Tort & Amaral 2011, PLoS ONE 6:e23113 (mismatch-based attractor model)
- **Reconstructive / constructive memory (the anti-database property)** — Human memory is not a faithful store: retrieval reconstructs from gist + schema, fills gaps, is subject to interference and schema-consistent false memories. This lossy, generative character is a design feature (flexible generalization/imagination), NOT a bug — and is precisely what an exact-recall store lacks. — *Ref:* Bartlett 1932, 'Remembering'; Schacter, Norman & Koutstaal 1998, 'The cognitive neuroscience of constructive memory', Annu Rev Psychol 49:289-318; Roediger & McDermott 1995, J Exp Psychol LMC 21:803 (DRM false memory)
- **Cholinergic encode-vs-retrieve gating** — High ACh (attentive encoding) suppresses CA3 recurrent transmission and favors feedforward storage; low ACh (quiet rest) releases recurrents for retrieval/replay — a native neuromodulatory switch between writing and reading, the biological form of the 'frozen-read' the repo currently does host-side. — *Ref:* Hasselmo 2006, 'The role of acetylcholine in learning and memory', Curr Opin Neurobiol 16:710-715 (SPEAR: Hasselmo & Schnell 1994)

### Role in the whole

To serve the whole loop this faculty must, continuously and in-substrate: (1) SCORE every candidate percept/concept/topic on TWO axes read from the brain's OWN representations — the epistemic gap (novelty/uncertainty from the perirhinal/hippocampal familiarity monitor) AND learning-progress (is attending to this actually reducing my uncertainty OVER TIME?); (2) convert that score into a graded neuromodulatory DRIVE (a DA/'SEEKING' appetitive push + an ACh learning-eagerness/expected-uncertainty signal) that BIASES attention (salience/top-down gate onto perception), BIASES action selection (BG/GNW routes it to an exploratory ACT — emit a wh-question to the teacher, orient, or explore), and GATES plasticity (learn harder where progress is being made); (3) feed the RPE/DA
critic an INTRINSIC reward = learning-progress, so exploration is self-rewarding and self-TERMINATING — it stops chasing the unlearnable (the honesty guard: don't confabulate about a 'noisy-TV' concept) and stops re-visiting the mastered (efficiency); (4) carry HISTORY so a slow-but-improving concept (positive learning-progress slope → keep exploring/protect) is NOT confused with an unlearnable one (flat slope → disengage) — a per-ask signal provably cannot make this separation; (5) hand the self-model/metacog an honest functional read-out ('this reads as novel AND my progress on it is positive → I want to ask'). It is a graded drive woven into attention+action, NOT a per-ask veto bolted onto a policy.

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Dopaminergic reward-prediction error (SNc/VTA)** — Midbrain DA neurons fire a phasic burst to unpredicted reward, nothing to fully-predicted reward, and a dip (pause) to reward omission — a Rescorla-Wagner/TD error delta = r - V that is the teaching signal for value and the substrate of the intrinsic reward here. — *Ref:* Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997), 'A neural substrate of prediction and reward', Science 275:1593; Schultz (1998), 'Predictive reward signal of dopamine neurons', J Neurophysiol 80:1
- **Learning-progress intrinsic motivation (Oudeyer-Kaplan / Schmidhuber)** — The intrinsically-rewarding quantity is not novelty per se but the RATE OF REDUCTION of prediction error (expected learning progress / compression progress) — this is what makes a curious agent seek the learnable and ABANDON both the already-known and the unlearnably-random, exactly the honesty property the repo needs. — *Ref:* Oudeyer, Kaplan & Hafner (2007), 'Intrinsic Motivation Systems for Autonomous Mental Development', IEEE Trans Evol Comput 11(2):265; Schmidhuber (2010), 'Formal theory of creativity, fun, and intrinsic motivation', IEEE TAMD 2(3):230
- **Information/curiosity as reward in the DA circuit** — DA neurons and the reward circuitry treat advance information and epistemic-curiosity resolution as rewarding, and curiosity states potentiate hippocampal encoding via the DA loop — grounding 'novelty/uncertainty drives asking' as a genuine reward-system signal, not a bolt-on. — *Ref:* Bromberg-Martin & Hikosaka (2009), 'Midbrain dopamine neurons signal preference for advance information about upcoming rewards', Neuron 63(1):119; Gruber, Gelman & Ranganath (2014), 'States of curiosity modulate hippocampus-dependent learning via the dopaminergic circuit', Neuron 84(2):486
- **Lateral-habenula / RMTg reward-omission (negative) signal** — The LHb fires to worse-than-expected outcomes and, via the GABAergic RMTg, INHIBITS DA neurons to produce the omission dip — the biological detector that lets a circuit register 'this ask yielded no learning' by synaptic subtraction rather than host arithmetic. — *Ref:* Matsumoto & Hikosaka (2007), 'Lateral habenula as a source of negative reward signals in dopamine neurons', Nature 447:1111; Hong, Jhou, Smith, Saleem & Hikosaka (2011), 'Negative reward signals from the lateral habenula to dopamine neurons are mediated by the RMTg', J Neurosci 31(32):11457
- **Acetylcholine = expected uncertainty / learning-eagerness** — ACh (basal forebrain) tracks EXPECTED uncertainty and up-regulates learning rate and bottom-up attention when the world is uncertain-but-structured — the neuromodulatory axis that should carry the 'lean in and learn' component of the drive (the from_novelty target). — *Ref:* Yu & Dayan (2005), 'Uncertainty, neuromodulation, and attention', Neuron 46(4):681; Doya (2002), 'Metalearning and neuromodulation', Neural Networks 15:495
- **Mesolimbic 'SEEKING' appetitive drive + hippocampal novelty->VTA loop** — A general-purpose appetitive foraging/seeking drive carried by mesolimbic DA (Panksepp), gated into memory by a hippocampal-novelty->VTA loop that admits novel information to long-term storage (Lisman-Grace) — the affective 'drive' and the novelty-gating that make curiosity a standing motivation rather than an episodic veto. — *Ref:* Panksepp (1998), 'Affective Neuroscience', ch. on the SEEKING system; Lisman & Grace (2005), 'The hippocampal-VTA loop: controlling the entry of information into long-term memory', Neuron 46:703
- **Perirhinal familiarity/novelty monitor (anti-Hebbian)** — Perirhinal cortex signals stimulus familiarity via anti-Hebbian/decorrelating novelty neurons — the source of the epistemic-gap scalar that should drive curiosity; in-repo this is the Bogacz-Brown gate (the no-confab moat) whose signal is inverted into the curiosity drive. — *Ref:* Bogacz & Brown (2003), 'Comparison of computational models of familiarity discrimination in the perirhinal cortex', Hippocampus 13:494

## 6. self-model + metacognition + honesty (no-confabulation)

### Role in the whole

The self-model must maintain an ongoing, queryable spiking representation of the brain's OWN processing state — what am I attending to, how reliable is the answer I'm about to produce, did I author this thought or hear it from outside — and the speech/action pathway must READ that representation, so that "I'm uncertain / I don't know" EMERGES as a downstream consequence of the actual internal state rather than being decided by a bolted-on gate. Three concrete obligations to the rest of the brain: (1) it must expose a confidence signal that tracks P(correct | my answer) — metacognitive SENSITIVITY (meta-d', Fleming-Daw), dissociable from first-order accuracy — not merely P(the cue is familiar); (2) it must expose a source/authorship tag so self-generated inner content is
not misattributed as heard evidence (Frith comparator), which is a precondition for grounded reference (P1); (3) it must be honest BY CONSTRUCTION — every self-report is a functional read-out of a real, lesionable internal variable (kill the read → the report collapses to chance; that lesion-collapse IS the honesty guarantee), and NEVER an assertion of phenomenal experience (P7). In the integrated loop (P2/P3) this region is what lets the global workspace broadcast a graded "trust this / hedge this" alongside content, so downstream language chooses to answer, hedge, or abstain on its own reliability estimate.

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Type-2 SDT / meta-d' metacognitive sensitivity** — Honest confidence is a SECOND-ORDER read that separates one's own correct from error trials (P(correct)), dissociable from first-order d'. This is the actual currency of 'honest uncertainty' — a familiarity/recall-strength signal is NOT it. meta-d' < d' quantifies metacognitive inefficiency; the in-repo monitor sits at meta_d~0 (margin-blind). — *Ref:* Maniscalco & Lau 2012, 'A signal detection theoretic approach for estimating metacognitive sensitivity from confidence ratings', Consciousness and Cognition 21(1); Fleming & Daw 2017, 'Self-evaluation of decision-making: A general Bayesian framework for metacognitive computation', Psychological Review 124(1).
- **Prefrontal (rostral-PFC/aPFC) + ACC metacognition & error monitoring** — A hierarchically separate PFC/ACC circuit reads the reliability of first-order processing; aPFC gray-matter/connectivity predicts introspective accuracy, ACC signals errors/conflict pre-emptively. Grounds the 'second-order region reads a first-order competition' architecture and the dual-stream retrospective metamemory the project cites. — *Ref:* Fleming, Weil, Nagy, Dolan & Rees 2010, 'Relating introspective accuracy to individual differences in brain structure', Science 329; Shimamura 2000, 'Toward a cognitive neuroscience of metacognition', Consciousness & Cognition 9; Miyamoto et al. 2017, 'Causal neural network of metamemory for retrospection in primates', Science 355; Botvinick, Braver,
Barch, Carter & Cohen 2001, 'Conflict monitoring and cognitive control', Psychological Review 108.
- **Attention Schema — the brain models & reports its own internal (attentional) state** — The brain constructs a simplified model of its own attention/awareness and can report it; provides the theoretical substrate for a self-schema region whose firing REPRESENTS 'what I'm attending to / how sure I am / whether I authored it' and whose report is a functional read-out, not a phenomenal claim. — *Ref:* Graziano 2013, 'Consciousness and the Social Brain', Oxford Univ. Press; Graziano & Webb 2015, 'The attention schema theory: a mechanistic account of subjective awareness', Frontiers in Psychology 6.
- **Source monitoring & corollary-discharge (efference-copy) comparator for agency/authorship** — Self-produced content carries an efference copy/corollary discharge available to a comparator; externally-parsed content arrives without it. The comparator emits a self/other tag; its failure = misattributing inner speech to an external voice. This is the authorship axis of the self-model (built + six-seed GO in-repo). — *Ref:* Sperry 1950, 'Neural basis of the spontaneous optokinetic response'; von Holst & Mittelstaedt 1950, 'Das Reafferenzprinzip'; Crapse & Sommer 2008, 'Corollary discharge across the animal kingdom', Nature Reviews Neuroscience 9; Johnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay 1993, 'Source monitoring', Psychological Bulletin 114; Frith 1992, 'The Cognitive Neuropsychology
of Schizophrenia' (comparator model).
- **Perirhinal anti-Hebbian familiarity/novelty (the current moat's mechanism)** — A fast anti-Hebbian familiarity/novelty signal (known cue ~0, novel ~1) — this is exactly what the in-repo no-confab moat computes. Biologically it is a KNOW-precursor (has this cue been seen?), NOT metacognitive sensitivity (am I right about the answer?), which is precisely why the current honesty gate is a template and not a self-model. — *Ref:* Bogacz & Brown 2003, 'Comparison of computational models of familiarity discrimination in the perirhinal cortex', Hippocampus 13(4); Brown & Aggleton 2001, 'Recognition memory: what are the roles of the perirhinal cortex and hippocampus?', Nature Reviews Neuroscience 2.
- **Global neuronal workspace / access (report-ability & integration)** — A metastable global broadcast makes a single content (plus its confidence/source tags) globally available to downstream systems including language — the integration prerequisite (P2/P3) that turns a self-model READ into an act of honest report. In-repo the workspace is a real spiking GNW (K assemblies + shared inhibition, one ignites). — *Ref:* Dehaene & Changeux 2011, 'Experimental and theoretical approaches to conscious processing', Neuron 70(2); Dehaene 2014, 'Consciousness and the Brain'.
- **Interoceptive / embodied self (affective grounding)** — The felt self is grounded in interoceptive inference (insula/ACC) that also underlies confidence and emotion; long-term this ties the self-model's confidence/authorship axes to the affective world-model rather than leaving them abstract scalars. — *Ref:* Craig 2009, 'How do you feel — now? The anterior insula and human awareness', Nature Reviews Neuroscience 10; Seth 2013, 'Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self', Trends in Cognitive Sciences 17(11).

## 7. Learning / plasticity / credit assignment / continual learning / developmental growth

### Role in the whole

This is the faculty that converts the whole brain's LIVED LOOP (perceive -> appraise/affect -> act -> outcome -> teacher-contingency) into durable synaptic change, CONTINUOUSLY, so that every other faculty (language, memory, world-model, self-model) is ACQUIRED and keeps developing rather than being hand-wired. Concretely it must, on the ONE shared spiking substrate, provide FOUR things at once: (1) write new experience fast (one-shot episodic capture at behavioral timescale); (2) integrate it slowly into cortex WITHOUT erasing prior knowledge (offline replay-driven consolidation + interference resistance); (3) gate what gets learned by neuromodulatory value/salience/novelty (three-factor: reward, curiosity, surprise decide WHICH synapses change and how much); and (4)
GROW capacity from small to large as content accrues (add cells/synapses on demand, open/close critical-period windows), so the brain can start on consumer hardware and expand. The load-bearing property for the WHOLE is that this runs as one integrated online loop -- not a per-faculty training run -- and survives graduation from the AI-teacher scaffold to real human interaction (learning must continue with no oracle error signal available).

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP)** — Pre-before-post potentiates / post-before-pre depresses on a ~20ms window; the substrate's base local associative rule -- present and used in-engine, but on its own it cannot assign credit through hidden layers. — *Ref:* Bi & Poo 1998, 'Synaptic modifications in cultured hippocampal neurons: dependence on spike timing, synaptic strength, and postsynaptic cell type', J Neurosci 18(24):10464-72
- **Three-factor / neuromodulated plasticity** — Hebbian coincidence x a global third factor (dopamine/ACh/NA) that gates and signs plasticity -- the biological basis for reward-, curiosity- and surprise-gated learning; this is the credit path the near-term build leans on INSTEAD of multi-layer directed credit. — *Ref:* Fremaux & Gerstner 2016, 'Neuromodulated STDP, and Theory of Three-Factor Learning Rules', Front. Neural Circuits 9:85 (also Reynolds & Wickens 2002 for DA-gating)
- **Behavioral-timescale synaptic plasticity (BTSP)** — A dendritic plateau opens a seconds-long eligibility window so a single pairing writes a place field one-shot -- the fast-write episodic capture that a spiking brain uses in place of gradient-based encoding; weight-dependent variants give a structured fixed point. — *Ref:* Bittner, Milstein, Grienberger, Romani, Magee 2017, 'Behavioral time scale synaptic plasticity underlies CA1 place fields', Science 357:1033-36; Milstein et al. 2021 eLife 10:e73046
- **Complementary Learning Systems (systems consolidation)** — Fast hippocampal store + slow interleaved cortical learner: the architectural answer to catastrophic forgetting -- new memories are captured by hippocampus and gradually interleaved into cortex so old knowledge is preserved. Directly validated in-repo (Phase 1.3). — *Ref:* McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995, 'Why there are complementary learning systems in the hippocampus and neocortex...', Psychological Review 102(3):419-457
- **Sharp-wave-ripple replay** — Offline (sleep/quiet-wake) compressed reactivation of waking sequences that drives the hippocampus->cortex transfer and the interleaving that prevents forgetting -- the mechanism that does the consolidation work; sparse replay is the biologically-matched schedule. — *Ref:* Buzsaki 2015, 'Hippocampal sharp wave-ripple: A cognitive biomarker for episodic memory and planning', Hippocampus 25(10):1073-1188
- **Adult dentate-gyrus neurogenesis** — New granule cells added throughout life sparsify/separate patterns and provide a substrate for GROWTH and time-stamping of memories -- the biological warrant for start-small-then-grow capacity expansion (the develop-loop GROWTH hook). — *Ref:* Aimone, Deng & Gage 2011, 'Resolving new memories: a critical look at the dentate gyrus, adult neurogenesis, and pattern separation', Neuron 70(4):589-596
- **Activity-dependent structural plasticity (synaptogenesis/pruning)** — Synapses form and are eliminated as a function of activity to keep the network in a homeostatic regime -- the mechanism for growing NEW wiring from the brain's own dynamics rather than pre-allocating it; distinct from grow-to-active-context (Hawkins-Ahmad 2016) which grows exactly co-firing pairs. — *Ref:* Butz, Worgotter & van Ooyen 2009, 'Activity-dependent structural plasticity', Brain Research Reviews 60(2):287-305
- **Critical-period plasticity** — Maturation-gated windows (opened/closed by inhibitory maturation) during which specific circuits are highly plastic then stabilize -- the biological expression of a developmental STAGE (plasticity_gate open->close per region along the caudo-rostral gradient). — *Ref:* Hensch 2005, 'Critical period plasticity in local cortical circuits', Nature Reviews Neuroscience 6:877-888
- **Segregated-apical dendritic credit (the MAPPED BOUNDARY)** — Two-compartment pyramidal neurons carry top-down feedback in an apical compartment to approximate multi-layer credit with local rules. Included to mark it as tested-and-negative on this substrate -- do NOT re-propose; the residual is that feedback-alignment does not converge on the Izhikevich forward. — *Ref:* Guerguiev, Lillicrap & Richards 2017, 'Towards deep learning with segregated dendrites', eLife 6:e22901; Sacramento, Costa, Bengio & Senn 2018, 'Dendritic cortical microcircuits approximate the backpropagation algorithm', NeurIPS

## 8. The embodied integration loop (world + body + the closed sensation→state→action→consequence→learning loop) + action selection

### Role in the whole

This is THE substrate of grounding (P1). Its concrete job for the rest of the brain: keep a minimal world+body running continuously so that perception, semantic/affective state, action-selection and speech close into ONE loop in which the CONSEQUENCES of the brain's own acts — not a corpus — drive learning. To serve that role on the short timescale it MUST provide four things the rest of the brain can lean on: (1) sensorimotor contingency — a body whose motor output changes what the sensors receive next, so that meaning can be internal reference tied to the brain's own sensory/motor loop (O'Regan-Noë), not a label; (2) commitment — basal-ganglia action selection that picks ONE act at a time (crucially, "speak" must be selectable as an act, so language becomes an action
to communicate rather than next-token emission); (3) a single integrator — a GNW ignition bottleneck so ONE coalition of content (a perceived object / retrieved fact / affect state) is what both drives the next action AND is what gets spoken (report==content), enabling one train of thought; (4) a consequence→learning arm — a reward/RPE (DA) signal returned by the act's outcome (or the teacher) that re-enters affect/value and gates synaptic plasticity, so the brain learns because its acts had outcomes. Without this loop closed, every other faculty (language, affect, self-model) is a static test-passer with no lived referent for its symbols.

### Biological mechanism(s)

- **Sensorimotor contingencies / enactive perception** — Perception is mastery of the lawful ways sensory input changes as the body acts; meaning is grounded in action-sensation regularities, not stored representations — the biological warrant for 'language as action to communicate' and for grounding on the SHORT (act-by-act) timescale. — *Ref:* O'Regan & Noë (2001), 'A sensorimotor account of vision and visual consciousness,' Behavioral and Brain Sciences 24(5):939-1031
- **Cortico-basal-ganglia-thalamo-cortical reentrant loops / action selection by disinhibition** — Parallel segregated CBGT channels resolve the selection problem by GPi/SNr disinhibition of one thalamic channel; selection is an emergent property of the WHOLE reentrant loop, and the thalamo→cortical return arc is what SUSTAINS a chosen act — directly maps to selecting cognitive/speech ops, not just motor moves. — *Ref:* Redgrave, Prescott & Gurney (1999), 'The basal ganglia: a vertebrate solution to the selection problem?' Neuroscience 89(4):1009-1023; Alexander, DeLong & Strick (1986), Annu. Rev. Neurosci. 9:357-381
- **Global Neuronal Workspace — ignition & broadcast** — Above a threshold, a recurrent workspace assembly IGNITES all-or-none into a sustained, self-amplifying state that broadcasts one content globally; this is the single-integrator bottleneck that makes one coalition available to drive the next action and the utterance (report==content). — *Ref:* Dehaene & Changeux (2011), 'Experimental and theoretical approaches to conscious processing,' Neuron 70(2):200-227; Baars (1988), A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness
