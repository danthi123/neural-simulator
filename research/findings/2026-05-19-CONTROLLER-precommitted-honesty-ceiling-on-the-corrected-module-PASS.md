# Controller pre-committed honesty ceiling on a PASS against the corrected necessity module — stated and propagated BEFORE any build, so no outcome can be rationalized

## Why this document exists (the deepest anti-cheat question of the arc)

The corrected necessity module moves exactly one lesion's partition
membership (the consolidation/replay lesion: episodic-helper ->
working-memory/concept-helper). That correction is biologically sound
and was independently implied by three convergent faithful
architectures, and it was pre-committed in writing before the outcome.
But it is also, by construction, exactly the membership that would let
a distinct-readout-pathways candidate clear the duty that VOIDed every
prior attempt. Legitimacy and convenience coincide. An internal
goalpost-move review can verify necessary conditions (bars verbatim,
exactly one change, the change biology-cited and not justified by "what
makes the candidate pass", the original module byte-unchanged) but it
cannot, being internal, fully eliminate the coincidence-of-convenience.
This is an irreducible epistemic limit, surfaced honestly by the
planning step itself.

## The pre-committed honesty ceiling (binding; stated before any build or run)

1. The load-bearing, scale-confident scientific result of this arc is
   the THRICE-CONVERGENT FALSIFICATION of the original pre-registered
   necessity prediction (the consolidation/replay lesion does not own
   episodic recall; episodic-sequence order is the online hippocampal
   trisynaptic store's responsibility; complementary-learning-systems
   consolidation builds the order-invariant neocortical schema). This
   finding is independent of any future build and stands on the
   original frozen verdict's preserved "cannot conclude" plus three
   faithful architectures.

2. A clean, scale-confident, validated PASS was only ever obtainable
   against the ORIGINAL frozen instrument. That is now known to be
   unobtainable from this architectural line at this slice, because the
   original prediction it encodes was falsified. This is stated plainly
   and is not a deficiency to be patched around — it is the honest
   terminus of the "validated-PASS against the original pre-registration"
   goal for this line.

3. Therefore: a PASS of the distinct-readout-pathways candidate against
   the corrected module is explicitly NOT claimable as the
   scale-confident validated deliverable. At most it is
   "consistent-with the corrected (biologically-revised) necessity
   structure" — a weak positive whose entire strength rests on a
   partition change whose one differentiating effect is exactly what
   enables the PASS. It must always be reported with this exact
   limitation, never as a validated success, never spun.

4. A VOID or FAIL of the candidate against the corrected module IS a
   strong, unambiguous, informative negative (a faithful architecture
   cannot realize even the biologically-corrected necessity structure)

5. No further partition edit is permitted, ever, on this line. The one

## What still proceeds, and why (honoring the standing mandate without overclaiming)

The standing mandate is autonomous, no hand-back, iterate following
biology. The experiment still has genuine scientific value under the
ceiling above: testing whether a faithful distinct-pathways
architecture can realize the biologically-corrected necessity structure
at all is informative regardless of outcome (a negative is strong; a
positive is honestly bounded to "consistent-with"). So the build
proceeds — corrected frozen module first, then its dedicated
goalpost-move adversarial review as a hard gate, then (only if that
gate clears) the candidate — with this honesty ceiling fixed in advance
and propagated to both remotes now, so neither a PASS nor a FAIL can be
rationalized after the fact.
