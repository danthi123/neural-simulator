## Mechanism interpretation

Phase 1.4 trains Tier 1 primaries (north/east/south/west) then
trains Phase B synonyms only (up/right/down/left), measures primary
retention. Synonyms share motor pool targets with primaries:
- "north" + "up"   -> motor_N
- "east"  + "right"-> motor_E
- "south" + "down" -> motor_S
- "west"  + "left" -> motor_W

When Phase B trains "up", motor_N gets MORE total training events
(primary "north" 200 + synonym "up" 200 = 400 events). This can
strengthen motor pool dynamics and IMPROVE primary retention.

**Pattern observed (4/6):**
- 2 seeds (42, 100): primary IMPROVED during Phase B (115%, 129%)
- 1 seed (43): primary slightly degraded but stayed strong (84%)
- 1 seed (44): real interference, primary dropped from 36% to 28% (78%)

Interference in seed 44 is concentrated in the WEST pool: Phase A
W->W = 12/25 (48%), Phase B W->W = 5/25 (20%). The "left" training
in Phase B paradoxically disrupted the W pool.
