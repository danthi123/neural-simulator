## UPDATE — DECISIVE ROOT CAUSE (systematic-debugging: measured, not guessed): the Hebbian is NOT potentiating on-substrate
Wiring occupancy-suppressed allocation DID make the bind entity-DISTINCT (A→slot0, B→slot3 via routing a novel barcode away from occupied slots), but retrieve stayed 1/2 through 5+ tuning fixes (transmission weight, FS, tonic bias, occupancy, 3× bind, max_weight). The **a0 weight measurement** found the root cause: **the barcode→slot weights DO NOT CHANGE after binding** (B→slot3 39.0 → 38.8) — the rate-window Hebbian never potentiates. **Why (read `bridge.py:6966-6989`):** the rate-window rule only potentiates synapses whose co-activity trace PRODUCT exceeds `hebbian_coactivity_thresh` (default **0.25**); the barcode trace is ~0.35 but the slots fire weakly (trace ~0.1) under the tonic-bias WTA, so the product is **~0.035 ≪ 0.25 → zero potentiation → no memory** (the retrieve=1/2 was baseline WTA + occupancy artifacts, not recall).

## The architectural tension (the "question the architecture" point, systematic-debugging)
Fixing it is COUPLED: specific potentiation needs a CLEAN HIGH-RATE WTA winner (winner trace > ~0.5 so its coactivity with the barcode exceeds the threshold, while the losers stay low) — but the tonic bias that makes the slots fire *at all* (needed because the raw barcode→slot conductance is ~2 pA, 250× sub-threshold) ALSO fires the losers, so there is no clean high-rate winner. The current compound (tonic-bias + weak FS-WTA + rate-window-Hebbian) can't simultaneously (a) fire the winner enough for potentiation and (b) keep the losers silent for specificity. This is the architecture to rethink, not another knob.

**These four are mutually coupled** (a strong synaptic drive to fire the winner ⇒ needs strong FS to suppress losers ⇒ crushes the drive; a clean winner needs a differential that only exists AFTER potentiation, which needs a clean winner).
