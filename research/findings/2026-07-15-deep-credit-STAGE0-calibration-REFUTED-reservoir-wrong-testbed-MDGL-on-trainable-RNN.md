## THE FIX — a proper TRAINABLE-RNN testbed (MDGL's own validation regime), BUILT + plumbing-validated
`_mdgl_offdiagonal_credit_derisk.py`: a small TRAINABLE RNN (W_in/W_rec/W_out all learned) — as MDGL's own paper used (delayed-match-to-sample / evidence-accumulation), NOT a fixed reservoir. Three credit arms: **BPTT** (through-time ceiling) · **e-prop** (diagonal forward eligibility, transport-free DFA) · **MDGL** (e-prop + the one-hop Γ term: Γ_pq = (Σ_j cell-type-affinity·a_j·1[q→j])·e_pq,t-1, each synapse sees its presynaptic neuron's postsynaptic partners' error contributions — Liu-2021 Eq.20). Anti-cheats: zero-Γ must collapse to e-prop; sign-flip-Γ must hurt; permuted-label → chance.
- **PLUMBING VALIDATED (smoke):** on delayed-match-to-sample (T=5) **BPTT SOLVES it (1.000)** where the reservoir couldn't, e-prop lags (0.767), zero-Γ collapses to e-prop cleanly. - **KEY NUANCE found:** at SHORT lag (T=5) the diagonal e-prop ALSO solves XOR (no gap → the off-diagonal isn't needed there). The off-diagonal only earns its keep at LONG lag where the diagonal eligibility fades but BPTT holds. **IN FLIGHT:** a lag-sweep (bznqky07y) to locate that regime; then the MDGL test runs there.
