# Generative-frontier scoping — getting genuine OPEN-ENDED generation that is THE BRAIN'S, grounded + moat-aware (2026-06-23)

**Read-only deep-research scoping (NO build, NO GPU, NO `sim/` edit). Per the standing deep-research-first practice
(new mechanism class: open-ended generation). The owner wants a genuinely substantive conversation; the deepest piece
is FREE generation — novel discourse the brain was never explicitly taught. This doc diagnoses what "free generation"
means here, ranks options for the two candidate sub-paths ((a) grounded-free-gen via the faculty; (b) the brain's own
spiking generator), gives the cheapest-first de-risk + anti-cheats for each, and recommends a sequence. Cross-checked
against current SNN-LLM + grounded-generation + neuro-generative-replay literature.**

---

## 1. DIAGNOSIS — what "free generation" means here, and the REAL gap

### 1.1 The current ceiling, measured
The production conversational agent (`RFPhasorComposer` / `OneBrainComposer` + `BrainConversationalAgent`) is a
**structured-retrieval** system. The decisive probe (`2026-06-22-generation-novelty-categorical-gap-MEASURED.md`,
`generation_novelty_probe.py`, V=320/D=128/N=64, seed 42) settled it: **0 novel sentences, distinct-generated/stored
ratio 1.0, novel-composition score 0.0, 0/16 held-out in-vocabulary triples produced (all abstained), moat 20/20 with
0 confabulations.** The held-out triples were structurally identical to stored ones — the ONLY difference was that
they were never stored — and the agent produced NONE. The K-capacity axis is meanwhile clean (recall + moat 1.0 to 64
facts). ⇒ **the gap to a small LLM is CATEGORICAL (free generation), not scale.** The exact-inverse FHRR algebra has no
generative path: it can unbind/clean-up what was bound, but it cannot SAMPLE a never-stored token sequence.

### 1.2 Three distinct meanings of "free generation" (disambiguate before building)
The word "generation" hides three increasingly-hard capabilities. They are NOT the same problem and the project sits
at very different places on each:

- **(G1) Novel SURFACE form for known content** — render a known fact (or a recombination of known facts) as fluent
  multi-word/multi-sentence prose the brain never stored verbatim. *Status: SOLVED, two ways.* The neural serial-order
  renderer (`neural_serial_order_renderer.py`, CQ/Grossberg, catalog G.07/H.19) produces the SVO word order in spikes;
  and the grounded-language faculty (the spiking Qwen, P1) produces genuinely novel, fluent surface form for gated
  facts ("The fox chased the rabbit through the forest" — never stored as a string). **This is real generativity, but
  of FORM, not of CONTENT.**
- **(G2) Novel PROPOSITIONS by recombination of known elements** — assert a fact/relation the brain was never told but
  that follows from / is consistent with what it knows (multi-hop chains, analogies, "compositional" novel SVO over
  the known vocabulary). *Status: PARTIAL.* `query_chain`/`reason_chain` already do role-structured multi-hop
  pointer-chases (production, 3-seed GO). But **producing a brand-new in-vocabulary triple the brain was never told,
  on its own initiative, scores 0.0** (§1.1). This is the categorical gap's near edge — and it is where biology's
  "constructive recombination" lives (hippocampal generative replay assembles never-experienced shortcuts/sequences,
  §3.3).
- **(G3) Genuinely OPEN-ENDED discourse** — generate coherent multi-sentence text on a topic that goes BEYOND any
  single stored fact: narrative, explanation, elaboration with new phrasings and connective tissue, the kind of thing
  a small LLM does. *Status: only via the off-bridge LLM faculty (P1).* The brain's own substrate cannot do this
  today (the composer scores 0; the BPTT-SNN is benched at toy scale).

**The owner's "genuinely substantive conversation / free generation = novel discourse the brain was never explicitly
taught" is G2+G3.** G1 is done. The frontier is: **can the BRAIN (not the bolted-on LLM) produce novel propositional
content (G2) and open-ended discourse (G3), grounded, on the spiking substrate?**

### 1.3 The crux: "whose generation is it?"
This is the load-bearing distinction for the whole scoping, and it splits the two sub-paths cleanly:
- **Path (a) — grounded-free-gen via the faculty:** the *fluency and the token-sequencing* are the LLM's; the *content
  and the verification* are the brain's. The novelty of SURFACE form is genuinely the faculty's (G1/G3-form). The
  novelty of CONTENT is bounded by what the brain can support — so it is **grounded but not the brain's own invention**.
  This is RAG/constrained-generation SOTA with the brain's exact-match store as a stronger gate. **Near-term, already
  largely built.**
- **Path (b) — the brain's own spiking generator:** the *sequence itself* is generated by spiking dynamics the project
  owns (a backprop-pretrained spiking LM consolidated onto the bridge, OR a from-biology generative-replay
  recombinator). The generation is genuinely the brain's. **Deeper; the BPTT-SNN half is de-risked-but-toy, the
  from-biology half is unbuilt.**

The honest framing: **(a) gives a substantive grounded conversation SOON with the brain as the knowledge+truth
authority but the LLM as the linguistic engine; (b) is the path to generation that is ontologically the brain's, and
it is further out.** They COMPOSE (the recommended sequence, §5) — (a) is the near-term product and the scaffold/teacher
that de-risks (b).

## 2. RANKED OPTIONS — Path (a): GROUNDED FREE GENERATION via the faculty

The architecture already exists end-to-end at de-risk scale: `gate (composer recall/abstain) → constrain (faculty
renders only the retrieved fact) → verify (re-parse, reject drift)`, validated with the REAL spiking Qwen2.5-0.5B
(`-INTEGRATION-GO.md`, `-SCALED-GO.md`, 67 facts, 3 seeds, moat 0-FA, the real LLM's role-inversion hallucination
CAUGHT). The frontier for (a) is **extending the gate/verify from SINGLE-FACT rendering to OPEN multi-sentence
generation**: let the faculty generate freely, then accept/reject each emitted CLAIM against the brain's knowledge.

**Reusable as-is:** the spiking Qwen faculty (P1b), the gate (composer recall + no-confab abstention), the verify
(spiking-parser re-parse → SVO match), the bridge-co-residence machinery (the 494M Qwen runs on the RF substrate,
14 GB local, bit-exact — `-bridge-coresidence-DEMONSTRATED.md`), and the SCALED demo harness.

### Option a1 — Claim-level generate-then-verify (RANK 1 for (a))
Let the faculty generate a multi-sentence response freely (a real open generation, not a single-fact template). Then
**decompose the output into atomic claims** (each candidate SVO / attribute), and **verify each claim against the
brain's store**: accept claims the brain can confirm (exact binding-match) or that are non-factual connective tissue;
REJECT (or flag/strike) any claim asserting a relation the brain cannot support. This is exactly the SOTA
"generate-then-verify / post-hoc grounding" family ([CaLM 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.05365); the hallucination-
detection literature), but with the brain's EXACT-MATCH store + abstention as a stronger verifier than cosine retrieval.
- **Pros:** maximal fluency + genuine open-ended discourse (G3); reuses the entire verify mechanism; the brain stays
  the truth authority; degrades gracefully (strike a claim → still a coherent reply). Near-term — the verify already
  exists, only the claim-DECOMPOSITION of a multi-sentence output is new.
- **Cons:** the GENERATION is the LLM's, not the brain's (the §1.3 caveat); connective/opinion sentences carry no
  verifiable SVO so the moat covers only the factual claims (acceptable per the owner: "moat is a plus, not a hard
  gate"); claim decomposition is itself an NLP step (do it with the spiking parser to keep it brain-side).
- **Moat implication:** the moat shifts from "the faculty may only render ONE gated fact" to "the faculty may say
  anything, but no UNSUPPORTED factual claim survives verify." Strictly weaker than the current single-fact gate,
  strictly stronger than an ungrounded LLM. This is the owner-sanctioned moat/generativity trade.

### Option a2 — In-decoder knowledge biasing (logit-fusion) (RANK 2 for (a))
Instead of post-hoc reject, bias the faculty's DECODING toward brain-supported continuations: at each step fuse the
brain's "what facts mention the current subject" into the logits (the GRAD / KCTS in-decoder family —
[GRAD 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03900), [KCTS 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.09044)). In-decoder grounding
suppresses hallucination BEFORE it propagates (the literature's finding that in-decoder beats post-hoc).
- **Pros:** prevents drift rather than striking it after; smoother text than reject→regen; the brain steers content in
  real time.
- **Cons:** much tighter coupling between the brain's store and the faculty's logits (a per-step brain→logit map),
  heavier to build than a1; on the spiking-Qwen-on-bridge path the per-token forward is already slow (161 s/token,
  `-bridge-coresidence-DEMONSTRATED.md`), so per-step logit fusion compounds the wall. Defer until a1 proves the
  product and the perf levers land.

### Option a3 — Reject→regenerate loop at the sentence level (RANK 3, a refinement, already partially built)
The P3/integration spec's reject→regenerate is already wired for single facts (the role-inversion was caught →
regenerated → faithful). Extend it to sentence-by-sentence open generation: emit a sentence, verify, on reject
regenerate THAT sentence with a tighter prompt. This is the production hardening of a1 (the recovery path), not a
separate option — fold into a1.

**Path-(a) verdict:** a1 (claim-level generate-then-verify) is the near-term unlock for substantive grounded
open-ended conversation. It is the smallest delta over what is already GO, and it is genuinely open-ended (G3) — at the
honest cost that the *generation* is the faculty's, the *truth* is the brain's.

## 3. RANKED OPTIONS — Path (b): THE BRAIN'S OWN SPIKING GENERATOR

Here the SEQUENCE is generated by spiking dynamics the project owns. Two sub-families: (b-i) the backprop-pretrained
spiking LM (the validated-but-toy Gen-F/BPTT-SNN arc, reframed as a development stand-in); (b-ii) a from-biology
generative recombinator (hippocampal generative replay / constructive imagination) that produces novel PROPOSITIONS
(G2) directly on the existing brain machinery.

### Option b1 — Scale the consolidated spiking generator (Gen-F) to demonstrate G3 (RANK 1 for (b))
The generative LOOP is already DEMONSTRATED at toy scale: train → generate → grow → confirm-no-catastrophic-forgetting
(`2026-06-23-generative-loop-DEMONSTRATED.md`). C1 (the 3.4M Gen-F consolidated on the RF bridge, generating
byte-identical, fully-spiking — all 3 nonlinearities GO: LayerNorm 0.962 / GELU 0.991 / softmax 0.9998) + C2 (grow
without forgetting, self-replay causally validated: retention 0.884 with replay vs 0.392 without, 2.25× contrast, 3/3
seeds) are BOTH met. The wall to a *substantive* generator is **model capacity** — the 3.4M can't hold two
distributions (`-C2-moderate-shift-NEGATIVE-scale-wall.md`); the sizing scoping (`-C2-scaleup-scoping.md`) puts the
demonstrating size at **~100M params, ~15 h LOCAL (≤11 GB VRAM, no cloud)**, staged 30M→100M→200M.
- **Pros:** the generation is genuinely the brain's (spiking dynamics on the bridge, G3); the WHOLE loop machinery is
  built + validated; reuse-by-import (only the TinyGPT hyperparams + FT batch change, NO `sim/` edit); the
  development-stand-in reframe resolves the biology objection (backprop = slow cortical development; the validated
  hippocampal consolidation + lineage = the continual-learning back half = CLS). This is the cleanest path to "the
  brain genuinely generates open-ended discourse."
- **Cons:** backprop-pretraining is OFF the local-learning path (the owner-accepted reframe makes it legitimate as
  development, but it remains the honest caveat — the SOTA spiking LLMs SpikeGPT/SpikeLLM/LAS/FAS are ALL
  backprop-trained transformers then spiked, [SpikeGPT](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.13939v5),
  [SpikeLLM ICLR'25], [LAS 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.09659)); the GENERATION is fluent but UNGROUNDED on its
  own (its knowledge is transformer-form, not brain-form — the owner's own insight that motivated the grounded-faculty
  split) so it STILL needs the path-(a) gate/verify to be hallucination-safe; ~15 h local per scale step.
- **Moat implication:** identical to path (a) — a free spiking generator hallucinates exactly like the LLM faculty, so
  it inherits the SAME gate→verify wrapper. The moat is NOT weakened by going to the brain's own generator; the
  generator is wrapped the same way.

### Option b2 — Hippocampal generative replay as a CONSTRUCTIVE PROPOSITION generator (RANK 2 for (b), the most "brain's own")
This is the biology-faithful route to G2 (novel propositions) and it is the one the literature most directly supports.
The hippocampal formation is a **hierarchical generative model** whose offline reactivations are **generative replay** —
resampling *fictive* sequences, INCLUDING never-experienced shortcuts and sequences implied by learned abstract
knowledge ([Stoianov/Maisto/Pezzulo 2022](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.01.16.908889v2.full);
[Barry/Love generative memory construction, Nat Hum Behav 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01799-z)).
"Replay assembles elements into compounds, each a hypothesis about a possible configuration of elements." The project
ALREADY has: engram tags (catalog D.14, shipped), concept-replay (`run_concept_replay_phase`), SWR-gated CA3 replay
infra, the composer's role-binding algebra, and the no-confab moat. The idea: during an offline "imagination" phase,
**resample role-filler bindings from the learned association graph to PROPOSE novel SVO triples**, then gate them
through the moat (a proposed triple is "plausible" if its constituents co-occur in the graph above threshold) and the
verify (it must not contradict a stored fact).
- **Pros:** the generation is genuinely the brain's AND genuinely from-biology (not a converted transformer); it
  produces novel CONTENT (G2), not just form; it reuses the existing composer + engram + replay machinery; it is the
  catalog G.09 (constructive imagination / future simulation) entry, currently "missing"; it directly attacks the
  measured 0.0 novel-composition score with a brain mechanism. Most aligned with the artificial-life north star.
- **Cons:** highest research uncertainty (it is a new mechanism class — the project has NEVER produced a novel
  proposition on its own); the hard part is CONTROL — what makes a recombination "good" rather than nonsense (biology
  uses the learned generative model's likelihood; the project would need a plausibility signal, e.g. the PPMI stream
  cortex's co-occurrence statistics as the "is this combination sensible" gate); it generates PROPOSITIONS (G2), not
  fluent open discourse (G3) — it would feed path-(a)/b1 for the surface form. Multi-seed validation will be needed
  per the standing rule.
- **Moat implication:** this is where the moat/generativity trade is sharpest and most interesting. A pure no-confab
  moat says "never assert what you weren't told" — which FORBIDS G2 by construction. Generative replay DELIBERATELY
  asserts never-told (but plausible, graph-supported) propositions. The owner's "moat is a plus not a hard gate; lossy
  reconstructive memory is fine" (`feedback_moat_not_hard_lossy_memory_ok`) explicitly licenses this: the gate becomes
  "plausible given the learned graph + not contradicting a stored fact" rather than "exactly stored." This is the
  reconstructive-memory stance, and it is biologically correct (real memory confabulates plausibly).

### Option b3 — Spiking competitive-queuing / HVC serial-order generation for novel sequences (RANK 3 for (b))
The project's `song_hvc.py` (synfire HVC, Hahnloser; babble+dopamine-reinforce, Fee-Goldberg) + the CQ serial-order
renderer already generate ORDERED sequences in spikes. In principle a babble-and-reinforce loop could learn to emit
novel word sequences. *But* this is sequence-ORDER machinery (it sequences a GIVEN set of items); it does not by itself
decide WHICH novel content to emit — that is b2's job. It is a component of b2/b1's output stage, not a standalone
free-generation path. **Keep as the spiking output stage; not a primary option.**

**Path-(b) verdict:** b1 (scale Gen-F) is the cheapest concrete path to the brain generating open-ended discourse (G3)
on its own substrate, but its content is ungrounded (needs the path-(a) wrapper). b2 (generative replay) is the
deepest and most biology-faithful — the only path that produces novel CONTENT (G2) from a brain mechanism — and the
one most aligned with the artificial-life goal, at the cost of being a genuine new-mechanism research arc.

## 4. CHEAPEST-FIRST DE-RISK for each path (the smallest probe that proves/refutes)

### Path (a) — a1 claim-level generate-then-verify
**Probe (numpy-CPU brain + the existing spiking-Qwen faculty, ~1 day, NO `sim/` edit):** take the SCALED demo's ~67-
fact brain. Prompt the faculty to write a SHORT multi-sentence answer to an open question ("Tell me about the
animals", "What do you know about the fox?") — a REAL free generation, not a single-fact template. Then run the
spiking parser over the output to extract every candidate SVO claim, and verify each against the store. **PASS =** the
emitted text is fluent + multi-sentence (G3 form), every FALSE/unsupported factual claim is caught by verify (the moat
holds over open generation), and the SUPPORTED claims are correctly accepted. The single cleanest refutation: inject a
prompt that elicits a plausible-but-false claim ("the lion eats grass") and confirm verify strikes it. This is the
`-INTEGRATION-GO` harness extended from single-fact to multi-sentence + claim-decomposition — the smallest delta.

### Path (b) — b1 scale Gen-F
**Probe (the staged scale-up, cheapest stage first): 30M Gen-F, ~4 h LOCAL** (`-C2-scaleup-scoping.md` stage 1). Run
the full loop (train → C1 consolidate on the RF bridge → C2 grow-no-forget at SH_FRAC=0.45). **PASS =** the three C2
bars (learns-new + retain ≥85% + no-replay forgets ≥1.3×) clear at 30M, OR clearly trend toward clearing (→ promotes
the 100M run). The generation-quality read: sample multi-sentence text from the 30M and eyeball coherence (the
[VERIFY]-style read the P1b work used). Refutation = 30M still caps retention ~55% AND generation is incoherent → the
capacity hypothesis needs 100M (the planned next stage), not a mechanism change. Reuse-by-import, NO `sim/` edit.

### Path (b) — b2 generative replay (constructive proposition)
**Probe (numpy-CPU, ~1-2 days, NO `sim/` edit):** on a small learned association graph (the PPMI stream cortex's
co-occurrence stats, V≈64), run an offline replay that resamples role-filler bindings to propose K candidate SVO
triples that were NEVER stored. Gate each by (i) graph-plausibility (constituents co-occur above threshold) and (ii)
non-contradiction with stored facts. **PASS =** the proposer emits novel-but-plausible triples (e.g. given "dog eats
meat", "cat eats fish", "wolf chase deer" stored, it proposes "wolf eats deer" / "dog chase cat" — graph-plausible,
never told) at a rate ABOVE a random-recombination baseline, AND it does NOT propose graph-implausible nonsense
("apple eats river") above chance. This is the first time the project would score >0 on novel-COMPOSITION from a brain
mechanism — the direct refutation of the measured 0.0. The decisive anti-cheat: a shuffled-graph control must collapse
the plausibility ranking (proving the graph statistics, not chance, drive the proposals).

**Cheapest overall single move:** the path-(a) a1 probe — it is ~1 day, CPU, reuses the GO integration harness, and
directly answers "can the system hold a substantive grounded open-ended conversation NOW." It is the product de-risk.
The b2 probe is the cheapest move toward "the brain genuinely INVENTS content," and it is also CPU/~1-2 days — worth
running in PARALLEL since it shares no state with the a1 probe.

## 5. ANTI-CHEAT CONTROLS (per the standing requirements)

Shared across all probes:
1. **No-confab / abstention floor (the load-bearing one).** A substantial set of UNSUPPORTED factual claims (or
   unstored cues) must be caught/abstained. For a1: prompt-elicited false claims ("lion eats grass") must be struck by
   verify. For b2: graph-IMPLAUSIBLE triples ("apple eats river") must NOT be proposed above chance. A drop here is a
   HARD FAIL — a "novel" emission that survives is only meaningful while the gate holds; otherwise it is a
   confabulation, not generation.
2. **Held-out vs stored separation (carried from `generation_novelty_probe`).** The novel content the system produces
   must be DISTINCT from the stored set (else it is retrieval relabeled as generation). For b2: assert the proposed
   triples are not in the stored set. For a1: the SUPPORTED claims may be stored, but the GENERATION (surface form)
   must be non-verbatim (distinct-n / verbatim-copy=0, as the P1b read already checks).
3. **Shuffled-structure control.** a1: a shuffled-store control (wrong cue→filler pairings) must make verify REJECT
   previously-accepted claims (the verify is load-bearing, not a string echo — exactly the P3 passive/active anti-cheat).
   b2: a shuffled-graph control must collapse the plausibility ranking (the co-occurrence statistics, not chance, drive
   the proposals). b1: the word-shuffle-corpus control (already in `generator_f_gate`) must fail to learn (the
   generator learns real structure, not n-gram noise).
4. **Lesion / ablation.** a1: disable the verify → confirm the false claims now leak (verify is causally responsible).
   b1: the no-replay control must catastrophically forget (already validated, 2.25× contrast). b2: ablate the
   plausibility gate → confirm nonsense floods in (the gate is causally responsible for sensibility).
5. **Multi-seed (≥6 for variable effects).** b1's C2 loop is already 3-seed; the scale-up + b2's proposer rate are
   variable effects → 6-seed before any GO claim, per `feedback_6seed_validation`. (a1's verify-catches-drift is
   mechanism-level like the novelty probe; 3-seed + a structural argument suffices for the moat-holds claim, but the
   FLUENCY/quality read benefits from multiple render seeds as the SCALED demo did.)

## 6. RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE — how they compose toward "the brain genuinely generates substantive grounded discourse"

The two paths are NOT competitors — they compose, and the near-term one scaffolds the deep one. Honest about far vs
near at each step.

**STEP 1 (NOW, ~1 day, CPU, near-term product): Path (a) a1 — claim-level generate-then-verify.** This is the
smallest delta over the GO integration demo and it DELIVERS a substantive grounded open-ended conversation immediately:
the faculty generates freely (genuine G3 fluency + novel surface form), the brain gates + verifies every factual claim
(the moat holds over open generation, owner-sanctioned trade). **What's the brain's:** the knowledge, the truth
authority, the verification, the abstention. **What's the LLM's:** the fluency, the sequencing, the connective tissue.
This is the honest near-term answer to "a genuinely substantive conversation" — and it is hallucination-safe by
construction.

**STEP 2 (PARALLEL with step 1, ~1-2 days, CPU, the deep direction's cheapest probe): Path (b) b2 — generative-replay
constructive-proposition probe.** The first attempt to score >0 on novel-COMPOSITION from a BRAIN mechanism (the
direct attack on the measured 0.0). If it works, the brain begins to INVENT plausible content — the genuinely-the-
brain's G2 capability. Its output (novel propositions) FEEDS step 1's renderer (the faculty renders the brain-invented
proposition fluently, still gated/verified). This is the first concrete bridge from "grounded retrieval" to
"constructive generation," and it is biology-faithful (hippocampal generative replay, catalog G.09).

**STEP 3 (after step 1 ships + step 2's direction is known, ~4-15 h LOCAL, the brain's own G3 engine): Path (b) b1 —
scale Gen-F to ~100M (staged 30M first).** Gives the brain its OWN open-ended discourse engine on the spiking
substrate (not the bolted-on LLM), demonstrating the full development→consolidation→continual-learning loop at a
substantive scale. It STILL wraps in step 1's gate/verify for grounding (a free spiking generator hallucinates like
any LLM). The endgame: **the brain's own spiking generator (b1) produces open-ended discourse (G3), seeded by the
brain's own constructive propositions (b2), all grounded + verified by the brain's store (a1)** — at which point the
generation is genuinely the brain's, the content is genuinely the brain's, and it is hallucination-safe.

**Why this order:** step 1 is the cheapest, ships the product, and BUILDS the verify wrapper that steps 2+3 both
reuse. Step 2 is the cheapest probe of the deep "brain invents content" question and shares no state with step 1 (run
concurrently). Step 3 is the most expensive (local GPU hours) and benefits from steps 1+2 existing first (it plugs
into the verify wrapper and can be seeded by the proposer). Each step independently advances "substantive grounded
discourse"; together they walk from "the LLM generates, the brain checks" → "the brain invents + the brain generates,
the brain checks."

**Far vs near, stated plainly:**
- **NEAR (days, CPU, already 90% built):** a substantive grounded open-ended conversation where the brain is the
  knowledge + truth authority and a spiking LLM faculty is the linguistic engine (step 1). This is achievable now.
- **MID (days-to-a-week, CPU + a probe):** the brain producing novel plausible PROPOSITIONS from a biology mechanism
  (step 2). New mechanism, real research uncertainty, but cheap to probe.
- **FAR-er (local GPU hours, staged):** the brain's OWN spiking open-ended generator at substantive scale (step 3).
  Built + validated at toy scale; the wall is capacity (≈100M, ~15 h local, NO cloud per the VRAM analysis).
- **DEEPEST / not on the critical path:** in-decoder logit-fusion grounding (a2) and a fully from-scratch local-
  learning generator (no backprop at all) — both deferred; a2 until perf levers land, the latter as the long-horizon
  biology-purity ideal.

## Honesty / caveats
- "The brain genuinely generates" is achieved in DEGREES, not as a binary. Step 1's generation is the LLM's (brain
  owns truth); step 3's is the brain's spiking dynamics but backprop-developed (owner-accepted as a development
  stand-in); only step 2 is from-biology constructive — and step 2 produces propositions (G2), not fluent discourse
  (G3), so even the deepest path leans on a renderer for surface form. There is no single move that makes ALL of
  {fluency, content, sequencing} the brain's from-biology at substantive scale today — that is the genuine far frontier.
- The SOTA spiking LLMs (SpikeGPT/SpikeLLM/LAS/FAS) are uniformly backprop-trained-then-spiked; there is no published
  large-scale spiking LM trained by local learning. So b1's backprop reframe is consistent with the field; a fully
  local-learning generative LM remains an open research problem everywhere, not just here.
- The moat/generativity trade is owner-sanctioned (`feedback_moat_not_hard_lossy_memory_ok`): genuine generativity
  REQUIRES relaxing "never assert the un-stored" to "never assert the un-supported / implausible." Every option above
  preserves the WEAKER (still strong) guarantee; none weakens it below "no unsupported factual claim survives."
- All probes are reuse-by-import, NO `sim/` edit. The only GPU need is step-3 (b1) scale-up, which the sizing scoping
  already confirmed is LOCAL (≤11 GB at 100M, no cloud until ~500M).
