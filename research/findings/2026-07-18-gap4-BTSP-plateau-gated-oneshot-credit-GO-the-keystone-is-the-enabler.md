## The mechanism (faithful, local, no weight transport, no global loss, ONE-SHOT)
- `IS(t)` = the postsynaptic dendritic PLATEAU voltage above `v_hold`, generated by the REAL bistable single-cell
  dynamics (`fused_coincidence_plateau`'s self-limiting self-regen sustain + KIR — the exact terms the keystone added).
- `E_i(t)` = presynaptic synaptic-eligibility trace (tau ~ seconds; Milstein).
- Update (one presentation): `dw_i = eta * [ integral_t E_i(t)*IS_pos(t) dt ] * (w_max - w_i)` (saturating potentiation).
  Local — each `dw_i` uses only its own pre-trace and the shared postsynaptic plateau; no `W` in the credit => no transport.
