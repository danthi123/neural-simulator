## The one thing 2.2 adds (and why it is "thinking", not retrieval)

The production `query_chain` (`reason_chain`) — GO 2026-06-17 — takes a **caller-supplied** relation list:
`query_chain('dog', ['eat','eat']) → 'mouse'`. Tier 2.2 is **SELF-CUED**: from a START concept with NO plan, the
agent picks the next relation to chase **by its own learned association strength** (`_relation_assoc`, a
relation-keyed co-occurrence over its facts — the same statistic dialogue-planning's `_assoc_graph` reads, keyed
by the RELATION so it picks *which* relation to chase), then chases it via the validated `query_patient` (match
the concept as AGENT under the chosen relation, read the PATIENT). The cleanup re-discretizes between hops so error
does not compound. That single change — **the agent choosing each hop instead of the caller** — is exactly the
front-3 §2.6 / roadmap-2.2 reframe that turns retrieve-and-render into a self-generated train of thought.

It does NOT use vector analogy (2.1 NO-GO is a separate boundary): 2.2 chains DISCRETE stored facts via the
role-structured pointer-chase, so it sidesteps that wall entirely.

## Reading it honestly (the burned-project bar)

- **The anti-cheats ARE the result.** A self-cued accuracy any control also achieved would be the 2026-05-14
  retraction repeated. Here: the chase beats spreading by **+0.92**; **lesioning the learned association the
  selector reads drives it to 0.00** (the decisive load-bearing lesion — the agent's CHOICE is what's load-bearing,
  not just the hop); permutation and re-cue collapse to chance; the moat holds at every hop. So this is genuine
  **self-cued role-structured relational chaining**, not co-occurrence smearing.
- **Two selector-lesion modes, and why ZERO is the load-bearing one.** ZERO (remove the learned association → the
  selector abstains) collapses to **0.00** at every depth/seed/D — unambiguous. A second "DEMOTE" lesion (scramble
  the relation ordering) is reported as a CORROBORATING control only and is NOT gated: it drops the chain toward
  the floor but leaves a seed-variable residual (~0.33 aggregate) because the toy's relation alphabet is tiny
  (`{eat,play,see}`) — a scrambled selector still picks `eat` ~1/3 of hops, and the goal-early-stop lets such a walk
  occasionally thread the chain. That is a **toy property of the 3-relation alphabet, not a mechanism leak** (the
  zero lesion already proves the learned association is load-bearing). Documented in the probe + the gate.
- **Honest scope (where the wall actually lives).** 40 well-separated concepts on the numpy fast path
  (spiking-cleanup parity is established multi-seed elsewhere). D-independence (128≡256≡512) shows cleanup is not
  yet stressed. The genuine depth/SNR limit appears at **320-concept** scale and on **grounded/correlated** codes —
  the same bounded follow-on the `query_chain` GO flagged (and it remains a deliverable either way). This is
  chaining over *stored* relational facts (Eichenbaum–Cohen relational memory; CA3 attractor cleanup) — NOT
  generalizing a relation to never-seen items (the factorised-relational-code / TEM frontier, out of scope).
