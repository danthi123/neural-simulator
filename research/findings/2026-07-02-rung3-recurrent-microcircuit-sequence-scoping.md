## 1. DIAGNOSIS — isolate the genuine question

The confirmed rung-2 mechanism (`_emerge3_microcircuit_derisk.py:MicrocircuitMLP`, `_emerge5c_microcircuit_noise_derisk.py:SpikingMicrocircuitMLP`) is **feedforward, layered**. Its credit is the self-predicting apical error (M2.11): a lateral SST-like interneuron learns to *predict/cancel* the recurrent/top-down-driven apical drive, so the apical residual is exactly `W_PP_td @ (r_upper − r_int) = W_PP_td @ e_{k+1}` — a local prediction error playing backprop's `δ_k`, descending layer-by-layer with a `φ'(u)` factor. No weight transport (`W_PP_td` fixed-random, `W_PI = −W_PP_td`), rate-limit steady state, credit read in the converged self-predicting form (`_emerge3_microcircuit_derisk.py:177-229`).

**The genuine rung-3 question is a two-parter, and it must not be conflated:**

1. **Where does the sequence STRUCTURE (temporal memory) live, and where does the CREDIT live?** In the feedforward microcircuit, "depth" is layers. For sequences, "depth" is *time* (recurrence unrolled). Two mechanistically distinct jobs must be filled:
   - **(J1) temporal store/recall** — hold and regenerate a spike trajectory over time (recurrent dynamics).
   - **(J2) temporal credit** — teach the recurrent weights *which* recurrent connections to change so the trajectory matches a target, done LOCALLY (no BPTT through time).

2. **Is rung-3 credit a genuinely NEW mechanism, or a temporal unrolling of the confirmed microcircuit?** The load-bearing finding of this scoping (see §2) is that the **microcircuit's active-cancellation credit has an EXACT recurrent analogue already published** — the Muratore–Capone–Paolucci **target-based** rule — whose local error is *literally* `[a* − a]` (target-apical-driven activity minus recurrent-apical-driven activity), the same "interneuron/cancellation predicts the recurrent drive; the apical residual IS the error" structure, but with the top-down teaching arriving as a **target spike trajectory injected into a distal apical compartment** instead of a per-example output nudge. So rung-3 credit is **~80% a temporal unrolling of what we have (the same cancellation-difference error), + ~20% genuinely new**: (i) the credit is now a *target trajectory* (a sequence of desired activity), not a single output-error nudge; (ii) the recurrent eligibility trace `e_t = ∂v_t/∂w` (spike-response function, iteratively computed, no BPTT) is the new temporal-credit machinery the feedforward microcircuit never needed. That combination is the genuine new-mechanism-class trigger — research-gate justified, but the *core credit signal* is a reuse, which is exactly why it should be cheap and likely to work.

## 2. REFRAME — how does real cortex do recurrent/temporal sequence credit, and which candidate is closest to our microcircuit?

Cortex does temporal/sequence learning through **segregated dendritic compartments + bursts + top-down teaching at the apical tuft**, and the 2024–26 literature has converged on this being *vectorized, single-phase, cellular-level* credit:

- **Francioni, Harnett et al. 2026 (Nature), "Vectorized instructive signals in cortical dendrites"** — the decisive fresh empirical authority. In L5 pyramidal neurons (retrosplenial cortex, a neurofeedback BCI task), the **relative magnitude of somatic vs distal-apical dendritic signals carries a vectorized, per-neuron instructive signal** (reward/error, sign set by each neuron's causal task role), predicts learning-driven activity change, and **optogenetic perturbation of it disrupts learning**. This is the biological ground truth: apical dendrites carry a *teaching signal tailored per neuron*, single-phase, computed from the surrounding network — exactly the ingredient the target-based recurrent rule uses, and the same apical-teaching channel our microcircuit already models.
- **Payeur et al. 2020 (Nat Neurosci, 325 cites)** — burst-dependent plasticity coordinates learning in hierarchical circuits via regenerative apical activity + feedback pathways (our rung-2 lineage).
- **Baronig & Legenstein 2024 (Front. Neurosci.)** — a derived apical-synapse plasticity rule associates top-down *context* with basal features via branch-local NMDA/Ca²⁺ events; supports context-dependent tasks + continual learning by allocating dendritic branches to new contexts (relevant to J1 context-disambiguation).

**The decisive reframe:** the Capone **unified framework** (`10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010221`, verified) proves error-based (e-prop) and target-based are the **two ends of ONE rule** parameterised by the rank `R` of the feedback matrix `D = Bᵀ₊B₊` (`Δw = η Σ_t [Σ_k D_ik (s̄*_{k,t+1} − s̄_{k,t+1})] p_i^t e_j^t`, Eq. 10). Low `R` (= output dim) → error-based/e-prop; max `R` (= N, D diagonal) → **target-based**, `Δw ≃ η Σ_t (s̄*_{i,t+1} − s̄_{i,t+1}) p_i^t e_j^t` (Eq. 12). At the target-based/diagonal limit the rule uses **only the post-synaptic neuron's own target-vs-actual + the pre-synaptic filtered spike + the eligibility trace** — which is precisely our microcircuit's active-cancellation credit made recurrent, with the target trajectory replacing the descending output nudge. So we are not choosing between competing families; we are choosing WHERE on the microcircuit's own credit axis to sit, and the target-based/diagonal end is the one that matches our confirmed noise-robust cancellation.

**Honest locality nuance (trust-but-verify, load-bearing):** Capone notes the target-based Eq. 12 is only *fully* local when `D` is diagonal; the general high-rank `D` still needs a broadcast of other neurons' errors. The Muratore–Capone–Paolucci *burst* paper resolves this biologically — the "error" `(a* − a)` is computed **locally at each neuron's apical compartment** by comparing its teacher-driven distal-apical burst against its recurrence-driven proximal-apical burst (the interneuron/cancellation is per-neuron), so the diagonal/local limit is the biologically-realised one. The de-risk MUST assert this locality (no cross-neuron error broadcast in the recurrent rule), or it is smuggling BPTT/weight-transport.

## 3. RANKED CHEAP-FIRST DE-RISK (recommended path)

### The recommended mechanism
**A recurrent target-based microcircuit (`RecurrentMicrocircuitRNN`): the confirmed active-cancellation credit, made recurrent per Muratore–Capone–Paolucci.** One recurrent population of rate-limit pyramidal units with (basal=input, apical-proximal=recurrent, apical-distal=teaching); the local recurrent update is `ΔW_rec ∝ (a*_{t+1} − a_{t+1}) · e_t` where `a` = recurrence-driven apical activity, `a*` = teacher-driven apical activity (target trajectory injected via a fixed-random distal-apical projection), and `e_t` = the iterative spike-response eligibility trace `e_t = α·e_{t-1} + (1−α)·r_{t-1}` (no BPTT). Read-out trained separately by MSE. This is the diagonal/local (R=N) target-based limit of the Capone unified rule = the recurrent unrolling of EMERGE-3's `(r_upper − r_int)` cancellation error.

### The cheapest de-risk (numpy, CPU, reuse-by-import — mirror the EMERGE-5c harness exactly)
- **Rung 3a (numpy, rate-limit, the FIRST run):** a single recurrent population; test on a small SEQUENCE task; report own recall + a clean-readout representation gate + a memorization floor + all anti-cheats. **No spike noise yet** (add it in 3b — do not conflate the recurrence question with the finite-sample question). Estimated wall clock ≈ EMERGE-3/5c scale (tens of seconds to a few minutes, CPU, arm-parallel via `ProcessPoolExecutor` as in `_emerge5c_...:196`).
- **Rung 3b (numpy, spiking / finite-sample noise, the SECOND run):** re-inject the EMERGE-5 noise model `r_obs = Binomial(S, r)/S` on the credit-carrying rates (reuse `SpikingMicrocircuitMLP`'s injection point + the strict `cancel_noise` mode, `_emerge5c_...:76-120`) to test whether recurrent active cancellation stays noise-robust when the credit rides on noisy spike-count rates over TIME (the honest risk — see §4).

### The concrete SEQUENCE task (small, two variants — start with A)
- **Task A — store/recall of a fixed spike trajectory (the Capone/Muratore canonical de-risk; recommended first).** A short target trajectory `s*_{1..T}` (T≈50–200 rate-limit steps) = a low-frequency superposition (e.g. sum of 3–4 sinusoids at 1/2/3/5 "Hz", exactly the Muratore setup) OR a fixed random binary spike raster over N_seq≈16–32 units. Cue with the first step; the recurrent net must autonomously regenerate the rest. **Metric:** trajectory MSE / spike-error `ΔS = Σ|s* − s|`; "recall" = reconstruction correlation ≥ a bar (e.g. ρ ≥ 0.8) after the teacher is withdrawn (teacher-neutrality: recurrence sustains it).
- **Task B — next-symbol prediction over a small discrete alphabet (the communication-relevant variant; run after A GOes).** A high-order sequence over a small alphabet (V≈6–8 symbols, e.g. two overlapping sequences `A B C D E` / `X B C D F` that need context to disambiguate the shared middle — the Bouhadjar high-order test). **Metric:** next-symbol accuracy on held-out continuations; require it to *generalize* the context rule, not memorize (held-out ≥ 0.75 ≫ a 1-step-Markov floor). This is the one that maps to language production.

## 4. VERDICT + honest risks

**VERDICT: rung 3 is a BOUNDED numpy de-risk (recommended path), and its credit rule is ~80% reuse of the confirmed active-cancellation microcircuit + ~20% a genuine new mechanism-class (target-trajectory teaching + a recurrent eligibility trace) — which crosses the research-gate "new mechanism class" trigger, but is exactly the kind of *compose-already-proven-pieces* build that should be cheap and is well-supported by published mechanisms + fresh empirical biology.** Recommend: build `RecurrentMicrocircuitRNN` (rate-limit) reusing the EMERGE-3 credit template + the EMERGE-1 harness scaffold; run **3a (rate, Task A store/recall) FIRST**, then **3b (spike-noise) and Task B (next-symbol)** only if 3a GOes. Do NOT start the protected `sim/` port (rung 4) until 3a+3b GO. Adopt the Muratore target-based *rule* + optionally the Predictive-E-prop predict-next *objective*; keep Bouhadjar self-organizing as the positive-control/fallback arm; defer GLE.

**Honest risks (foreground, do not gloss):**
1. **Recurrent feedback-noise is the SAME finite-sample family as rung 2 — and over TIME it may compound.** Rung-2's GO was carried by the *exact top-down output nudge*. In the recurrent target-based rule the "nudge" is a target *trajectory* injected through a fixed-random distal-apical projection — still an exact teacher, so the structural noise-robustness argument SHOULD carry (the credit magnitude comes from the exact teacher, not a noisy local estimate). BUT the eligibility trace `e_t` integrates noisy rates over many timesteps, and errors could accumulate across the unrolled recurrence in a way the 2-hop feedforward case did not exhibit. **This is precisely what rung-3b tests** — and a BOUNDARY there is build-informative (it would point, per the shortlist, to Urbanczik–Senn 2009 population-*feedback* factors or NMNC noise-geometry, not another point-credit rule), exactly as EMERGE-5c's own BOUNDARY branch anticipated.
2. **Recurrence may break the microcircuit's self-predicting-cancellation ASSUMPTION.** EMERGE-3 read credit in the *converged* self-predicting interneuron form (a separable pre-training concern). In a recurrent net the interneuron/cancellation must track a *moving* recurrent drive over time — self-prediction convergence is no longer a one-shot pre-training step. The Muratore burst rule sidesteps this by computing `(a* − a)` as a per-timestep burst-coincidence comparison (not a converged interneuron), which is cleaner — so the recommended rule uses the *burst-comparison* form, not the held-converged-interneuron form. Flag: verify the per-step `(a* − a)` is stable (doesn't oscillate) as EMERGE-3's debugging arc found the converged-form necessary feedforward; the recurrent case may need the burst-window gating to stabilise it.
3. **The task might not NEED credit (Hebbian suffices).** Bouhadjar shows high-order next-symbol prediction is achievable with *zero* teaching (pure structural Hebbian + WTA). If the `hebbian_selforg` arm matches `recurrent_microcircuit` on Task B, the honest read is that *store/recall next-symbol* is a self-organizing capability and the target-based credit's value is elsewhere (arbitrary target trajectories / motor sequences / faster convergence) — build-informative, not a failure, and it would re-point rung 3 toward Task A (arbitrary trajectory reproduction) where credit is provably needed.
4. **Locality smuggling.** The Capone target-based limit is only fully local when the feedback matrix is diagonal; a lazy implementation could broadcast cross-neuron errors (BPTT-in-disguise). The code-level locality assert (§3 anti-cheat 5) is mandatory and load-bearing.
