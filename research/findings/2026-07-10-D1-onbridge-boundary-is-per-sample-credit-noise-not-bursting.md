## Root cause (a0 reads: the kernel + the reference)
The `sim/` BDSP update is a correct 3-factor outer product applied EVERY teach step: `dw = lr · E_pre · (B − P̄·E)_post` (`bridge.py:7247-7273`), with `E_pre` the presynaptic event-rate eligibility and `(B − P̄·E)` the postsynaptic burst-deviation — mechanically sound, moat-clean. The difference from the reference that learns (0.964): `MicrocircuitBDSPNet._train` (`_gnw_d1_spiking_bdsp_derisk.py:545-550`) trains **BATCHED** — `dw = mean-over-batch(Etilde^T @ dev) + momentum`, with E/P analytic sigmoids (smooth, zero finite-sample variance). The on-bridge net updates **per sample (batch=1)** on **measured, finite-sample-noisy** spike-count B/E. That reference is itself documented **batch-fragile** (alignment credit 0.924 @ batch 32 → 0.513 @ full batch). ⇒ the on-bridge boundary is **per-sample spike-count credit NOISE**: each sample's burst-deviation is a noisy finite-sample estimate, the weight is stepped per-sample, and the noise swamps the class signal before it can accumulate into a discriminative map.

## The named surpass (biology + the SNN field both solve per-event noise by AVERAGING credit)
Online (per-event) spiking credit assignment is noisy by nature; biology and neuromorphic learning both average it: **slow synaptic eligibility traces that integrate the plasticity signal over many events/samples before it acts** (three-factor rules; Gerstner e-prop's eligibility accumulation; Payeur-Naud's moving-average burst rate), and/or **batch-like consolidation during replay**. The cheap-first de-risk (in flight): reduce the per-sample step noise by **credit averaging** — a much slower `bdsp_lr` + many more epochs (many tiny steps ≈ one averaged step), and a longer low-pass on E/B — on a balanced n_train=16 set. If slow averaging lifts train-fit decisively above chance → per-sample noise confirmed + the lever is credit averaging (then: an explicit host-batched-credit accumulation, or a slow-eligibility `sim/` path). If it stays flat → a deeper credit-timing issue (eligibility vs burst-deviation alignment within the per-sample teach) and a fresh deep-research gate.
