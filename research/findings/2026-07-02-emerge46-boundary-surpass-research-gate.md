## Move (b) — reframe via real biology
The ventral visual hierarchy achieves invariance NOT by discriminative learning but by **hierarchical pooling of SHARED features**: V1 complex cells pool simple-cell outputs with (near-)uniform weights to gain position-invariant orientation tuning (Kandel 6e Ch 17, Hubel–Wiesel 1965); IT encodes object identity invariant to view/position (Kandel Ch 29; Op de Beeck 2010); **divisive normalization** (Kandel Ch 28; Carandini–Heeger 1999) makes a pooling layer tune to RELATIVE structure (input minus context), which is exactly local normalization. Our own prior reframe is the direct precedent: **conversation-cortex GENERALIZATION came from feedforward LOCAL NORMALIZATION (PPMI: log + per-hub + per-concept mean-subtract + threshold), NOT cross-neuron decorrelation** ("off-diagonal red herring", EMERGE-19). ⇒ the on-substrate L2 pooler is testing the WRONG hypothesis (discriminate) when it should pool for INVARIANCE over shared structure.
