# 2026-05-09 — Phase 2.3b 50M cosine smoke REFUTES Phase 2 scale thesis

**Status:** Phase 2.2b v3 50M-param checkpoint produced inter-word
cosine **0.85** (worse than Phase 2.3a 134K's 0.65-0.80). Chain's
auto-fire SKIPPED the full Phase 2.3b Bridge transfer test. Phase 2's
central hypothesis ("scale will fix transfer") is REFUTED at this
scale class.

## Result

```
Phase 2.2b v3 training complete:
  Architecture: 66 -> 4096 -> 4096 -> 4096 -> 66 LIF (~50M params)
  10000 samples × 50 epochs (= 500K sample-views, no overfitting)
  Initial loss: 9.7509
  Final loss:   3.2205   (67% reduction)
  Wall clock:   1405 sec (23.4 min on RTX 3090)
```

Cosine similarity smoke (CPU-side, 4 direction words at the
last-hidden layer, sum-pooled spikes over the word's chars):

```
                   v3 (50M, 4096-d features)    Phase 2.3a (134K, 256-d)
  north vs east    0.837                         (0.65-0.80 range)
  north vs south   0.861
  north vs west    0.835
  east vs south    0.847
  east vs west     0.870
  south vs west    0.852
  AVERAGE          0.850                         ~0.72
```

**Bigger model produced features that are MORE alike across
direction words, not less.**

## Why this is the strong refutation

Phase 2.3a NEGATIVE at 134K params left open the question:
"is the failure due to under-parameterization, or due to the
objective being wrong for word-action transfer?"

If it were under-parameterization: scale should help.
If it were wrong objective: scale should NOT help (might even hurt).

v3 at ~375× more params (134K → 50M) gave WORSE inter-word
discrimination (0.72 → 0.85). The hypothesis "scale fixes it" is
falsified — the curve goes the wrong direction.

This is consistent with the wrong-objective explanation:
- Char-level next-char prediction doesn't push features to be
  word-discriminative. It pushes them to encode statistical
  regularities (which char follows which).
- Bigger models memorize those regularities better, but the FEATURE
  SPACE for "north"/"east"/"south"/"west" doesn't differentiate
  the words — they're all just sequences with similar char-level
  statistical patterns.
- More params → tighter packing of similar-looking features →
  HIGHER cosine across direction words.

## Why cosine smoke (not full Bridge run) is sufficient

The Phase 2.3a NEGATIVE Bridge run took ~3-6 hr to produce
22% W2A. We can predict the Bridge result from the cosine smoke:

- 134K cosine 0.72 → Bridge 22% W2A (NEGATIVE)
- 50M cosine 0.85 → Bridge ?% W2A (predicted equally NEGATIVE
  or worse — features are MORE confused, not less)

Spending 3-6 hr to confirm what the 30-sec smoke already shows
is wasteful. The chain script's smoke gate (avg cosine >= 0.65 →
SKIP) caught this correctly.
