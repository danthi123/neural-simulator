# objrel reservoir robustness — research gate: the seed-fragile objrel signal is a LINEAR-MEMORY failure; fix cheapest-first with ORTHOGONAL recurrent W + ρ→1 + lower input-scaling/leak

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Type:** read-only deep-research gate (ESN memory-capacity literature + Hinaut-Dominey), for the two-part objrel residual's UPSTREAM half (reservoir info-absence on 2/10 seeds; `2026-07-06-objrel-basin-escape-restart-NEGATIVE-*.md`).

## Diagnosis (cited)
The objrel non-local dependency ("the ball THAT the dog chased" → slot0=THEME, fixed by a marker several tokens AFTER the head) is a **LINEAR delay-memory** demand: the reservoir must hold a linearly-readable delayed copy of the head until the cue arrives (the ridge is linear). This is a **textbook ESN seed-fragility on a memory-demanding task** (Lukoševičius Practical Guide §3.3.2: reservoir performance variance is "ever present," larger for smaller reservoirs). By the **Dambre et al. 2012 capacity-conservation law** (Sci.Rep. 2:514, Thm 4 ΣC≤N / Thm 7 =N): linear delay-memory and nonlinear computation share a FIXED total budget ~N; on the 2 bad seeds the random draw spent too much budget on nonlinear cross-terms, leaving the specific delay channel below the noise floor. **So the fix buys LINEAR memory + reduces its variance — NOT more nonlinearity.**

## Ranked cheapest-first fixes (each a single-variable de-risk: build reservoir on all 10 seeds → measure analytic-ridge objrel; NO read-out training → fast; fan across cores)
1. **ORTHOGONALIZE / cyclic-structure the recurrent W (keep the nonlinearity).** STRONGEST support, LOWEST cost (a construction change, no extra inference cost). Drives linear MC to the N ceiling **on EVERY seed by construction** — removes seed-dependence at its source. Rodan-Tiňo 2011 (IEEE TNN 22(1), Thm 1: cyclic reservoir MC=N−(1−r^{2N})→N; deterministic, variance MC 19.48±1.29 / DLR ±0.89 vs random ESN 18.25±1.46). Farkas-Bosák-Gergeľ 2016 (Neural Networks 83:109, orthogonalization → near-maximal MC "up to ~500 units" = our ~300-neuron regime). Mechanism: orthogonal/permutation W keeps the delayed input images {W^k V} exactly independent → every delay channel preserved, nothing left for the seed to vary.
2. **ρ (spectral radius) → 1 (sweep 0.9→1.0→1.1–1.3) + LOWER leak (0.3→~0.15).** Very strong, near-zero cost. Both lengthen the fading-memory timescale (Jaeger 2002 MC max as ρ→1; Practical Guide §3.2.4/§3.2.6 "small leak can dramatically increase STM duration"; edge-of-chaos optimum Bertschinger-Natschläger 2004 / Legenstein-Maass 2007 KR−GR). Stay AT/near 1 — Dambre: total capacity collapses PAST the fading-memory boundary.
3. **LOWER input scaling (1.0 → ~0.2).** Strong, near-zero cost. Keeps the units in their linear regime so the fixed budget isn't spent on nonlinearity/saturation ("virtual loss of useful memory," Practical Guide §3.2.5).
4. **ENSEMBLE ≥5 full-size reservoirs (concatenate states → one ridge).** Strong, K× cost. Directly launders the "8/10 carry it" pattern (average over enough seeds → the 2 bad draws irrelevant). Use full-size members (task is memory-heavy; Practical Guide §4.4 caveat).
5. **Grow N** (300→600→1000). Solid, highest cost, dominated by #1 (a random large reservoir still wastes off-diagonal capacity).

**Recommended recipe:** orthogonalized recurrent W (#1) at ρ≈1.0–1.1, leak≈0.15, input-scaling≈0.2 (#2/#3); if residual, a 3–5 reservoir state-concatenation ensemble (#4). **Substrate note:** ours is a SPIKING Izhikevich LSM (not a tanh ESN) — map ρ→recurrent-weight-matrix spectral radius, input-scaling→drive gain, leak→neuron time constant, orthogonal-W→recurrent connectivity matrix orthogonalization. De-risk each on the analytic-ridge-objrel-per-seed metric.
