## What this means

**Cortex internal recurrence carries the entire W→A binding signal post-consolidation.**

The strict anti-cheat removes:
1. **Hippo activity** — 10× silencing current ensures hippo neurons can't fire at all
2. **Hippo→cortex synaptic transmission** — zeroing all ca1→cortex edge weights ensures no signal can propagate even if hippo did fire

Removing both produces NO change in retention. The post-consolidation accuracy comes entirely from the cortex's own internal weights (motor↔motor recurrence, motor↔language_output, the topographic prior, and the FS lateral inhibition).

Sleep replay during training transferred the binding pattern from hippo↔cortex synapses INTO cortex internal weights. Once consolidated, hippo is no longer needed.

This is exactly what the McClelland 1995 / Buzsaki 2013 CLS theory predicts. Empirical confirmation at 3-seed in the Tier 2.1 v4 architecture.
