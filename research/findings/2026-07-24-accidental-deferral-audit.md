# Accidental-deferral audit — faculties silently parked while the frontier moved (2026-07-24)

**Type:** process audit / record-keeping (no experiment). **Trigger:** the 2026-07-23 pivot's HARD RULE #1 — *do not
defer any needed functionality* — plus the doc-sync review of the master roadmap's faculty map. Under the pivot, a
faculty that is "buildable-now" or "named-but-unbuilt" and has quietly fallen off the queue **is an accidental
deferral**, not a legitimately-parked item. This audit lists every one found, its status (being TAKEN now vs QUEUED),
and the biological surpass each needs so none is lost. The (A)-tagged memory/perception items are fed into the master
roadmap's §7 walls ledger.

## Why these were "accidental"
The 5-gap → genuine-cognition pivot re-pointed effort at the Phase-0 keystones (affect / curiosity / self-schema /
workspace / ToM). That was correct, but several already-scoped, high-value faculties — most of them memory- and
perception-side — were never explicitly parked; they just stopped being the frontier. The pivot's no-defer rule makes
that a bug. Each item below either has a runner/mechanism already scoped or is a compose-GO-pieces build; none is
genuinely blocked.
