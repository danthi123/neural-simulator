## Two independent proofs that the fading STATE (not the credit rule) is the long-range limit
**(1) Extending the CREDIT horizon amplifies WITHIN the state's reach only (6-seed).** A stabilized dual-timescale eligibility (the ALIF horizon lever, at lr002 where it does not destabilize) makes e-prop's credit trace reach further back — and it robustly AMPLIFIES the within-state-reach gain (plastic→adaptive: d4-5 −0.056→−0.137, d6-9 −0.092→−0.145, **6/6** deeper) — **but d10-99 is UNCHANGED (−0.073, both)**. Longer credit cannot help where the STATE holds no context to credit. (Trades short for mid: d1 +0.08→+0.15.)

**(2) Extending the STATE memory (heterogeneous time constants) FAILS on real text (6-seed).** A heterogeneous-leak reservoir (α_i ~ log-uniform[0.03, 0.6] → effective windows ~2-33 tokens, slow units READ by the read-out) vs the homogeneous α=0.3 baseline, absolute held-out CE by depth:

- **A1 (passive slow state) is WORSE than homogeneous at EVERY depth.** Adding slow-leak units does not recover deep context — it dilutes. Mechanism: a slow LEAKY state `h_t=(1-α)h_{t-1}+α·tanh(·)` with small α is a slow AVERAGE (a `cumsum/t`-like 1/t dilution of any distal item), NOT a specific-item hold. It blurs the recent ~30 tokens; it cannot represent "the token at position t−15."
- **A2 (hetero + e-prop) claws back only a TINY deep win** (d6-9 −0.015, d10-99 −0.040 vs homogeneous) at a LARGE short-context cost (d1 +0.162) — learning the recurrent weights makes the slow units marginally useful at depth, but it is small and not worth the short-context loss.

## Why longer τ cannot be the answer (the mechanism, not just this spectrum)
A different hetero spectrum might shift the balance, but the limit is fundamental: **any leaky/fading state averages and dilutes; it cannot hold a specific distal item against same-category interference.** The clean contrast is the project's own RUNG-2 result: the fading reservoir's dependent-token accuracy decays 0.55 (D=2)→0.125 (D=32, chance), and a **non-fading single-slot latch restores it to 0.97**. Non-fading HOLD, not slow FADE, is the mechanism. (The research gate isolated this as the A1-vs-A2 fork: A1 = state-fades-too-fast, cheaply extendable but bounded; **A2 = a fixed leaky projection has no content-addressable hold** — and A2 is what governs real text. This de-risk resolves the fork to A2: the homomean/lesion controls, designed to attribute a POSITIVE hetero signal, are moot because there is no positive signal — A1 is refuted directly.)
