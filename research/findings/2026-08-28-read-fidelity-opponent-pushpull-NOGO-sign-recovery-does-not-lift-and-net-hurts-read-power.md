# Read-fidelity: the opponent/push-pull spiking read is NO-GO (0/6) and, on average, WORSE than the single rectified channel it was meant to fix

Artifact: `research/findings/raw/_read_fidelity_opponent_pushpull_derisk_6seed.json` (numpy, 6 seeds; same trained cross-edge + same captured rasters iterations 1-4 used — no retraining confound). Runner iteration 5 of the read-fidelity arc, `research/runners/_read_fidelity_opponent_pushpull_derisk.py`.

## The arc so far (one crux, six reads)

The surprise->`source_provenance` F2 crux asks whether a spiking read can separate GENERATED from PERCEIVED on the trained cross-edge. Scalar reads failed: mean-rate 0/6, first-spike-latency 0/6 (clean instrument), ISI-CV/Fano dispersion 1/6. A linear+MLP DECODER over the full 10-bin per-neuron profile then separated 6/6, shuffle-clean ([decoder-SIGNAL-FOUND](2026-08-28-read-fidelity-decoder-SIGNAL-FOUND-its-a-read-limit-not-wiring.md)) — proving the signal IS in the distributed pattern. A biological spiking read (iteration 4: a population-vector/matched-filter TEMPLATE, rectified to a single non-negative channel, driving one LIF readout) then went NO-GO 0/6 ([popvec-template-NOGO](2026-08-28-read-fidelity-popvec-template-biological-read-NOGO-power-gap-not-signal-gap.md)), diagnosed as a READ-POWER gap: the rectified channel discards every bin where the PERCEIVED pool discriminates more than the GENERATED pool, exactly the sign (negative-weight) information an unconstrained decoder is free to use.

## This rung — an opponent (push-pull) read recovering the discarded sign — still NO-GO, and net WORSE

The mechanism (module docstring has the full derivation): fit TWO Dale's-law-respecting, always-non-negative channels on the TRAIN fold — `template_E = clip(mu_gen-mu_perc, 0, None)/pooled_std` (the PUSH/excitatory channel, identical to iteration 4's own rectified template) and `template_I = clip(mu_perc-mu_gen, 0, None)/pooled_std` (the PULL/inhibitory OPPONENT channel: the magnitude of exactly the bins iteration 4 zeroed out). The readout LIF neuron's input current is the NET of the two channels' contributions, `I_push - I_pull`, applied to the TEST fold's own raw spikes. Algebraically this reconstructs the full SIGNED template (`template_E - template_I` == the un-rectified mean-difference) iteration 4's own docstring "considered and rejected" as a single sign-flipping synapse — but now realized as two separately non-negative populations whose antagonism carries the sign, exactly the biological push-pull motif of cat V1 simple cells (Hirsch, Alonso, Reid & Martinez 1998, J Neurosci 18(22):9517-9528, "Synaptic integration in striate cortical simple cells", PMID 9801388 — a simple cell's response sign is set by the antagonism between a separate excitatory PUSH and inhibitory PULL conductance, never by a synapse flipping polarity; the same motif recurs in Troyer, Krukowski, Priebe & Miller 1998 J Neurosci PMID 9671678, Martinez et al. 2005 Nat Neurosci PMID 15711543, and Taylor, Sedigh-Sarvestani, Vigeland, Palmer & Contreras 2017 J Neurosci PMID 29196320). Everything else — pool build/train/lesion, raster capture, binned features, the stratified K_FOLDS=5-fold neuron-identity CV, the LIF readout model, threshold calibration protocol, R_REPEATS=5/K_SHUF=20 anti-cheat budget, and the `delta_held_base` primary gate — is reused VERBATIM from iteration 4, so this is a clean single-variable A/B.

- **`GO = False`; `n_seeds_pass_primary = 0/6`.** Per-seed primary z (`delta_held_base`): seed42=-1.2068, seed43=+0.5106, seed44=+0.089146, seed100=+1.1388, seed101=+0.8090, seed102=-0.0614. None clears Z_FLOOR=2.0.
- **Instrument VALID:** neuron-identity permutation null collapses on 24/24 combo-seed pairs (`n_seeds_shuffle_ok=6`); `seed_trap_build_twice.identical=True` (build hashes `df4c33e6...` both times); all 3 preconditions (`shuffle_anticheat_collapses_on_every_combo`, `emergence_grew_from_near_zero`, `anti_cheat_random_assignment`) OK. So the 0/6 is a real read verdict, not an instrument failure.
- **Lesion attributability:** seeds 42 and 102 are `lesion_ok=True` (frac_attributable 0.69 and 0.95); the other four are not — the same rough shape iteration 4 showed (only its z=1.32 seed 102 was lesion-attributable there), so the opponent read did not systematically improve the cross-edge-attributable fraction either.

## What this settles (NO-DEFER — a verdict on THIS mechanism, not the capability)

**Recovering the discarded sign information is not, by itself, the missing read power.** The opponent channel is mathematically equivalent to simply not rectifying iteration 4's template (`template_E - template_I` reconstructs the raw signed mean-difference exactly) — so this result is really a measurement of what the RECTIFICATION in iteration 4 was doing. The most consistent reading of a net DOWN shift: the "negative" bins (`mu_perc > mu_gen` in TRAIN) are largely **noisy estimates near zero**, not a real anti-correlated signal — with K_FOLDS=5 stratified CV over ~64 union neurons, each TRAIN fold estimates `mu_gen`/`mu_perc` per bin from roughly two dozen neurons per class, and a small-sample mean-difference in a genuinely near-zero bin is as likely to have the "wrong" sign from pure estimation noise as the "right" one. Iteration 4's rectification was therefore functioning as an (accidental) **noise-suppression / regularization** step, not merely a Dale's-law compliance cost — clipping those noisy bins to zero threw away noise along with whatever real signal they carried, and on net the noise dominated: unclipping them (this run) added variance without adding matching signal, most visibly on the two seeds (42, 102) where iteration 4's own z was closest to informative. This refines, not reverses, iteration 4's diagnosis: the READ-POWER gap is real, but it is not simply "the rectified channel discards sign" — a full-power estimator built the naive way (mirror the rectification) is not automatically more powerful than a well-regularized one.

## Honest residual and the next lever (NO-DEFER)

The opponent/push-pull motif itself is not refuted as biology (Hirsch et al. 1998 and its replications are solid ground) — what is refuted is this SPECIFIC realization: a **fixed 1:1 push-minus-pull gain**, with no mechanism to down-weight a channel whose TRAIN-fold estimate is noise-dominated. Two concrete next levers, ranked:

1. **A LEARNED opponent gain**, not a fixed 1:1 subtraction — a biologically-plausible local rule (e.g. a delta/perceptron-style update, or a variance-weighted combination akin to how Salinas & Abbott's optimal linear estimator itself down-weights noisy channels) that can learn to suppress the inhibitory channel's contribution in bins where it is estimation noise rather than always subtracting it at full strength. This targets the actual mechanism this result implicates (unregularized full-power subtraction), not the RECTIFIED-VS-OPPONENT framing that motivated this run.
2. **The dendritic-nonlinear readout** (Jadi, Behabadi, Poleg-Polsky, Schiller & Mel 2014, Proc IEEE 102(5), PMID 25554708) remains untried and is not disfavored by this result specifically (it targets a different constraint — the MLP-like nonlinearity a single point-neuron LIF lacks — orthogonal to the sign-recovery question this run answered). The internal vision-2layer precedent ([2026-08-25-vision-nonlinear-2layer-granule-expansion-readout-does-not-lift-the-c2-linear-ceiling](2026-08-25-vision-nonlinear-2layer-granule-expansion-readout-does-not-lift-the-c2-linear-ceiling.md)) still counsels caution, but it no longer has to be weighed against a working opponent alternative — this run showed the naive opponent realization does not clear that bar either.

If BOTH the learned-gain opponent refinement and a dendritic-nonlinear readout fail, the honest reading moves upstream: the decoder's separability may rely on read power (unconstrained arbitrary-signed weights fit with proper regularization over the FULL bin x neuron feature space) that no single spiking readout neuron realistically approximates from this little TRAIN data, and the fix is not the READ but how the cross-edge SHAPES `source_provenance` in the first place (a wiring lever, restated from iteration 4's own honest residual).
