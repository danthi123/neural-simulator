# Heterosynaptic depression on reverse edges is REAL, dose-dependent, and eliminates the reverse-edge deepening the prior NO-GO pinned as the cause -- but weak-cue recall depth still clears the GO bar on only 2/6 seeds. Decisive 6-seed NO-GO, converted from a clear degradation to a flat/mixed result, not forced further by dose.

Artifact: `research/findings/raw/gap5_ecker_adex/reverse_edge_hetero_depression_ltu.json` (decisive 6-seed run, lam_dep=0.05, identical write hyperparameters to the graded-recall NO-GO: fwd_delay_steps=90, btsp_elig_tau=80ms, btsp_plat_tau=1ms, swr_period=650) + `reverse_edge_hetero_depression_ltu.json.byte_identical_check.json` (lam_dep=0.0 vs the established write, exact SHA-256 match) + three per-seed dose-response scans (`..._seed42.json`, `..._seed101.json`, `..._seed44.json`).
Runner: `research/runners/_gap5_reverse_edge_hetero_depression_ltu_derisk.py` (new file; reuse-by-import of `build_store`/`encode`/`rest_and_replay`/`measure_band`/`_load_weights`/`_smooth` from `_gap5_ecker_adex_ca3_stdp_band_derisk`, `measure_band_from`/`consolidate_by_btsp_replay_delayed` from `_gap5_ecker_replay_learn_through_use_derisk`, `_score_periods_graded`/`_read_graded`/`verify_instrument` from `_gap5_graded_recall_learn_through_use_derisk`, and `fused_btsp_update` from `sim.kernels` -- NO `sim/` edit. SIM_BACKEND=numpy, 500 neurons, CPU-only, RSS well under budget.)

## Question
<!--derived-->
(the numbers in this section quote the PRIOR finding's own reported values, not measured by this run's artifacts)
[[2026-08-27-graded-recall-instrument-learn-through-use-NOGO]] pinned the root cause of the recall-depth regression precisely: the established directional write (BTSP-eligibility x forward-edge conduction delay) stays net-forward in WEIGHT space (dw_fwd 203.6 > dw_rev 170.9, 6/6 seeds) but is PURE POTENTIATION -- nothing depresses the reverse edges, which still deepen at ~84% of the forward rate. That residual absolute reverse growth was large enough to intrude on weak-cue completion and terminate the forward prefix earlier than before training (weak-cue depth_frac 0.586->0.497, 5/6 seeds DECREASE, 100% lesion-attributable).
The named next mechanism: heterosynaptic DEPRESSION / competitive normalization on the reverse edges, so the reverse band is actively suppressed rather than merely out-grown. Does adding that suppression restore a genuine weak-cue recall GAIN?

## Result -- reverse deepening is eliminated (6/6), but recall only individually clears the GO bar on 2/6 seeds
<!--derived-->
**First attempt, banked as a negative for that specific implementation (not reused).** The substrate already has a general per-postsynaptic-neuron heterosynaptic-competition kernel, `sim.kernels.fused_btsp_hetero_update` (gap#4<->gap#5 unification, wired behind `cfg.btsp_hetero_dep`, previously exercised at hetero_dep=0.2 by place-field runners). A single-seed (42) dose scan (lam_dep 0.05-1.5) showed the reverse/forward weight ratio INCREASING with dose (0.894 at lam_dep=0 up to 0.90-1.04) rather than decreasing: Ecker AdEx post-neurons re-fire repeatedly through a burst, and by the LATER re-firings the forward partner's own eligibility has also decayed, so this kernel's per-postsynaptic-neuron competition depresses forward and reverse comparably (sometimes forward MORE). Not pursued further.

**The mechanism actually tested: band-level heterosynaptic LTD keyed to the forward drive.** The forward slice keeps calling the established `fused_btsp_update` unmodified; the reverse slice computes the SAME established potentiation then subtracts `lam_dep * (mean forward potentiation this step) * (reverse weight - w_min)`. **Byte-identical-off, verified in the data (not inferred from code):** at lam_dep=0.0 the new write's output weights hash EXACTLY (SHA-256 `47291ba2...`) to the established write's output on seed 42 -- a true exact match, unlike the first-attempt kernel's reordered-formula path which left a ~2e-3 max-abs-diff (float32 rounding, not a behavior difference, but not an exact hash either).

**Dose-response (seed 42, pre-registered before the decisive run):** dw_rev falls monotonically as lam_dep rises from 0 (219.5) through 0.005 (142.2), 0.01 (101.4), 0.02 (60.6), 0.03 (40.4), 0.05 (20.1), plateauing near -11.5 (net depression) by lam_dep>=0.4 -- while dw_fwd stays within 0.4% of its lam_dep=0 value throughout (244.6-245.6), confirming the forward path is genuinely undisturbed. Weak-cue depth_frac gain rises with the same dose in lockstep (+0.049 -> +0.050 -> +0.096 -> +0.096 -> +0.115 -> +0.173), saturating by lam_dep~0.05-0.1. This is a real, graded, causal effect, not noise.

**Decisive 6-seed test at lam_dep=0.05 (the smallest dose that already saturates the seed-42 effect).** Directional 6/6 (mean dw_fwd 202.3 vs dw_rev 15.1). Reverse suppressed 6/6: the reverse/forward ratio falls from the NO-GO's ~0.84 to 0.074 (mean) -- reverse deepening is reduced to roughly a tenth of its former size. Headroom holds 6/6 (weak depth_frac_before 0.586, unchanged from the NO-GO since BEFORE reads do not depend on lam_dep).
Lesion-controlled 6/6: dw_fwd_noseed is exactly 0.000 on every seed and weak_depth_frac_noseed equals weak_depth_frac_before exactly (the NO-SEED path never ignites, so e_pre/p_post/the forward-drive signal all stay zero and the depression term is inert too -- the same null-control logic as the established write).
The MEAN weak-cue depth_frac now RISES after consolidation (0.586->0.612), reversing the sign of the prior NO-GO's mean change (0.586->0.497) -- but only 2/6 seeds individually clear the +0.05 per-seed gain bar (42: +0.173, 100: +0.105); the other four are flat-to-slightly-negative (43: -0.016, 44: +0.006, 101: -0.077, 102: -0.038). GO bar (>=5/6) NOT met: 2/6.

**Ruling out "just needs more dose" (not forced further, per the task's own instruction).** Follow-up dose scans on the two clearest non-gaining seeds (101, 44) swept lam_dep across a 20-30x range (0.05 to 1.0-1.5), driving dw_rev fully negative (net depression, well past the point where reverse deepening is merely eliminated) on both. Neither seed's weak-cue depth_frac moved at all: seed 101 stays pinned at gain -0.077 (except a single point at lam_dep=0.8 reading -0.038) and seed 44 stays pinned at exactly +0.006 across the entire range. Eliminating (and reversing) reverse-edge growth entirely does not rescue these seeds' recall -- the residual is NOT explained by reverse-band absolute growth for them.

## Why -- reverse-edge growth was A cause, not THE cause, of the per-seed recall variance
<!--derived-->
The mechanism does exactly what it was designed to do: it is dose-dependent, band-selective (forward path numerically undisturbed, verified byte-identical-off), and drives reverse deepening from ~84% of forward down to near-zero-or-negative on every seed. For the two seeds where it works (42, 100) the recovery is large (+0.173, +0.105) -- clear evidence reverse-edge crosstalk WAS the limiting factor there. But for the other four, driving reverse suppression far past the point of full elimination changes nothing, which means some OTHER seed-dependent factor (forward-band absolute strength varies substantially across seeds at encode time -- band fwd 145-320 across the 6 seeds' `dw_fwd` values here -- or an intrinsic noise floor in the graded depth-completion read at the weak-cue operating point) is co-determining weak-cue completion depth and was never touched by this lever.

## Verdict + next mechanism (a wall defers a METHOD, not the capability)
NO-GO by the stated bar (2/6 < 5/6) -- but a materially DIFFERENT, improved negative than the prior one: the write is no longer merely "net-forward with a large absolute reverse residual" -- reverse deepening is now reduced to a small fraction of forward (or net-depressed) on every seed, and the MEAN weak-cue recall change flips from a clear decrease to a genuine increase. Banked positives carry forward: the heterosynaptic-LTD mechanism itself (dose-verified causal, band-selective, byte-identical-off), and the negative result for the general per-postsynaptic `fused_btsp_hetero_update` kernel applied to this specific defect (that implementation is NOT reverse-selective on this substrate's burst dynamics).
Residual, precisely isolated: for 4/6 seeds, recall depth is INSENSITIVE to reverse-edge suppression even when driven far past full elimination -- the limiting factor for those seeds is something the write side does not touch.
Named next mechanism: characterize the READ-side noise floor directly (repeated graded-instrument reads of the SAME frozen weights, to separate genuine per-seed substrate variance from instrument read noise at the weak-cue operating point) and/or investigate whether forward-band ABSOLUTE magnitude at encode time (not just its ratio to reverse) is the actual limiting factor for weak-cue completion depth -- a homeostatic forward-band strengthening process (rather than further reverse suppression) is the next candidate lever.
Source: Kim and Kim 2025, PLoS Comput Biol, doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013267 (PMID 40623085). <!--derived-->
