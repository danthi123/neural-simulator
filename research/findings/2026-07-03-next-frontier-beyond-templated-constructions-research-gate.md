# Research gate — the NEXT FRONTIER past a hand-templated construction inventory: a fronto-striatal RESERVOIR is the anti-whack-a-mole substrate (form↔role learned, not enumerated)

**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Type:** read-only deep-research gate (the standing "deep-research FIRST at a new mechanism-class / roadblock" gate; master directive "structure must EMERGE from experience, no feature-by-feature capability-matching"; owner flag that hand-adding construction templates is "custom-design-not-biology + whack-a-mole"; `feedback_deep_research_at_roadblocks`, `feedback_spiking_structure_must_self_organize`).
**Scope:** the EMERGE-56..77 "simulate Broca" producer discovers function words, slot order, and slot inventory from corpus — BUT the per-construction **slot-TEMPLATE dictionary** (a python `CONSTRUCTIONS`/`FRAME_LEXICON` of ordered role-slot lists) and the per-shape `label_sentence` rules are HAND-WRITTEN, and every new construction shape (transitive → PP → adjective → ditransitive: EMERGE-72/73/74/77) required a new hand-coded label/template branch. That IS the whack-a-mole. This gate does NOT ask "which construction next"; it asks: **what biological mechanism dissolves the templated-inventory boundary entirely and moves toward open-ended fluent grounded conversation, on the emergent single spiking substrate, without ever hand-writing another construction?**
**Verdict up front:** **SURPASSABLE, and there is ONE mechanism that is simultaneously (a) the anti-whack-a-mole answer, (b) the canonical neural precedent, and (c) the project's OWN pre-registered next move — a fixed-recurrent-pool RESERVOIR (echo-state / liquid-state machine, Dominey-Hinaut fronto-striatal grammar model) with a TRAINED read-out.** Because the reservoir's recurrent weights are FIXED-random (untrained), it side-steps the EMERGE-6/6b recurrent-credit wall that killed the from-scratch trainable recurrent generator; because it reads grammatical roles off the ORDER + POSITION of the already-discovered closed-class words, it needs NO per-construction template and GENERALIZES to constructions never enumerated. The cheapest first de-risk is a rate-level **comprehension** reservoir (form → thematic-role labeling) that generalizes to a HELD-OUT construction shape the current label-rule pipeline cannot, with the project's decisive input-destruction anti-cheats. This retires the `CONSTRUCTIONS` dict as the frontier and puts the system on the Dominey-Hinaut path that scaled to 90,582 constructions in the literature.

### The GENUINE residual — pinned to exact bytes (small in line-count, but it IS the boundary)
Two hand-designed artifacts, and they are the whack-a-mole surface:

1. **The construction slot-TEMPLATE inventory is an ENUMERATED, hand-written set of ordered role-slot lists.** The mining "discovers which template matches," but the *space of templates it can match* is fixed by hand: `label_sentence` (EMERGE-64) hard-codes the clean `det (func)* SUBJ (func)* VERB` shape and **SKIPS** any content word between subject and verb; each richer shape needed a new hand branch — `label_sentence_ext` (EMERGE-72, post-verbal OBJECT), the adjective attributive/predicative rules (EMERGE-73), the transitive/ditransitive role signatures (EMERGE-74/77). **The role-type LABELER is the residual: a sentence's who-did-what-to-whom is read by hand-written positional rules, one per construction family, not learned.** Every "new construction" = a new labeler branch. That is precisely the pattern the owner flagged.

2. **The message→construction ROUTER is a signature-keyed registry** (EMERGE-72 `ConstructionRegistry` + a Dominey-Hinaut-style selector). This is thinner and largely correct — the selection *is* biological (Dominey production = construction selection). But it still selects from the hand-enumerated inventory of (1).

**Quantified:** of "go past templated constructions," the irreducible hand-designed part is **the per-construction role-LABELER (form → thematic-role map)** — the thing that says "in THIS word pattern, the agent is here, the object is there." Today that is `label_sentence`/`label_sentence_ext` + the argstructure `FRAME_LEXICON` role lists: roughly a few hundred lines of positional if-rules that GROW by one branch per construction shape. Everything upstream (closed-class discovery, order, spell, moat) is already emergent and construction-agnostic. So the residual is **one function class** — and it is exactly the function the Dominey-Hinaut reservoir *learns from data* instead of enumerating.

**The prior gate stopped one level too shallow.** `2026-07-03-broaden-construction-inventory-research-gate.md` correctly cited Dominey-Hinaut and Jon-And-Michaud, but its recommendation was "generalize the router + feed a richer corpus + **extend the labeler** (`label_sentence_ext`)." Extending the labeler by hand IS the whack-a-mole. This gate's correction: **replace the hand labeler with the reservoir's learned form→role read-out**, so no future construction needs a hand branch.

**What is NOT the residual — an honest partial precedent to position against.** EMERGE-22 (`2026-07-02-emerge22-pos-frame-grammar-GO.md`, GO 6/6, held-out recombination 1.000) already showed a high-order sequence cortex over POS-class micro-columns with open content slots generates a HELD-OUT novel grammatical sentence — "a construction grammar over shared POS-class micro-columns." That is a genuine emergent-grammar win and it dissolves the "novel-content-recombination" (F5) residual. But EMERGE-22's construction still comes from the corpus's ATTESTED POS-frame sequences fed through the HTM sequence memory; the *reply-side producer* the EMERGE-59..77 arc built on top of it re-introduced the hand `label_sentence`/`FRAME_LEXICON` role rules for who-did-what-to-whom in *richer* shapes (post-verbal objects, PP arguments, ditransitive). So the residual this gate targets is specifically the **hand-written form→THEMATIC-ROLE labeler in the producer's construction pipeline**, not the POS-frame recombination EMERGE-22 already made emergent. The reservoir is the mechanism that makes the ROLE-labeling emergent too (EMERGE-22 = emergent surface order over classes; the reservoir = emergent role assignment over configurations), and it composes with the HTM sequence memory rather than replacing it.

## MOVE 2 — REFRAME VIA REAL BIOLOGY / CHILD LANGUAGE (are we testing the wrong hypothesis?)

**The wrong hypothesis (the current implicit one):** "grammar = an inventory of slot-template objects; to say more, enumerate more templates and write a labeler branch for each." This is the generative-slot-and-filler picture, and it is what forces whack-a-mole.

**The right hypothesis (usage-based + fronto-striatal neuroscience):** grammar is a **learned mapping from surface FORM (the sequence, dominated by the configuration/position of closed-class morphology) to MEANING (thematic roles)** — not an enumerated template store. Three convergent literatures, all already partly cited by the project:

- **(A) Usage-based construction grammar (Tomasello; Goldberg; Bybee).** Children do NOT install templates; they abstract item-based chunks ("She is Ving") into schemas via distributional pattern-finding, and *generalize the FORM→ROLE mapping* to novel item combinations they never heard. WebSearch (Tomasello, "First steps toward a usage-based theory" / "item-based nature of children's early syntactic development"; MPI-EVA): early utterances are "item-based constructions" using "morphology, adpositions and word order to mark roles," abstracting gradually to "abstract representations like syntactic schemas … closely linked to frequency-driven entrenchment and distributional learning." The learner is a **pattern-finder over usage**, not a template-adder. This says: the boundary isn't "too few templates," it's "we hand-wrote the pattern-finder's output instead of learning the pattern-finder."

- **(B) Dominey-Hinaut fronto-striatal reservoir — the canonical NEURAL model of exactly this, and it GENERALIZES to constructions never enumerated.** Hinaut & Dominey, "Real-Time Parallel Processing of Grammatical Structure in the Fronto-Striatal System" (PLoS ONE 2013) + "Corticostriatal response selection in sentence PRODUCTION" (Brain & Language 2015). Mechanism (verified from the PLoS paper): closed-class function words drive a **fixed recurrent prefrontal reservoir**; open-class words (nouns/verbs) go to a separate memory stack; a **trained read-out (ridge regression → striatum)** maps the reservoir's closed-class-sequence dynamics to thematic-role assignments. **Cue-competition hypothesis:** "a limited set of cues including the configuration of grammatical function words … word order and prosody are used to encode the grammatical structure." Results: leave-one-out on **26 constructions** (14.8% meaning error) → **462 constructions** (7.4–9.2% meaning error, incl. relative clauses, subject/object-relative, dative-passive) → a **90,582-construction corpus** (1.78% meaning error training on 50%). **The decisive control** (the project's own anti-cheat, independently): scrambling word order collapses it to **73.4% meaning error / 99.9% sentence error** — it learns STRUCTURE, not memorization. Production (2015): input = predicate + thematic roles ("focus hierarchy"), read-out activates word-coding units *in the correct order* — **this is precisely the project's competitive-queuing spiking producer, but with the construction inventory LEARNED by the read-out instead of hand-templated.** And it is cross-lingual (English + Japanese) with no per-language templates.

- **(C) Reservoirs BEAT LSTMs on developmental (small-data, single-pass) language acquisition and scale without re-tuning.** Inria/Hinaut group, "Recurrent Neural Networks Models for Developmental Language Acquisition: Reservoirs Outperform LSTMs" (2021): the reservoir "uses [the data] only once" (single pass, no backprop-through-time), and "only the reservoir model seems to scale to larger corpora without … adapt[ing] specifically the hyperparameters." Cross-situational grounding (Inria hal-02594725): reservoirs "ground complex sentences into a perceptual representation space" from word-percept **co-occurrence** — matching the project's Gabor-V1 grounding + no-confab moat. This is the emergent, low-data, corpus-driven, perceptually-grounded regime the project *is*.

- **(D) Catalog grounding.** G.12 Broca (feature-catalog.md:2774) = "speech production + grammatical processing"; agrammatism strips closed-class + morphology, spares nouns — the EMERGE-59 function-word-ablation dissociation. G.11 dual-stream + the fronto-striatal comprehension pathway; G.07/H.19 pre-SMA/SMA internally-generated **serial order** (feature-catalog.md:2710,3110) = the competitive-queuing substrate the project already runs 6/6 GO. The reservoir sits naturally on the project's existing recurrent Izhikevich/RF cortical pools (a liquid-state machine IS a recurrent spiking pool with a trained read-out).

**Reframe verdict:** the frontier is NOT "more templates." It is **learn the form→role mapping (a pattern-finder over usage) instead of hand-enumerating it** — the usage-based / Dominey-Hinaut fronto-striatal reservoir. This is a mechanism-CLASS change (research-gate condition (d)), not another construction, and it is the honest path from a bounded hand-labeled inventory toward open-ended grounded production.

## MOVE 3 — RANK CHEAP-FIRST BIOLOGICALLY-GROUNDED MECHANISMS (single-variable, anti-cheated)

All rate-level CPU/numpy first, reuse-by-import, NO `sim/` edit until a rate GO, multi-seed 42/43/44 (→6 for the GO gate), with the project's decisive **input-destruction** anti-cheats (permuted/scramble/lesion/held-out/memorization-floor — NOT fixed-random controls, per the control-validity methodology fix).

### ★ RANK 1 — the FRONTO-STRIATAL RESERVOIR replaces the hand LABELER (comprehension: FORM → thematic ROLE) — CHEAPEST, HIGHEST-LEVERAGE, dissolves the whack-a-mole at its root
- **Mechanism (Hinaut-Dominey 2013 / echo-state):** a FIXED-random recurrent pool (echo-state reservoir; later the spiking LSM on the project's own recurrent RF/Izhikevich pool) is driven by the **already-discovered closed-class stream** (EMERGE-62 function-word tags) + open-class slot markers; a **trained read-out** (ridge regression at rate level; the project's already-validated A→W / population read-out on-substrate) maps the reservoir dynamics to per-open-word thematic-role labels (Agent / Object / Recipient / of-which-verb). **No `CONSTRUCTIONS` dict, no `label_sentence` branch** — the role labeling is READ from the reservoir, and it GENERALIZES to construction shapes never enumerated (the literature's whole point).
- **Reusable project machinery:** the EMERGE-62 closed-class discovery (the reservoir's input cue); the corpus stream + spaCy front-end (legitimate offline syllabus prep, the accepted `_bucketB`/stream-cortex boundary); the recurrent RF/Izhikevich pools + the A→W population read-out (for the spiking rung); the moat (gate-first, unchanged — a low-confidence/abstain read still abstains); the anti-cheat harness.
- **Cheapest single-variable de-risk (EMERGE-78):** at RATE level, train the reservoir read-out on the FORM→ROLE map for the constructions the current pipeline already handles (modal/intransitive/negated-modal/transitive), then measure role-labeling accuracy on a **HELD-OUT construction shape** the current `label_sentence` rules do NOT cover (e.g. object-relative "the dog that the cat chased ran", or a dative-passive) — the reservoir should label its roles correctly *with no new hand rule*, where the hand labeler scores 0 (it skips it). GO ⇒ the learned mapping generalizes past the enumerated inventory — the whack-a-mole is dissolved.
- **Anti-cheats (must collapse):** WORD-ORDER-SCRAMBLE (Dominey-Hinaut's own decisive control — structure destroyed → role accuracy → chance; the project's permuted-corpus analogue); CLOSED-CLASS-LESION (ablate the discovered function-word cue → collapse, proving the reservoir reads the closed-class configuration, matching agrammatism G.12); HELD-OUT-CONSTRUCTION (train on N−1 shapes, test the unseen shape — the generalization claim); MEMORIZATION-FLOOR (a look-up-table baseline that can only reproduce trained sentences must fail the held-out shape). Moat: abstains preserved.
- **Risk:** a plain reservoir has FADING memory (bounded, not unbounded — see RANK 3); it handles the *bounded-embedding* constructions Dominey-Hinaut demonstrated (≤1–2 relative clauses) but not unbounded center-embedding. That is a FEATURE for the cheap-first rung (human recursion is itself WM-bounded to ~2–3 embeddings) and is the correct scope. Second risk: the rate→spiking (LSM) port is a follow-on rung, gated behind the rate GO (do NOT port first) — but note this is the SAME "trained read-out on a fixed recurrent pool" the EMERGE-6b gate already named as the fix for generation stability, so the port lands on already-pre-registered ground.

### RANK 2 — the reservoir replaces the hand ROUTER too (production: thematic ROLES → ordered word emission) — the natural second step, unifies with the spiking producer
- **Mechanism (Dominey 2015 production model):** input = the reasoner's predicate + thematic-role decision ("focus hierarchy"); a reservoir read-out drives **which word-coding pool fires in which order** — i.e. it SELECTS the construction and its order without a template dict. This is a drop-in for the current `decision_from_emerge` + `ConstructionRegistry` + FrameCQ order, but LEARNED. The spiking emission stays the validated competitive-queuing rate-ranking + A→W spell + moat.
- **Reusable:** the entire EMERGE-59/63/67/68/69 spiking emission + spell + moat stack (unchanged — only the "which slots, what order" upstream becomes a learned reservoir read-out instead of the mined registry); the reasoner's already-computed SVO/relational decisions (`_fluidconv_chat_repl`).
- **De-risk:** produce the trained constructions from role-decisions via the reservoir read-out (rate), then produce a HELD-OUT construction's word-order from its role structure alone; CROSS-CONSTRUCTION control (construction A's roles must not emit B's order); scramble/lesion collapse; moat 0-on-abstain. Risk: production is harder than comprehension (the 2015 sentence-error rates are higher); scope it to the bounded inventory first.

### RANK 3 — BOUNDED RECURSION via a WM-multiplexed buffer (theta-gamma / stack-lite) — the honest "true productivity" rung, AFTER the reservoir
- **Mechanism:** a plain reservoir's fading memory caps embedding depth (WebSearch: "impossible to train a reservoir on tasks which require unbounded-time memory"; the fix is a **reservoir stack machine** — Ororbia/Paaßen-style — that "provably recognizes all deterministic context-free languages" by adding an explicit stack). The BIOLOGICAL stack candidate is catalog **N.15 theta-gamma cross-frequency coupling** (feature-catalog.md:992; Lisman-Idiart 1995): a theta cycle nesting 7±2 gamma-locked assemblies is a **time-multiplexed working-memory buffer** — the mechanistic substrate for holding a bounded stack of ~2–3 embedded clauses (which is exactly the human recursion limit; deeper center-embedding is unparseable for people too). This is where *genuine open recursion/coordination* comes from, not more flat templates.
- **Reusable:** the NM framework can add theta + theta-phase-modulated gamma as `excitability_drive` modulators (catalog says "straightforward"); the reservoir supplies the per-clause role read-out; HTM temporal memory (already committed) supplies high-order sequence context WITHIN a clause. **The project's OWN prior gates already named two routes specifically past DEEP recursion** (`2026-07-02-open-domain-surface-fluency-research-gate.md` F4): **dendritic multiplication (the D2 two-compartment substrate)** OR **Assembly-Calculus disinhibition-controlled recursion (Mitropolsky et al., "The Architecture of a Biologically Plausible Language Organ" / center-embedding, arXiv:2206.13217)** — the latter is a strong biologically-grounded candidate for the stack-control the reservoir lacks, and pairs naturally with the theta-gamma buffer. Note the same gate's honest framing: humans also fail past ~depth-2 center-embedding (Kandel G.12, "the girl that the boy is chasing is tall" fails), so bounded recursion (~2–3) is the biological target and the substrate is argued to already reach depth-2 (nested-composition / `query_chain`).
- **De-risk (later):** a reservoir + a 2–3-slot multiplexed buffer parses/produces a 1-embedding relative clause and generalizes the embedded role assignment held-out; buffer-lesion collapses the embedding (flattens to the matrix clause only); depth-scaling degrades gracefully at ~3 (the human signature). Risk: higher variance, a real mechanism build — correctly ranked AFTER the reservoir dissolves the flat-template boundary.
- **Note on HTM:** HTM temporal memory (Bouhadjar-Diesmann, the committed `fused_htm_permanence_update`) gives **variable/high-order Markov context** within a sequence and "maintains multiple predictions until context resolves" — but it is a *sequential predictor, not a stack*; it does not by itself give unbounded recursion. It is the right within-clause context engine, complementary to the reservoir's role read-out and the theta-gamma buffer's cross-clause stack.

### RANK 4 — the deep substrate lever (dendritic / recurrent-generation credit) — NOT on this frontier's critical path
- The owner's dendritic / burst-multiplexed credit-assignment lever is the months-scale unlock for a *trainable* recurrent generation cortex. But the EMERGE-6/6b arc already showed that training the recurrence from scratch (target-based AND proper e-prop) hits an autonomous-generation-STABILITY wall, and its own research gate named the fix as **"reservoir + trained feedback read-out" / FORCE (Sussillo-Abbott) / Laje-Buonomano innate stable trajectories.** ⇒ the RANK-1 reservoir is the CHEAP way to get generative recurrent dynamics NOW (fixed pool, trained read-out) while the dendritic lever matures in parallel. Keep the dendritic priority unchanged; it is not the next step for going past templated constructions.

**Ranking rationale:** RANK 1 is the single cheapest and it dissolves the whack-a-mole at its root (retires the hand labeler, generalizes past the enumerated inventory — the exact owner-flagged problem), is the canonical neural precedent (Dominey-Hinaut, scaled to 90k constructions), and rides the project's own pre-registered "fixed pool + trained read-out" gate from EMERGE-6b. RANK 2 unifies it with the spiking producer. RANK 3 is the honest true-productivity (bounded recursion) rung, correctly deferred to after the flat-template boundary falls.
