## Interpretation

**What works:**
- Direction-only at 42% confirms Tier 1 backward compat preserved
- Verb-only at 100% quiet confirms anti-action mechanism works
- 2/4 directions show clear phrase composition signal

**What doesn't:**
- Phrase composition is inconsistent across directions
- East specifically is anti-aligned
- Architecture works for some bindings but doesn't generalize

**Possible causes:**
1. action_gate drive_pA=50 may be too weak (motor teacher 300pA
   dominates; small +50pA boost from PFC doesn't change selection)
2. Or too strong / mistimed: PFC fires during verb stage and boosts
   ALL motor pools indiscriminately, distorting direction selection
3. PFC NMDA bistability may not maintain "go" context across the
   100ms gap to direction stage
4. STDP at language_input -> dlpfc_verb may not have established
   strong "go" -> PFC binding (single verb, only 200 phrase events)
