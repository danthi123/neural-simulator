## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (read this first)

The learned visuomotor read-out (a plastic 2-D Gaussian-bump → cardinal-action mapping trained by reward-modulated STDP) ceilings at COARSE precision (~4.0, vs the innate reflex's ~2.0) and "more teaching does not tighten it" for a **single, identifiable reason: the precision bottleneck is in the LEARNING RULE and the READOUT, not in the bump's spatial resolution.** Two facts converge. (1) **The learning rule is the dominant limit:** reward-modulated STDP is a *coarse, scalar-credit, correlational* rule — it learns "this bump region correlates with reward when paired with this cardinal," which is exactly the credit-assignment regime the project has *already proven hits a noise-floor at biological scale* (`2026-05-05-W-to-A-VERDICT-global-scalar-feedback-fails.md`: global scalar feedback 1/6; identical architecture under a *supervised/gradient* signal 3/3 PERFECT). The reflex is precise because it computes the cardinal from a clean continuous offset *analytically*; the learned circuit must *discover the decision boundaries through reward correlation*, which saturates well short of that. (2) **A sharper/denser sensory bump will NOT help on its own** — counterintuitively — because the encoded variable is **2-D** (dx, dy), and the canonical population-coding result (Pouget, Denève, Ducom & Latham, *Neural Comput.* 1999) is that for a **2-D** variable, *coding accuracy is INSENSITIVE to tuning width* (narrowing σ helps a 1-D code, not a 2-D one). So "shrink σ / finer grid" — the obvious lever — is predicted *a priori* to be a dead end at fixed neuron count, matching the owner's observation that the bump-resolution knob is not where the action is.

**Recommended cheapest-first tightening lever: a SUPERVISED motor-teacher (feedback-error-learning) applied at the `cortex_X` SELECTION layer during teaching** — i.e., while the SC reflex teaches, *also* clamp/drive the TARGET `cortex_X` pool the reflex is pushing, so the plastic `sensory→cortex_X` synapses learn against a *clean supervised target* (a desired-output teaching signal), not against a delayed scalar reward correlation. This is the project's own already-validated precision lever (the conversational arc's `--motor-teacher-pA` during co-firing sharpened STDP targets to PERFECT recall; `compose_engram_demo`/`-sequential`), and it is *exactly* the biology of cerebellar **feedback-error-learning** (Kawato 1987; Kawato & Gomi 1992; Wolpert, Miall & Kawato 1998): a *crude feedback controller* (here, the SC reflex) supplies the error/teaching signal that trains a *feed-forward inverse model* to refine and then replace it. It is the cheapest because it reuses (a) the SC reflex (already shipped, already computes the correct cardinal from vision), (b) the existing `sensory→cortex_X` plastic pathway + `sensory_to_cortex` plasticity gate, and (c) the existing teacher-clamp pattern (the heuristic already drives `cortex_X` directly; the motor-teacher is the same clamp, applied to the *target* pool, *as the supervised label* rather than as an additive nudge). Likely an additive, default-off runner flag, **no `sim/` edit**.

**The single cheapest de-risk (falsifiable, before any heavy build):** add a `--sensory-cortex-teacher` smoke that, during the teaching window, drives the reflex's chosen `cortex_X` target pool with a strong supervised current *while the `sensory→cortex_X` gate is open*, then WEANS both the reflex and the teacher (via `transmission_gate` / gate→0) and measures the **post-wean cheat-5 multi-goal nav score**. **Falsifiable prediction:** supervised teaching tightens the post-wean ceiling *below* the reward-STDP plateau (toward ~3.0–3.5 or better) where 3000 reward-STDP teaching steps could not (`3.96@3000 == 3.93@2000`); if the post-wean score does NOT improve over the reward-STDP plateau, the bottleneck is the **4-way categorical WTA readout** (a continuous goal offset forced onto 4 cardinals), and the next lever is a **population-vector / cosine-tuned cortex read-out** (Georgopoulos; catalog H.17) — the second-cheapest option. Anti-cheat throughout: gate on the *real* nav score (not a proxy); the teacher target must come only from the *image-sourced* reflex cardinal (no `(gx,gy)` leakage); multi-seed (≥3 → 6); reflex-taught-then-FULLY-weaned protocol (the durable-consolidation test); run inside the N8+N6 biologized back-end. **This composes with the existing cluster-F cerebellum but does NOT need it** — cluster-F as wired refines `motor_X` (downstream of selection) via the *global reward*, so it cannot fix *selection* precision; the load-bearing biology (FEL supervised error) is what the motor-teacher imports cheaply at the right stage.

## 1. DIAGNOSIS — why a reward-STDP-trained 2-D-bump → action read-out ceilings at coarse precision

### 1.1 What the circuit actually is (verified against code)

The learned-perception path (`g11_bg_runner.py`):
- **Sensory code (the bump):** 49 neurons on a **7×7 grid** of preferred offsets `(dx, dy) ∈ [-3,3]²` (`:3034–3040`). Each step, the goal's egocentric offset is clipped to `[-3,3]` and a **Gaussian bump** is painted: `rate = sensory_drive_max_pA(600) · exp(−d²/2σ²)`, `σ = sensory_drive_sigma(1.5)` (`:4207–4214`). With `--learned-perception-from-vision`, the *same* bump is driven from `sc_salience_offset_from_image(...)`, which returns a **continuous (dx,dy)** read from the rendered image's agent/goal blob centroids (no coordinates; `:106–128`, `:4462–4472`).
- **The plastic read-out:** `sensory → cortex_{N,E,S,W}`, `density=1.0`, `weight_mean=10`, **plastic, gate `sensory_to_cortex`** (`:1007–1014`). This is the single learning site for perception.
- **The selection back-end (unchanged):** `cortex_X → BG cascade → thal_X → sel_X soft-WTA → commit_X`, a **4-way categorical** decision (`ACTION_NAMES = ["N","E","S","W"]`, `:68`; N8 GPi→thal disinhibition + N6 accumulate-then-commit; `:163–207`).
- **The teacher:** today's teaching is the coordinate heuristic / SC reflex driving `cortex_X` *additively* (`:4196–4203`), with `sensory→cortex_X` learning via **reward-modulated STDP** alongside it.

So the learned circuit is: **continuous (dx,dy) → coarse 2-D bump (49 cells, σ=1.5) → reward-STDP-plastic synapses → 4-way categorical WTA.** Three candidate loci for the coarseness: the CODE (bump resolution), the LEARNING RULE (reward-STDP), the READOUT (4-way WTA). The evidence below localizes the dominant limit to the **learning rule**, with the **readout** as the secondary limit, and **largely exonerates the code**.

### 1.2 The CODE (bump resolution) is NOT the primary limit — and "sharper σ" is predicted to fail

The intuitive fix — more sensory neurons, narrower σ, finer (dx,dy) grid — is, for *this* code, predicted *a priori* to be ineffective at fixed neuron count, by the canonical population-coding result:

> **Pouget, Denève, Ducom & Latham (1999), "Narrow versus wide tuning curves: what's best for a population code?", *Neural Computation* 11(1):85–90.** The Fisher information's dependence on tuning width is **dimension-dependent**: for a **1-D** feature (e.g. interaural time difference), *sharper* tuning codes more information per neuron; but for a **2-D** feature (explicitly: "spatial representation by hippocampal place cells"), **coding accuracy is INSENSITIVE to the tuning width.** (PubMed 9950723.) Zhang & Sejnowski (1999, *Neural Comput.* 11:75–84) give the general scaling: in `D` dimensions, Fisher information `∝ σ^(D−2)` — so at `D=2` it is *independent of σ*; only at `D≥3` does broad tuning win and at `D=1` does sharp tuning win.

The project's bump encodes a **2-D** offset `(dx,dy)` — exactly the place-cell-like 2-D regime. Therefore **narrowing σ (1.5 → smaller) cannot, by itself, increase the bump's coding accuracy** — which is precisely the owner's empirical observation that the bump-resolution lever is exhausted. Catalog **E.03** independently flags this exact validation axis ("tuning-curve width vs. discrimination acuity"; line 1377) and notes the sim "has no proper population code over a continuous variable" — i.e. the bump is a *coarse* 2-D code, but coarseness of a *2-D* code is not σ-fixable. (The *one* code change that WOULD help is raising the *neuron count / coverage density* — coarse-coding accuracy scales with `√N` of independent tuned units, Zhang & Sejnowski 1999 — but that is a weaker, more expensive lever than fixing the learning rule, §2 Rank 3.) **Net: the code is a coarse 2-D representation, but the standard "sharpen the tuning" intuition is a category error here; do not spend the build on σ.**

### 1.3 The LEARNING RULE (reward-modulated STDP) is the DOMINANT limit — the project has already proven this exact point

This is the load-bearing diagnosis, and the project has *already run the decisive control* in a sister task:

> **`research/findings/2026-05-05-W-to-A-VERDICT-global-scalar-feedback-fails.md`:** under an *identical architecture*, **global scalar (reward-modulated) feedback** bound word→action at **1/6** (classical sign-only DA) and **0/6** (magnitude-graded DA) — the noise floor — while a **supervised/gradient** signal (B3) bound the same mapping at **3/3 PERFECT.** The CLAUDE.md synthesis of that arc: *"Architecture is sufficient; the credit-assignment rule is the bottleneck."*

The mechanism is general and applies directly to the visuomotor read-out. Reward-modulated STDP is a **three-factor correlational rule**: `Δw ∝ (pre⊗post eligibility) × (scalar reward − baseline)`. It can only discover that "bump-pattern P paired with cardinal C tends to precede reward," via slow, noisy, *temporally-credit-assigned* averaging over many trials. Its precision is fundamentally capped by (a) the **scalar bandwidth** of the teacher (one signed number per step carries no information about *which* of the 49→4 synapses should change, or by how much, to sharpen the *boundary* between e.g. "go N" and "go E" for a near-diagonal goal), and (b) **eligibility-trace temporal blur** (the reward arrives after the movement, so credit smears across the recently-active bump region). This is *exactly* why "more teaching does not tighten it": additional reward-STDP steps keep averaging the *same coarse correlation*; they cannot inject the missing *gradient* information (which synapse, which direction, how much) that a supervised target carries. The reflex, by contrast, has *zero* learning-rule limit — it computes the correct cardinal from the clean continuous offset analytically (`sc_orienting_cardinal_from_image`), so it sits at the task's intrinsic precision (~2.0). **The ~4.0 ceiling is the gap between correlational reward-STDP and analytic/supervised computation of the same mapping.**

Information-theoretically: a supervised teaching signal is a *high-bandwidth vector* target (the desired post-synaptic activity pattern, here "drive cortex_C, suppress others"), whereas reward is a *1-bit-ish scalar*. The credit-assignment literature is unambiguous that scalar-reward learning has *far* higher sample complexity and *lower asymptotic precision* than supervised error for the same function class — and the project's own 1/6-vs-3/3 result is a clean in-house replication of that gap.

### 1.4 The READOUT (4-way categorical WTA) is the SECONDARY limit

Even with a perfect sensory→cortex mapping, the back-end forces the continuous goal offset onto **4 cardinal pools** (`ACTION_NAMES`, the sel_X soft-WTA; §1.1). For a 4-connected gridworld the *action set* is genuinely 4 cardinals, so this is partly intrinsic. But the *precision* cost shows up at the **decision boundaries**: a goal at a near-diagonal offset (e.g. dx=+3, dy=+2) should pick E *most* steps but N *some* — the optimal policy is a graded, position-dependent mixture, and a hard categorical WTA over a *learned-coarse* drive will mis-route near boundaries (the documented residual concentrates in the post-goal-change phases, where the offset geometry shifts; cf. `2026-06-06-N6-decision-biologized-CONCLUSION.md`). A **population-vector readout** (Georgopoulos; catalog **H.17**) — decode a *continuous* movement direction from cosine-tuned cortex units, then quantize *late* — preserves the graded direction through the decision and is the biologically-grounded fix for the categorical-boundary loss. The catalog explicitly notes (H.17, line 3092): the motor pools "are categorical (one pool per action) rather than vector-tuned. Could be tested by adding cosine-tuned input layer; would naturally yield population vector readout." This is the secondary lever (§2 Rank 2), gated by the de-risk in §4.

### 1.5 Where cluster-F cerebellum sits in this diagnosis (it's the right BIOLOGY, wired at the wrong STAGE)

The project HAS a cerebellum (`--enable-cluster-f-cerebellum`/`-v2`), and the *a-priori* strongest candidate ("the cerebellum's whole job is precision refinement of cortically-initiated movements") is correct *as biology* — but the **current wiring cannot tighten selection precision**, for two concrete reasons verified in code:

1. **It refines the wrong stage.** Cluster-F's output is `purkinje_X → dcn_aip_X → motor_X` (`:1816–1831`) — it modulates the **motor pool** that fires *after* the cardinal is already selected (same-action-only, additive). It has **no path to the `cortex_X` selection** or to the `sensory→cortex` read-out. So it can smooth/scale the chosen action's motor output (which is why the eval found a real **variance reduction** but **no mean improvement**: `2026-04-29-cluster-f-results.md`, A+F std −45%, mean NEUTRAL) — it *cannot* change *which* cardinal wins or sharpen the bump→cardinal map.
2. **Its teaching signal is the global reward, not a graded error.** Cluster-F v1's `inferior_olive → purkinje_X` climbing fiber (`:1832–1844`) drives plasticity *through the existing reward-modulation path* — "the cerebellum learns like the BG learns — same teacher signal" (`2026-04-29-cluster-f-results.md` §"Why this is NEUTRAL"). v2's CF-gated LTD (`enable_cluster_f_v2`) fixes the *rule* but still on the same `motor_X` stage with a reward-derived CF. So even v2 inherits limit (1).

**The cerebellum's defining biological contribution — feedback-error-learning (FEL): a crude feedback controller's error trains a feed-forward inverse model (Kawato 1987; Kawato & Gomi 1992; Wolpert-Miall-Kawato 1998; catalog F.07) — is EXACTLY the mechanism the recommended motor-teacher imports.** The cheap win is to take *that biology* (supervised error from a crude controller) and apply it at the *selection/read-out* stage where the precision is lost, rather than to wire the full cerebellar microcircuit at the `motor_X` stage where it demonstrably doesn't move the mean. (A *future* arc could route a *graded position error* as a climbing-fiber signal into a cerebellar refiner of the `sensory→cortex` map — §2 Rank 4 — but that is a heavier build than the motor-teacher and is not the cheapest first lever.)

**Net diagnosis:** the ~4.0 ceiling is **primarily the correlational reward-STDP learning rule** (proven in-house: scalar feedback 1/6 vs supervised 3/3), **secondarily the 4-way categorical WTA readout**, and **only marginally the bump code** (whose 2-D coarseness is NOT σ-fixable, so the obvious code lever is correctly exhausted). The fix is to **change the teaching signal from delayed scalar reward to an immediate SUPERVISED target at the cortex selection layer** (feedback-error-learning) — cheapest — with a **population-vector readout** as the second lever if the categorical boundary turns out to dominate.

## 2. RANKED MENU of biologically-grounded tightening mechanisms (cheapest-first)

Ranked by *cheapest-biologically-grounded-lever-that-tightens-precision* first. Each: mechanism, grounding, composition with Rank-1 reflex + N8/N6 back-end + cluster-F, reusable machinery, cost.

### Rank 1 (RECOMMENDED — cheapest, highest-ROI) — SUPERVISED motor-teacher at the cortex selection layer (feedback-error-learning)
- **Mechanism:** During teaching, while the `sensory→cortex_X` gate is OPEN, *also* drive the **target `cortex_X` pool that the SC reflex selects** with a strong supervised current (a desired-output clamp), so the plastic `sensory→cortex_X` synapses learn against a *clean immediate target* (Hebbian/STDP "this bump → this cortex pool firing NOW") instead of a delayed scalar reward. Then wean BOTH reflex and teacher. This converts the read-out's learning from *reinforcement* (correlate bump with reward) to *supervised* (map bump → the reflex's known-correct cardinal).
- **Grounding:** **Feedback-error-learning** — Kawato, Furukawa & Suzuki (1987, *Biol. Cybern.* 57:169–185); Kawato & Gomi (1992, *Trends Neurosci.*); **Wolpert, Miall & Kawato (1998), "Internal models in the cerebellum," *Trends Cogn. Sci.* 2(9):338–347.** FEL: a *crude feedback controller* generates a feedback motor command from sensory error; that command-error is the **teaching signal** (climbing-fiber-conveyed) that trains a *feed-forward inverse model* which progressively refines and *replaces* the crude controller. Here the **SC reflex IS the crude feedback controller** and the `sensory→cortex` map is the feed-forward model being trained. Also catalog **F.07** (forward/inverse internal models; "compute motor commands needed to achieve a desired sensory state"). In-house precedent: the conversational arc's **`--motor-teacher-pA`** during co-firing took engram composition from 80% → **PERFECT (20/20)** by clamping the target motor pool during encoding (`compose_engram_demo --motor-teacher-pA 1500`; `compose_sequential_engram`, CLAUDE.md "Motor teacher during encoding"). And the decisive in-house control that supervised >> scalar-reward: `2026-05-05-W-to-A-VERDICT` (3/3 vs 1/6).
- **Composes with Rank-1 reflex + N8/N6 + cluster-F:** The reflex *is* the teacher (it already computes the correct cardinal from the image; `sc_orienting_cardinal_from_image`). The teacher clamp enters at `cortex_X`, *upstream* of the unchanged N8/N6 cascade — same injection point the heuristic already uses (`:4196–4203`), so the back-end is untouched. It does NOT need cluster-F — it imports cluster-F's *biology* (FEL supervised error) at the *right* stage without the microcircuit. Weaning uses `transmission_gate`/`set_plasticity_gate` (already supports the `sensory_to_cortex` curriculum, `:3708–3911`).
- **Why it tightens:** Replaces the §1.3 bottleneck (scalar reward → coarse correlation) with a high-bandwidth supervised target → the read-out learns a *sharp* bump→cardinal boundary, which the reflex demonstrably possesses and reward-STDP demonstrably cannot reach.
- **Cost/tractability:** **LOW.** Additive default-off runner flag; reuses the reflex, the `sensory→cortex` pathway+gate, and the existing teacher-clamp code path. Likely **no `sim/` edit**. This is the §4 de-risk.

### Rank 2 (the readout fix, if the boundary dominates) — Population-vector / cosine-tuned cortex read-out (Georgopoulos)
- **Mechanism:** Replace (or augment) the 4 categorical `cortex_X` pools with **cosine-tuned** units whose preferred directions tile the circle; decode the commanded direction as the **population vector** `Σ rᵢ·θᵢ`, and quantize to a cardinal *late* (after the graded direction is formed) — preserving graded direction through the decision so near-diagonal goals route correctly.
- **Grounding:** **Georgopoulos et al. (1986)** population-vector coding (catalog **H.17**, Kandel 6e Ch 34 p 825–840); catalog **E.03** population coding/vector averaging; the catalog's own note that the current pools are "categorical … rather than vector-tuned … adding cosine-tuned input layer … would naturally yield population vector readout" (H.17, line 3092). Validation axis already in the catalog: "decode angle from population vector; tuning-curve width vs discrimination acuity" (E.03, line 1377).
- **Composes:** Sits between the read-out and the cardinal commit; the SC reflex (Rank 1) and motor-teacher both transfer (the teacher targets the cosine units around the reflex's direction). N8/N6 either consume the late-quantized cardinal unchanged, or (cleaner) the population vector feeds `sel_X` as graded evidence.
- **Why it tightens:** Removes the §1.4 categorical-boundary loss — the dominant *residual* once the learning rule is fixed.
- **Cost/tractability:** **MEDIUM.** Needs a cosine-tuned cortex layer + a population-vector decode; more than a flag but reuses the bump→cortex plumbing. Gate it on the §4 de-risk result (only build if supervised teaching plateaus at a categorical boundary).

### Rank 3 (the code fix, weakest lever) — Denser sensory coverage (more tuned units), NOT narrower σ
- **Mechanism:** Increase the number of sensory tuned units / coverage density (e.g. 7×7 → 11×11 or 15×15 over the same offset range), keeping σ matched to spacing. Do **not** shrink σ alone (§1.2).
- **Grounding:** Coarse-coding accuracy `∝ √N` of independent tuned units (Zhang & Sejnowski 1999, *Neural Comput.* 11:75–84; Pouget-Denève-Ducom-Latham 1999 — for 2-D, accuracy comes from *N/coverage*, not from σ). Catalog **E.03**/**E.02** (receptive-field density / population code over a continuous variable, "missing/partial").
- **Composes:** Pure front-end change; reflex/teacher/N8/N6 all unchanged.
- **Why it's Rank 3:** It addresses the *least* important locus (§1.2 says the code is only marginally limiting), and it costs neurons/VRAM for a `√N` (sub-linear) return. Only worth it if Ranks 1–2 land and a residual *code-resolution* limit is demonstrated (it likely won't be).
- **Cost/tractability:** **LOW–MEDIUM** (more neurons), **LOW expected payoff.**

### Rank 4 (the "proper" cerebellar refiner — heavier, future arc) — Graded-error climbing fiber into a cerebellar refiner of the `sensory→cortex` map
- **Mechanism:** Compute a *graded position error* (the reflex's correct cardinal vs the agent's actual move, as a vector error) and route it as a **climbing-fiber teaching signal** into a cerebellar module that refines the **`sensory→cortex` read-out** (not `motor_X`), via CF-gated PF→PC LTD. This is FEL realized in the *actual* cerebellar microcircuit, at the selection stage.
- **Grounding:** Marr (1969) / Albus (1971) / Ito (CF-gated PF→PC LTD); Kawato FEL; catalog **F.04** (climbing fiber = the teaching signal), **F.07** (internal models), **F.01/F.02** (Purkinje/DCN). The eLife/PNAS prediction-signal literature (Sokolov/Sokolov-style; "Prediction signals in the cerebellum," eLife 2020) supports cerebellar refinement beyond pure SL.
- **Composes:** Would need to re-target cluster-F from `motor_X` to a refiner of `cortex_X`/`sensory→cortex`, and replace the reward-derived CF with a *graded vector error*. Fixes both cluster-F limits from §1.5.
- **Why it's Rank 4:** It is the *biologically deepest* answer but a **substantial build** (re-wire cluster-F's output stage + a new graded-error CF + multi-seed validation), and it delivers — at the read-out — the *same supervised-error benefit* that Rank 1's motor-teacher delivers for a flag. Do it only if Rank 1 proves supervised error is the lever AND a microcircuit-faithful refiner is independently wanted (e.g. for online error-driven adaptation, which the static teacher lacks).
- **Cost/tractability:** **MEDIUM–HIGH.** Reuses the cluster-F regions but needs output re-targeting + a new error channel.

### Rank 5 (do NOT build for this) — Full PPC gain-field / basis-function coordinate-transform read-out
- **Mechanism:** A gain-modulated basis-function layer (retinal position × a modulating signal → product-tuned basis units) that performs the visuomotor coordinate transform "precisely."
- **Grounding:** Zipser & Andersen (1988); **Pouget & Snyder (2000)**; **Salinas & Abbott (2001), "Coordinate transformations in the visual system…", Prog. Brain Res.**; Deneve, Latham & Pouget (2001) basis functions. Catalog **E.13** (PPC egocentric/visuomotor transform), **E.15** (gain-field on RF response).
- **Why excluded for THIS ceiling:** Gain fields solve a *reference-frame* problem (retinal → head/body/world coordinates, modulated by eye/head position). The project's task is **already egocentric** (the bump is the goal offset relative to the agent; there is no eye/head-position variable to gain-modulate, and the gridworld render is body-centered). A basis-function layer would add machinery to transform between frames that the toy task does not require — it is over-build for an egocentric, single-frame problem. The *precision* limit here is the learning rule + categorical readout, not a missing coordinate transform. (Legitimate future addition when an allocentric place/grid frame is introduced — cf. Rank-3 of the perceptual-bootstrap doc — but not the cold lever for the ~4.0 ceiling.)
- **Cost/tractability:** **HIGH, wrong problem.**

## Net for the nav arc

The learned visuomotor read-out's ~4.0 precision ceiling is **primarily a learning-rule limit** — reward-modulated STDP is a coarse, scalar-credit correlational rule, and the project has *already proven in-house* (scalar 1/6 vs supervised 3/3) that the same architecture is precise under a *supervised* signal and coarse under scalar reward — **secondarily a 4-way categorical-WTA readout limit** (continuous goal offset forced onto 4 cardinals, mis-routing near decision boundaries), and **only marginally a bump-code limit** (whose 2-D coarseness is *not* σ-fixable per Pouget-Denève 1999 / Zhang-Sejnowski 1999, which is exactly why "more teaching / sharper bump" is the exhausted lever). The cheapest biologically-grounded tightening is a **supervised motor-teacher at the `cortex_X` selection layer — feedback-error-learning (Kawato; Wolpert-Miall-Kawato 1998), with the already-shipped SC reflex as the crude feedback controller** — reusing the reflex, the `sensory→cortex` pathway+gate, the existing teacher-clamp, and `transmission_gate` for the wean, composing with N8/N6 unchanged, and NOT needing the cluster-F microcircuit (which as wired refines `motor_X` via the global reward and so demonstrably reduces variance but not the mean). The de-risk is a falsifiable post-wean nav-score smoke that *localizes* the bottleneck: if supervised teaching tightens below the reward-STDP plateau, the learning rule was the limit (proceed multi-seed); if not, the categorical readout is, and the population-vector read-out (Georgopoulos / H.17) is the next cheapest lever. Anti-cheat throughout: real nav-score gate, image-sourced teacher target only (no coordinate leakage), complete reflex+teacher wean, multi-seed, biologized back-end.
