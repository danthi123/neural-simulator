## What v1-v6 tried and why they failed

All v1-v6 used 2048+500 (small) architecture:

| Variant | Treatment | Result |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | 100 events/synonym | W→A noise (29%), A→W noise (33%) |
| v2 | 200 events/synonym | W→A 24%, A→W 37% (group aligned!) |
| v3 | + cofire 0.3 | W→A 25%, A→W 39% — same |
| v6 | + two-stage curriculum | W→A 28%, A→W 30% — same |

Pattern: secondary synonym ('up', 'right', 'down', 'left') gets
~0/100 predictions per motor. STDP winner-take-all per synapse —
primary synonym wins, secondary doesn't consolidate.

**Capacity hypothesis confirmed**: at 1000-neuron motor pools (vs
500), STDP has enough parameters to develop functional sub-
populations within each motor_X. Different sub-populations bind to
different synonyms; both contribute to motor_X action.

Per-motor balance (motor_N, n=primary 'north', sec='up'):
- v1-v6 (small):  n=16-18, sec=0-1 (WTA failure)
- v4 (scaled):    n=12-23, sec=1-6 (both contribute, asymmetric)

The asymmetry is real (primary fires 3-23× more than secondary)
but enough to pass permuted-label control at group level. That's
the Tier 2.1 success criterion.

   pure local plasticity + co-firing teachers.
