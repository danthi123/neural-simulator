## 1. DIAGNOSIS — why the wall is generation-STABILITY, not credit

The rung-3a 2×2 is decisive because it separates two jobs that a naive read conflates. **(J2) temporal credit assignment** — teaching the recurrent weights *which* synapse to change so the one-step map matches the target — is SOLVED by the local rules: teacher-forced, the memoryless forward eligibility reaches one-step correlation 0.963 and the proper e-prop first-order eligibility (the pre-registered fix) reaches 0.695, both far above every non-credit control (~0). So credit *quality* is not the bottleneck. **(J1) the autonomous stability of the learned recurrent dynamical system** — once the teacher is withdrawn and the network is fed its OWN output (free-run / closed-loop generation), does the learned map keep the state on the target trajectory instead of diverging? — FAILS for both rules, and fails two distinct ways. Trained teacher-forced (always fed ground-truth prefixes), recall collapses (0.008 / −0.031): the classic **exposure bias** — small one-step errors, never seen in training, compound the instant the loop closes. Trained free-run (scheduled sampling, the exposure-bias fix), the learning signal now rides on the network's own noisy trajectory and **destabilizes the very map it is trying to learn** (e-prop free-run one-step −0.282), and the leaky unit is *more* fragile because membrane integration accumulates the free-run error. This is a different problem class: it is about the *dynamics* of a closed autonomous loop (contraction toward the target vs. divergence away from it), which no point-wise credit rule addresses. The canonical engineering fix is FORCE (Sussillo–Abbott 2009) — keep the network *on* the target during training via fast feedback so errors never get a chance to compound — but its recursive-least-squares (RLS) update is non-local (a running inverse-correlation matrix P shared across a unit's afferents). The mandate is to find the LOCAL / biologically-plausible members of that family — OR to establish that the failing task (single-cue autonomous continuous-trajectory recall) is the wrong milestone for a communication cortex (§4).

**Prose on the load-bearing candidates:**

- **#1 Asabuki–Clopath 2025 Predictive Alignment (the biology-grounded generation-stability rule; recommend as the Fork-1 mechanism).** This is the newest and best-fit member of the family and it directly displaces FORCE as the biologically-plausible way to tame chaotic recurrent dynamics. The insight: instead of *minimizing output error* on the recurrent weights (FORCE) — which needs the non-local RLS-P, fast plasticity, and output-clamping — PA trains the plastic recurrent weights M to **predict a feedback signal while ALIGNING that prediction with the network's own chaotic activity**, so chaos is *gently suppressed* rather than fought. The rule `ΔM = η_M(Qz − Ĵr)r^T` with `Ĵ = M − αG` is fully local (verified verbatim: pre-activity, post-activity, and their predicted activities only; **no inverse-correlation matrix, no transport, no BPTT**), uses **slow** plasticity (biologically realistic, unlike FORCE's fast weights), and needs **no output-clamping**. A separate readout is trained by a plain delta/LMS rule; the readout feedback enters only the learning rule, not the dynamics. It is provided in **both rate and spiking (LIF)** forms, produces genuine **autonomous free-run** attractors (patterns persist after plasticity is turned off — exactly our teacher-neutrality/recall test), and it handles **short-term temporal memory** (Ready-Set-Go delay-match with interpolation to untrained delays) and **high-dimensional targets** (22,080-pixel video from 800 units). Crucially for us, its own biological story — apical dendrite = teaching/feedback, proximal-basal = plastic recurrent, distal-basal = fixed chaotic drive — is *the same dendritic motif as our confirmed Sacramento–Senn microcircuit*, so PA is not an alien mechanism bolted on; it is the recurrent-stability form of the compartmental credit we already have. It is also the cheapest biology-grounded stability arm to add (~35 lines: a fixed chaotic recurrent G, a plastic recurrent M with the align rule, a delta-rule readout). **This is the mechanism that makes Fork 1 genuinely cheap AND biologically apt** (a status FORCE could never have, being non-local).

- **#2 Miconi 2017 (the reward-based alternative if PA disappoints or if only a scalar critic is available).** A recurrent net in the near-chaotic regime; each trial, occasional random perturbations are injected into unit activations; a synapse-local eligibility trace accumulates the *supralinear* (cubic) product of pre-activity and post-activation fluctuation `S(r_j·(x_i−x̄_i))`; at trial end a single scalar reward `(R−R̄)` gates the accumulated trace into a weight change. The cubic nonlinearity is the key trick — it amplifies genuine perturbation-driven fluctuations and suppresses relaxation artifacts, which is what makes a scalar reward enough. **It directly attacks our diagnosed double-bind:** the network is trained *in closed loop on its own dynamics* (there is no teacher-forced prefix at all, so no exposure-bias gap), and the perturbation+reward loop is a stochastic policy-gradient that shapes the attractor rather than the point-wise map, so it does not destabilize what it is learning the way scheduled-sampling gradient descent did. Locality is exactly our standard (verified: "Hebbian and uses only synapse-local information, without requiring instantaneous reward signals"). **Honest caveat (verified):** node-perturbation is a zeroth-order/policy-gradient estimator, so its variance grows with N and with trial length T — Miconi himself had to drop the learning rate 0.1→0.03→0.003 as delays/timing-variability grew; for a long continuous trajectory this could be slow or hit a variance wall. This is why PA (#1) — which uses an exact aligned prediction, not a noisy scalar-reward gradient estimate — is the preferred Fork-1 mechanism; Miconi is the fallback when only a scalar critic signal is biologically available.

- **#3 Murray 2019 RFLO (a *supervised* continuous-error stability arm — smallest delta from what we built).** RFLO is the local, no-transport approximation to real-time recurrent learning: it drops the non-local terms of RTRL and replaces the readout-weight transpose with a fixed random feedback matrix B (feedback alignment), keeping a proper per-synapse eligibility trace with the `φ'(u)` pseudo-derivative. It is *closest to what we already built* — it IS a target-based recurrent rule with (a) a proper eligibility (which e-prop also had) PLUS (b) error delivered through the same kind of fixed-random top-down feedback our microcircuit uses for `W_PP_td`. **But note the sober risk:** RFLO uses a continuous supervised error at every timestep — it is *still a teacher-forced-style training signal*, and Murray reports it underperforms BPTT on long horizons. Whether RFLO's proper eligibility + feedback-alignment error *alone* closes the free-run gap that our e-prop eligibility did NOT close is uncertain — the difference is only the error-delivery path (fixed B vs. our exact target error), which is unlikely to be the thing that stabilizes free-run. So RFLO is a legitimate, cheap arm but I rank it *below* Miconi for *this specific wall* because Miconi changes the training *loop* (closed-loop policy gradient) whereas RFLO changes only the *error delivery* of the same teacher-forced-family training that already failed.

- **#4 Gilra–Gerstner FOLLOW (the stability existence-proof, spiking).** FOLLOW is the one candidate with a *proven* Lyapunov-stable autonomous attractor in spiking LIF neurons (it learns the Lorenz strange attractor and van der Pol oscillator to free-run). Local, spiking, no transport. The catch for us: stability is achieved by a persistent negative-gain error-feedback loop that keeps the network on target — i.e., there is an always-on supervisor channel during operation, not a self-sustaining attractor the net free-runs unaided. That is a legitimate biological motif (cerebellar/olivary error feedback; efference-copy correction) but it is an ADDED architectural component, so it is more expensive than a Miconi arm and further from our current microcircuit. It is the right *escalation* if both Fork-1 Miconi and the reframe (§4) disappoint.

## 3. HOW EACH RELATES TO THE CONFIRMED SACRAMENTO–SENN MICROCIRCUIT CREDIT

The microcircuit credit (rung-2) is an **exact, per-neuron target/error signal** delivered to the apical compartment through fixed feedback, with a local SST-interneuron cancelling the top-down prediction so the apical residual = the error. The generation-stability mechanisms sit at different points relative to it:

- **Predictive Alignment (#1) is the SAME dendritic motif made recurrent-stable — the tightest composition.** PA's own biological realization (apical = teaching/feedback `Qz`; proximal-basal = plastic recurrent `M`; distal-basal = fixed chaotic drive `G`; the plastic dendrite learns to *predict/align* the drive) is structurally identical to our confirmed microcircuit (apical teaching + a dendritic prediction that cancels/aligns the recurrent drive). So PA is not a wrapper *around* the credit; it IS the recurrent-stability form of the compartmental credit, differing only in that the recurrent weights are trained to align-with-chaos (stability) while a separate readout carries output error. This is why it is ranked #1: minimal conceptual distance from the confirmed rung-2 mechanism, and it delivers the missing autonomous-stability property natively.
- **Miconi and Fiete–Seung COMPOSE cleanly** as a *training-loop wrapper*, not a credit replacement. The microcircuit's exact `(a*−a)` cancellation error is a far *better* per-neuron learning signal than a global scalar reward — so the natural fusion is: keep the microcircuit's per-neuron target error as the credit, and adopt Miconi's *closed-loop, perturb-and-evaluate training schedule* to solve the free-run stability that the credit rule alone cannot. In effect: "the microcircuit says which way each neuron should move; the closed-loop perturbation schedule ensures those moves stabilize the *autonomous* trajectory, not just the teacher-forced map." This is the cleanest composition and the reason Fork 1's cheap arm is a *schedule change on the rule we already have*, not a new credit rule.
- **RFLO is the microcircuit's rule with a proper eligibility + feedback-alignment** — the smallest delta from our current e-prop arm, but (see above) it does not obviously change the free-run dynamics.
- **FOLLOW ADDS an error-feedback loop** alongside the credit — composes as an extra channel, more machinery.
- **Laje–Buonomano and FORCE REPLACE the credit** with RLS — non-local, so they are concept-donors / upper-bound controls, not the brain-based rule.

## 4. REFRAME FORK: is autonomous continuous-trajectory recall even the right task?

**The sharp question:** rung-3a's Task A is *single-cue autonomous free-run of a continuous sinusoid-superposition trajectory* — cue with s\*₀, withdraw the teacher, and demand the recurrence regenerate the whole periodic waveform unaided. That is exactly the closed-loop-stability problem §1 diagnoses. But the rung-3 GOAL is a **communication / language cortex**, and it is worth asking, skeptically, whether Task A's failure mode is partly a **task-choice artifact** rather than a substrate wall.

**(a) Our own result already shows the predict-next map is learnable.** Teacher-forced one-step correlation is 0.963 (forward) / 0.695 (e-prop) — the local rule *can* predict the next state given the current one. What fails is only the *autonomous free-run* of that map. So if the milestone were "predict the next symbol given the ongoing input," we would already be at/above the bar with the machinery in hand.

**(b) Biology of a communication cortex arguably predicts-next-given-input, not free-runs a fixed waveform.** Language comprehension/production in cortex is overwhelmingly an *input-driven, context-conditioned next-element* computation — the network continually receives the ongoing token stream and predicts/produces the next element in context (predictive coding of speech; the entire "brain as a next-word predictor" framing). It is NOT "cue once, then autonomously replay a fixed 140-step waveform from memory with the input channel silent." Free-running a fixed trajectory from a single cue is really a *motor-pattern / birdsong / CPG* task (HVC replaying a learned song) — a real biological capability, but a *different* faculty from communication, and one biology solves with dedicated machinery (a HVC clock, Fiete–Seung reward-modulated perturbation) rather than a generic recurrent readout. So Task A may be testing the *harder, less communication-relevant* of the two capabilities.

**(c) Predictive E-prop is the closest published match to our substrate — and it is a predict-next objective.** Noè, Yamamoto, Katori & Sato (bioRxiv 2026, `10.64898/2026.02.12.705507`, shortlist #2, controller-verified real) optimizes exactly a **self-supervised predict-the-next-input objective via the online + local e-prop rule in a recurrent SPIKING net** — no BPTT, no transport, the tightest single published fusion of our rung-3 constraints. It does *not* rely on single-cue autonomous free-run; the ongoing input is present and the objective is next-element prediction. And Bouhadjar–Diesmann–Morrison (PLoS CB 2022, `10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010233`) is *input-driven, context-specific next-element prediction* by construction (the spiking-TM predicts the next element of the ongoing sequence; two overlapping sequences A-B-C-D-E / X-B-C-D-F force *context* to disambiguate the shared middle) — and it does it with *zero* teaching (pure Hebbian + dendritic-AP pre-activation + WTA), so it is also a positive-control that the *task itself* is achievable without any generation-stability trickery.

**What Task B buys us (the case FOR Fork 2):** it makes our **already-working local one-step map the deliverable**. Task B (high-order next-symbol prediction, teacher-forced-by-construction because the ongoing input is always present) is graded by *held-out next-symbol accuracy on the input-driven continuation* — no single-cue autonomous free-run, so the exposure-bias / free-run-destabilization wall is **sidestepped, not fought**. Autonomous generation (sampling the model's own next-symbol distribution and feeding it back = free-run) becomes a *later, optional* capability (sampling + feedback), deferred exactly as language models separate "next-token prediction training" from "autoregressive generation."

**The honest counter (the case AGAINST fully abandoning generation):** *producing* language ultimately requires emitting a sequence, which is autoregressive — at some point the cortex must feed its own output back and stay coherent. Deferring generation forever would leave a real gap. BUT: (i) the correct place to solve generation is with the dedicated production machinery (HVC-clock / CPG / Fiete–Seung), not by demanding a generic predict-next recurrence also free-run a continuous waveform; (ii) even in LLMs, robust generation emerges *on top of* a well-trained next-token predictor plus a sampling/feedback loop — get the predictor right first; (iii) the shuffled-target and context-disambiguation anti-cheats on Task B still prove genuine temporal-order/context credit, so Task B is not a cop-out. So the reframe is *sequencing*, not *evasion*: nail the biologically-central predict-next capability now (where we already succeed), defer autonomous generation to a dedicated production circuit later.

## 5. VERDICT + honest risks

### The two forks (both legitimate)
- **Fork 1 — add a generation-stability mechanism to Task A.** Best biological member: **Asabuki–Clopath 2025 Predictive Alignment** (fully local, slow plasticity, rate+spiking, autonomous free-run attractors, and the *same dendritic motif* as our confirmed microcircuit). Directly fights the diagnosed wall — it tames the chaotic free-run dynamics that destabilized our map. (Miconi 2017 is the reward-based fallback; FORCE is the non-local upper-bound control only.)
- **Fork 2 — reframe the milestone to Task B (high-order next-symbol prediction).** Make the already-working local one-step map the deliverable; defer autonomous free-run generation to a dedicated production circuit. Sidesteps the wall.

### RECOMMENDATION — do **Fork 2 FIRST**, then Fork 1 (Predictive Alignment) if/when autonomous production is required.
Ranked on the mandate's own criteria: **biological aptness for a communication cortex** (Fork 2 wins decisively — a language cortex predicts-next-given-input; single-cue autonomous continuous-trajectory free-run is a motor/birdsong faculty, §4b), **cheapness** (Fork 2 wins — it *reuses the local rule that already reaches 0.96/0.70 teacher-forced*; only the task generator + next-symbol readout + context anti-cheats are new, ~50 lines, exactly the Task-B path already scoped in `2026-07-02-rung3-recurrent-microcircuit-sequence-scoping.md §3`), and **closeness to the confirmed microcircuit credit** (Fork 2 wins — it changes NOTHING about the credit rule; it changes only the task the credit is scored on, and Predictive E-prop shows this exact predict-next + local-e-prop + recurrent-spiking fusion is published and real). Fork 1 with Predictive Alignment is now a *genuinely attractive* second move — its discovery (a fully-local, spiking, dendritically-motivated chaos-taming rule) closes the "the only stability fix is non-local FORCE" gap, so autonomous generation is no longer blocked on a biologically-implausible mechanism. But it remains the *second* move: Fork 2 delivers the communication-central capability first, with what we already have.

**Why not Fork 1 first, even with Predictive Alignment in hand:** Fork 1 still (a) requires building new dynamics machinery (a fixed chaotic reservoir G + the align rule + a separately-trained readout — a different architecture than our current single-population target-based net), and (b) — most importantly — spends that effort *forcing* a capability (single-cue continuous free-run) that §4 argues is the *wrong* capability to prioritize for communication. Fork 2 delivers the communication-central capability now, with what we already have, for ~50 lines. This is the SURPASS move (isolate the genuine residual: the residual is "the *generation* half of a *production* faculty," not "the communication cortex can't predict") rather than accepting the Task-A boundary as the communication verdict. Note that Predictive Alignment ALSO makes Fork 2's eventual generation upgrade cheap: when the predict-next cortex (Fork 2 GO) later needs to *produce* autoregressively, PA is the local, spiking, biology-grounded rule to stabilize that generation loop — so the two forks are complementary, sequenced, not competing.

### Concrete cheap-first de-risk — Fork 2 as a new arm-set in `_emerge6_recurrent_microcircuit_seq_derisk.py`
Reuse-by-import, NO `sim/` edit, CPU, ≤4 workers, seeds 42/43/44. **What changes / what stays:**
- **New `make_seqB_task(seed)`** (sibling of `make_seq_task`): two overlapping high-order symbol sequences over a small alphabet (V≈6–8), e.g. `A B C D E` / `X B C D F`, one-hot encoded; the shared middle `B C D` forces *context* to predict the divergent last symbol (the Bouhadjar high-order test). Emit an input stream + the target next-symbol at each step. Train/held-out split = held-out *continuations* (never-seen prefix orderings), the memorization floor.
- **Training loop: teacher-forced next-symbol, NOT free-run.** The input stream is ALWAYS present (input-driven); the target is the next symbol. Keep the EXISTING local target-based / e-prop credit rule verbatim (`elig="forward"` and `elig="eprop"`) — this is the whole point: the rule already works teacher-forced. Add a small linear/softmax readout trained by the SAME local target error (or a fresh clean readout for the representation gate). **Do NOT add** any FORCE/RLS, any P matrix, any autonomous free-run recall metric as the GO gate (free-run generation is explicitly DEFERRED).
- **Metric = held-out next-symbol accuracy** on the input-driven continuation (chance = 1/V ≈ 0.14–0.17), plus the clean-readout representation gate (frozen recurrent state → fresh readout ≥ 0.70 above untrained) to prove a usable *context* representation, not a lucky readout.
- **What stays local (assert it):** the credit path reads only {pre-rate, post `(a*−a)`, local eligibility} — no `W_rec.T`, no BPTT, no cross-neuron broadcast — the existing `used_transpose` locality flag, unchanged.

**Mandatory anti-cheats (carried + Task-B-specific):**
- `apical_feedback_lesion` (kill the target error → floor), `wrong_sign` (negate target → anti-learn), `no_teaching_null` (no target → flat), `untrained` (random W → chance) — all as today.
- `shuffled_target` (shuffle the next-symbol labels → must fail) — temporal-order credit load-bearing.
- **`context_lesion` (NEW, Task-B-specific, load-bearing):** collapse the two overlapping sequences to a single-symbol (1st-order Markov) predictor of the shared middle — the model must BEAT this 1st-order-Markov floor on the divergent last symbol, or it is memorizing local bigrams, not learning *context*. This is the anti-cheat that makes Task B a real high-order-sequence result and not a bigram lookup.
- `hebbian_selforg` (Bouhadjar-style, NO teacher): does the task self-organize without credit? If it matches the credit arm, report honestly — the deliverable is then "predict-next self-organizes; the microcircuit credit's value is arbitrary-mapping/faster-convergence," which is still a genuine, build-informative finding.
- Multi-seed 42/43/44; locality asserted every arm/seed.

**GO / BOUNDARY criteria:**
- **GO** = `recurrent_microcircuit` (and/or `eprop`) held-out next-symbol accuracy ≥ 0.75 AND ≫ the `context_lesion` 1st-order floor by ≥ 0.15 AND > `apical_feedback_lesion`+0.15; `wrong_sign` anti-learns; `no_teaching_null`/`untrained` at chance; `shuffled_target` fails; clean-readout ≥ 0.70 above untrained; locality True. ⇒ rung-3 milestone met on the communication-central task → proceed to rung-3b (spike noise) on Task B, then the `sim/` port scoping.
- **BOUNDARY** (build-informative) = if the credit rule CANNOT beat the `context_lesion` 1st-order floor even teacher-forced, the wall is *context/high-order credit* (not generation) → escalate to Predictive E-prop's exact predict-next objective (shortlist #2) and/or Bouhadjar's dendritic-AP context mechanism, and re-scope. If `hebbian_selforg` matches → re-point (above).

### Honest risks — what could make even Fork 2 fail, and the fallback
1. **The task might be too easy (bigram-solvable).** Mitigation = the `context_lesion` anti-cheat is mandatory and load-bearing; if we can't beat the 1st-order floor the result is void by construction. Risk that even the high-order task is trivially separable at V≈6 — if so, raise the order / overlap length.
2. **`hebbian_selforg` solves it (task doesn't need credit).** This is Bouhadjar's own result. Not a failure — an honest re-point (the microcircuit credit's value is arbitrary target mappings / faster convergence, tested on Task A-style arbitrary mappings *without* the free-run requirement). Fallback: a *prompted* arbitrary-mapping task (input cue → produce a specific short target sequence step-by-step, teacher-forced) that provably needs credit but still has no single-cue autonomous free-run.
3. **Deferring generation leaves a real production gap (§4 counter).** If autonomous *production* later proves to be on the critical path, escalate to **Fork 1 = Asabuki–Clopath 2025 Predictive Alignment** (the fully-local, slow-plasticity, spiking, chaos-taming rule whose dendritic motif matches our microcircuit). Its honest risk is that PA needs a fixed *chaotic* reservoir G to align against (it tames chaos, it does not create the generative substrate from nothing) and a suitable feedback matrix Q — i.e. an architectural addition, not a drop-in on our current net; and its published targets are continuous/pixel trajectories, so its behavior on *symbolic* output must be checked.
