## What the substrate physics forced (the honest reframe)
The task's naive bet was: overlapping patterns + divisive normalization make a *shared GAIN* whose WITHDRAWAL de-ignites all content. On this substrate that specific reading is a **NO-GO**: an ignited pattern is a period-3 bistable attractor that is SELF-SUFFICIENT once on, so removing the tonic thalamic support does NOT de-ignite it (measured below — it reproduces the STN finding's own "thalamo-cortical remove-excitation self-sufficient" horn, now from the distributed side). Pure divisive-norm COMPETITION likewise cannot EVICT a self-sufficient incumbent (the active-overwrite boundary below). The DEEPEST lesson (CLAUDE.md): the companion process the bistable attractor needs is a way to attack the RECURRENCE, not the drive — exactly what Rung-2d proved with STD. So the working global stop combines BOTH ideas the synthesis pointed at: divisive normalization (which makes the patterns clearable) + a conflict-triggered depression of the SHARED recurrent resource (which clears them).

## The mechanism that WORKS (GLOBAL STOP, GO 6/6)
Three regions, all wiring explicit: `workspace` (exc, NMDA; K=3 distributed cliques of P=100 over a shared pool, adjacent cliques overlap O=15, private cores ~[85,70,85]) + `norm_pool` (60 inh; pools ALL workspace activity via workspace->norm, returns broad CONDUCTANCE feedback inhibition norm->workspace = the divisive divisor) + `thal` (60 exc; tonic shared support). Short-term DEPRESSION (Tsodyks-Markram; host-tracked per-presynaptic x, written into the workspace->workspace recurrence weights each step BEFORE transmission — the Rung-2d pattern, native STP OFF) carries a baseline U=0 (a confident commit never self-depletes) and a CONFLICT BOOST. The conflict SENSOR reads the workspace's OWN late-window per-clique spiking margin; boost = boost_gain*max(0, margin_ref - margin)*scale -> 0 at a confident commit, ~0.13 at a co-ignited conflict (inside the divisive-norm-load-bearing window [0.09,0.15]). Frozen op (no per-seed tuning): w_rec=34, ws_norm=5, norm_ws=16, thal_tonic=700 pA, thal_ws=5, boost_scale=0.85, pulse=110 steps, OU=30 pA, dt=1.

The conflict state co-ignites all K contents (n=3, margin ~0 = maximal ambiguity). The conflict-triggered depression burst depletes the shared recurrence; because every ignited pattern draws on it, ALL content de-ignites UNIFORMLY -> n_ignited -> 0. It is gain-withdrawal from a SHARED resource (not a huge g_i), so there is no driving-force reversal / rebound — self-extinction is the natural collapse. This is the function the STN external inhibition FAILED at (it stuck at min n_post=2: weak inhibition left a survivor, strong inhibition destabilized upward).

## CHARACTERIZED boundaries (reported, not hidden — the honest deliverables)
- **Naive tonic GAIN-WITHDRAWAL is a NO-GO (self-sufficiency):** ignite one content, then free-run with the thal tonic support OFF -> the content does NOT de-ignite (n 1->1 on every seed). The period-3 bistable attractor is self-sufficient once on; a graded tonic-support withdrawal cannot touch it. This is the SAME horn the STN finding hit ("once ignited, self-sufficient"), reproduced here from the distributed side, and it is WHY the working stop had to attack the recurrence (STD), not the drive. Next lever (already realized here): withdraw the SHARED RECURRENT resource (STD), not the tonic gain.
- **Active OVERWRITE (fast eviction-and-swap) is a BOUNDARY:** ignite an incumbent A, then drive a salient overlapping challenger B (A not re-driven) -> A is NOT evicted; B co-ignites (n 1->2) — pure divisive-norm competition cannot displace a self-sufficient incumbent (the Rung-2c eviction boundary, from the distributed side). A depression-based clear removes ALL content (it cannot selectively retain the challenger — the challenger cannot re-ignite through the depleted recurrence before it recovers). Next levers: (1) the Rung-2d self-eviction path (STD depletes the held incumbent -> empty window -> the persistent challenger re-ignites, a SLOW overwrite already GO at rung-2d); (2) a much larger overlap so the incumbent's private core is sub-critical alone (blocked here by the confident-commit leak — driving one content spuriously co-ignites its neighbor at large overlap); (3) a targeted, USE-driven depression of ONLY the shared A-B neurons so the challenger's drive evicts A while B's private core (externally driven) survives.

## Why this is a genuine surpass of the STN NO-GO (not the same negative)
The STN veto delivered a graded external inhibitory brake into a LOCALIST self-sufficient attractor and could not reach n=0 (survivor at low g_i; destabilize-UP via the g_i driving-force reversal at high g_i). The two things the STN finding named as the fix — a shared resource whose withdrawal is uniform, and the conflict sensor as its effector arm — are exactly what carries this GO.
