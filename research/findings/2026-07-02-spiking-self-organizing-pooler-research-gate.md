## The isolated residual
The fixed-random-projection coincidence pooler works at 2 categories (0.83) but fails at 4 (0.33 ≈ chance) — a fixed projection cannot SEPARATE 4 categories' column responses. The missing piece: the pooler columns must LEARN (plastic feat→col) to (a) tune to distinct feature patterns, (b) stay disjoint across categories, (c) preserve within-category similarity, unsupervised, scaling to ≥4 categories. That is competitive representation learning + homeostasis (column-usage balancing).

## How biology / SNN theory does it (+ the KEY substrate realization)
Competitive representation learning in cortex = Hebbian feed-forward plasticity + lateral inhibition (winner-take-all) + homeostasis (intrinsic excitability / synaptic scaling balancing which units are used) — realized in SNNs by Diehl-Cook (2015, unsupervised STDP MNIST: STDP + lateral inhibition + adaptive per-neuron threshold), Masquelier-Thorpe (STDP feature learning), and the HTM Spatial Pooler (Cui-Ahmad-Hawkins 2017: competitive Hebbian permanences + homeostatic BOOSTING). **The decisive realization: this project's committed `sim/kernels.fused_htm_permanence_update` (the HTM three-term rule) IS a competitive-Hebbian-with-homeostasis rule already** — its terms are (1) STDP-windowed potentiation of co-active pre→post (competitive Hebbian), (2) presynaptic depression of non-co-active (decorrelation), and (3) the dAP-rate homeostasis `hfac = 0.5 + 0.5·max(0, z* − z)` — which is EXACTLY the Spatial Pooler's boosting (under-used units get a larger update). So the "homeostatic boosting" the numpy pooler needs is already in the committed kernel.
