# Metacog recall-margin evidence: a genuine spiking winner-vs-runner-up read tracks the host formula and is load-bearing by lesion (6/6 seeds); a precision residual remains in the ambiguous middle band (PARTIAL)

**Verdict: PARTIAL, default-OFF (`BRAIN_METACOG_SPIKING_MARGIN`).** Per [`docs/TERMS.md`](../../docs/TERMS.md) this is **de-risked / validated opt-in**, not **closed** — the host formula stays the production default and this mechanism is additive. The spiking recall-margin (a) tracks the host confidence formula strongly on continuous measures (Pearson r=0.959, Spearman rho=0.954 over 60 seed×condition points) and agrees with it on unambiguous cases 97.6% of the time, and (b) is unambiguously **load-bearing** by the strongest available test — a targeted lesion of the recall circuit's own discrimination collapses the margin and flips a confident answer to a hedge on **6 of 6 seeds**. The honestly characterized residual is in the *ambiguous middle band* (a genuinely borderline recall): there the two channels' binary confident/hedge classification agrees only ~50% of the time, a precision limit checked against a window-size sweep (not resolved by more integration time) rather than an unexamined gap. <!--derived--> (rounded reads of `research/findings/raw/_metacog_spiking_recall_margin_derisk/6seed_results.json`'s `verdict` block, reproduced in full in the "6-seed results" section below)

## The shortcut (backlog rank 9)

`metacog_production_organ.mean_role_confidence` derives the evidence fed to the metacog workspace's honesty hedge from `OneBrainComposer._margin` / `RFPhasorComposer._cleanup_all_score_stats`'s `margin`/`margin_norm`/`margin_snr` fields — all **host arithmetic** comparisons of the matched-filter cleanup's scores (`(peak-runner_up)/peak`, or a z-score against the candidate bulk). The hedge's own *settled* decision is genuinely spiking (the metacog workspace's own NMDA-conductance/firing-rate balance, `2026-08-12-laneC-...`, `2026-08-13-metacog-robust-confidence-GO.md`) — but the evidence *entering* that competition was a Python comparison of numbers, not a read of the recall circuit's own spiking. (A **sibling but distinct** mechanism, the metacog workspace's OWN confidence-margin READ METHOD (`nmda_norm` vs a `spiking_acc` presynaptic-count candidate), was separately explored and reached a 6-seed NO-GO (`2026-08-26-metacog-spiking-acc-6seed-NOGO.md`, on main; a follow-on continuation of that arc sits on the unmerged `research/metacog-spiking-6seed-land` branch) — that arc targets the workspace's *settled-margin* computation; this one targets the *evidence derivation* upstream of it. Rank 9 itself was still "fresh" (unattempted) as of the 2026-09-05 backlog map.)

## The retirement mechanism

`RFPhasorComposer._spiking_margin(scores, lesion=False)` (new, `research/runners/rf_phasor_composer.py`) drives the SAME cached Izhikevich concept bank (`_izh_bank`) that `_spiking_cleanup`/`OneBrainComposer._spiking_select` already use for the on-substrate winner-**pick** (2026-06-05-phase1-tpam-cleanup-derisk-GO.md), and reads a genuine winner-vs-runner-up **spike-count** margin — `(firing[winner]-firing[runner_up])/(firing[winner]+eps)` — instead of a host comparison of the matched-filter membrane amplitudes. `lesion=True` replaces the input scores with a uniform (all-tied) vector before the competition runs, so any resulting margin can only be competition noise, never a read of the (now-absent) score differential — the same "remove the evidence differential" pattern `metacog_production_organ.nmda_norm_margin(..., lesion=True)` already uses one level downstream. The lesion is a deterministic per-call substitution (not a persisted state that could regrow), so it trivially holds at the moment of measurement per `docs/TERMS.md`'s lesion condition.

`margin_spiking` is populated additively (default `None`) on every role chip in `OneBrainComposer._block_role_scores`/`_trace_query` and in `RFPhasorComposer._cleanup_all_score_stats`, gated by `self.spiking_recall_margin` — a constructor kwarg with an env fallback (`BRAIN_METACOG_SPIKING_MARGIN=1`, the `enable_sparse_index` precedent) so it reaches the real production call chain with **zero changes** to any intermediate constructor (`MultiTurnAgent`, `BrainConversationalAgent`, `webapp/server.py`). `mean_role_confidence` now prefers `margin_spiking` (top of its preference chain) whenever present — since population is flag-gated, a query's evidence is either ALL-spiking or ALL-host, never a silent per-role blend. Default OFF: every existing test and the production default are byte-identical (verified below).

## A real wall found en route, and how it was surpassed (not banked)

<!--derived-->

The winner-pick's existing, validated `_cleanup_drive_pA=60` constant drives **zero spikes over the 120-step cleanup window** on the tiny-demo's Izhikevich concept bank (measured directly: a single neuron driven continuously at 60pA settles to a sub-threshold fixed point around -49mV to -50mV and never crosses its heterogeneous firing threshold, which this population draws in the -30 to -55mV range; exact per-neuron parameters and the drive sweep are in `calibrate_margin_output.json`). This was never noticed before because a single-outcome argmax-over-firing winner-**pick** never needs the *loser* to fire — a silent competition just falls through to `argmax(scores)`, the byte-identical host answer. A margin, unlike a pick, needs graded spike **counts**, so this residual is load-bearing specifically for a margin read. Per CLAUDE.md's wall discipline, the fix was a **calibrated operating point** for this specific read, not a banked limitation: `_margin_drive_pA=300` (a dedicated constant, NOT touching `_cleanup_drive_pA`, so the validated winner-pick path is byte-unchanged) reliably crosses threshold within the *same* 120-step window (`_cleanup_window` reused unchanged — a longer-window sweep at 300/500/800pA × 300/600 steps did not clearly improve tracking over the 120-step baseline; see `calibrate_margin_output.json`'s `operating_point_sweep`). 300pA/120-steps was chosen as the cheapest point that already reaches the top of that sweep's tracking band.

## Calibration: SPIKING_MARGIN_LO/HI

<!--derived-->

`margin_spiking`'s raw numeric range correlates strongly with the host `margin` at the chosen operating point (see `calibrate_margin_output.json`'s `linear_fit`) but is not identical to it. Reusing `ROLE_CONF_LO/HI` (0.30/0.50) unmodified tracked the clean/heavily-degraded extremes but measurably misaligned the confident/hedge threshold crossing in the ambiguous middle band. `metacog_production_organ.py` now carries `SPIKING_MARGIN_LO`/`SPIKING_MARGIN_HI` (see the constants' own docstring for the exact values) — a linear fit of `margin_spiking` against the host `margin` over those same 25 points, inverted onto the ROLE_CONF band — the same anchor-remap methodology `margin_snr` already uses via `SNR_LO`/`SNR_HI` for the identical reason (a different-substrate signal has no single "the" reference recall to anchor by hand; a regression over multiple measured points is the more defensible version of the same idea). This recalibration did not change rank-order agreement (a monotonic remap cannot), but is the correct, honest way to compare the two channels on the same footing rather than leaving `margin_spiking` unscaled.

**Tracking (a):** over all 60 seed×condition points (clean + 9 synaptic-noise levels, sigma 0.3–4.0, per-seed-fixed noise draws), Pearson r=0.959, Spearman rho=0.954. Split by whether the host margin sits clearly outside the confidence band (host mrc <=0.30 or >=0.50, an unambiguous case) vs inside it (0.30<host mrc<0.50, a genuinely borderline case): **unambiguous-case agreement 97.6% (41/42)**, **ambiguous-case agreement 50% (9/18)** — the honestly characterized residual (see below).

**Load-bearing via lesion (b):** on the SAME clean, otherwise-confident turn, forcing the recall circuit's own discrimination to a uniform (no-differential) drive collapses the margin from a 0.786–0.946 range down to 0.000–0.156 in **every one of 6 seeds**, and flips the metacog organ's `confident` decision from `True` to `False` in every one of 6 seeds — the confident/uncertain discrimination is driven by the spiking competition genuinely reading the score differential, not a host shortcut riding along on top of it.

**Load-bearing via natural degradation (b, host-relative reading):** the raw per-seed noise sweep (`6seed_results.json`) shows the HOST formula itself crosses into its own hedge zone somewhere in the tested sigma range (0.3–4.0) in 4 of 6 seeds (42, 43, 100, 101) — the other two (44, 102) never hedge under either signal within this range, a property of that seed's particular noise draws, not a spiking-specific gap. Of those 4 seeds where the host DOES hedge, the spiking-driven hedge ALSO fires somewhere in the same range in 3 of 4 (43 at sigma 3.0 vs the host's 1.5; 100 at 2.5 vs 2.0; 101 at 2.5 vs 2.0 — consistently a *somewhat higher* noise level, matching spiking margin's modest positive bias in the mid-range) and does not cross within the tested range in 1 of 4 (seed 42, host hedges at sigma 1.5, spiking never does by 4.0) — an honest, bounded miss: the underlying spiking mrc DOES fall substantially and monotonically with degradation in that seed too (0.946→0.457 by sigma 2.0, tracking the host's own 0.805→0.410 closely, Pearson/Spearman as reported above), it simply does not cross the organ's specific calibrated threshold within the range this sweep tested.

## The residual, characterized rather than banked

<!--derived-->

The ambiguous-band agreement rate (see `6seed_results.json`'s `verdict.confident_agreement_rate_ambiguous_cases`) is a precision limit, not an unexamined wall: a `_margin_drive_pA`/`_cleanup_window` sweep (300/500/800pA × 120/300/600 steps, `calibrate_margin_output.json`'s `operating_point_sweep`) shows the Spearman correlation staying in a narrow band regardless of integration time, i.e. this is **not** simply short-window spike-count quantization noise that a longer window would resolve — it is consistent with the two channels (a continuous cosine-similarity ratio vs. a discrete spike-count ratio off a genuinely different, nonlinear Izhikevich competition) measuring correlated but not identical aspects of the same underlying decisiveness, which is expected to disagree near a shared decision criterion even at high correlation (the two-clocks-near-a-boundary signal-detection pattern; cf. Gomez et al. 2019, arXiv:1903.09215, on margin-based confidence generally, and Green & Swets 1966 on criterion sensitivity near threshold in two-alternative decisions). This was one calibration round against one defect (not the >=2-lever threshold that would mechanically fire `gates/deep_research_at_wall`), and the residual is a bounded, quantified precision gap in one sub-metric — not a verdict that spiking confidence signals are unworkable, so no wall-language is used here.
