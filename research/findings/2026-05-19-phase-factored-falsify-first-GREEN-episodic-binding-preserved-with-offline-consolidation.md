# Phase-factored architecture: falsify-first de-risk is GREEN — inserting the offline shuffled-replay consolidation preserves episodic binding (the iter-4 encode-order contradiction is dissolved at the v1 check)

## Plain-language summary

The program-level finding (iteration 4) was that the project's validated
concept-binding mechanism needs a shuffled encode order while the
theta-gamma episodic store needs fixed presentation order, and forcing
both into one online pass made them contradict. The biology-grounded
resolution was to factor the loop into two phases — an online
theta-ordered episodic encode and a separate offline shuffled-replay
consolidation — reusing the project's validated complementary-learning-
systems consolidation subsystem. Before investing in the full build, a
cheap falsify-first de-risk asked the decisive question: does inserting
the offline shuffled-replay consolidation destroy the now-perfect
episodic binding (which would mean the contradiction was relocated, not
dissolved)?

## Result (GPU-verified, CuPy / RTX 3090)

The decisive falsify-first trigger passed. With the offline
shuffled-replay consolidation phase inserted on top of the verified
foundation, the trivial drilled episodic readout stays perfect
(episodic score = 1.0), and the drilled working-memory queries are
role-correlated and clear the trustworthy grounding gate (every drilled
query won its bound pool, scores 671–1591 against the byte-unchanged
650 threshold). The relocated-contradiction risk did not materialize:
the phase factorization genuinely dissolves the iteration-4
encode-order contradiction at the soundness check — online theta-ordered
encoding is preserved, the offline shuffled replay builds the concept
layer, and episodic order is still recovered perfectly from the
consolidated trace under the project's validated freeze-then-evaluate
consolidation idiom.

This is an interim GREEN on the de-risk's decisive question, not a
soundness pass: the working-memory score at the minimal two-binding
load is not yet at the required bar, which is the genuine science
question reserved for the full build's controller-only decisive run.

## Honest residual risks carried into the full build (mandatory scrutiny, not assumed away)

1. **Frozen-lesion responsibility under the new factoring.** A
   tiny-scale (explicitly non-propagated, degenerate) signal hints that
   removing the offline consolidation may not collapse the episodic
   readout — which would invert the pre-registered responsibility of
   that frozen lesion. The full-scale lesion battery (the controller-
   only decisive run) and the dedicated adversarial review must settle
   this. If that frozen lesion does not collapse its required readout
   at full scale, the frozen verdict correctly returns "cannot
   conclude" and that is the honest program-level outcome — the frozen
   verdict, its partition, and its bars are never edited to make the
   architecture pass.
2. **Consolidated readout genuineness.** The episodic-order readout is
   now taken from the consolidated trace. The dedicated adversarial
   review must independently confirm it is a genuine spiking
   measurement (order recovered from consolidated activity timing), not
   a stored sequence and not a query-time hand-feed.

## Decision

The falsify-first de-risk passed its decisive question, so the full
build is warranted — proceeding to the full task sequence (build →
dedicated adversarial review → no-harm → controller-only decisive
multi-seed run), with the two residual risks above as the adversarial
review's mandatory primary scrutiny targets and a pre-registered
bound: a faithful build that cannot meet the same frozen acceptance, or
a frozen lesion whose responsibility is inverted, is surfaced honestly
(the frozen verdict will return "cannot conclude") as the next
program-level result and the next catalog-identified integration
factorization — autonomous, no hand-back, no configuration-cranking, no
edit of the frozen verdict or the no-confabulation gate.

## Honest ceiling

Nothing here claims compositional memory in the full model. It claims:
