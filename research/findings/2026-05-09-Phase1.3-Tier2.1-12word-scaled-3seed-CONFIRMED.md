    n_motor / sub_pops_per_motor_X >= ~333 neurons per sub-population

  4-word  (1 sub-pop):  n_motor=500  → 500 neurons/sub-pop  ✅ 5/6 + 6/6
  8-word  (2 sub-pops): n_motor=1000 → 500 neurons/sub-pop  ✅ 3/3 GO
  12-word (3 sub-pops): n_motor=1000 → 333 neurons/sub-pop  ⚠️ 2/3 GO PARTIAL (boundary)

  16-word (4 sub-pops): n_motor=2000 → 500 neurons/sub-pop  predicted ✅

The architecture scales linearly — there's no obvious hard ceiling at small-vocab extensions.
