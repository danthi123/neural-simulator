## MOVE 1 — ISOLATE + QUANTIFY THE TRUE RESIDUAL (which bytes limit the producer to 3?)

I read the full producer chain end-to-end: `_emerge65_self_organized_producer_derisk.py` (the capstone `SelfOrganizedProducer`), `_emerge64_mine_slot_inventory_derisk.py` (S1a slot-inventory mining), `_emerge63_corpus_taught_slot_order_derisk.py` (S1b order), `_emerge62_discover_function_words_derisk.py` (S2 function words + the corpus stream), `_emerge59_spiking_broca_frame_slots_derisk.py` (FRAMES + `FrameSlotCQ` + `decision_from_emerge` + the spiking render), plus the broader reuse candidates (`argstructure_composer.py`, `_bucketB_corpus_mined_frames_derisk.py`, `_fluidconv_chat_repl.py`).

### What is ALREADY general (NOT the residual) — the load-bearing finding

The mining is **construction-agnostic by design**. `_emerge64:197-209 _slot_signature` keys a construction by its *ordered role-type sequence* (with FUNC/DET payloads + verb inflection), and `_emerge64:262-298 mine_inventory` groups exemplars by that signature and takes the dominant ordering. **The "3 frames" are just what the EMERGE corpus attests** — the mining discovers the inventory of *any* construction whose signature appears. `_emerge64:147-194 label_sentence` already handles any single-clause `det (func)* SUBJ (func)* VERB` construction; it *skips* multi-content-word constructions (an object/PP after the verb) only because the current label rule requires the clause-final content word to be the verb (`_emerge64:170-174`, "any content word strictly between subject and verb → skip"). That is a **corpus-domain restriction, not a mechanism wall** (it is the same clean-single-clause discipline `_bucketB` uses).

- **The spiking order production is fully general** (`_emerge59:262-288 FrameSlotCQ.emit`): `N_SLOT_POOLS=6` (`_emerge59:118`) already holds up to 6 ordered slots, and the per-pool spiking-rate ranking IS the emission order for *any* slot count. `MAX_SLOTS` is computed from the frames (`_emerge59:107`); more/longer constructions just use more of the 6 pools.
- **The A→W spell is vocab-general** (`_emerge59:138-150 realize_slot`): every slot — FUNC and CONTENT — is spelled by the same pluggable A→W callback (`concept_speak_demo`, 100% multi-seed). Function words are just more lemmas.
- **S2 function-word discovery is general** (`_emerge62:189+ compute_stats` + `discover_closed_class`): frequency + coverage over the stream; adding constructions that introduce new closed-class words (prepositions `to`/`on`/`in`, copula `is`, conjunction `and`) needs *no change* — those words are ALREADY in the stream and ALREADY discovered (`_emerge62:130 GROUND_TRUTH_CLOSED = {the,a,can,does,not,to,on,in,and,is,it}`).

### The GENUINE residual — pinned to exact bytes (small, and mostly DATA)

**The corpus already attests ~10 constructions; the producer renders 3.** `_emerge62:153-179 build_stream` generates TEN sentence templates — ability-affirm, negated-modal, intransitive-3sg (the rendered 3) **plus** PP-goal "the owl flies to the pond", PP-location "the owl flies on the rock", copular-locative "the owl is in the pond", conjunction "a big owl and a nest", existential/pronoun "it is a big owl", adj+ability "the big owl can fly", predicative-adjective "the owl is big" (the 7 attested-but-unrendered). So the *data for richer constructions already exists in the very stream the producer mines* — but they never reach render. **Why?** Four hard seams, in decreasing size:

1. **(R-route, the ONE real mechanism residual) The frame-SELECTION routing is hard-keyed to the 3 EMERGE frames.**
   - `_emerge64:325-336 match_inventory_to_frames` maps mined signatures ONLY against `by_sig = {_frame_signature(fr): fr for fr in FRAME_NAMES}` (`:328`) — a mined construction whose signature isn't one of the 3 ground-truth frames is **silently discarded** (`mined_slots` only populated `for fr in FRAME_NAMES`, `_emerge65:208-212`). The producer *mines* the extra constructions and *throws them away*.
   - `_emerge59:316-329 decision_from_emerge` emits ONLY `F_MODAL` / `F_INTR` / `F_NEGMOD` (three `if` branches on `polarity`/`negated_modal`). There is no route from a reasoner decision to a PP-goal or copular or conjunction frame.
   - **This is the genuine mechanism residual: a general construction-SELECTOR (message → construction id) replacing the 3-way `decision_from_emerge`, and a general signature→frame registry replacing the `FRAME_NAMES`-keyed match.** It is ~2 small functions, not a new substrate.

2. **(R-corpus, DATA) The corpus's construction set is bounded to the domain templates** (`_emerge62:153-179`). Richer production = a richer corpus with more attested templates. The mining then discovers them near-free (Move 3). This is the *largest* lever and it is DATA, not mechanism.

3. **(R-label, a small mechanism extension) `label_sentence` skips post-verbal content** (`_emerge64:170-174`). To mine transitive/ditransitive/PP constructions (object or PP-goal *after* the verb), the label rule needs to admit a post-verbal CONTENT slot (a THEME/GOAL/OBJECT role) and its leading preposition — exactly what `argstructure_composer.FRAME_LEXICON` already encodes and `_bucketB` already mines. This is a bounded extension of one labeling function, with a strong existing precedent.

4. **(R-reasoner, DATA/small) The upstream reasoner emits only 3 decision types.** EMERGE-54's `answer` (`_emerge54:115-138`) returns inherit / per-dimension-cancel / abstain. Richer *speech acts* (a transitive assertion "the dog chases the cat", a locative "the owl is in the nest", a possessive "the owl has feathers") need the reasoner to emit more (subject, RELATION, object) tuples — but the fluid-conversation stack ALREADY produces exactly these (SVO facts, `who`/`what`/`describe`/`discuss`/`compare`, `_fluidconv_chat_repl.py:5-11`). So the richer decisions are already computed elsewhere; the work is *routing them into the self-organized producer*, not inventing them.

**Quantified residual:** of "broaden the construction inventory," **~70-80% is DATA** (a richer bounded corpus → the mining discovers more constructions, near-free — R-corpus + R-reasoner-content), **~15% is ONE mechanism seam** (generalize the 3-way construction router / signature registry — R-route), and **~10% is one bounded label extension** (admit post-verbal content in `label_sentence` — R-label, precedented by `_bucketB`/argstructure). None of it is the R4 open-prose wall. This mirrors every prior surpass round: the "3-frame limit" is a small host residual (a hard-coded router) sitting on a large already-general base (mining + order + spell + function-word discovery, all construction-agnostic).

**Am I testing the wrong hypothesis?** Guard checked. The tempting-but-wrong framing is "make the producer OPEN-ended" (which IS the R4 wall — a from-scratch spiking LM, ~4 orders too small, `2026-05-07-Phase-2.3a-NEGATIVE`). The RIGHT framing (Move 2) is **grow a BOUNDED-but-larger inventory of usage-based constructions**, discovered from a richer corpus by the already-general mining — the child's item-based → schematic-construction path, not "learn open grammar."

## MOVE 2 — REFRAME VIA REAL BIOLOGY / DEVELOPMENT (how does a child's construction inventory GROW?)

A child's construction inventory does not grow by adding rules; it grows by **abstracting more item-based constructions from more usage, then schematizing shared slots** — exactly the mining the project already has. Convergent, directly-reusable literatures:

**(A) Usage-based construction grammar — the inventory IS a structured set of constructions abstracted from frequent chunks (Tomasello, Goldberg, Bybee; already in memory `project_conversational_primary_robust_multicue_parser`).** A child abstracts "the X can Y", then "X V-s to the Y" (motion), then "X V-s the Y" (transitive) as *separate item-based constructions* that gradually schematize. Broadening the inventory = attesting more construction TYPES in usage and letting the learner abstract each — precisely `_emerge64`'s signature-grouping. Confirmed by current computational work: **Jon-And & Michaud (2024), "Usage-based Grammar Induction from Minimal Cognitive Principles"** (Computational Linguistics 50(4):1375) — a minimal chunk-memory learner identifies "frequent and informative multi-word chunks," reuses them, and "gives rise to grammatical abstractions at higher levels," navigating increasing combinatorial complexity by **reuse of frequent chunks** — the exact mechanism the project's dominant-ordering mine implements. This directly warrants "feed richer usage → the mining abstracts more constructions."

**(B) Dominey-Hinaut corticostriatal / reservoir grammatical-construction model — the canonical NEURAL precedent, and it GENERALIZES TO NEW CONSTRUCTIONS.** The fronto-striatal reservoir maps sentence FORM → meaning by reading thematic roles from **the ORDER and POSITION of the closed-class elements**, and — decisively for this direction — **"learns to associate sentence forms with semantic role labeling and can generalize to new constructions not present in the training set"** (Hinaut & Dominey, PLoS ONE 2013; "Corticostriatal response selection in sentence PRODUCTION," Brain & Language 2015). Their production model explicitly frames production as **selecting the appropriate construction to express an event's predicate + thematic roles from multiple perspectives** — which is EXACTLY the R-route residual (a construction-SELECTOR). This is the strongest biological validation that (a) a bounded but *open-to-new-constructions* inventory is the right target, and (b) the missing piece is the construction-selector, not a bigger substrate.

**(C) Broca as the grammatical-frame + closed-class furniture engine — catalog G.12 / H.19.** Catalog **G.12** (feature-catalog.md:2774-2784; Kandel 6e Ch 55 pp 1382-1384): Broca = "speech production + grammatical processing"; agrammatism selectively strips the closed class + grammatical morphology while sparing content nouns — the dissociation the EMERGE-59 function-word-ablation control already reproduces. **G.07 / H.19** (pre-SMA/SMA serial order, feature-catalog.md:2710, 3110): internally-generated sequences / competitive-queuing — the substrate for the per-construction slot ORDER (the FrameCQ engine, already 6/6 GO). Broadening the inventory adds *more frames to the same Broca engine*, biologically the correct move (one grammatical-encoding system, many constructions), not a new organ.

**(D) The project ALREADY has a richer construction inventory built on the SAME engine** — the strongest "reuse" evidence. `argstructure_composer.FRAME_LEXICON` (`:65-77`) encodes **transitive** (`_default`: agent-action-patient "the dog chases the cat"), **intransitive-motion** (go/come/walk/run: agent-action-GOAL-PP "the boy goes to the park"), **ditransitive** (give/send: agent-action-THEME-RECIPIENT "give the ball to the boy"), and **caused-motion** (put: agent-action-THEME-LOCATION "put the cup on the table") — each with its closed-class scaffold (`lead` prepositions/determiners) and its FrameCQ-ordered content slots (the SAME serial-order engine as the EMERGE producer). And `_bucketB_corpus_mined_frames_derisk.py` (GO) already **MINES these verb-frames from corpus argument co-occurrence** over the brain's vocab, with the permuted-mining anti-cheat. So a much richer construction inventory is *already discovered-from-corpus AND rendered* in the deployed conversational stack — just via the RFPhasorComposer/argstructure path, not yet unified with the fully-self-organized EMERGE producer. **"Broaden the EMERGE producer's inventory" is largely "route the argstructure/`_bucketB` construction inventory through the self-organized S1a/S1b/S2 pipeline + the spiking FrameCQ render."**

**Reframe verdict:** the child grows her inventory by abstracting MORE usage-based constructions from MORE usage (Jon-And-Michaud/Tomasello), selecting the right one at production (Dominey-Hinaut), all on ONE Broca serial-order engine (G.12/G.07). Every piece maps onto machinery the project has already validated (the signature-mine, FrameCQ, function-word discovery, the argstructure frame lexicon, the `_bucketB` frame miner). Broadening the inventory is **overwhelmingly "feed richer usage + generalize the construction router,"** NOT a new mechanism.

## MOVE 4 — VERDICT: surpassable-and-how-cheaply vs where the R4 wall actually begins

**SURPASSABLE, and the first step is almost pure DATA. This is NOT a wall.** The honest accounting:

- **The construction-count limit is surpassable NOW, EMERGE-72, near-free.** The mining/order/spell/function-word machinery is ALREADY construction-agnostic; the EMERGE-62 corpus ALREADY attests ~10 templates while the producer renders 3; the ONLY mechanism residual is de-hard-coding the 3-way router into a signature-keyed registry. Field-validated: Dominey-Hinaut reservoirs generalize to new constructions with exactly this closed-class-position mechanism; Jon-And-Michaud (2024) confirm the inventory grows by abstracting more frequent chunks from more usage.
- **Richer construction SHAPES (transitive / PP / ditransitive) are surpassable, EMERGE-73, precedented.** One `label_sentence` extension (post-verbal content) + the already-built `argstructure_composer.FRAME_LEXICON` + the already-GO `_bucketB` corpus frame miner. This unifies the project's two construction inventories onto the spiking FrameCQ render.
- **The richer speech-act decisions already exist** (the fluid-conversation stack) — EMERGE-74 routes them in.

**WHERE THE R4 OPEN-PROSE WALL ACTUALLY BEGINS (precisely — this is the honest boundary):**
The tractable side is **a BOUNDED inventory of usage-based constructions, each with a discoverable role-type signature attested in the corpus, selected at production by a construction-router (Dominey-Hinaut).** You can keep ADDING constructions (transitive, ditransitive, caused-motion, locative, possessive, comparative, relative-clause-lite, …) as long as each is (i) attested enough in the corpus to clear the dominance threshold, (ii) a clean labellable slot-structure, and (iii) selected by an upstream reasoner/parser decision. **The R4 wall begins exactly when production must go BEYOND a fixed, discoverable construction inventory** — i.e. (a) generating a NOVEL construction never attested in the corpus (unbounded compositional syntax: arbitrary recursion, long-range dependencies, center-embedding depth, discourse-level planning across many clauses), or (b) free open-domain generation where the message is not a role-filler decision the reasoner can hand off. That is the from-scratch spiking-LM regime (~4 orders too small, `2026-05-07-Phase-2.3a-NEGATIVE`; 2024-26 spiking LMs are off-substrate-backprop-trained + sub-scale). **Everything up to "a large but bounded, corpus-attested, router-selected construction inventory" is tractable and cheap; genuinely-open novel-construction prose is the deferred wall.** The direction should ride the tractable side as far as it goes (many constructions), and name the R4 boundary at exactly that line — not one construction sooner.

**The genuinely-irreducible parts (small, honest, NOT blockers):**
1. The token-surface / spaCy parse for corpus statistics is a legitimate host front-end (offline syllabus prep — like rendering a retinal image the neural retina reads; the accepted `_bucketB`/stream-cortex boundary). The DISCOVERED structure renders on real spikes.
2. A held-out construction's DISTINCTIVE slots (its own function words / inflection / internal order) are not recoverable from OTHER constructions alone — the same precisely-named residual as EMERGE-63/64/65; the next signal is one attestation of that construction's own furniture. Named, not gated, not a wall.

**THE EXACT NEXT CHEAP EXPERIMENT (EMERGE-72 candidate):** a CPU/numpy runner `_emerge72_broaden_construction_inventory_derisk.py` that (1) generalizes `match_inventory_to_frames` + `decision_from_emerge` into a signature-keyed construction REGISTRY (any dominance-clearing mined signature → a stable construction id + render route); (2) renders the ALREADY-attested-but-discarded `build_stream` constructions that keep the clean labellable shape (predicative-adjective "the owl is big", adj+ability "the big owl can fly", existential "it is a big owl") — ≥5 constructions total; (3) the PERMUTED-CORPUS + CROSS-CONSTRUCTION + HELD-OUT-CONSTRUCTION + NO-CORPUS anti-cheats collapse; (4) gate-first moat 0-productions-on-abstain; (5) 6 seeds. Reuse-by-import (`_emerge62` stream, `_emerge64` mine, `_emerge63` order, `_emerge65` pipeline, `_emerge59` render); NO `sim/` edit; moat untouched. GO ⇒ the self-organized producer scales from 3 to N≥5 constructions with ~zero new mechanism — the thesis validated. NEGATIVE (e.g. a new construction's signature collides / the dominance threshold mis-mines a longer construction) ⇒ the precise residual is named (the labeling / dominance-threshold tune, or R-label for post-verbal content) and becomes the next single-variable de-risk (EMERGE-73) — still not a wall.
