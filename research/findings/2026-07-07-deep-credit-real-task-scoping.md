## TL;DR (the 10-line summary)
1. **The instrument problem is the crux, not the mechanism.** The mechanism (`enable_bdsp` Burstprop + `enable_bdsp_microcircuit` clean-error/interneuron, + the KP learned-apical-feedback variant) is BUILT + adversarially validated on spikes; D2 rung-2 showed the 10-bit XOR toy has no accuracy headroom and a seed-noisy metric — a TOY limitation, not a wall.
2. **A "real task" must satisfy FIVE gates:** non-synthetic · **depth genuinely required** (a shallow/1-layer net provably underfits) · feedforward · cheap + infra-reusing · as goal-relevant as possible.
3. **THE load-bearing trap (from the 2026-05-18 VOID, must not be re-hit):** on MNIST-class tasks a **wrong-sign (inverted) hidden rule still reaches ~0.95 held-out** because a correctly-trained linear READOUT over rich hidden features rescues accuracy regardless of hidden-credit correctness. ⇒ **held-out accuracy is NOT a valid deep-credit instrument.** The valid metric is **per-layer credit-ALIGNMENT vs the fenced backprop oracle** (already the D1/D2 metric) with wrong-sign/lesion genuinely failing that metric.
4. **The literature confirms the depth-genuineness filter:** MNIST / Fashion-MNIST / sklearn-digits do NOT require depth for MLPs (shallow ≈ deep ± 1-2pp; FA ≈ BP) → they FAIL gate #2. **CIFAR-10 FC** genuinely separates by depth (1 hidden 59% err → 2 hidden 52% → deeper better) AND is where random-feedback/FA visibly underperforms symmetric feedback (BurstCCN 38.99% vs 22.92%) — the canonical place deep credit is load-bearing.
5. **#1 RECOMMENDED task: a FIXED nonlinear feature front-end (the project's real Gabor/V1 bank) on real images (CIFAR-10, 2–3 classes), then a depth-required fully-connected deep-credit classifier** — "Gabor-CIFAR-k." Non-synthetic, depth-required at the FC stage, feedforward, cheap (numpy rate-reference first, on-bridge after), reuses `sim/visual_cortex.py` + `_emerge34`'s `encode_v1` + the CIFAR cache already in the repo + the `DendriticMLP`/`_gnw_d1` nets.
6. **Why the front-end is legitimate, not a cheat:** V1/Gabor is a FIXED sensory transform (the "environment/body" boundary in CLAUDE.md), exactly what the field uses (random/fixed conv front-end + FC head) to isolate the FC depth-credit question without the conv-FA trainability boundary the 2026-05-18 conv gate already hit.
7. **The baseline that isolates "depth-credit is load-bearing":** a 1-hidden-layer control that UNDERFITS the same features (the depth-genuineness control, à la D2 rung-1's H6 probe) + per-layer alignment + wrong-sign/lesion/permuted/no-transport anti-cheats. GO = the deep net's deep-layer alignment tracks the oracle AND beats plain-FA AND wrong-sign fails ALIGNMENT (not just accuracy).
8. **#2 (goal-relevant sibling): real word-category classification from the EMERGE PPMI/co-occurrence codes** — supervised POS/semantic-category readout with a depth-required nonlinear target — more toward-language, but the EMERGE infra is *unsupervised + 2-hop-pointer*, so this needs the most new plumbing and a constructed depth-required label; ranked below Gabor-CIFAR as an instrument.
9. **#3: a real-data COMPOSITIONAL parity task (feature-XOR over real V1 features)** — guarantees depth by construction on real inputs; cleanest depth-genuineness but least "capability-meaningful."
10. **Verdict: build Gabor-CIFAR-k (rate-numpy reference FIRST, reusing `DendriticMLP` + `encode_v1` + the CIFAR cache), the depth-genuineness 1-layer control as the load-bearing baseline, per-layer alignment as the gated metric.** It is the cheapest instrument that is simultaneously real, depth-required, and instrument-valid — and it sidesteps BOTH prior boundaries (the XOR no-headroom AND the MNIST readout-rescue).

## PART 1 — DIAGNOSIS: what "a real task" must satisfy (five gates), and the two prior traps it must clear

Two established project boundaries define the problem. A candidate task is only viable if it clears BOTH.

- **Trap A — no accuracy headroom (the XOR toy, D2 rung-2).** A 10-bit Boolean function does not representationally separate depth-2 from depth-3 at practical width (Eldan-Shamir depth-separation needs exponential width). Both the depth-2 and depth-3 oracle fit; the KP alignment "lift" was strong on dev seeds 42/43 but null-to-negative on blind 100/101/102 (6-seed +0.067 ± 0.160, 2/6). ⇒ the toy cannot show a depth FIX robustly.
- **Trap B — the readout-rescue confound (the 2026-05-18 MNIST VOID, the STRONGEST prior triangulation).** On real MNIST with a 4-hidden-layer MLP + a verified-sound backprop positive control (oracle 0.977), the **WRONG-SIGN dendritic rule still reached 0.949/0.961/0.964 held-out**, while `local_correct` was wildly seed-variable (0.65/0.95/0.20). A correctly-trained output layer rescues generalization *regardless of the hidden rule's sign/correctness* → **held-out accuracy does not test whether deep credit is load-bearing.** The gate correctly returned VOID. The 2026-05-18 conv escalation then hit a second boundary: a sigmoid-faithful conv instrument is not cheaply trainable (oracle at chance) — so *raw-pixel conv* is off the cheap table.

**⇒ The five gates a real task must satisfy:**
1. **Non-synthetic** — real sensory/linguistic data (not a hand-built Boolean function).
2. **Depth genuinely required** — a 1-hidden-layer net over the SAME inputs provably UNDERFITS (measured, à la D2 rung-1's H6 depth-genuineness probe), so the deep layers are doing necessary work.
3. **Feedforward** — no credit-through-time (recurrent = the separate unsolved D3 frontier).
4. **Instrument-valid under Trap B** — the load-bearing metric is **per-layer credit-alignment vs the fenced oracle**, NOT held-out accuracy, and wrong-sign/lesion must fail THAT metric. (Accuracy is reported as a secondary sanity number, never the GO.)
5. **Cheap + infra-reusing + goal-relevant** — numpy rate-reference first, then on-bridge; reuse existing nets/data; bias toward the language/perception north-star.

**Key consequence (settles the MNIST question definitively):** MNIST/Fashion-MNIST/sklearn-digits FAIL gate #2 (shallow ≈ deep for MLPs; the literature is unanimous) AND FAIL gate #4 (they are exactly where Trap B was demonstrated). The field uses MNIST as a "does the rule run on real data at all" smoke, but it is NOT a valid deep-credit instrument. **Do not gate on MNIST accuracy.** (An MNIST *alignment* smoke is fine as a cheap first "the rule runs on real 784-dim data" check, but the GO must be the depth-separating task.)

## PART 2 — RANKED candidate real tasks

Ranked by (real ∧ depth-required ∧ instrument-valid ∧ cheap ∧ goal-relevant), biased to the cheapest that clears all five gates.

### ★ #1 — Gabor-CIFAR-k: fixed V1/Gabor front-end on real CIFAR-10 images → depth-required FC deep-credit classifier
- **What:** take real CIFAR-10 images (already cached in the repo, `research/findings/raw/_download_cifar.py` / `_realobject_cifar.json`), pass them through the project's **FIXED Gabor/V1 front end** (`sim/visual_cortex.build_v1_simple_weights`, reused exactly as `_emerge34` does via `encode_v1` / `build_gabor_response_matrix`), and train a **fully-connected deep net** (the `DendriticMLP` rate reference, then the `_gnw_d1` spiking BDSP/microcircuit net) to classify k=2–3 CIFAR classes from the V1 feature vector.
- **Why depth is genuinely required (gate #2):** CIFAR-10 FC classification measurably separates by depth — 1 hidden layer ≈ 59% error, 2 hidden ≈ 52%, deeper better (ICLR-2016 all-MLP study; corroborated across the FC-on-CIFAR literature). Over FIXED Gabor features (no conv learning) the *nonlinear class boundaries still need multiple layers* to reach the ceiling, and a 1-hidden control underfits — the depth-genuineness control is measurable, unlike MNIST. This is precisely where random-feedback/FA underperforms symmetric feedback (BurstCCN CIFAR: FA-regime 38.99% vs symmetric 22.92% vs ANN 36.30% → the FA gap is real and large on CIFAR, ~0 on MNIST).
- **Why the front-end is legitimate (not a cheat):** the Gabor/V1 transform is a FIXED sensory front-end — the "environment/body renders the retinal input" boundary in CLAUDE.md (host-legit for sensation). This is exactly the field-standard rig for isolating the FC deep-credit question (fixed/random conv or feature front-end + trainable FC head) — and it deliberately **sidesteps the 2026-05-18 conv-FA trainability boundary** (which was about training the *conv* stack under sigmoid-faithfulness; here the conv/Gabor stage is FIXED, only the FC head learns).
- **How the mechanism attaches:** unchanged from D1/D2. The V1 feature vector is the input layer; `[N_v1 → H → H → k]` sigmoid (rate) then two-compartment spiking; the clean-error microcircuit + KP learned-`Y` + per-layer homeostasis are the exact D2-gate arms; the fenced backprop oracle is the ceiling + the per-layer alignment reference.
- **Infra reused (minimal new plumbing):** `sim/visual_cortex.py` (V1 bank) · `_emerge34_perception_grounded_emergence_derisk.py` (`build_gabor_response_matrix`, `encode_v1`, `build_shape_set`) · the CIFAR cache + `_download_cifar.py` loader · `sim/dendritic_mlp.DendriticMLP` (rate net, committed Urbanczik-Senn rule + fixed-random feedback + hand-derived oracle) · `research/runners/dendritic_fair_gate.py` (kill-safe multi-seed loop + real-image cache-verify + the 5-condition control harness, incl. wrong-sign/permuted/global-scalar already wired) · `research/runners/_gnw_d1_spiking_bdsp_derisk.py` (the spiking BDSP/microcircuit/FANet nets + per-layer alignment metric + KP `_kp_update`). Net-new: a small `make_task_gabor_cifar` (load CIFAR-k → V1-encode → held-out split) and pointing the existing nets at it.
- **Cheapest-first scale:** (a) rate-numpy reference — `DendriticMLP` on V1-CIFAR-2, confirm oracle ≥ 0.80 AND the 1-hidden control underfits (depth-genuineness), then plain-FA-vs-microcircuit-vs-KP per-layer alignment; (b) on-bridge spiking depth-3 (GPU, multi-seed) — the decisive arm.
- **Goal-relevance:** MEDIUM-HIGH. It is the perception half of the north-star (a brain that SEES real objects and learns hierarchical visual categories with a biologically-plausible deep rule) and directly continues the EMERGE-34 "learn what a category is by looking" line — but with the deep-credit LEARNED classifier replacing the fixed pooler.
- **Risk:** the 2-class V1-CIFAR task might be *too easy* (1-layer nearly matches) → mitigate by k=3–4 classes and/or narrow H (the D2 rung-1 H6 lesson: pick the width where the 1-layer oracle underfits but the deep oracle fits). If even k=4 doesn't separate at feasible width, escalate to harder class subsets (cat/dog/deer/horse — the semantically-confusable animal classes) before abandoning.

### #2 — Real word-category classification from EMERGE PPMI / co-occurrence codes (the goal-relevant sibling)
- **What:** use the EMERGE stream cortex's REAL-corpus co-occurrence/PPMI feature codes (the `_emerge30/32/34` co-occurrence machinery; real corpus words) as inputs to a SUPERVISED deep-credit classifier predicting a nonlinear word-category label (e.g. POS class, or a semantic category that is NOT linearly separable in PPMI space).
- **Why depth could be required:** semantic/POS categories over raw co-occurrence codes are plausibly non-linearly separable (why PPMI+MLP is used) — but this must be MEASURED (a 1-layer control), and it is a real risk that a linear readout suffices (making it fail gate #2, exactly like MNIST).
- **Goal-relevance:** HIGH (directly toward the learned language cortex).
- **Why ranked BELOW #1:** (a) the EMERGE infra is UNSUPERVISED + 2-hop-pointer (HTM), NOT a supervised feedforward deep net — so this needs the MOST new plumbing (build real feature vectors + a depth-required supervised label + wire to `DendriticMLP`); (b) depth-genuineness is UNPROVEN for word-category-from-PPMI and could collapse to a linear-readout task (Trap B / gate #2 failure); (c) no existing real-corpus→feature-matrix→label pipeline exists to reuse cheaply. **Do #1 first (proven depth-separation, proven infra); promote #2 once the instrument is validated on Gabor-CIFAR.**

### #3 — Real-data compositional parity: feature-XOR over real V1 features
- **What:** label = parity/XOR of two learned-threshold predicates over real V1-CIFAR features (a compositional target that provably needs ≥2 nonlinear layers, but computed over REAL inputs, not synthetic bits).
- **Pro:** GUARANTEES depth by construction (the cleanest gate #2 pass) on non-synthetic inputs; a 1-layer net provably can't represent it.
- **Con:** it is a semi-synthetic LABEL on real features (weaker on gate #1 "non-synthetic" than #1/#2), and least capability-meaningful (a constructed parity, not a real category). **Use as a depth-genuineness CONTROL arm inside the #1 build (proves the harness detects depth when depth is truly needed), not as the headline task.**

### Rejected: raw-pixel CIFAR conv (the 2026-05-18 boundary), MNIST/Fashion-MNIST/digits accuracy (Trap B + no depth), any recurrent/sequence task (D3 frontier, not feedforward).

### Net shape + how the rule attaches
- Input: V1-encoded CIFAR-k feature vector (top-T active cells; `N_v1` ≈ the Gabor bank size). Net `[N_v1 → H → H → k]` (depth-3 hidden as in D2). Rate reference (`DendriticMLP`) first; then the two-compartment spiking net (`_gnw_d1` with `enable_bdsp_microcircuit`).
- The clean-error microcircuit credit + KP learned-apical-`Y` + per-layer homeostatic gain are the D2-gate arms attached unchanged; the fenced backprop oracle provides the ceiling + the per-layer alignment reference; the fixed-random apical feedback stays a runner-side `RegionPathway(plastic=False)` (no transport).

### The load-bearing baseline (isolates "deep credit is load-bearing", defeats Trap B)
- **Depth-genuineness control (the primary baseline):** a **1-hidden-layer** net over the SAME V1 features. GATE PRECONDITION: it must UNDERFIT (held-out clearly below the depth-3 oracle) — else the task doesn't need depth and is INCONCLUSIVE (escalate k / narrow H, per D2 rung-1's H6 lesson). This is the direct analogue of the D2 depth-genuineness probe, now on real data.
- **The gated metric is PER-LAYER ALIGNMENT, not accuracy** (defeats Trap B): cos(the rule's per-layer update, the fenced oracle-BP update), reported per layer, with the DEEPEST-from-output layer the load-bearing one. Accuracy is a secondary sanity number only.

### Anti-cheats (reuse D1/D2's seven + the three depth-specific)
Leakage-free held-out split (tested images never in train) · like-for-like (same seeds/batch/epochs/lr across arms; report the batch sweep — the D1 lesson) · oracle-ceiling ≥ 0.80 (else INCONCLUSIVE) · **wrong-sign apical must fail the ALIGNMENT metric** (NOT just accuracy — the explicit Trap-B defeat: on MNIST wrong-sign passed accuracy; here it must fail alignment) · apical-lesion → alignment floor · permuted-error → chance/zero alignment · no-teaching null → hidden-drift ~0 · single-layer floor · **no-weight-transport probe on the learned `Y`** (KP `Y`-update never reads `W`/`Wᵀ`) · **depth-causality** (the fix helps the deepest layer's alignment MORE than a shallow layer's; the depth-3 vs depth-2 delta).

### Cheapest-first ladder (single-variable, gate each rung)
1. **Rate-numpy reference (hours, CPU):** `DendriticMLP` on V1-CIFAR-2/3. GATE: oracle ≥ 0.80 AND 1-hidden control underfits (depth-genuineness) AND (microcircuit/KP deepest-layer alignment > plain-FA) AND wrong-sign fails alignment. (A quick V1-MNIST alignment smoke first is fine — "the rule runs on real 784→V1 data" — but NOT the GO.)
2. **On-bridge spiking depth-3 (decisive, GPU, multi-seed 6-seed):** the two-compartment microcircuit + plastic apical `Y` + per-layer homeostasis, credit through 3 spiking layers on real V1-CIFAR features; per-layer on-bridge alignment; the interneuron-cancellation on-vs-off ablation at depth. GATE (pre-registered): deepest-layer alignment tracks oracle AND > plain-FA + 0.10 AND wrong-sign fails alignment AND all anti-cheats hold; accuracy reported secondary.

### Pre-registered GO
**GO** = on real V1-CIFAR-k, at a width where the 1-hidden control underfits and the depth-3 oracle fits (≥ 0.80): the microcircuit/KP rule's **deepest-from-output per-layer credit-alignment tracks the fenced oracle and exceeds plain-FA by ≥ 0.10**, the **wrong-sign control FAILS the alignment metric** (the explicit Trap-B defeat), and every anti-cheat collapses — 6-seed on the spiking bridge. Accuracy is a secondary sanity report, never the gate. A boundary (alignment does not lift, or wrong-sign passes alignment) is diagnostic and localizes the residual — not a failure.

## PART 4 — The honest risk
1. **Depth-genuineness on V1-CIFAR-k is the #1 risk** — if 1-hidden nearly matches depth-3 over Gabor features at feasible width, the task fails gate #2 (the MNIST fate). Mitigation is pre-registered: measure the 1-layer control FIRST; escalate k / narrow H / harder confusable-class subsets before proceeding. The literature says CIFAR FC DOES separate by depth, so the prior is favorable, but it must be measured, not assumed.
2. **The multi-order SCALE wall stands behind any GO** (the standing flag) — a depth-3-on-2-CIFAR-classes GO is still orders below language-scale generation; this is a MECHANISM de-risk (does the rule do real deep credit on real data), NOT a capability/scale claim. No expensive training is authorized by this scoping.
3. **Trap B is subtle and could re-bite** — if the k-class readout is powerful enough to rescue accuracy even here, the alignment metric (not accuracy) is what protects us; the plan already gates on alignment + a wrong-sign-fails-alignment control specifically for this. If wrong-sign ALSO passes alignment, that is the honest boundary (the instrument still can't isolate hidden-credit at this scale) → escalate depth/task per the D2 gate, do not spin.
4. **Goal-relevance is medium, not maximal** — Gabor-CIFAR is the perception half of the north-star, not language. The most language-relevant instrument (#2, EMERGE word-category) is deferred because its depth-genuineness is unproven and its infra is unsupervised/pointer-based; it becomes the natural follow-on once the instrument is validated on the proven-depth-separating Gabor-CIFAR.
