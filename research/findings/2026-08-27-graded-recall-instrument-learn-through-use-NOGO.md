## Question
[[2026-08-27-conduction-delay-directional-replay-learn-through-use-PARTIAL]] SOLVED write-directionality (6/6, a 9ms forward-edge conduction delay separates the SWR volleys and makes dw_fwd > dw_rev on every seed) but the learn-through-use GO bar stayed at 1/6 because weak-cue `forward_frac` read ~1.0 BEFORE consolidation -- apparently no headroom. [[2026-08-27-braun-gapcoding-learn-through-use-NOGO]] then DECISIVELY diagnosed why, independently converging on the same fix: `forward_frac` is BINARY-ORDER -- "it only drops on ORDER ERRORS or reverse intrusions; a truncated forward run (A->B->C then stop) still scores forward" -- so it reads ~1.0 at any non-degenerate op-point regardless of how deep the completion actually runs. Two independent analyses named the same next step: fix the INSTRUMENT (a graded readout decoupled from the volley-separation op-point), then re-ask the decisive question. Does the already-directional write produce a recall GAIN once the read has real headroom?

## Result -- the instrument IS graded (verified first); the decisive re-test is a genuine 0/6 NO-GO, not the metric artifact
<!--derived-->
**Instrument validation (pre-registered, seed 42, known-good STDP-encoded store, at the DECISIVE swr_period=650 -- no easier op-point was picked).** A cue-strength sweep (cue_pa x {1.0, 0.85, 0.7, 0.5, 0.35, 0.2}) shows `depth_frac` reading 0.709, 0.655, 0.655, 0.583, 0.510, 0.000 -- a real 0.709-wide gradient with 5/6 levels strictly intermediate (0.05-0.95) -- while the LEGACY `forward_frac` reads EXACTLY 1.000 at every one of the five non-degenerate levels (n_multi 9,8,8,6,4) and only drops when the cue is so weak nothing multi-assembly ignites at all (mult=0.2, n_multi=0, both metrics collapse together). This is the artifact made visible directly: over the entire range where the old instrument is even defined, it cannot distinguish a 9-event clean full completion from a 4-event, more-truncated one -- both read 1.000 -- while `depth_frac` tracks the degradation smoothly. `tau` (the pairwise-concordance cross-check) reads 1.000 throughout this sweep because cue attenuation here reduces HOW MANY assemblies ignite, not their relative ORDER once ignited -- confirming DEPTH and TAU catch different failure modes (truncation vs. scrambling) exactly as designed, and that this particular degradation mode is truncation, which only DEPTH is built to see.
**Decisive 6-seed test (identical established write, new read).** dw_fwd 203.6 vs dw_rev 170.9, DIRECTIONAL on 6/6 seeds (matches the PARTIAL). Weak-cue depth_frac_before 0.586 (mean) -- well under the 0.90 headroom ceiling on 6/6 seeds, confirming the read is NOT pinned at ceiling this time. But depth_frac FALLS after consolidation: 0.586->0.497 (mean), and 5/6 seeds individually DECREASE (42: 0.442->0.491 marginal +0.049, just under the 0.05 gain bar; 43: 0.672->0.526; 44: 0.673->0.532; 100: 0.563->0.450; 101: 0.631->0.515; 102: 0.537->0.468). Weak-cue tau also falls (1.000->0.762 mean). 0/6 GO. LESION-THE-REPLAY (NO-SEED): dw_fwd_noseed=0.000 on ALL 6 seeds (exact) and weak_depth_frac_noseed is BYTE-IDENTICAL to weak_depth_frac_before on every seed (zero weight change -> zero read change) -- `attributable_to` computes the seeded depth_frac change is 100.0% attributable to the replay itself, 0% present in the no-replay control. The negative is CLEAN, not a dead lever.

## Why -- net-directional in WEIGHT SPACE does not guarantee a recall GAIN in READ SPACE
<!--derived-->
The write is directional (dw_fwd > dw_rev, confirmed 6/6) but the ABSOLUTE reverse deepening is still large (mean dw_rev 170.9, not near zero) -- pure potentiation on both arms (`fused_btsp_update` has no depression term), so the reverse edges still grow, just slower than forward. Under a degraded cue, that residual reverse growth is now large enough to intrude on the completion path and terminate the forward prefix EARLIER than before training, which is exactly what `depth_frac` (and the legacy forward_frac's own after-value of 0.807, itself now visibly below 1.000 for the first time in this arc) shows. The PARTIAL's "no headroom" reading concealed this: forward_frac could not fall below 1.000 in the earlier regime because it never distinguished a partial derailment from a clean run, so the very recall COST of the residual reverse band was invisible to the old instrument. The graded instrument recovers it directly.

NO-GO -- but a converted one: the recall-instrument fix that two independent analyses named DID remove the ceiling artifact (instrument verified graded, headroom held 6/6), and the resulting NO-GO is now a substrate-level negative about the WRITE, not an instrument failure. Residual, precisely isolated: pure-potentiation BTSP leaves a residual reverse deepening (dw_rev ~84% of dw_fwd) that is small enough to keep the write net-forward in WEIGHT space but large enough to DEGRADE weak-cue completion DEPTH in READ space -- net-directional weights are not sufficient for a recall gain when the losing arm still grows.
Named next mechanism: an explicit DEPRESSION/erasure arm on the reverse edges -- heterosynaptic depression / competitive synaptic normalization (biology runs a homeostatic competitive process ALONGSIDE potentiation that a pure-potentiation BTSP write omits; candidates: heterosynaptic LTD co-occurring with BTSP plateaus, or multiplicative weight normalization across the between-assembly band) so the reverse band is actively suppressed rather than merely out-grown by the forward band.
