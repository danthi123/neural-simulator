## Why this rung exists (the workflow's caught confound)
The naive "does NP learn W_in on a reservoir" is a **static 2-layer problem in a recurrent costume**: with zero-input fillers, W_in enters at exactly ONE timestep, so a GO certifies only feedforward input-credit (already validated by emerge1). The redesign (**order-gated delayed next-class**: gate cue P@pos0 → input-bearing fillers → content cue Q@pos d+1 → blank read; `target = class(Q)` if `class(P)%2==0` else `(class(Q)+1)%G`) forces BOTH axes — a learned input representation AND recurrent nonlinear binding — and the RUNG-0 controls (frozen / linear-reservoir / bag / recurrence-lesion) **arbitrate** rather than assume the credit.

- **Weight-perturbation of W_in is variance-limited** (NP ≈ frozen): perturbing ~4k weights is too noisy to accumulate learning at k=8, even though the anti-cheat STRUCTURE is right (shuffle ~ frozen, wrong_sign anti-learns) and headroom exists (oracle 0.72). NP's variance scales with the perturbed dimension — the emerge1 NP that worked had a ~240-dim layer, ~17× smaller.
- **Node perturbation** (perturb the `n` hidden pre-activations, dim `n×T`, project to W_in via the three-factor `outer(ξ_t, x_t)`) — the lower-variance + more biological form — **LEARNS W_in** (NP 0.396 > frozen 0.182, toward oracle 0.760): clearly better than weight-perturbation. BUT the shuffle anti-cheat does NOT cleanly collapse (0.322 > frozen 0.182): the ordering is NP > shuffle > frozen, so the GENUINE-credit margin (NP−shuffle = +0.074) is real-but-modest while a STRUCTURAL component (shuffle−frozen = +0.140) survives — node-perturbation-on-W_in aligns W_in with input directions even under mismatched credit. Honest status: the MECHANISM learns, but the clean-GO gate (shuffle collapses) is not yet met (3-seed; could be noise or a real structural effect).
