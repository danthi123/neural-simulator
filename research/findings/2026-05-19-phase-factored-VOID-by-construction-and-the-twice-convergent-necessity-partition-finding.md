## The honest deeper reading (twice-convergent; the load-bearing finding)

This is the second convergent structural refutation, and together with
the first they characterize the difficulty precisely:

1. Concept selectivity (the validated co-firing/topographic mechanism)
   requires a shuffled / interleaved encode order to break the
   winner-take-all attractor.
2. Episodic-sequence memory (the validated theta-gamma store) requires a
   fixed presentation order equal to the binding index to recover the
   sequence.
3. In a single online pass these are directly contradictory (the first
   program-level finding).
4. Separating them into an online order-preserving phase and an offline
   order-shuffling phase does not dissolve the contradiction — it
   relocates it: the offline shuffled replay needed for concept
   selectivity destroys the very episodic order the consolidated trace
   would have to carry.

Crucially, both the original design's wiring (episodic readout from the
online store) and the plan's refinement (episodic readout from the
consolidated trace) reach "cannot conclude" against the frozen verdict,
for opposite reasons: the first because the remove-consolidation lesion
then fails to collapse the episodic readout (episodic comes from the
online store), the second because consolidation destroys the order
(full episodic = 0). Under the pre-registered necessity partition — in
which removing consolidation must collapse episodic recall — no
biology-faithful phase-factored wiring can satisfy both that the full
loop preserves episodic order and that the remove-consolidation lesion
collapses it.

The disciplined conclusion: the evidence increasingly indicates that the
falsified element is the pre-registered necessity partition itself. That
partition encoded a specific hypothesis (formed for the original
single-pass integrated-loop conception) about which systems are
necessary for which readout. The biology-faithful architectures
realize a different causal necessity structure than the partition
assumes. The frozen verdict correctly refuses to certify them ("cannot
conclude") rather than be edited to fit — which is exactly its purpose.
A pre-registered necessity prediction that no faithful architecture
realizes is a falsified prediction; that is a legitimate, valuable
scientific result, not a local-implementation deficiency to keep
patching around.
