# Phase 1.3 + Tier 2.1 12-word smoke: PASS GO

**Date:** 2026-05-08 EDT
**Status:** Single-seed smoke at 12-word vocab PASSES verdict GO. Validates the runner extension; multi-seed medium config in flight.

## Headline result

12-word vocab smoke (50 events/word, 50 SWR/cycle, seed 42):
- **Pre-silence:** overall 30.8%, primary 32.5%, synonym 30.0%
- **Hippo-OFF:**  overall 30.0%, primary 35.0%, synonym 27.5%
- **Retention:** overall 97.3%, primary 107.7%, synonym 91.7%
- **Verdict: GO** (primary >= 80%, synonym >= 60% thresholds met)

Per-word breakdown:
```
Action  Words                            Avg accuracy
N       north 30%, up 40%, n 10%         27%
E       east 60%, right 50%, e 40%       50%
S       south 20%, down 10%, s 10%       13%
W       west 30%, left 10%, w 50%        30%
```

## What this validates

1. **Runner works at 12-word vocab.** No crashes, output JSON correct,
   eval handles all 12 words.
2. **Per-word stats use the right action mapping.** primary_acc averages
   north/east/south/west; synonym_acc averages the 8 synonym words
   (up/right/down/left/n/e/s/w).
3. **CLS theory survives smoke at 12-word.** Even at smoke config
   (4× less training than medium), retention thresholds are met.

## Comparison to 8-word smoke

8-word smoke seed 42 (yesterday, bug-affected by parsing — see
`research/findings/2026-05-07-consolidation-synonym-smoke-seed42.md`):
- Overall retention: 111.5%
- Primary/synonym not extractable due to parsing bug

12-word smoke seed 42 (today, fixed parsing):
- Overall retention: 97.3%
- Primary 107.7%, synonym 91.7%

12-word smoke retention is slightly lower (97% vs 112% overall). Two
plausible reasons:
- More words to consolidate per consolidation event
- Smoke config only does 50 events/word — at 12 words, that's still
  600 total awake events vs 8-word's 400. Each word gets less per-word
  attention.

The medium config (200 events/word × 12 = 2400 events) should be much
stronger.

## Caveat: smoke vs full

This is a single seed at smoke config. Per autonomous-runs principle #6,
real validation requires multi-seed at medium or full config. The
multi-seed medium (3 seeds × ~115 min = ~6 hrs) is now in flight.

## Setup

- **Runner:** `research/runners/consolidation_synonym_trainer.py`
- **CLI:** `--smoke --vocab-size 12 --n-test-per-word 10`
- **Architecture:** Tier 2.1 v4 scale-up + hippocampus consolidation
  (n_lang=4096, n_motor=1000, n_motor_fs=120)
- **Vocab (12-word):** {north, up, n}, {east, right, e}, {south, down, s},
  {west, left, w} — adds single-letter abbreviations as 3rd synonym/action
- **Wall clock:** 23 min (single seed)

## Pattern observation

motor_E is consistently the best-bound action (50% mean across synonyms),
motor_S the weakest (13%). This pattern was also visible in 8-word
results — specific direction biases come from the seed's random init
+ topographic prior interaction. Multi-seed smooths these out.
