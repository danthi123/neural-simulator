# Third convergent signal: the program's original pre-registered necessity hypothesis is falsified — the precise, catalog-grounded correction (with strong anti-goalpost-move safeguards)

Through a disciplined, pre-committed, three-times-convergent path the
program has falsified one specific claim in its own original
pre-registered necessity hypothesis and derived the biologically-correct
correction from the project's reference catalog. This was the exact
outcome a bound pre-committed in writing (before the result) specified.
The original frozen verdict is not edited; its "cannot conclude"

adversarially reviewed for goalpost-moving BEFORE any architecture is
exercised against it.

## The three convergent signals (each a faithful, GPU-verified, honestly-propagated negative)

1. Single online pass: the validated concept-binding mechanism needs a
   shuffled encode order; the validated theta-gamma episodic store needs
   a fixed order equal to the binding index. Directly contradictory.
2. Phase-factored (online theta-ordered encode, then offline
   shuffled-replay consolidation, both readouts ultimately from the
   consolidated trace): the shuffled offline replay required for concept
   selectivity destroys episodic order (full episodic = 0), while
   skipping consolidation preserves it. The original frozen partition's
   "removing consolidation must collapse episodic recall" duty inverts.
3. Distinct readout pathways (the biologically-correct division of
   labor — episodic order from the order-preserving hippocampal
   trisynaptic pattern-completion path; concept/working-memory from the
   order-invariant neocortical schema): the same frozen-partition
   refutation, reached from the opposite direction. Biologically,
   episodic-sequence order lives in the online trisynaptic store, so
   removing the consolidation/replay system collapses only concept and
   working memory and leaves episodic recall intact — the opposite of
   what the original frozen partition requires for that lesion.

Three faithful, biology-grounded architectures, three independent
directions, one convergent conclusion: the original partition's
assignment of the consolidation/replay lesion to the episodic-helper
role is a wrong prediction.

## The precise, catalog-grounded correction (derived from biology, not from what would pass)

Complementary-learning-systems consolidation builds the order-invariant
neocortical schema (McClelland 1995; Buzsaki 2013; the project's own
validated multi-seed strict-anti-cheat consolidation work). Episodic
sequence order is the hippocampal trisynaptic store's responsibility
(pattern separation and completion; the project's validated trisynaptic
loop). Therefore the consolidation/replay lesion belongs in the
working-memory / concept helper set, not the episodic helper set. This
is a single minimal partition correction, justified entirely by the
catalog's documented hippocampal-neocortical division of labor — not by
any observed run and not by what makes a candidate architecture pass.
