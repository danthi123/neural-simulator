# 2026-05-09 — Phase 1.5 v400 interference: under-training hypothesis REFUTED

**Status:** Single-seed test of `phase_1_5_interference_only_v400` (seed 42).
v200 baseline 0.340 → v400 0.345. Δ = +0.005. **REFUTED.**

## Result

| Run | events_per_word | seed | score | pass | min_word | mean_word |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1.5 seed 42 (v200) | 200 | 42 | 0.340 | ✗ | 0.08 (right) | 0.34 |
| v400 interference test     | 400 | 42 | 0.345 | ✗ | 0.16 (right) | 0.345 |
| **Δ** | +200 | — | **+0.005** | **no change** | +0.08 | +0.005 |

Per-word accuracy comparison (seed 42, both runs):

```
word    v200   v400   Δ
north   0.52   0.60   +0.08
up      0.28   0.32   +0.04
east    0.56   0.44   -0.12
right   0.08   0.16   +0.08    ← still below chance
south   0.40   0.40    0.00
down    0.24   0.20   -0.04
west    0.32   0.40   +0.08
left    0.32   0.24   -0.08
mean    0.34   0.345  +0.005
```

`right` stays catastrophically weak (16% < 25% chance). `north` lifts a
bit. `east`/`down`/`left` slightly DROP. Net mean change: +0.005, well
within seed-noise.

## Verdict

The 2026-05-09 hypothesis (interference fails because events_per_word=200
is below the validated Tier 2.1 dose of 400) is **FALSE**. Doubling the
event count produced essentially no change in the benchmark score or
the per-word accuracy distribution.

**The interference failure is architectural, not dose-bound.**

## What this DOES tell us

- 8-word interleaved training has a structural ceiling at this
  architecture (n_motor=1000, scaled arch). Per-word accuracy is bimodal
  — strong words (north, east, west at 40-60%) and weak words
  (right, down, left at 16-32%, with `right` consistently below chance).
- The bimodality is similar across both v200 and v400 — same words
  weak. Suggests the problem is which-word-competes-with-which, not
  total training dose.
- `right` consistently at chance or below across BOTH dose conditions
  suggests its drive pattern (sparse vector via vocab_to_drive_pattern)
  collides with another word's drive pattern, leading to per-word
  catastrophic forgetting under interleaved training.

## What this DOES NOT tell us

- Whether the long_tail rare-word failure is also architectural (not yet
  tested; `phase_1_5_long_tail_relaxed` preset pre-staged but not run).
- Whether a different architecture (e.g. n_motor=2000 — the same scale-up
  that fixed 12-word default→scaled) would lift interference.
- Whether the per-word collision pattern (right ⇄ ?) is reproducible
  across seeds (only seed 42 tested at v400 so far).

The first half of that math is now MOOT — interference does NOT lift at
v400. The Phase 1.5 milestone threshold cannot be reached by event-count

   — single-seed ~50 min. Tests the OTHER failing benchmark.
