### Bucket A — needed capability deferred / could-not-solve / relabelled-open

These are the honest gaps. Note that several were *claimed* solved and demoted here by the VERIFY pass:

- **Open-ended fluent generation / open prose (R4)** — the "~4 orders too small" wall. Confirmed field-wide (a from-scratch spiking LM, even a transformer, loses to a bigram at few-million-token scale). Supplied today ONLY by the ~21M TinyStories ANN scaffold. **This is the biggest one.**
- **The learned cortical binder replacing the fixed FHRR/VSA algebra** — multi-attribute BUNDLING from scratch is TESTED-NEGATIVE (`efc5e8b1`: "not just deferred"), additive 0.193 / learned-linear-inverse 0.056 ≈ chance vs the fixed ±1 self-inverse 0.989. Single-attribute learned bind is on-spikes GO; bundling is not. The deployed stack runs on the host-designed exact-inverse idealization.

Today these are two disjoint tracks (composer binds FLAT generated codes that cannot relate dog/cat; generalization rides a separate cross-modal + host-routed hybrid). - **R-ii counterfactual recombination** — named, never attempted.
- **The dendritic / deep-credit lever** meant to buy the structured learned binder — DEFERRED, then actively RETRACTED: the "feedforward deep-credit GO (0.877)" headline is ~80% a frozen random reservoir (control never run), and NOT-GO on seed-fixed data (unseeded-substrate confound, `2026-07-17`). ## 2. WHICH PATTERN DOMINATES

**Mostly B, with a genuine cluster of A — and one dominant A (open generation).** That is the honest headline, but "mostly B, one big A" undersells it slightly.

For the **core chat-turn cognition** — parse, store, recall, abstain, negate, inheritance, transitive/structured inference, bounded grammatical production — the pattern is decisively (B). This is the project's real strength, and it is not fake: multiple times a specific method failed, was banked with the failure documented, and the capability was recovered by a *different, controlled, multi-seed* mechanism, often redeeming a public retraction (transitive inference, hierarchical-320, concept-concept association). The adversarial-verification and 6-seed discipline are doing real work — several would-be GOs were caught as artifacts *by the project's own controls* (the RUNG-1/2 weak-baseline correction; the analogy false-GO; the deep-credit reservoir unmasking).

But the (A) column is not a single item. It is a coherent cluster with a common root: **everything hard sits on the same two unfinished foundations — (i) a learned/dendritic binder to replace the fixed FHRR idealization, and (ii) a substrate-native generative sequence model.** Multi-referent disambiguation, CA3 completion, the SWR imaginative-replay loop, structured-code generalization, and multi-attribute bundling are all gated by (i) or the dendritic/deep-credit lever, which was itself just retracted to NOT-GO. Open prose is gated by (ii).

**The single biggest genuinely-unsolved-and-needed capability is open-ended fluent generation (open prose / R4), currently supplied by the temporary external ~21M transformer scaffold, not simulated circuitry.**

**Is the project honest about it? Yes — conspicuously so.** It is labelled "the field's wall is the project's wall," "the one forbidden permanent external model, so it must go," and every EMERGE-59..77 GO explicitly names "NOT open prose (R4)." The ROADMAP states plainly "Not LLM-fluent." The 88.6M spiking-forward conversion that *would* make it substrate-native is validated (C1 GO) but deliberately not deployed (path-by-efficiency). This is not hidden; it is the most openly-flagged gap in the record. The generation-novelty categorical-gap measurement (0/16 held-out) exists precisely to keep the gap from being papered over as "just scale."

## 3. THE REAL GAPS FOR LLM-LIKE CONVERSATION (ranked by load-bearing weight)

1. **Open-ended fluent generation (open prose / R4).** Without it there is no "talk to it like an LLM about any topic." Met only by the ANN scaffold; the spiking replacement is unbuilt at required scale (the home-grown emergent ladder wins over a bigram only by ~0.18 nats in a regime the bigram then overtakes).
2. **A learned binder over structured/correlated codes (the FHRR-idealization replacement).** The whole comprehension/composition stack is a host-designed exact-inverse algebra. It works, but it forces FLAT codes, which is why generalization and composition are disjoint tracks. This blocks fluid reasoning over the brain's *own* semantic codes.
3. **Multi-referent disambiguation.** Real dialogue holds several entities and must bind a bare pronoun to the salient one. NEGATIVE, no working method, named fix unbuilt. This is a small, concrete, high-value target that has simply not been picked up.
4. **The dendritic / deep-credit learning lever.** The intended engine for (2) and for a "proper" generalizing cortex — currently RETRACTED to NOT-GO on spikes after an unseeded-substrate confound. Until this or an equivalent local credit rule works, (2) stays scaffolded.
5. **Multi-hop / open-world inference at realistic scale.** query_chain is 3-seed on the scaffold; the multitag path has a demonstrated hub-crowding boundary; open-world spreading-activation is synthetic-only and unwired.
6. **CA3 completion / imaginative-replay loop (R-iii).** Least load-bearing for chat specifically (recall-as-function exists via the composer scan), but it is the honest open frontier for episodic memory and imagination, and it is genuinely at chance on the substrate.

## 4. IS THE ROADMAP-HOPPING A PROBLEM?

**On its own terms as engineering: mostly sound, with one systemic risk the owner already named.**

Banking a failed method and re-obtaining the capability elsewhere is legitimate — *when the capability is genuinely re-obtained under controls, and the record says so.* Here it largely is: the retractions are explicit and foregrounded (the `2026-05-14` bug is cited as the canonical cautionary tale in every later transitive/analogy finding), the redemptions are multi-seed with the specific anti-cheat that would have caught the original artifact, and the scaffolds are labelled as scaffolds rather than claimed as biology. That is the good version of "bank a method, move on."

**But it tips into avoidance in exactly the way the owner's "whack-a-mole" concern predicts, and the audit shows two concrete symptoms:**

- **The hard, shared foundations get routed *around* rather than *through*.** Multi-attribute bundling, structured-code generalization, and multi-referent disambiguation are each individually deferred to "the learned/dendritic binder later." Each hop is locally reasonable; collectively they leave the one mechanism that would close all of them (a working learned binder / dendritic credit rule) perpetually next-quarter — and when that lever was finally pushed, it came back NOT-GO. The capabilities look solved one-by-one (via the fixed algebra), but the *emergence bar* the owner set — "does it EMERGE from a learning substrate?" not "did I build it?" — is not met for the fact-binding core. It is a hand-designed exact-inverse algebra, spiking at runtime but not learned in structure. That is precisely the "hand-built capability that doesn't self-organize" pattern the emergence-bar pivot demotes to scaffold.
- **Stranding and single-seed wire-ups.** The D3 event register is deployed but never wired into the console the owner actually talks to; EMERGE-29's inference console is a single-seed composition over 6-seed parts. The method is banked, the *usable* capability is not delivered. Repeated across arcs, this is how "solved" accumulates without the end-goal moving.

**Verdict.** The hopping is sound *tactics* on a foundation that has two unfinished *strategic* pieces — the learned binder and substrate-native generation — and the risk is that continued lateral banking keeps making the pile of solved-methods taller while those two load-bearing walls stay standing. The project is honest about the walls (open prose is flagged; the binder is labelled an idealization; the deep-credit retraction is in the record). The corrective is the one the owner already articulated: stop scoring hand-built capabilities as wins and put the primary effort on the emergence engine — a learning substrate that *develops* the binding structure and a generative sequence model that *grows* fluency — because that is the single lever under gaps 1, 2, and 4 simultaneously. Until then, the honest one-line answer to the owner's question is: **we have mostly been banking methods and re-solving the bounded capabilities, but the two capabilities that most define "LLM-like" — open fluent generation and a learned binder over the brain's own semantic codes — have been genuinely deferred-and-unsolved, are correctly labelled as frontiers, and are converging on the same un-built learning substrate.**
