## What is wired (per turn, on the ONE bridge)

- **KNOWN turn.** (1) BRAIN-BASED retrieval of the topic's SVO neighbourhood from the RF-phasor store
  (`comp.query_patient` = a spiking VSA unbind, abstaining where nothing is bound) — the world-model/memory
  faculty supplies the CONTENT. (2) The retrieved neighbourhood conditions the spiking generator MOUTH, which
  writes a short multi-sentence reply. (3) The graded-affect ladder differential (SEAM-C) sets the TONE prefix.
  (4) POST-HOC MOAT: each sentence is re-parsed to an SVO and checked against the store (`query_patient`); an
  unverified proposition is DROPPED, never emitted. The old frame-render is the FALLBACK (used when the
  neighbourhood is empty or nothing verifies).
- **NOVEL turn.** The unstored cue makes the hard moat ABSTAIN. The mouth writes a fluent curiosity QUESTION; the
  fm-reservoir (SEAM-A) prediction rides as a certainty-TAGGED *"predicted, not observed"* channel. The post-hoc
  moat lets NO declarative fact about the novel cue through (any confabulation re-parses → `query_patient` None →
  dropped).

## Attribution & honest-negatives (declared)

- **Brain-based:** the CONTENT is the RF-phasor-store neighbourhood (spiking VSA unbind) + the fm-reservoir spikes
  (SEAM-A); the TONE is the ladder rate differential (SEAM-C); the MOAT accept/reject is `query_patient` (the RF
  unbind). Lesioning any of these changes the prose (the battery).
- **Declared host shortcuts (unchanged from the grounded-language boundary):** the GENERATOR is a converted 0.5B
  spiking transformer — a SCAFFOLD mouth to biologize later; generator fluency itself is the UNCHANGED field wall.
  Brain→generator conditioning is a HOST TEXT INTERFACE (facts rendered to a prompt string), not synaptic drive.
  Sentence-split + SVO re-parse + a leading-pronoun coreference to the topic are HOST parsing; only the accept/
  reject decision is neural.
- **Instrument note.** The DEFAULT_VOCAB motion verbs are irregular (went/ran) or double their consonant
  (running); the shared inflection map missed those forms, blinding the re-parse and reading a held sham as a
  spurious 0.0. A subject-independent surface-inflection table + leading-pronoun coreference restore the
  instrument. This is instrument repair, not a mechanism change — the accept/reject decision is untouched.
