## Biology (decisive) + the mechanism-class pick
Real cortex carries the top-down teaching signal on a physically SEPARATE wire — the apical dendrite in layer 1 (Kandel 6e Ch 4 Fig 4-12; Felleman-Van Essen 1991: feedforward→L4/basal, feedback→L1/apical). Apical/basal COINCIDENCE → Ca²⁺ plateau → BURST (Larkum 1999; Kandel Ch 13 pp 296-297). One axon MULTIPLEXES two streams — event rate = feedforward message, burst probability = top-down error (Naud-Sprekeler 2018, demuxed by short-term plasticity). Payeur et al. 2021 turns this into the local online spiking rule **`dw = η[B − P·E]·Ẽ`** (apical sets LTP/LTD sign without touching the feedforward event rate) — no weight transport, no backward pass. **Mechanism-class pick for THIS substrate: burst-dependent plasticity, MICROCIRCUIT variant (interneuron cancels the predictable feedback → apical carries ERROR not raw teaching; noise-robust per EMERGE-5c), on the existing `cp_v_apical` two-compartment neuron** — the only class avoiding BOTH weight transport AND settling phases. External evidence it ports: Stuck-Naud 2024 (spiking Burstprop, MNIST at BPTT-comparable error), BurstCCN (single-phase, `github.com/neuralml/BurstCCN`). e-prop (Bellec 2020) is the complement for within-region recurrence (deep e-prop unsolved). µPC/EqProp scale deepest but are non-spiking + settling (borrow depth-stability ideas, don't adopt).
