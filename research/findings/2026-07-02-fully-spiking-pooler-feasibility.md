## What was tried + found
- **A naive spiking WTA** (feature cells → column cells via standard conductance-based SYNAPSES + a column→FS→column lateral-inhibition WTA, reusing EMERGE-11's FS pattern) — **the columns do NOT fire** (0/40) even at feature-drive 500 + 2× feedforward weight. Diagnosis: standard synaptic current with naive weights doesn't reach Izhikevich threshold; EMERGE-11's WTA works because it drives the column cells DIRECTLY.
- **The fix — drive the columns via the VALIDATED coincidence pathway** (option b): each column = a cell that samples a random subset of feature cells through coincidence synapses; a column responds when ≥ act_th of its features are active (the `coincidence_weighted_drive` that already fires reliably across EMERGE-9..34). Result:
  - **Similarity-preserving codes:** input A vs a similar A′ (3 of 4 features shared) → column-response overlap **0.56**; A vs a dissimilar B → **0.00**. Sparse response (6/40). This is the pooler's core property (random-projection LSH-like), fully on the spiking substrate.
  - **Inheritance on the spiking-pooler codes:** teach a property on training objects' column responses (the committed `sim/` three-term kernel) → a held-out object inherits via the overlapping response. Real **1.0 on 2/3 seeds** (mean 0.83; seed 44 weak at 0.5).

## Status + the boundary found: a FIXED random projection does not scale — spiking SELF-ORGANIZATION is needed
FEASIBLE at 2 categories with a FIXED random-projection coincidence pooler (real 0.83; permuted collapses). But scaling to **4 categories** (BIRD/FISH/MAMMAL/TREE, 9 members + 3 held-out each) with the SAME fixed random projection **fails**: real inheritance **0.33 (≈ chance 0.25)** while the permuted-features control cleanly collapses (**0.03**). Diagnosis: a fixed random projection does not cleanly SEPARATE 4 categories' column responses (cross-category response overlap grows), and the property teaching over 4 categories cross-talks. **⇒ the genuinely-hard piece is the SPIKING SELF-ORGANIZATION** — the columns must LEARN (Hebbian competitive learning + FS-WTA on the substrate) to tune to categories and stay disjoint, exactly what the numpy Spatial Pooler (EMERGE-33) does with boosting. The fixed random projection is a feasibility stepping-stone, not the endpoint.

**⇒ EMERGE-35 (the focused build):** a spiking SELF-ORGANIZING pooler — feat→col with a plastic (Hebbian/three-term) projection + an FS-WTA lateral-inhibition k-winner selection + homeostatic excitability (boosting), on the bridge, so same-category members converge on a shared column block that scales past 2 categories. Then inheritance + the robustness treatment (≥3 held-out/category, gate on input-destruction permuted-features + lesion, 6-seed). This is a substantial spiking-competitive-learning build (the genuinely-hard part deferred by EMERGE-33/34's numpy pooler), deserving focused execution — NOT a quick completion. The FS-WTA firing issue (naive synapses don't fire columns; use the coincidence drive) is already solved above; the open piece is the plastic self-organization + homeostasis tuned on spikes.
