## MOVE 2 — REFRAME VIA REAL BIOLOGY / DEVELOPMENT (how does a child's grammar self-organize?)

Grammar in a child is NOT rule-programmed; it self-organizes from distributional experience. Three convergent literatures, each with a directly-reusable computational mechanism:

**(A) Function words are identified by DISTRIBUTIONAL FREQUENCY — the field's answer to S2.**
- **Yang & Getz (2026), "Function Words as Statistical Cues for Language Learning"** (arXiv 2601.21191): a cross-linguistic corpus analysis of **186 languages** confirms function words are UNIVERSALLY marked by **three distributional properties: (1) high frequency, (2) reliable syntactic association, (3) phrase-boundary alignment.** The "Goldilocks effect": a function word must be frequent enough to be reliable yet diverse enough (occurs before many different content words) to stay informative. **This is exactly a frequency+contextual-flatness statistic** — high token frequency AND high context entropy / low content (a word that precedes/follows *many* different words, carrying little of its own semantic content). This is the S2 discovery rule, biology-confirmed and universal.
- **Word frequency as an infancy cue** (Shi/Gervain line; "Word frequency as a cue to identify function words in infancy"): infants use raw frequency to segregate function words BEFORE they know their meaning — the closed class is discovered *first, by frequency*, exactly the developmental order S2 needs.
- **Redington, Chater & Finch (1998); Cartwright & Brent (1997)** — the classic distributional POS-induction results: cluster words by their context-vector similarity (a 2-word window before/after) → grammatical categories (incl. the closed class) emerge unsupervised. This is the SAME co-occurrence-clustering the project's PPMI stream cortex + EMERGE-30 pooler already do.
- **PMC "Distributional Language Learning: Mechanisms and Models of Category Formation"** — review confirming distributional statistics suffice for category (incl. function-word class) formation.

**(B) The closed/open distinction is a neurolinguistic DOUBLE DISSOCIATION — catalog G.12 Broca.**
- Catalog **G.12** (feature-catalog.md:2774-2784, Kandel 6e Ch 55 pp 1382-1384): Broca damage → "retained noun selection, **lost function-word/verb use**" — agrammatism selectively strips the closed class while sparing content nouns. This is the biological warrant that closed-class function words are a SEPARABLE subsystem (the EMERGE-59 function-word-ablation control `b3` already reproduces this dissociation behaviorally). The distinction is real and dissociable — so discovering it as a distinct statistical population is biologically principled, not a modeling convenience.
- **ERP evidence** ("Differences in brain potentials to open and closed class words: class and frequency effects", Neuropsychologia): open vs closed class evoke distinct potentials with strong FREQUENCY modulation — the brain itself tracks the frequency signature that S2 would use.

**(C) Constructions are ABSTRACTED from repeated exemplars — the S1 answer (usage-based / item-based).**
- **Tomasello usage-based / construction grammar; Goldberg; Bybee frequency effects; MacWhinney competition model** (already in project memory `project_conversational_primary_robust_multicue_parser`): a child does not learn a "grammar rule"; she abstracts item-based constructions ("the X can Y") into schematic slots from repeated exemplars, driven by frequency. The frame's slot-INVENTORY + slot-ORDER are the STATISTICAL regularities of high-frequency exemplar sequences. **The project already has the machinery:** `_bucketB_corpus_mined_frames_derisk.py` MINES per-verb argument frames from corpus co-occurrence (GO), and FrameCQ LEARNS a per-frame order from a teacher. S1 = "supply FrameCQ's order-teacher from corpus n-gram statistics of the exemplar constructions" — reusing both.
- **Dominey & Hinaut fronto-striatal reservoir model** (PLoS ONE 2013 "Real-Time Parallel Processing of Grammatical Structure…"; Hinaut & Dominey "Self-Organized Artificial Grammar Learning in Spiking Neural Networks"): the canonical neural precedent for THIS EXACT decomposition — **thematic roles are read from the ORDER and POSITION of the CLOSED-CLASS elements**; open vs closed class are separated on input; grammar is learned from a corpus WITHOUT explicitly representing rules, and generalizes to new constructions. This is the strongest biological validation that (a) the closed/open split is the right primitive and (b) the grammar is the STATISTICS of closed-class positions — precisely S1+S2.
- **STDP as the statistical-grammar mechanism**: a spiking-statistical-learning line reports "spike-based statistical learning leads to synaptic representations that comply with human acquisition performances… STDP [is] a practicable neural mechanism underlying human statistical grammar learning" — i.e. the project's own on-bridge co-occurrence learning (EMERGE-30's committed three-term kernel) is a validated substrate for this.

**Reframe verdict:** the child self-organizes grammar by (i) segregating the closed class by frequency (S2, Yang-Getz/Shi), and (ii) abstracting constructions — slot inventory + closed-class-position order — from repeated high-frequency exemplars (S1, Tomasello/Dominey). Both are DISTRIBUTIONAL and both map onto machinery the project has already validated (PPMI/running-frequency, EMERGE-30 co-occurrence pooler, `_bucketB` frame mining, FrameCQ). The hard "learn a grammar from scratch" wall is the WRONG hypothesis; the cheap "discover the distributional structure" path is the right one.
