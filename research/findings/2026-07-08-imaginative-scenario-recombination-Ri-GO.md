## The mechanism
Pick a latent theme; sample one PLAUSIBLE filler per role from the flat role pool, WEIGHTED by the learned plausibility signal (graph-relatedness) -> a coherent scene. Bind each role-filler (FHRR phase-add) and BUNDLE into ONE composite scene phasor. A resonator/cleanup FACTORS the composite back: unbind each role, cleanup to the nearest filler by phasor-cosine. The scene goes to a FLAGGED imagined/hypothesis channel — never written to the factual store. Biology: Schacter-Addis constructive episodic simulation; Spens-Burgess 2024 (replay trains a generative model); catalog G.09.

## Anti-cheats (all from the gate, all pass)
- **(A) FACTOR-RECOVERY = 1.000** — the single bundled composite genuinely unbinds back to each role's correct filler. This is the crux: it proves the scene is a structured composite, not bundle mush. (Holds to 6 roles at D=1024; the capacity scales with D — a real ceiling exists at higher role-count / lower D, not hit here.)
- **(B) SHUFFLED-graph collapse (0.913 -> 0.015)** — scrambling the plausibility signal makes the sampled fillers no longer share a theme -> coherence collapses to chance. The coherence rides the learned graph, not a template artifact.
- **(C) ROLE-COUNT stress** — the honest FHRR bundle-capacity probe; comfortable to 6 roles at D=1024.
- **(D) NOVELTY = 0.987** — the recombined scenes are new (not the stored/experienced tuples).
- **(E) MOAT = 0.000 leak** — imagined scenes are flagged (a hypothesis channel), never asserted as stored facts; the factual store is untouched (the owner-sanctioned reconstructive-memory trade: an imagined item is offered as a plausible guess, never as knowledge).
