# GNW ignition bus — a HETEROGENEOUS NON-COMPOSER organ as one of the N ignition votes (6-seed GO)

## The claim being de-risked (the named #1 residual on both GNW findings)
The N-organ ignition bus (`_gnw_norgan_bus_derisk`, 6/6 GO; production flip 2026-08-13) proved the SUBSTRATE combines N>=3 subthreshold organ reads via consensus-ignition + shared-inhibition WTA + re-entry. But the N organs there are all CORROBORATING RELATIONAL reads of ONE composer (forward recall + a second relation + a reverse-binding VERIFY — all `query_patient`/`query_agent`). Both GNW findings name the same #1 residual: *"a non-composer organ (a spiking surprise/familiarity monitor, the affect organ) as one of the N votes is the immediate follow-on rung."* This de-risk closes that rung: it routes N=3 votes through the SAME spiking workspace where ONE vote is a genuinely HETEROGENEOUS, NON-COMPOSER organ, and asks whether the substrate still combines the heterogeneous vote by ignition. This deepens the one-substrate claim: the substrate integrates DIFFERENT KINDS of organ by ignition, not just corroborating reads of one composer.

## Mechanism (reuse-by-import: the N-organ bus hop + workspace + the PRODUCTION surprise organ; NO `sim/` edit)
- organ A — spiking RECALL (composer): `query_patient(agent, EAT)` -> cand_A.
- organ B — spiking CORROBORATION (composer): `query_patient(agent, CONFIRM)` -> a second-relation recall of the same edge.
- organ H — spiking FAMILIARITY / SURPRISE monitor (NON-COMPOSER): votes cand_A IFF its predictive-coding mismatch circuit reads (agent,action)->cand_A as a FAMILIAR / non-surprising fact. The read is `cp_firing_states[surprise]` off a DIFFERENT mechanism (Hebbian topographic cue->expected + GABA_A subtractive inhibition; `surprise_production_organ.SurpriseProductionOrgan`, D2 6/6-GO, wired at its robust operating point cue_to_expected_weight=0.8). It NEVER calls `query_patient`.

Organ H's continuous surprise Hz is thresholded by the organ's OWN calibrated decision (`surprised = hz >= threshold`, a legitimate spiking read-out) into a binary "familiar -> vote cand_A / surprising -> withhold", then written as the IDENTICAL subthreshold d_sub=1000 pA drive as the composer votes. So the heterogeneous vote is subthreshold-commensurate BY CONSTRUCTION: three agreeing votes accumulate to 3*d_sub=3000 pA >= the measured K=4 workspace knee (~2400 pA) and IGNITE; drop / silence / mis-route / disagree organ H and the true slot falls to 2*d_sub=2000 pA < knee -> abstain.

## Result — 6 seeds 42/43/44/100/101/102 (SIM_BACKEND=numpy; the effect is a subthreshold-vs-suprathreshold bifurcation, not GPU-scale-dependent). Cites `research/findings/raw/_gnw_bus_heterogeneous/summary.json`.

**The gated claim is the SUBSTRATE-COMBINATION, measured two ways that isolate it from the het organ's own read precision:**
- **ignite-when-voted = 1.000 (all 6 seeds).** On EVERY edge where organ H cast its vote, the workspace ignited the correct winner. The substrate combines the heterogeneous vote whenever it is present.
- **abstain-when-withheld = 1.000 (all 6 seeds).** On EVERY edge where organ H withheld (a marginal familiarity read), the workspace ABSTAINED — it never confabulated an answer from the two composer votes alone (2*d_sub < knee). `all_substrate_combines = True`.

**Every load-bearing collapse control = 0.000 on every seed** (chance 0.025; collapse threshold 0.10): single-organ (1 vote subthreshold) 0.000; het-DROPPED / leave-one-out-worst (organ H, or any organ, removed -> 2 votes) 0.000; DISAGREE (organ H reads a contradiction as surprising -> withholds, `het_disagree_withheld_frac`=1.0) 0.000; shuffle-off-slot (organ H's drive routed to an empty slot) 0.000; het-ORGAN-lesion (organ H's OWN prediction edges zeroed -> can't recognize familiar -> withholds, `het_organ_lesion_withheld_frac`=1.0) 0.000; workspace-lesion (assembly self-recurrence zeroed) 0.000. The single-hop composer recall reflex SURVIVES the workspace lesion at 1.000 (the dissociation). The moat abstains on an unstored agent + a stored agent under a wrong action (`all_moat_ok=True`).

**The het organ genuinely discriminates (instrument, all seeds `all_het_discriminate=True`):** mean agree-read 0.58 Hz < the organ's own threshold (2.49-2.88) <= mean disagree-read 5.14 Hz. The `cp_firing_states[surprise]` read that DRIVES the binary vote genuinely flips between a confirmed and a contradicted assertion.

## The two metrics, and why the substrate claim is 6/6 while end-to-end tracks the het organ (full transparency)
The strict END-TO-END consensus (does the bus answer match host recall on every stored edge?) is 0.9375 mean vs host 1.000 — 3 seeds 8/8, 3 seeds (44/100/101) 7/8. The 1/8-per-seed shortfall is ENTIRELY the het organ's single-read confirm precision: `het_vote_rate` = 0.9375 mean (the fraction of genuinely-familiar edges its single spiking confirm-read recognized). On the one marginal edge per affected seed, that concept's confirm read lands just above the organ's threshold (an outlier vs the 0.58 Hz mean), so organ H WITHHOLDS — and the substrate then correctly ABSTAINS (2 votes < quorum). This is the DESIGNED consensus-veto / moat behaviour, not a substrate error: a withheld vote is a form of "the organ does not agree", exactly the case the substrate must abstain on. So the het organ can only ADD abstentions vs bare host recall (a STRONGER moat), never invent a fact — identical in spirit to the reverse-VERIFY organ's veto in the original bus. The GATED claim is therefore the substrate-combination (ignite-when-voted / abstain-when-withheld, both 6/6); the het organ's single-read precision (0.9375) is a REPORTED reliability that rides the surprise organ's own burn-down, not a property of the bus.

## What the anti-cheats establish (each targets "the heterogeneous organ GENUINELY participates")
- HET-DROPPED / leave-one-out collapse: a host `if recall: return r` (or "the two composer organs suffice") would SUCCEED; the collapse proves the heterogeneous vote is part of the substrate AND, load-bearing, not a decorative extra.
- HET-DISAGREES (consensus-veto by the heterogeneous organ): organ H's mismatch circuit fires high on a contradicting assertion -> it withholds -> collapse. The disagreement is a SPIKING read over threshold, not a host flip (`het_disagree_withheld_frac`=1.0).
- HET-ORGAN LESION (its OWN spiking prediction is load-bearing): zeroing the mismatch circuit's patient_expected->surprise edges makes CONFIRM fire as high as CONTRADICT -> organ H can no longer recognize the familiar fact -> withholds -> collapse. The heterogeneous vote is caused by the learned SPIKING prediction, not a fixed input artifact.
- NON-COMPOSER by construction: organ H's read is `cp_firing_states[surprise]`; it never calls `query_patient`/`query_agent` (verified in code — only organs A/B and the reflex/host-baseline touch the composer).
- WORKSPACE LESION + reflex survives: the ignition + broadcast are load-bearing on the combined answer, while the direct-recall reflex dissociates.

## Honest scope + residual (this is a de-risk that CLOSES a named rung; it is not a production wire)
1. **The het organ's single-read confirm precision is the residual** (het_vote_rate 0.9375; 1/8-per-seed marginal edge on 3 of 6 seeds). This is the surprise organ's OWN documented precision boundary (its finding: "at LOW prediction gain the GO drops to 3/6 ... the divisive-normalization / gain-match companion process is proxied by a fixed weight"). The named next mechanism to lift end-to-end parity to host: the surprise organ's homeostatic-gain / divisive-normalization companion (equalize the topographic prediction strength across blocks), or an evidence-integrated SETTLED read (the source-monitor's brief-viewing integration, as `SpikingConjunctiveFamiliarityGate.novelty_settled` already justifies). This rides the surprise organ's burn-down, NOT the bus's — the substrate-combination is already clean.
2. **The heterogeneous vote is a familiarity/CONFIRM read of the recalled patient** — it votes the composer's candidate iff its own spiking circuit confirms familiarity. A het organ that contributes an INDEPENDENT concept (e.g. an affect-appraisal patient or a surprise-driven alternative) is the richer follow-on; this de-risk establishes the load-bearing participation of a different KIND of organ in the ignition consensus.
3. **Co-residency.** Organ H runs on ITS OWN circuit bridge alongside the composer + the workspace, not merged onto one bridge — rides the one-brain merge burn-down, exactly as the affect / surprise organs already do in production.
4. **NOT "closed"** (per `docs/TERMS.md`): a default-off de-risk runner, not the shipped path. Closure = wiring a heterogeneous non-composer organ into the production bus (`webapp/gnw_bus_shadow.py`) as one of the routed votes.
