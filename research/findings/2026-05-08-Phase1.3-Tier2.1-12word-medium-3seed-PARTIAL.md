## Comparison to 8-word vocab

| Metric | 8-word (3-seed validated) | 12-word (this run) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean primary retention | 91.2% ± 6.5% | **84.0% ± 12.0%** | -7.2 pp |
| Mean synonym retention | 128.4% ± 6.7% | **99.6% ± 9.2%** | -28.8 pp |
| 3/3 GO? | **YES** | **NO (2/3)** | failed at 12-word |

The 12-word case shows:
1. **Lower mean primary retention** (84% vs 91%) — primary patterns
   degrade slightly as vocab size increases.
2. **Higher variance** (std 12% vs 6.5%) — seed 43 is the outlier
   at 71% primary retention.
3. **Synonym retention drops below 100%** (99.6% vs 128.4%) —
   no longer the "synonym binding actually IMPROVES post-lesion"
   pattern.

## Interpretation

**Hypothesis: motor pool capacity limit at 12-word.**

8-word case: each motor_X (1000 neurons) needs to differentiate 2
synonym sub-populations. Worked cleanly (122% synonym retention,
stronger than primary).

12-word case: each motor_X needs to differentiate 3 synonym
sub-populations from 1000 neurons. The N-second-letter abbreviations
(n, e, s, w) compete for the same motor pool capacity. Result:
- Synonyms still differentiate, but slightly more diluted (99.6%
  vs 122% retention)
- Primary words (the strongest binding) get crowded out in some
  seeds (seed 43 at 71%)

This matches the Tier 2.1 BREAKTHROUGH lesson (CLAUDE.md entry #7):
> **Capacity hypothesis confirmed**: bigger motor pools give STDP
> enough room for functional sub-populations within each motor_X
> (different synonyms activate different sub-pops).

The 8-word v4 scale-up was n_motor=1000 (vs Tier 1's 500, 2x bigger).
For 12-word, we may need another 1.5-2x bigger.

## Anti-cheat note

Today's strict anti-cheat result (8-word, 3-seed) confirmed cortex

active here — these 12-word retention numbers reflect real cortex

should:
- Lift primary retention to >= 90% across all seeds
- Lift synonym retention back into the 100%+ range (matching 8-word)

Scaled-up arch:
- ~22K neurons (1.7x), ~28M synapses (2x), ~5 GB GPU (well within headroom)
- Wall clock estimated ~150-170 min/seed (~1.5x slower)
- Single-seed scaled-up at seed 43 (the failing seed) launched
  immediately to test capacity hypothesis

If scaled-up seed 43 lifts primary retention from 71% to >=80%, the
capacity hypothesis is validated → run multi-seed scaled-up.

If scaled-up seed 43 stays at ~71%, the issue is something else
(synonym training interference, sleep replay coverage gap, or seed-
specific topology issue). Would need different mitigation.
