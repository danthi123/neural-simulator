## ✅ CORRECTED CONCLUSION (Run-A control sweep, the honest bottom line)
The rigorous controls (matched-read-out denominator, magnitude-decoupled EMA a_slow, multi-timescale, deep sub-buckets, ctx1 diagnostic — all seed 42, 2M tokens) dismantle the preliminary "GO" and leave these SOLID findings:

Extending the eligibility HORIZON (with magnitude held) adds essentially nothing to the deep-context credit.
2. **The "% of BPTT recurrent credit" framing was fragile and is dropped.** The ctx1 read-out-only diagnostic (position 1, where h_0=0 so W_rec has zero effect) shows the e-prop-family arms + `BPTT_matched_readout` all read ~−3.25, while `BPTT_fixed_win` reads −2.08 (a +1.17 pure-read-out advantage) and `BPTT_same_net` −0.16 — i.e. the big apparent "recurrent-credit ceilings" were dominated by READ-OUT (AdamW W_out + trained bias) and, for `BPTT_same_net`, INPUT-EMBEDDING learning, NOT recurrent credit.
