## Interpretation

**The mean 33.3% matches the Phase 1.4 BRANCH A baseline** — same
Tier 1 architecture's W→A baseline was ~33% in the validated
6-seed catastrophic forgetting test (5/6 PASS at >= 80% retention,
mean 103%).

**Seed-to-seed variance is high (±11.8%)** — this is the small-sample
noise floor for 12-turn chat demos. The documented range in
`CHAT-DEMO-GUIDE.md` was "~33-45% per seed" which captured the
expected best case but missed the lower outlier. Should update the
guide to reflect actual range 17-50%.

Per-seed pattern:
- Seed 42 at 16.7% is the worst-case outlier (matches earlier
  chat_demo seed 42 result of 16.7% from commit f00b465)
- Seed 101 at 50% is the best case
- 4 seeds cluster at 25-42%
- Mean (33.3%) matches the documented baseline exactly

**Caveat:** at 12 turns/seed, single-seed variance is wide enough
that any single chat_demo run is a noisy point. For statistical
claims use `evaluate_word_to_action` (200 trials/word) on the same
architecture.
