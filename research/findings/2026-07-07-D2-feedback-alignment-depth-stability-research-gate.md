## TL;DR verdict (the 10-line summary)
1. **The depth wall is REAL and quantified:** plain fixed-random feedback alignment (FA) collapses to ~chance on deep nets — **97.4% top-1 error on ResNet-18** (Akrout 2019, Table 1), i.e. it barely learns, while backprop is 30.1%. On the burst substrate specifically, **fixed-random feedback "showed the worst performance"** and its updates were most misaligned with backprop (Greedy-Costa 2026).
2. **The failure decomposes into two independent axes:** (a) **credit-DIRECTION decay** — the FA update rotates toward orthogonal in early layers, and lower layers align last/least (Lillicrap 2016; Moskovitz 2018 Fig. 3a; Refinetti 2021); (b) **per-layer MAGNITUDE/variance drift** — ‖B‖/‖W‖ mismatch compounds exponentially with depth (Moskovitz 2018 §5.1). Both must be fixed at depth-3+.
3. **The decisive on-substrate precedent already exists:** Greedy-Costa 2026 ("Cell-type-specific cortical feedback…", Costa lab, the BurstCCN successor) surpasses the depth wall on the SAME two-compartment burst substrate, holding <3% F-MNIST error through **8 hidden areas** — via **LEARNED apical-local burst-feedback weights (Y-plasticity)** that establish "QY-symmetry" (the interneuron balance), single-phase, no transport, no settling.
4. **What fixes DIRECTION (transport-free, single-phase):** learn the feedback weights toward Wᵀ without sharing them — **Kolen-Pollack** (symmetric decay, single-phase, matches backprop on ImageNet; Akrout 2019) OR the burst-native **Y-plasticity** (apical-targeting Hebbian, the same idea realized on our exact substrate; Greedy-Costa 2026). Sign-concordance (Liao 2016; Xiao 2018) is a cheaper partial-rescue.
5. **What fixes MAGNITUDE (transport-free, single-phase, spiking-native):** a **per-layer scalar gain/rate controller** — homeostatic synaptic scaling / intrinsic homeostasis (Turrigiano 2008), divisive normalization (Carandini-Heeger 2012), or a static **µP/depth-µP** `1/√(N·L)` init/gain scaling (Yang-Hu 2021; borrowed from µPC arXiv 2505.13124 WITHOUT its settling phase).
6. **The interneuron earns a genuine DEPTH role (new, decisive):** Greedy-Costa 2026 shows the SST-interneuron population "constrains the DIMENSIONALITY (rank) of the burst-mediated error feedback," and that **reductions in LATER areas hurt most** — preserving feedback rank "becomes increasingly important deeper in the hierarchy." This is exactly the D2 hypothesis (the interneuron cancellation is not load-bearing for accuracy at depth-2, but IS at depth).
7. **Settling methods are diagnostic-only (disqualified):** predictive coding/µPC and Equilibrium Propagation scale deep but need a relaxation phase — BORROW their insight (per-layer scaling; small-perturbation feedback), don't adopt the machinery.
8. **#1 recommended first surpass = LEARN THE APICAL FEEDBACK (Y-plasticity / Kolen-Pollack) + a per-layer homeostatic gain**, both already substrate-native (`cp_v_apical`, existing homeostasis kernels), both single-phase/transport-free. The learned-feedback fixes direction; the homeostatic gain fixes magnitude; together they are the exact pair the literature says depth needs.
9. **Cheapest de-risk:** rate-numpy first (add Y-plasticity + per-layer gain to the existing `MicrocircuitBDSPNet`/`dendritic_mlp` at depth-3), gated on plain-FA ACTUALLY degrading at depth-3 in the same harness (the D2 rung-1 precondition) and oracle ≥0.80; then the on-bridge depth-3 spiking run.
10. **Honest prior:** expect depth-3 to HOLD but degrade MILDLY (spiking precedent: Stuck-Naud signed-Burstprop lost only ~1.3 pts over 1→4 hidden layers, and width HELPED — so the wall is the error channel, not forward collapse), and expect boundary-then-surpass rather than a clean GO; the multi-order SCALE wall stands behind any depth-3-on-a-toy GO. But the surpass mechanism is NOT speculative — Greedy-Costa 2026 demonstrated it (learned apical feedback + interneuron rank-preservation) to depth-8 on this exact substrate class, and Payeur 2021 reached conv-net (CIFAR/ImageNet) depth with the interneuron baseline-cancellation.

## PART 1 — DIAGNOSIS: why FA breaks at depth, quantified, decomposed

### 1a. The magnitude of the wall (established)
| Method | ResNet-18 top-1 ERROR | ResNet-50 top-1 ERROR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Backprop (transport) | 30.1% | 22.9% | Akrout 2019 Table 1 |
| Weight Mirror (learned feedback) | 30.2% | 23.4% | Akrout 2019 Table 1 |
| Kolen-Pollack (learned feedback) | 29.2% | 23.9% | Akrout 2019 Table 1 |
| Sign-symmetry | 39.2% / 37.9% | 33.8% | Akrout 2019 / Xiao 2018 Table 1 |
| **plain fixed-random FA** | **97.4% (≈chance)** | **98.9%** | Akrout 2019 Table 1 |

Plain FA does not diverge — it **plateaus far below BP as depth/task scale** (Bartunov 2018: near-BP on MNIST, a widening gap on CIFAR, near-failure on ImageNet). The two-compartment/burst version reproduces the ordering: fixed-random feedback learns but is "the worst performance," most misaligned with BP (Greedy-Costa 2026, Fig. 5).

### 1b. The decomposition (the load-bearing diagnostic — Moskovitz, Litwin-Kumar & Abbott 2018, arXiv 1812.06488)
Two DISTINCT, independent failure axes — D2 must not conflate them:
- **(a) credit-DIRECTION decay (primary).** FA works only if the update stays within 90° of BP: `eᵀ W B e > 0` (Lillicrap 2016). Empirically the angle **starts ≈90° (orthogonal) and never reaches 0** (Lillicrap 2016 Fig. 2b, floors ~45° shallow). In DEEP nets the alignment is **non-uniform**: top layers converge, **lower/earlier layers fail to align** (Moskovitz 2018 Fig. 3a). Alignment proceeds **bottom-up sequentially, layer by layer** — layer 2 aligns first, layer 3 much later (Refinetti 2021, arXiv 2011.12428, Fig. 7), so with finite budget the deepest-from-output layers get the least alignment. **This is why the wall is a DEPTH wall specifically.**
- **(b) per-layer MAGNITUDE/variance drift (secondary, independent).** ‖B‖ is fixed while ‖W‖ grows with training; the ratio ‖B‖/‖W‖ mismatch **compounds exponentially in depth** (a product over L layers; Moskovitz 2018 §5.1, Fig. 2a). You can have correct direction and still get vanishing/exploding credit scale.
- **(c) forward-signal collapse (conv-specific, mostly N/A here).** In weight-shared conv layers the parameter space is too constrained for W to rotate into alignment with a generic random B (Refinetti 2021 §4.2; the root of the ImageNet-conv failure). **Not a concern for the D2 fully-connected depth-3 MLP** — flag it only if the substrate later uses heavy weight-sharing.

### 1c. Direct Feedback Alignment (DFA) does NOT dissolve the wall
DFA (Nøkland 2016) routes the output error directly to every layer (parallel, not sequential) — this removes the *sequential*-alignment bottleneck of (a) but replaces it with a per-layer "can this layer align to the output error at all?" problem; it scales to modern FC/graph/recommender nets (Launay 2020) but still fails convolutions and deep attention. **Verdict:** DFA is a plausible cheap ALTERNATIVE routing for the FC depth-3 net (worth a control arm) but is not the depth *fix* — it does not learn feedback and does not control magnitude.

### 1c-bis. The decisive "which axis" tell from the SPIKING burst literature (width-helps / depth-hurts)
The one direct spiking-substrate depth experiment is **Stuck & Naud 2024/25** (bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.07.19.604308 → IOP 10.1088/2634-4386/adb511): rate-encoded MNIST, signed Burstprop **97.2% (1 hidden) → 95.9% (4 hidden)** — depth HURT by ~1.3 pts — while **WIDTH HELPED**. This asymmetry is decisive: more forward CAPACITY (width) improves accuracy, so the depth degradation is **NOT forward-signal collapse (c)**; it lives in the **top-down ERROR channel** (axes a+b: the burst-multiplexed error is an approximation of BP whose error compounds per extra layer). Their own fixes target exactly that channel — a **regularizing soma→dendrite feedback** (baseline/homeostatic control) + a **signed two-event error scheme** (the unsigned scheme was ~64%, far worse) — confirming the read. Corroboration: BurstCCN documents "it was more difficult to produce updates closely aligned to backprop" as depth grows (alignment angle rises with depth but stays <90°, still-useful; Greedy 2022 §3.4.1 Fig 5D), and **Appendix B.1 proves the apical-error norm is BOUNDED (‖u_ℓ‖ ≤ ‖u_{ℓ+1}‖) IF the Y feedback weights stay small** — a concrete magnitude-control lever. Meanwhile Payeur 2021 reached CONV-NET depth (CIFAR ~20.1% err, ImageNet ~56.1% top-5) BECAUSE the interneuron baseline-cancellation kept credit clean per layer — so the burst family's depth limiter is feedback STRUCTURE/alignment, not forward collapse.

### 1d. Which axis dominates on OUR substrate (the honest prior for D2 rung-1)
The D1 finding already isolated that the depth-2 microcircuit accuracy is carried by the **clean-error feedforward channel** (M2.6 somatic-rate FF descending the clean apical error via fixed-random `Y`), i.e. clean-error FA. At depth-3 the literature predicts BOTH axes bite: direction (lower layers under-align with a fixed `Y`) AND magnitude (per-layer drift compounds; on spikes the burst-fraction error is also a noisy estimator whose variance can compound). **D2 rung-1's job is to MEASURE which** — via per-layer cos(update, oracle-BP-update) (direction) and per-layer ‖update‖ ratios (magnitude) — so the surpass is targeted, not scattershot.

## PART 2 — REFRAME: how does real cortex carry credit through many layers?
The biology gives a coherent, cited answer that maps 1:1 onto the surpass mechanisms:
- **Physically separate feedforward and feedback wires.** Pyramidal neurons segregate feedforward (basal / L4) from feedback (apical tuft / L1) input across compartments (Kandel 6e Ch 19 Fig 19-13/14, pp 453-454: L4 → supragranular pyramid dendrites AND infragranular apical dendrites; Felleman-Van Essen 1991: feedforward→L4, feedback→L1). The teaching signal never shares the forward synapse ⇒ **no weight transport is the biological default.** The project already has this wire: the two-compartment `cp_v_apical` apical membrane (`sim/bridge.py:6474-6494`, plateau regenerates on the apical, not the soma).
- **Apical/basal coincidence → Ca²⁺ plateau → BURST; the burst sets the plasticity sign.** (Larkum 1999; Kandel Ch 13 pp 293-298; Greedy-Costa 2026's rule: burst events → LTP factor, isolated spikes → LTD factor; the time-averaged postsynaptic term ∝ burst probability `p_i`.) This is the credit CHANNEL, already validated on-substrate (EMERGE-4 multiplexing R²=0.936; EMERGE-10 predictive-vs-active).
- **Interneurons make the apical carry ERROR, not raw teaching — and constrain its RANK.** An SST/inhibitory ensemble cancels the predictable component of the top-down so the apical residual is the ERROR (Sacramento-Senn 2018; Payeur 2021). NEW at depth (Greedy-Costa 2026): the **SST population size constrains the DIMENSIONALITY (rank) of the burst-mediated feedback**, and this matters MORE deeper in the hierarchy — matching the empirical cortex-wide gradient in interneuron density. ⇒ the interneuron is the biology's own **per-layer feedback-conditioning** mechanism.
- **Homeostasis keeps every layer's firing rate in-range** (Turrigiano 2008 synaptic scaling — multiplicative, so it preserves learned relative weights; divisive normalization Carandini-Heeger 2012 — the canonical cortical gain-control). This is the biology's per-layer MAGNITUDE controller — exactly axis (b)'s fix.

**The reframe:** cortex carries credit deep by (i) a separate apical error wire (no transport), (ii) a LEARNED, interneuron-conditioned feedback that keeps the apical residual aligned AND low-rank/clean, and (iii) per-layer homeostatic gain that keeps the forward+feedback signals in-range. The surpass mechanisms below are exactly these three.

## PART 3 — RANKED surpass mechanisms (each: what · cite · why it helps · transport-free? settling-free? single-phase? spiking-realizable? · cheapest de-risk)

> All ranked by "cheapest to add to the existing single-phase spiking clean-error FA rule (`MicrocircuitBDSPNet` / `dendritic_mlp` + `cp_v_apical`)". The two axes (direction, magnitude) are largely independent, so the top recommendation COMBINES a direction-fix and a magnitude-fix.

### RANK 1 — LEARN the apical feedback weights (Y-plasticity / Kolen-Pollack) — fixes DIRECTION
- **What:** stop using fixed-random `Y`; make the apical burst-feedback weights PLASTIC so they converge toward the transpose of the forward path WITHOUT sharing weights. Two equivalent realizations:
  - **Y-plasticity (burst-native, our exact substrate).** Apical-targeting Hebbian plasticity on the burst-feedback pathway drives it into "QY-symmetry" (the event- and burst-feedback inputs balance at baseline burst probability). Local, apical, single-phase. **Greedy-Costa 2026** (Costa lab; DOI 10.64898/2026.06.16.732595): "random-plastic feedback substantially improved performance… approaching the symmetric-plastic variant"; holds <3% F-MNIST through **8 hidden areas** (online 2.72%, ideal QY-symmetric 1.89%, vs ANN-FA 1.90%).
  - **Kolen-Pollack (KP).** ΔW = −η δ yᵀ − λW; ΔB = −η y δᵀ − λB (identical increment transposed + shared decay λ) ⇒ (W−Bᵀ) decays geometrically → Bᵀ→W. **Single-phase, transport-free, matches BP on ImageNet** (Akrout 2019: KP 29.2% vs BP 30.1% ResNet-18; FA 97.4%).
- **Why it helps depth:** directly repairs axis (a) — the feedback stops being a random projection the lower layers can't align to; it becomes (approximately) the correct backward operator at every layer, so alignment no longer has to propagate bottom-up through under-aligned layers.
- **Transport-free:** YES (only activity vectors cross; no forward W is copied). **Settling-free:** YES. **Single-phase:** YES (both KP and Y-plasticity update in the normal forward/apical pass). **Spiking-realizable:** YES — Y-plasticity is literally apical-local Hebbian on `cp_v_apical`; it IS the burst-substrate form.
- **Cheapest de-risk:** in the rate-numpy `MicrocircuitBDSPNet`, flip `Y` from fixed to plastic (KP update, or the apical-Hebbian Y-rule) at depth-3; gate on: held-out > plain-FA + 0.10 AND per-layer-1 alignment does not collapse; anti-cheat: assert no forward `W`/`Wᵀ` ever enters the `Y` update (no-transport probe), and that a FIXED-`Y` control at the same depth degrades (else there is no wall to fix).

### RANK 2 — Per-layer homeostatic gain / rate control (+ small-Y bound, + signed error, + longer averaging) — fixes MAGNITUDE and the burst-noise sub-axis
- **What:** a per-layer scalar controller that pins each layer's mean firing rate (or activation norm) to a set-point, so the forward signal and the error riding on it neither vanish nor explode across depth. Realizations: intrinsic/threshold homeostasis (fast set-point), multiplicative synaptic scaling (**Turrigiano 2008**, *Cell* 135:422-435 — multiplicative ⇒ preserves learned relative weights), or divisive normalization (**Carandini-Heeger 2012**, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 13:51-62 — the canonical cortical gain-control). Four cheap burst-native sub-levers the family already validates, in priority: (i) **keep the apical/burst feedback weights `Y` SMALL** — BurstCCN Appendix B.1 proves this bounds the per-layer apical-error norm (`‖u_ℓ‖ ≤ ‖u_{ℓ+1}‖`), preventing depth blow-up/decay directly; (ii) a **regularizing soma→dendrite feedback** for baseline burst-rate control (Stuck-Naud's own depth remedy); (iii) a **signed two-event error channel** (separate +/− error events — Stuck-Naud show unsigned ~64% vs signed 97.2%; a large depth-relevant gain if the current substrate uses a one-sided burst estimate); (iv) **longer burst-averaging windows at deeper layers** to fight per-layer estimator noise (the literature-omitted, cheap, untested lever — the burst fraction is a moving-average estimator whose sampling noise plausibly compounds ~per layer).
- **Why it helps depth:** directly repairs axis (b) — kills the exponential ‖B‖/‖W‖ (equivalently the per-layer scale) drift Moskovitz 2018 identifies; keeps the burst-probability error estimator in its usable range at every layer (the burst signal saturates/vanishes outside a rate window).
- **Transport-free:** YES. **Settling-free:** YES (runs concurrently, no phase). **Single-phase:** YES. **Spiking-realizable:** YES — **the project already has homeostasis kernels** (`fused_homeostasis_update`, `sim/kernels.py`); this is the cheapest possible add.
- **Cheapest de-risk:** add a per-layer target-rate homeostatic gain to the depth-3 net; measure whether per-layer ‖update‖ ratios flatten across depth (they should) and whether held-out improves; anti-cheat: the gain must be set-point-only (no error/label leakage into the gain), and a permuted-error arm must still collapse.

### RANK 3 — µP / depth-µP static per-layer init+gain reparameterization — fixes MAGNITUDE in closed form (borrow from µPC WITHOUT settling)
- **What:** a STATIC per-layer scaling of init variance + gains so activations/updates stay O(1) across depth: hidden `(N·L)^{-1/2}` (the 1/√L residual-branch scaling), input `d_in^{-1/2}`, output `N^{-1}`, with standard-Gaussian init (µPC Table 1; the general principle is µP / Yang-Hu 2021 "Tensor Programs V" arXiv 2203.03466, and depth-µP / Tensor Programs VI 2023).
- **Why it helps depth:** µPC (arXiv 2505.13124) identifies the deep pathology as (i) ill-conditioning growing with depth AND (ii) **the forward pass vanishing/exploding with depth**; the `1/√(N·L)` scaling makes ‖z_ℓ‖~O(1) depth-invariant. It is architectural, NOT tied to any phase — so it drops into a single-phase spiking rule as a fixed per-layer gain.
- **Transport-free:** YES. **Settling-free:** YES (it is init/gain, not a phase — the settling is µPC's PC machinery, which we DISCARD). **Single-phase:** YES. **Spiking-realizable:** YES (just per-layer scalar gains). **Caveat:** µPC's BP-equivalence only holds width≫depth — treat it as an init/gain recipe, not a guarantee.
- **Cheapest de-risk:** a one-line init change; complements RANK 2 (static prior vs runtime controller). De-risk together with RANK 2; if the runtime homeostat already flattens the scale drift, µP is redundant polish — try it only if homeostasis alone leaves residual depth-scale drift.

### RANK 4 — Sign-concordant feedback (uSF) — a cheaper PARTIAL direction-fix
- **What:** keep only the SIGN agreement of forward/backward weights: `B = |B₀| ⊙ sign(W)` (Liao 2016 CBMM; Xiao 2018 arXiv 1811.03567). Rescues depth where FA fails (ImageNet ResNet-18: sign-sym 37.9% vs FA 90.5% vs BP 33.1%).
- **Why it helps depth:** restores per-layer alignment (axis a) with a weaker requirement than full KP — only sign, not magnitude.
- **Transport-free:** PARTIAL (the feedback synapse must know the SIGN of its forward partner — a weaker, but non-zero, transport of information). **Settling-free:** YES. **Single-phase:** YES but **needs BatchNorm/Batch-Manhattan to be stable** (Liao 2016 calls it "indispensable") — BatchNorm is awkward online/spiking (though RANK 2 homeostasis is its spiking-native substitute). **Spiking-realizable:** partial.
- **Cheapest de-risk:** trivial rate-numpy arm (sign(W) feedback) as a CONTROL/fallback if RANK-1 learned feedback is unstable; not the primary because sign-transport is a (small) plausibility compromise vs RANK-1's fully-local learning.

### RANK 5 — Interneuron feedback-rank preservation (the D2-specific on-substrate arm) — the microcircuit's genuine DEPTH role
- **What:** the SST/inhibitory ensemble that cancels the predictable top-down (already built as `enable_bdsp_microcircuit`, `W_PI = −Y`) is, at depth, ALSO the mechanism that keeps the burst-mediated feedback FULL-RANK/clean. Greedy-Costa 2026: reducing SST population size reduces the feedback rank and impairs learning, and "reductions in LATER areas produced the strongest impairment… preserving the dimensionality of burst-mediated feedback becomes increasingly important deeper in the hierarchy."
- **Why it helps depth:** this is the direct test of the D2 hypothesis — that the interneuron cancellation (not load-bearing for accuracy at depth-2 per D1) becomes CAUSALLY load-bearing at depth-3, because a point-neuron spiking layer cannot carry a clean, full-rank continuous error without the physical cancellation, and burst-noise/low-rank compounds over depth.
- **Transport-free / settling-free / single-phase / spiking-realizable:** YES on all (it is the existing on-bridge cancellation).
- **Cheapest de-risk:** this is NOT a separate build — it is the DECISIVE on-bridge depth-3 arm the D2 spec already names: run the depth-3 spiking microcircuit WITH vs WITHOUT the interneuron cancellation and measure per-layer alignment + held-out. If cancellation-on holds and cancellation-off degrades toward the FA floor AT DEPTH-3 (where at depth-2 it did not matter), the interneuron's depth role is demonstrated. If it does NOT differ, the honest boundary → escalate to RANK-1 (learned Y).

### DISQUALIFIED (diagnostic-only — borrow the insight, not the machinery)
- **Predictive coding / µPC (arXiv 2505.13124):** needs a settling/relaxation inference phase (DISQUALIFYING). Borrowed: the per-layer `1/√(NL)` scaling (RANK 3) and the "forward-pass vanish/explode is the depth killer" diagnosis.
- **Equilibrium Propagation (Scellier-Bengio 2017):** needs free+nudged settling phases (DISQUALIFYING) and the base form is weight-symmetric (NOT transport-free). Borrowed: the small-perturbation/weak-feedback regime insight — keeping feedback a small, matched perturbation of the forward equilibrium makes the local rule approximate the true gradient (this is WHY QY-symmetry / balanced feedback stabilizes depth).
- **Target propagation / DTP (Lee 2015; Meulemans 2020):** transport-free and (vanilla) single-phase, and theoretically better-conditioned (Gauss-Newton-hybrid, Meulemans Thm 2), BUT costs a learned inverse network per layer, its strong DRL variant reintroduces phases, and demonstrated depth is modest/invertibility-dependent. A second-wave option only if RANK 1-3 plateau.
- **Weight Mirror (Akrout 2019):** matches BP but needs a separate noisy "mirror mode" phase (an extra operating mode) — KP is the single-phase sibling to prefer.

## PART 4 — THE #1 RECOMMENDED FIRST SURPASS (if D2 rung-1 confirms a depth-3 FA wall)
**LEARN the apical feedback (RANK 1: Y-plasticity / Kolen-Pollack) + a per-layer homeostatic gain (RANK 2), applied to the existing microcircuit at depth-3.**

Rationale:
- The two are the exact pair the literature says depth needs — RANK 1 fixes DIRECTION (the primary axis; the deepest-from-output layers stop being served by a random projection they can't align to), RANK 2 fixes MAGNITUDE (kills the compounding per-layer scale drift AND keeps the burst estimator in-range). Neither alone is predicted to suffice at ImageNet depth (Moskovitz 2018 needs sign+norm together); at depth-3 one may suffice, which is exactly what the ablation measures.
- Both are **already substrate-native and single-phase/transport-free**: Y-plasticity is apical-local Hebbian on the existing `cp_v_apical`; the homeostatic gain reuses `fused_homeostasis_update`. Likely **NO new `sim/` mechanism class** — additive to the `enable_bdsp_microcircuit` path.
- It has a **direct, demonstrated precedent on this exact substrate class**: Greedy-Costa 2026 rode precisely this (learned apical burst-feedback establishing QY-balance, interneuron rank-preservation, homeostatic feedback control) to depth-8. The surpass is not speculative.
- It is **never-wasted**: learned apical feedback + per-layer homeostasis is the correct deep-cortex substrate regardless of the depth-3 outcome, and it directly enables the RANK-5 on-bridge interneuron-at-depth test.

**Cheapest de-risk ladder (single-variable, gate each rung):**
1. **Rate-numpy precondition (hours):** first CONFIRM plain-FA actually degrades at depth-3 in the same harness (per-layer alignment + held-out vs depth-2), and the fenced-backprop oracle clears ≥0.80 at depth-3 (else INCONCLUSIVE — the D1 lr-destabilizes-oracle lesson). If plain-FA does NOT degrade at depth-3, the wall is deeper → escalate to depth-4 before adding any fix (don't fix a non-existent wall).
2. **Rate-numpy surpass (hours):** add Y-plasticity (or KP) to `MicrocircuitBDSPNet` at depth-3, ± per-layer homeostatic gain (4 arms: neither / Y-only / gain-only / both). GATE: (learned-Y and/or gain) held-out > plain-FA + 0.10 AND per-layer-1 alignment does not collapse. Report the batch sweep (D1's like-for-like lesson).
3. **On-bridge depth-3 spiking (decisive, GPU, multi-seed):** the two-compartment microcircuit with plastic apical `Y` + per-layer homeostasis, credit through 3 spiking layers; per-layer on-bridge alignment; the RANK-5 interneuron-cancellation-on-vs-off ablation AT depth-3. Pre-registered GATE: held-out ≥ 0.75 AND > plain-FA + 0.10 AND layer-1 alignment does not collapse; anti-cheats hold.

## PART 5 — Anti-cheat controls the surpass needs (reuse D1's 7 + 3 depth-specific)
Reuse the D1 seven verbatim: **fixed-vs-learned feedback** (both work ⇒ no transport — but NOTE: for RANK-1 the whole point is learned>fixed, so the no-transport proof shifts to the explicit probe below) · **permuted-error → chance** · **wrong-sign apical → anti-learns** · **apical-lesion → memorization floor** · **no-teaching null → hidden-drift ~0** · **oracle-ceiling ≥0.80 else INCONCLUSIVE** · **memorization-floor (single-layer)**.

Three DEPTH-specific additions (new for D2 / the learned-feedback surpass):
1. **No-weight-transport probe for the LEARNED feedback (load-bearing for RANK 1/3/4).** Assert the `Y`/`B` update NEVER reads any forward `W` or `Wᵀ`: it uses only local pre/post activity (KP: `y`, `δ`) or the apical Hebbian term. For sign-concordance (RANK 4), explicitly FLAG the sign(W) read as a partial-transport compromise (which is why RANK 4 ranks below RANK 1). A learned-`Y` net whose `Y` update secretly used `Wᵀ` would be backprop-in-disguise — the primary cheat to exclude.
2. **Like-for-like at matched depth AND batch (the D1 lesson, elevated).** Compare every arm (oracle / plain-FA / microcircuit / learned-Y / +homeostasis / burstprop / single-layer) at the SAME depth, SAME batch, SAME epochs/lr; report the batch sweep. NEVER a batch- or epoch-mismatched headline (the D1 0.66-vs-0.96 confound).
3. **Depth-CAUSALITY controls (the surpass must be DEPTH-specific, not a generic accuracy bump).**
   - The surpass mechanism must help MORE at depth-3 than depth-2 (else it is a generic regularizer, not a depth-stability fix). Report the depth-2 vs depth-3 delta for each fix.
   - **Per-layer alignment must be the gated metric, not just accuracy:** cos(update, oracle-BP-update) per layer, and it must be layer-1 (deepest-from-output) that the fix rescues (Moskovitz's failure is specifically lower layers). An accuracy win with FLAT-vs-oracle layer-1 alignment would be a forward-memorization artifact, not credit propagation.
   - For RANK 5, the interneuron ablation must degrade AT depth-3 while (per D1) it did NOT at depth-2 — the differential is the claim.
