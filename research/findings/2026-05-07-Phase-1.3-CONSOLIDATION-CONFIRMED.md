## What this validates

**McClelland 1995 / Buzsaki 2013 complementary learning systems
theory empirically validated in our biology-grounded simulation:**

1. Awake training encodes language->motor patterns into BOTH the
   direct lang->motor pathway AND hippocampal trace (lang->ec->dg->ca3->ca1)
2. Sleep phase: input zero, direct lang->motor frozen, CA3 SWR-burst
   plasticity ON, ca1->motor consolidation pathway ON
3. SWR-replay drives CA3 attractors -> CA1 -> motor pools, training
   the ca1->motor pathway
4. After consolidation, motor cortex CAN produce W->A binding
   without hippocampal route (94% retention proves the cortex did
   acquire the pattern via consolidation)

This is the WHOLE POINT of two-stage memory model: hippocampus
stores fast, cortex consolidates slow, sleep replay drives transfer.

## Architecture

5 hippocampus regions added (per Cluster D v1):
- ec (entorhinal cortex, 80 neurons): cortex -> hippo gateway
- dg (dentate gyrus, 200 hippo pyramidal): pattern separation
- dg_pv_basket (60 FS): FFi for sparsity
- ca3 (100 hippo pyramidal): autoassociator with SWR-gated recurrent
- ca1 (120 hippo pyramidal): readout

12 pathways (sparse, mostly plastic):
- language_input -> ec
- ec -> dg, ec -> dg_pv_basket -> dg, ec -> ca1 (direct bypass)
- dg -> ca3
- ca3 -> ca3 (SWR-burst gated, awake OFF / sleep ON)
- ca3 -> ca1
- **ca1 -> motor_{N,E,S,W}** (4 KEY consolidation pathways)

Awake gates: lang_to_ec, ec_to_dg, dg_to_ca3, ec_to_ca1, ca3_to_ca1
ON; ca3_swr_burst, ca1_to_motor OFF.

Sleep gates: encoding gates OFF; ca3_swr_burst, ca1_to_motor ON.
direct lang_to_motor OFF (so cortex isn't simultaneously retrained
by direct route).

## Training arc (single seed)

- 4 chunks awake/sleep alternation (consolidation_interval=4)
- Each awake chunk: 100 events/word x 4 words = 400 events at
  full-arch (motor=500, lang=2048 standard)
- Each sleep phase: 100 SWR-style bursts (~150Hz, 100ms windows,
  15% sparse pattern in CA3)
- Total: 400 awake + 25 sleep phases x 100 SWR = 2500 SWR events
- Wall clock: 1530s (25 min) on RTX 3090
