## The program-level finding (precise, structural, not a tuning gap)

Two faithful applications of the validated concept-binding mechanism
(interleaved encode order; single-cue-to-target co-firing) each
recovered working-memory role-selectivity **but destroyed the loop's
now-perfect episodic binding** (episodic score fell from perfect to
zero). The cause is structural and was confirmed on the GPU:

- The validated concept-binding mechanism requires a **shuffled encode
  order** — that is precisely how it breaks the within-kind
  winner-take-all dominant-attractor so multiple same-kind pools become
  individually selectable.
- The theta-gamma episodic store structurally requires **presentation
  order to equal binding index** — the recovered sequence is read from
  the order of per-item activity peaks, so a shuffled encode order
  destroys episodic recall by construction.

These two validated subsystems therefore impose **contradictory
encode-order requirements** when forced into a single online encode
pass at the minimal two-binding slice. No configuration reconciles
them — verified by direct GPU measurement, not inferred.

## The biology reading of the conflict (why this redirects rather than ends the program)

The conflict is itself diagnostic. In the brain these two operations
are **not one encode pass with one shared order constraint**. Online
hippocampal episodic encoding binds items-in-sequence in a single
theta-ordered pass (order is carried by phase). Neocortical concept
selectivity is built by **interleaved replay during offline
consolidation** (replay reorders experience across many offline cycles
— complementary learning systems), not during the online episodic
encode. The encode-order contradiction iteration 4 measured is exactly
the signature expected if the integration has **conflated two
biologically distinct phases** into one pass.

The project already has the validated subsystem for the offline phase
(the complementary-learning-systems consolidation work, validated
multi-seed with strict anti-cheat). The biology-identified, genuinely
distinct next architecture is therefore: **factor the loop into an
online theta-ordered episodic encode and a separate offline
shuffled-replay consolidation that builds the concept selectivity** —
so the two validated subsystems each operate in the phase whose
order-requirement they satisfy, instead of contending for one pass.
This is not a configuration iteration and not a tweak of the binding
rule; it is a distinct architectural factorization that the reference
biology and the project's own validated consolidation subsystem
support.
