## What 2.5 is (and what it deliberately is NOT)

A bare SVO triple has no TIME: "boy go park" can't say whether the boy WENT, GOES, or WILL GO. **Tense is the
cheapest temporal-representation step** (generation-control research front-4 #3; Levelt's positional level inserts
tense; Hagoort MUC's closed-class scaffold; Bock & Levelt 1994). This binds a TENSE role-tag (PAST/PRESENT/FUTURE,
cleaned ONLY against a 3-word codebook) onto each stored fact, reads it back at render time, and DRIVES the surface
verb form via a tense-aware inflection table.

**HONEST BOUNDARY (NOT claimed):** this is tense/aspect as a single **3-valued metadata TAG** on a fact (the
cheapest viable temporal layer). It is NOT full event-semantics — no Reichenbach reference-time, no perfect-vs-
progressive aspect, no Davidsonian event-argument representation, no event chaining over a timeline. Those are the
deferred temporal-reasoning frontier (Tier 3). **PBWM gating is a SEPARATE control layer and a deferred follow-on**
(see below) — this task is scoped to the tense/aspect REPRESENTATION only, per the roadmap split.

## The mechanism it composes (no research gate)

`RFPhasorComposer` binds a POLARITY role (AFFIRM/NEGATE) onto a fact, cleaned only against `pol_words`, and reads
it back at query time (`ask_yes_no`; `rf_phasor_composer.py:159-162, 528-544, 802-822`). `CommonGroundComposer`
(commit `43f6bda4`) is the SAME bind-a-tag-on-a-role-cleaned-against-a-small-codebook pattern at a SHARED/PRIVATE
role. **Tense is that pattern AGAIN** at a TENSE role: PAST/PRESENT/FUTURE cleaned only against `tense_words`. Plus
the no-confab moat (a never-stored fact reads tag=None; a render over an unknown subject returns None).

The render integration is the new piece: `ArgStructureComposer` already produces agreement morphology — its
`_decode_unit_word` TENSE branch reads the verb from the RF unbind (`unbind(comp,'action')`) and applies the
PRESENT-3sg inflection (`TENSE_3SG`). The Tier 2.5 subclass **overrides that branch** to first READ the bound
tense tag off the composite (RF unbind + cleanup against `tense_words`), then inflect the verb ACCORDINGLY:
PAST via an irregular/regular past table, PRESENT via the parent's `TENSE_3SG`, FUTURE via "will &lt;bare verb&gt;".
So the bound tag DRIVES the surface form; the rest of the render is the parent's frame path (FrameCQ orders the
content slots; the closed-class scaffold supplies determiners/prepositions).

### The D=64 → D=128 lever (honest)

At **D=64** the gate read **NO-GO by a hair** (aggregate tag-fidelity 0.981): seed 101 dropped to 0.889 on ONE
fact. Root-caused precisely — it is **NOT a tense-mechanism failure**:
- On the dense `dog come home [FUTURE]` composite (4 bound roles: agent + action + GOAL + TENSE), the **GOAL** cue
  role mis-cleaned (`home` → `go`) at D=64; the TENSE tag itself read back **FUTURE faithfully**.
- `read_tense` matches the whole-fact cue (agent+action+obliques), so a mis-cleaned GOAL made the cue match fail →
  `read_tense` returned **None** (abstain), **never a WRONG tense**. (And `render_fidelity` stayed 1.000 even at
  D=64 — the render reaches the composite via the looser agent+action match.)

This is the well-documented ±-bundle cleanup SNR limit at a too-tight D with 4 bound roles, hitting an *unrelated*
cue role — and **raising D is the standard VSA bundle-SNR lever** (the production composers run D=2048). D=128
clears all 6 seeds cleanly (tag + render fidelity 1.000), so D=128 is the de-risk + CI-guard default. The miss was
abstain-safe (the moat property — never a fabricated/wrong tense), which is the safe failure mode.

## Anti-cheats (each reported)

- **tag read-fidelity ≫ chance:** 1.000 vs chance 1/3 (D=128, 6 seeds). ✓
- **correct surface form (went/goes/will-go):** render-fidelity 1.000 — every fact's rendered surface tense matches
  its stored tag; `inflect('go', PAST/PRESENT/FUTURE) = went/goes/will go` (irregular `come`→`came`, regular
  `walk`→`walked`, unknown `jump`→`jumped`). ✓
- **permuted collapses:** storing the same facts with a SHUFFLED tag assignment makes the rendered tense track the
  permuted tag → agreement with the true tense drops to 0.315 ≈ chance. The form follows the REAL tag. ✓
- **lesion collapses:** severing the tense read forces PRESENT regardless of the stored tag → agreement collapses to
  0.333 (the present-only fraction) and every lesioned render is in fact present-tense. ✓
- **moat 0-FA:** a never-stored cue (incl. right-(agent,action)/wrong-GOAL `boy go school` vs stored `boy go park`)
  reads tense=None; a render over an unknown subject (`horse go park`) returns None. 0 false-accepts across 6 seeds. ✓
- **6 seeds:** 42/43/44/100/101/102, every metric above. ✓
