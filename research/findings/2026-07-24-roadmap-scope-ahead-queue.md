**mechanism:** GNW ignition + re-entry (Dehaene). The workspace is a small pool of long-range recurrent neurons that IGNITES all-or-none: sub-threshold input produces feed-forward waves that (Dehaene-Changeux 2011, Neuron 70:200-227, full-text read in the 2026-07-07 research gate) "died out without triggering late global activation because insufficient self-sustaining reverberant activity was generated," while above threshold NMDA-dependent recurrent excitation yields a self-amplifying, all-or-none, SUSTAINED assembly. The re-entry: workspace neurons "selectively mobilize or suppress, through descending connections" the modular processors (Dehaene-Kerszberg-Changeux 1998, PNAS 95:14529), and the ignited content is broadcast BACK — so feeding the ignited conclusion of cycle k in as cycle k+1's drive is Dehaene's recurrent ignition ITERATED = a train of thought. Per-cycle COMMIT = bounded evidence accumulation to a threshold: LIP firing "≈ accumulated logLR ... reaches a common bound right before action" (Kandel 6e Ch 56 pp 1402-1404, catalog G.17; the Wang-2002 NMDA attractor / Lo-Wang commit-burst already in-project for nav). Single-content mutual exclusion (one coalition ignites at a time = one train of thought) is enforced by shared FS lateral inhibition (Baars spotlight; validated in Rung-2). So each hop = PROPOSE (the currently-ignited content's learned relational neighbours drive candidate assemblies) -> EVALUATE (mutual-inhibition WTA + accumulate to ignition threshold) -> COMMIT (winner ignites, sustained, broadcast back to re-cue the next hop; moat abstains if nothing crosses threshold). This surpasses the transformer the GWT paper (Gurnee et al. 2026, §9.3) says only emulates broadcast "within a single feedforward pass rather than through recurrent loops."
