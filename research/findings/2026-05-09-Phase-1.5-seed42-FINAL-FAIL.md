## Failure mode 1: interference (0.34)

Trains 8-word synonym vocab INTERLEAVED (random word per event), then
evaluates all 8. Per-word accuracy:

```
north: 52%   up:    28%
east:  56%   right:  8%   ← right is the worst, near chance
south: 40%   down:  24%
west:  32%   left:  32%
mean:  0.34   min:    8%
```

`right` at 8% is well below 25% chance — catastrophic forgetting of
that specific word. Other words cluster in the 24-56% range.

**Hypothesis (from earlier doc):** events_per_word=200 is below the
validated Tier 2.1 dose of 400. The v400 chain script
(scripts/chain_phase_1_5_to_v400_interference.sh) auto-fires after
the seed 43+44 batch completes to test this.

**Counter-evidence:** sequential_expansion got 0.885 at the SAME
events_per_word=200. So the event count works for sequential
training. The interleaved training is what changes — different
words compete for the same motor pool capacity, and at n_motor=1000
the per-word allotment may be too narrow.

## Failure mode 2: long_tail (0.17)

Trains 8-word vocab with imbalanced frequency:
- 4 "common" words at 200 events each (matches sequential)
- 4 "rare" words at 10 events each (events_per_word // 20)

```
Common words (200 events):   Rare words (10 events):
  north: 68%   south: 28%      up:    24%   down: 20%
  east:  36%   west:  56%      right:  8%   left: 16%
  common_acc: 0.47              rare_acc: 0.17
```

Score = rare_acc = 0.17 (threshold: 0.30). Common words bind partially
(47%); rare words do not (17%, near chance 25%).

**This is a structural low-shot problem.** 10 events is genuinely few
— biology has the same limit. Critical period plasticity in real
brains lasts months and exposes infants to thousands of word events.
Asking the network to bind a word from 10 events is asking for
human-level few-shot learning, which is well beyond current neural
architectures.

**This benchmark may be too strict for the current architecture.**
Options:
1. Raise events_per_word_rare from `events_per_word // 20` to
   `events_per_word // 4` (= 50 events at default, 100 at v400)
2. Accept long_tail as a known limitation and exclude from the master
   plan threshold (or weight it less)
3. Architecturally — add a meta-learning component (synaptic tag-and-
   capture, tagged eligibility) that lets few-shot bindings persist

For the immediate Phase 1.5 milestone, option 1 (relaxing the dose
ratio) is the simplest. Option 3 is the right long-term answer.
