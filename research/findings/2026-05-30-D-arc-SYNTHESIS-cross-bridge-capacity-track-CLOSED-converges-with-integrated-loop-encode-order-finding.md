This was acknowledged from the start (the "validated engineering scaffold, not a biological compositional result" reframe in capability_status). Brains do not compose by circular convolution over a fixed vocabulary.

## The one biology-translatable insight (the real deliverable)

Across the four tiers the arc produced exactly one durable, biology-translatable result, and it is genuinely worth keeping:

**Cortical-column-style DEDICATED representation is the cleanest cross-area composition substrate -- near-orthogonal grounded symbols -- but its orthogonality has a vocabulary ceiling set by per-region common-mode noise.**

- V=80 (n=108) / V=160 (n=109): dedicated-pool grounded symbols are near-orthogonal (abs mean-centred cos ~0.04), seed-robust, and BEAT both the G.20 sparse-distributed substrate (0.790) and the FHRR algebra's own uniform-random capacity prediction (which said composition should degrade with vocab). Dedicated > distributed > random, for clean composition.
- V=320 (n=110): the orthogonality becomes seed-SENSITIVE (seeds 42/44 ~0.06, seed 43 ~0.085) and one seed drops below bar. The per-region mean-centring (cortical pooled-inhibition analogue) that subtracts the common-mode gets noisier as concepts-per-region grows, so the near-orthogonality erodes. The envelope begins to bend at or below V=320.

The mechanism is fully characterized. Locating the bend to the concept (380? 450? 600?) would refine a scaffold number without adding biological insight -- hence the track is closed here.
