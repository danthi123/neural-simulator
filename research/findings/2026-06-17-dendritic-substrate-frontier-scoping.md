## 1. DIAGNOSIS — what the dendritic substrate genuinely unlocks (post-reframe, honest)

### 1.1 What a point neuron provably cannot do (the real, citable limit — unchanged)
A point neuron (`v,u` Izhikevich; `v,w` AdEx; `V,m,h,n[…]` HH) sums **all** synaptic input — excitation, inhibition, NMDA, the coincidence plateau, neuromodulator drive, OU noise — into one scalar `total_input_current_pA` at a single somatic compartment, then updates a single soma state **before** the spike decision (`sim/bridge.py::_run_one_simulation_step`, the current-accumulation phase + the model dispatch at ~line 5956). Because excitation and inhibition are **averaged together at one point**, a single global inhibitory pool can only apply *uniform* suppression. The peer-reviewed limit (**Mikulasch, Rudelt, Priesemann, PNAS 2021**, arXiv 2010.12395, PMC8685685, re-verified from the abstract this pass): whitening/decorrelation of *correlated* inputs requires lateral inhibition that matches each correlated pair's *specific* shared correlation — an **input-specific** quantity that must be computed **locally, per dendritic compartment, at the analog membrane-voltage level BEFORE the soma spikes**, where the recurrent inhibition locally cancels the predictable common mode (`u_ji ∝ F_ji·(x_i − x̂_i)` = the local coding error). The paper's own claim, verified: this works "robustly even for complex, high-dimensional and correlated inputs, and with inhibitory transmission delays, where Hebbian-like plasticity fails." **This limit is real and the catalog confirms the capability is MISSING** (`sim-catalog/.../feature-catalog.md` **G.02 Active dendrites — MISSING**, "one of the largest abstractions in the simulator," ~10× compute/neuron; plus B.17 dendritic linearization, D.* CA1 distal apical, F.* cerebellar granule max-pool, I.* multi-compartment channels — all flagged needing compartments). So **morphologically and per the catalog, the dendrite is a genuine, unbuilt capability.** The question this doc answers is the *functional* one: does the project's actual open goal still NEED it?

### 1.2 The five point-neuron NEGATIVEs — real, and they DID converge on the limit
All five committed NEGATIVEs (vanilla Hopfield common-mode collapse; Storkey locality wall; spiking dentate-gyrus sub-reproducible read; fixed-random/Marr-Albus expansion common-mode survival; **Option C learn-from-text `Pearson(S_learned,S_true) = −0.008` while host PPMI+SVD control = +0.532**) operate on the **spike side** (or a single threshold on a summed-at-soma activation), where the small concept-distinguishing residual sits under a large correlated common mode at low SNR. Option C is the sharpest: the structure IS in the data (host +0.532, all 8 categories) but the point-neuron Hebbian learn recovered none of it — exactly the Mikulasch-Priesemann prediction. **This was the basis for the 2026-06-14 fork-resolved decision** (`docs/plans/2026-06-14-step3-cortex-fork-resolved-dendritic-D2-decision.md`): "(B) a semantically-structured cortex cannot be done on point neurons; the dendrite is the validated escape; build D2 (owner-gated)."

### 1.4 So what IS the genuine residual dendritic question? (narrow, and artificial-life-flavoured, not conversational)
After the reframe, exactly one piece of the original motivation survives as a real point-neuron limit that the dendrite *might* address — and it is small and uncertain:

> **The OFF-DIAGONAL residual.** PPMI's *diagonal* normalization caps at ~+0.31 of the off-diagonal-inclusive structure on the *real* corpus; the host PPMI+SVD optimum is +0.44; the offline rank-8 ZCA (which *does* the cross-neuron whitening) reaches +0.49. The +0.13 gap between diagonal-PPMI and host is the **off-diagonal (cross-neuron, low-rank) decorrelation**, and **eight prior multi-seed point-neuron online probes provably cannot reach it** (`2026-06-15-natural-learning-over-time-CONVERGES-on-locality-wall.md`, `-offdiagonal-decorrelation-local-mechanism-deep-research.md`): a LEARNED feedforward Oja-W collapses onto 1–4 PCs (the "vicious cycle"); a FIXED random W over-whitens (eff-rank 44); only the offline SVD selects exactly the top-8 informative directions AND whitens them. The dendritic-predictive-coding family (Mikulasch error-gated feedforward `ΔF ∝ z·(x−x̂)` + the Duong-Lipshutz fixed-frame+plastic-gains circuit) is the *named biological escape* that breaks both failure modes by construction (error-gating defeats the Oja collapse; a bounded interneuron pool sized = rank defeats over-whitening).

**Honest framing of this residual (the crux of the whole scoping):**
1. **It is the OFF-DIAGONAL, not whitening-to-bind.** The binding-input version of "whiten to bind" was *separately falsified* even for an ideal whitening (`2026-06-11-option-B-whitening-derisk-NEGATIVE`: denoise64 codes are sub-reproducible at σ=0.1 *raw*; whitening amplifies the low-variance noise) — a dendrite cannot manufacture missing input SNR. That version is dead and already solved by generated decorrelated codes + the dual/CLS architecture. The off-diagonal residual is a *different*, narrower target: the small cross-neuron decorrelation that *learning the cortex from experience* leaves on the table beyond local PPMI.
2. **Its recoverability is genuinely uncertain — and the uncertainty is DATA, not mechanism.** Even the *offline* optimum on this corpus is only +0.44; the off-diagonal residual is +0.13; the project has repeatedly found this corpus's structure weak/overlapping (k-means ceiling +0.217, not cleanly block-separable). Whether a +0.13 residual on a moderate-SNR corpus is cleanly separable *online* is the open question — and a NEGATIVE would be a *data-marginality* result, not a mechanism failure.
3. **It is not load-bearing for the conversational product.** The PPMI cortex at +0.518 *already exceeds* the +0.49 offline-ZCA target and ships the full capability. The off-diagonal residual matters for the *artificial-life / "how much structure can the substrate learn from raw experience with local rules"* goal, NOT for shipping conversation. This is the correct, honest place to locate the dendrite's value: **a biology-translatable science question (can local dendritic predictive coding recover cross-neuron structure a point neuron cannot, on real experience), not a capability the product needs.**

### 1.5 What Phase 3 (the pending step) concretely is — and why it is now mostly redundant
**Phase 3, exactly** (`docs/plans/2026-06-14-D2-dendritic-cortex-build-plan.md` line 21–22, verbatim): "plug into the validated dual/CLS pipeline + conversational gates — the learned graded codes → the already-validated bind/unbind → attractor cleanup → the full conversational matrix. **GATE:** generalization-in-conversation (answer about a held-out concept via a *similar* known one — what the idealized composer algebra could NOT) WITH the no-confab moat intact (zero false-accepts), multi-seed."

Generalization-in-conversation about a held-out/novel concept via a similar known one, with the moat intact, multi-seed, is **DONE** (`2026-06-16-generalization-capstone-verbalize.md`: a novel object's spiking concept recalls the matched category's fact, 0.92 3-seed, moat refused to weaken; `2026-06-16-harden-320-stream-cortex-learned-moat-GO.md`: 320-concept stream cortex, recall 1.00, moat clean). **So D2 Phase 3 would re-deliver, via a months-scale dendritic build, a capability the point-neuron substrate already delivers.** It is only non-redundant if the *off-diagonal residual* (§1.4) materially improves generalization beyond what PPMI achieves — which is exactly the cheap de-risk's job to measure, and is currently un-measured.

**Critical caveat on Phase 1+2:** the BUILT Phase 1 gain is the **per-hub DIAGONAL divisive gain** — the *diagonal* operation that the PPMI reframe showed point neurons already do (threshold + integration). It is **NOT** the **OFF-DIAGONAL** mechanism (§1.4 — the Mikulasch error-gated feedforward / Duong-Lipshutz interneuron circuit), which is *cross-neuron* and was **never built and never even de-risked in numpy.** So the project built the wrong-half dendritic mechanism (diagonal, which the substrate didn't need) and has **not** built or de-risked the right-half (off-diagonal, the only piece that might still matter).
