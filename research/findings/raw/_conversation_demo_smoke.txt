=== Spiking conversational agent (backend=cupy, seed=42, vocab 16) ===
(statements are bound + stored in spiking; questions answered by spiking relational query)

-- Turn 1: the user tells the agent some facts --
  > dog go north.            [stored]
  > cat come south.            [stored]

-- Turn 2: the user asks questions --
  ? who go north?            -> dog
  ? what does dog go?        -> north
  ? who come south?          -> cat

-- Turn 3: persist + reload (a fresh session), then keep answering --
  [reloaded 2 facts into a fresh substrate]
  ? who go north?            -> dog   (recalled across the session boundary)
  + the user adds a new fact this session:
  > cat stop big.            [stored]
  ? who go north?            -> dog   (old fact still known)

Everything -- storing facts, answering wh-questions, persisting across sessions -- runs as spiking compositional binding on real substrate concepts. Honest scope: 16-word vocab, SVO; the recognition front-end (not the composition) caps vocabulary growth.
