## The claim + mechanism
The surface-fluency research gate (`2026-07-02-open-domain-surface-fluency-research-gate.md`) reframed open-domain fluency: a high-order sequence model over word CLASSES (parts of speech) with open content slots IS a construction grammar (Goldberg; Diessel; Tomasello), and its signature is SYSTEMATIC RECOMBINATION (Fodor-Pylyshyn) — producing a grammatical sentence whose content-word combination was never seen. The `sim/` kernel is unchanged; the only change is a two-level word encoding: each word = its POS-CLASS micro-columns (SHARED by all words of that class) + a content micro-column (unique). The cortex learns the FRAME as a high-order sequence over the shared class micro-columns, so the learned frame GENERALIZES to novel content: after "the dog chased the" (DET NOUN VERB DET), the shared class cells drive the learned "…comes NOUN" pathway → it predicts a NOUN for ANY content → a held-out content combination is predicted grammatically.

## Result — GO (6/6 seeds)
Frame DET NOUN VERB DET NOUN; TRAIN on 4 content combinations; TEST the next-POS-class prediction on 3 HELD-OUT novel content combinations ("the dog ate the fish", "the fish saw the man", "the cat chased the bird" — never trained); seeds 42/43/44/100/101/102:
- **Held-out systematic recombination (next-POS-class) 1.000** (all seeds) — every held-out novel content combination is predicted grammatically (the correct POS class at each frame position).
- **CLASS-DERANGEMENT control ~0.17 (collapses)** — when content words carry the WRONG class blocks (the POS-class structure is scrambled), the frame cannot generalize. This isolates the POS-CLASS structure as the cause of the recombination.
- **dAP-LESION 0.000, untrained 0.000.** No teacher. Multi-seed.
- The PERMUTED-frame control (train on POS-shuffled sentences) is NOISY (mean 0.417 across 6 seeds, with a seed-43 outlier of 1.000) — inherent to POS-class prediction with only 3 classes, where a scrambled training frame can coincidentally reproduce correct transitions on the small held-out set. Held-out recombination (1.000 all seeds) still clears it by margin, but the CLEAN discriminator is the class-derangement control (0.181, collapses) — that is what isolates the POS-class structure as the cause. A stronger consistent-wrong-frame control is a noted follow-on for rigor.
