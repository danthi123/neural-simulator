## The decisive diagnosis — three converging facts
1. **The distillation premise is SOUND.** Offline, clip-aware weights reproduce the teacher through the stacked `clip` chain to 0.872 (pure clip) / 0.815 (df-aware) — vs the verbatim 0.288. The deterministic clip-compression wall the popcode-NEGATIVE identified IS trainable-around in the idealized forward, exactly as the scoping argued.
2. **The trained gains do NOT survive the install — the [VERIFY] `g·(V−E)` gap is the wall.** The installed per-block fidelity stays pinned at the verbatim levels for the pure-clip arm (`[0.846, 0.623, 0.307]` ≈ verbatim `[0.846, 0.620, 0.288]`): the live conductance `g·(V−E)` driving-force + the per-block gain calibration **re-impose the same compression regardless of what the trainer learned**. The driving-force-aware ARM2 — which approximates that sub-linearity in the trainer — lifts the install 0.307 → 0.444 (CONFIRMING the gap IS the conductance non-linearity), but the idealized 1-param-per-block `tanh` squash does not capture the live AdEx-settling + per-feature driving-force enough to close it.
3. **The shuffled-target anti-cheat FAILS, unmasking a metric confound.** The control (train to a deranged permutation of targets → install → score vs the REAL teacher) reaches **0.542 ≥ the real arms (0.307/0.444)**. Cause: the teacher's FINAL-block reps are **highly correlated across characters** — the shuffled trainer's early blocks correctly diverge from the real teacher (offline L0 0.417, L1 0.66 vs the real arms' 0.98/0.90) but its **final block still scores 0.772 vs the REAL teacher** (offline), because all chars' `t_3` look alike. The live bridge then produces a char-independent ~0.54 final-stage floor. Confirmed by **every arm's specificity margin being tiny** (verbatim 0.030, ARM1 0.036, ARM2 0.065 — matched ≈ mismatched): the final-block `a_cont` readout barely discriminates which char drove it, even for verbatim weights. ⇒ the cumulative-Spearman-vs-teacher metric at the final block is only weakly char-specific, so a generic high-activation pattern partly reaches it.

**Two honest secondary findings worth carrying forward:**
- **The driving-force term IS the dominant residual** (ARM2's +0.137 install lift over ARM1 from folding `g·(V−E)` into the trainer). An option-(a) backward through the *actual* `g·(V−E)` — not a `tanh` proxy — is the principled fix, and ARM2's partial success is positive evidence it would close more.

A future de-risk should add a CHAR-DISCRIMINATIVE readout objective (e.g. the next-token end-task head, the scoping's named polish) and/or a cross-char-decorrelated target so the shuffled-control can land at true chance — the current metric cannot cleanly separate "real recovery" from "generic pattern" on the live bridge.
