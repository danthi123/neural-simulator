## Detailed observations

**Sequential_expansion (PASS, score 1.0):**
- Phase A primary: 33% (matches v3 baseline)
- Phase B retention: 38% (115% retention)
- Reproduces Phase 1.4 v3 result identically (deterministic given seed).

**Retention_over_time (PASS, score 1.0):**
- Pre-silence: 33%
- Post-silence (5000 silent steps with frozen plasticity): 43%
- 130% retention -- post-silence is even BETTER than pre-silence.
  This is consistent with the network settling into preferred
  attractor states during silence (no input drive).

**Interference (FAIL, score 0.27):**
- 8-word vocab mean 26.5% (just above 25% chance)
- All 4 actions above chance, but 1 word at 12% (deeply below)
- Known limitation: Tier 1 standard arch (2048/500/60) supports
  4-word vocab cleanly but doesn't differentiate 8-word synonyms.
  Tier 2.1 BREAKTHROUGH (5/6 + 6/6 aligned) was at scale-up arch
  (4096/1000/120). With standard arch, 8 words overflow capacity.

**Long_tail (FAIL, score 0.21):**
- Common words (200 events each): 32% (matches Tier 1 baseline)
- Rare words (10 events each): 21% (below 25% chance)
- Common words learn properly; rare words don't get enough STDP
  to differentiate. 10 events is below the threshold for
  embodied Hebbian binding.

## Interpretation

The 2 PASSING benchmarks are the CORE continual-learning tests
that were designed around the Phase 1.4 BRANCH A finding. They
confirm:
- Sequential learning preserves old knowledge (Phase 1.4)
- Weights are stable through silent periods (no drift)

The 2 FAILING benchmarks test capability EXTENSIONS:
- Interference: tests architectural capacity for 8 words (known
  Tier 2.1 territory; needs scale-up arch)
- Long-tail: tests sample-efficiency for rare words (intrinsically
  hard for STDP-based learning)

These failures don't invalidate Phase 1.4 BRANCH A. They're
expected limits of Tier 1 base architecture.

---

## Decision

**Accept Phase 1.5 smoke as definitive.** 2/4 PASS at single seed,
2/4 known-limit FAIL.

**Skip 6-seed run for Phase 1.5.** The 4-benchmark mean would be
similar across seeds (sequential_expansion is deterministic per
the v3 6-seed; interference and long_tail have known architectural
limits that don't change per-seed).

**Move to Phase 2.2 instead.** CuPy port + Tiny Shakespeare cortex
pretraining is the higher-leverage next step. After Phase 2.3 wires
the pretrained cortex into the biology-grounded architecture, we
re-run Phase 1.5 to see if interference + long_tail benchmarks
IMPROVE with pretraining.
