## What this means

**The project's conversational foundation extends to 8-word
vocabulary with synonyms.** Combined with Tier 1 6/6 PASS today:

- 4-word direction vocab: 6/6 PASS, mean W→A 86%, A→W 98%
- 8-word synonym vocab: 6/6 PASS, mean W→A 60%, A→W 95%

Both architectures validated multi-seed on current code. The
multi-vocab conversational capability is solid.

A→W is rock-solid in both (95-98%). The W→A is harder for
synonym vocab (60% vs 86%) because synonyms with hash-based
embeddings are structurally less distinct than primary words.
This was documented in the 2026-05-06 finding and replicated
here.
