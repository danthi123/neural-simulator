## VALIDATED 6/6 on the real spiking substrate (GO)
The runner makes the EMERGE-82 spiking reservoir's RECURRENT synapses PLASTIC (`plastic_internal=True`), turns on the committed `enable_bdsp` Burstprop rule (`dw = eta·Ẽ_pre·(B − P̄·E)`), and injects the read-out error as apical credit `k·(Y @ δ)` — Y a fixed-random `[n_reservoir, V]` matrix from its own `RandomState` (**no weight transport**). Two-phase: phase-1 BDSP learns W_rec online; phase-2 a clean read-out is trained on the frozen learned reservoir; next-token CE-by-context-depth measured (fixed vs plastic vs true apical=0 lesion).
- **Wall #1 (RS Izhikevich neurons barely burst → B≈0) HANDLED.** `bdsp_apical_couples_soma=True` + a swept `bdsp_apical_soma_g` raises the measured burst rate B monotonically (0 → 0.059) and steers the recurrent-weight movement up to **~25× the apical=0 lesion** on a short probe. Apical → measured bursts → directed credit → steered W_rec.
- **The directed credit is CLEAN + DOMINANT, and the undirected drift is METRIC-INERT — 6/6 seeds.** With the corrected apical=0 lesion: plastic directed |Δw| (= plastic − lesion) is **4–7× the undirected `−P̄·E` drift**, and the drift alone (the lesion arm) changes the CE by ≤0.005 (|lesion CE − fixed CE|), i.e. it is metric-inert. So the credited weight movement is the real signal, not the drift. No weight transport, deterministic, byte-identical re-run.

Plastic beats fixed on **2/6** seeds (43/44, decisively, incl. mid/deep context), is neutral on 1, and LOSES on 3 — **mean plastic−fixed ≈ −0.003 (≈ neutral)**. So the clean, dominant, credited weight movement only SOMETIMES reduces next-token CE. Diagnosis: on-bridge BDSP credit is **burst-multiplexed FEEDBACK ALIGNMENT on a clean error** (`B` set by the fixed-random apical `Y@δ`) — a COARSER, noisier gradient approximation than the rate e-prop's forward-eligibility RFLO (which reached a clean 6/6 within-reach recovery). The coarse spiking credit steers W_rec in a roughly-credited direction that helps on some seeds and not others; it does NOT robustly realize the rate result.

## The microcircuit (clean-error) variant is REFUTED as the fix (do NOT pursue it for this)
A follow-on build wired the `enable_bdsp_microcircuit` interneuron cancellation (`cp_bdsp_int_drive = W^PI @ φ(u^I)`, W^PI a separate learned population, no weight transport) onto the reservoir. It does NOT help, for two structural reasons: (1) the injected apical is ALREADY the clean FA error `k·(Y@δ)`, whose target-innovation is not linearly predictable from the reservoir rate, so the interneuron cancels only ~8% (`resid_frac 0.92`) and has nothing meaningful to cancel; (2) **the microcircuit branch only reshapes the effective apical (`raw − int_drive`); it does NOT touch the `−P̄·E` weight-update baseline** — so by construction it cannot change the drift (which is anyway metric-inert). Micro ≈ plastic (~92% mechanically identical). The microcircuit's distinct D1 benefit was the M2.6 somatic-FF rule, which is NOT in the committed bridge kernel. ⇒ the microcircuit is not the lever; my prior pre-registration of it was wrong.

## ⇒ the honest rung-1 status + the real levers
The spiking on-bridge BDSP realization VALIDATES the credit mechanism on the substrate (clean, dominant, burst-coupled, no weight transport, drift metric-inert, 6/6) — the machinery genuinely brings the rate credit onto real spikes — but the CE-improvement is seed-variable because burst-multiplexed FA is a coarser credit than RFLO. The pre-registered levers to make the CE-win robust (NOT the microcircuit): (a) **scale** — 100 train sents is tiny; more data/epochs may average the coarse credit into a robust modest win; (b) a **less-coarse spiking credit** — a burst-*fraction* (graded) read rather than the binary burst detector, or more bursts-per-token (longer teach windows / higher rate) to lower the FA-credit variance; (c) accept the honest rate→spike gap (the coarse spiking credit is a characterized cost of the substrate). The rate e-prop within-reach credit (REAL-WITH-SCOPE) remains the validated result; this maps how much of it survives onto real spikes (the mechanism yes, the robust CE-win not-yet).
