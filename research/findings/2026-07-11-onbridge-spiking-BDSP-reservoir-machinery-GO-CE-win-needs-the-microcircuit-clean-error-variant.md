## The boundary (honest NEGATIVE on the CE-win) + its diagnosed cause
Over the FULL training arm, `plastic` does NOT beat `fixed` on the context-depth CE (plastic CE ≈ fixed, ~flat/slightly worse at d3-5), and **plastic |Δw| ≈ apical_lesion |Δw|** at every tried operating point (g=80..640, p0 0.08..0.30, k 150..200, bdsp-lr 0.005..0.01). Diagnosis: the Burstprop update has a large UNDIRECTED component — the `−P̄·E` baseline moves every active synapse regardless of the apical credit (the "leaky-P0-moat" drift).
