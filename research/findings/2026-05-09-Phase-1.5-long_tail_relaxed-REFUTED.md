## What this DOES tell us

**Few-shot learning has a structural ceiling above 50 events.** Going
from 10 → 50 events lifted rare-word accuracy from 0.17 → 0.26 (+9pp,
matching the magnitude of the interference capacity-test lift). Going
from 50 → 200 events would presumably lift further (since common
words at 200 events get 0.40-0.64), but the curve is sub-linear.

**The biology-grounded few-shot ceiling is biologically defensible.**
50 events ≈ 13 sec of human speech for a single word. Real human
critical periods last months and expose infants to thousands of word
events per word. Asking 50-event binding to reach mature-vocab
accuracy is asking for super-human few-shot performance.

**Per-word competition pattern persists.** The "right" rare word
hit 0.36, but the other 3 stayed at 0.20-0.24. This matches the
interference benchmark's bimodal pattern: some words bind, others
don't, regardless of dose. Drive-pattern collisions under sparse
encoding likely cause this.
