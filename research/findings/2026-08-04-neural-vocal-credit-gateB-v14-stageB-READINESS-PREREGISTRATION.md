# Gate B V14 Stage B Readiness

## Purpose

Stage A proved that the explicit SNr conductance bundle is executable, exact
when disabled, checkpointable, and fast enough on the RTX 3090. It did not
prove that the current equations and parameter surface can reproduce a
preparation-matched SNr cell. Stage B therefore begins with a seed-free
readiness gate rather than a physiology sweep.

The machine authority is
`research/specs/v14_snr_stageB_readiness.json`. It binds the Stage A spec,
engineering finding, performance receipt, research gate, and biology review by
hash. Calibration seed `590297` and every later partition remain forbidden
until readiness is committed as GO and a separate executable Stage B spec is
source-sealed.

## Why Readiness Is Required

Three mismatches would currently make a calibration result ambiguous.

1. The engine exposes the five SNr conductance maxima by region, while several
   channel kinetics, reversals, calcium/SK coupling terms, and the generic HH
   spike-current family are fixed or global.
2. The evidence spans juvenile rat intrinsic physiology, juvenile mouse NALCN
   genetics, adult mouse inhibition, and a later whole-system firing contract.
   These preparations cannot be collapsed into one target vector.
3. The adaptive workflow proposes several candidates, but its execution
   handoff must bind each candidate identity and parameter digest all the way
   through the runner and receipt before it can safely launch a batch.

## Readiness Deliverables

- A preparation-target packet with primary-source locators, uncertainty,
  recording conditions, and explicit simulator-unit conversions.
- A representability table showing that every searched parameter is actually
  independently controllable in the intended SNr population.
- A scorer tested on positive source-derived fixtures and deliberate failures,
  including wrong lesion direction, rate-only compensation, depolarization
  block, and hidden burn-in.
- A multi-candidate execution dry run proving that parameters cannot be mixed
  between jobs and that valid scientific failure remains distinguishable from
  infrastructure failure.
- A source-sealed executable Stage B spec with all numerical gates resolved.

No item may be filled from calibration output. Missing evidence is
`READINESS_UNDEFINED`, not permission to widen a range or weaken a gate.

## Future Search Shape

After readiness GO, the fixed budget is exactly `512` cheap NumPy intrinsic
screens, `64` complete NumPy population-and-lesion evaluations, and `12` CuPy
calibration finalists. A tier produces scientific NO-GO when it has too few
hard-gate survivors to fill the next tier; an infrastructure failure is retried
and does not consume the budget. Exactly four final survivors are frozen by a
greedy maximin rule over Euclidean distance in the preregistered normalized
parameter space. The first member and every distance tie are resolved by the
preregistered objective score and then candidate digest, making selection
reproducible while retaining a diverse ensemble.

Replication uses the three reserved replication seeds across the mini-PC pool
first and the local GPU second. Held-out work opens only after replication GO,
runs all four frozen vectors, and uses adult inhibition, phase, duration, and
temperature conditions that were not optimization targets.

## Scientific Boundary

Stage B concerns isolated SNr physiology. A future GO authorizes only a fresh
Stage C continuous-selector specification. It does not validate GPi, close the
tonic-output scaffold, establish reward learning, or demonstrate any language
or conversational capability.
