## 0. One-paragraph answer

Across analogy / transitive / common-ground the regime-B residual is **the SAME thing, stated at the right level of abstraction**: each capability needs a *typed relational STRUCTURE* (a relation axis: a bijective offset for analogy, a 1-D ordinal scale for transitive, a binary listener-state per fact for common-ground) and in regime A that structure is **handed to the operator pre-built**; in regime B it must be **MINED from the corpus**. The operators themselves are validated and substrate-faithful — the boundary is never the spiking substrate or the binding algebra, it is *where the typed structure comes from*. The decisive question the brief poses — *could the SAME ordinal/relational-learning mechanism MINE relations from the corpus, turning regime-A curated structure into regime-B learned structure?* — **answers YES for the structures that can be read off a small set of attested premises (orderings, bijective parallels, part-of, kinship), and NO for analogy that must EMERGE from raw similarity geometry (king−man≈queen−woman with no premise told).** Crucially, the project ALREADY has the two halves of the converter: (1) a corpus-attested relational-fact extractor (`_corpus_svo_extract.py`, with provenance anti-cheat) that turns corpus sentences into typed premises, and (2) the validated ordinal-map objective (Tier 2.3) that learns a relation axis FROM adjacent premise pairs. Wiring (1)→(2) — mine "A is bigger than B" premises from the corpus, learn the size axis, infer unstated comparisons with the symbolic-distance signature — is an **incremental** de-risk (reuse-by-import, no new mechanism class), and it is the cheapest decisive test of whether regime-B reasoning over the brain's own knowledge is unlockable now. The biology agrees and is unusually clean: the brain does relational reasoning via a **single factorisation mechanism** (Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine: medial-EC learns a *structural basis* — an ordinal/relational axis — factored from hippocampal content; the SAME machinery generalises across space, social rank, and abstract orderings), and it *acquires* the premises for that structure from experience (distributional learning + Hearst-pattern relation extraction are the literature's mining mechanisms). **Verdict: regime-B is unlockable INCREMENTALLY for at least one capability (transitive/ordinal over corpus-mined premises) — days-to-weeks, reuse-by-import, no `sim/` edit; the unifying mechanism is "mine typed premises from the corpus, learn the relation axis with the existing ordinal/Hebbian machinery." The genuinely-months-frontier part is narrow: fluid analogy that must EMERGE from raw similarity codes (no premise told) — that one is gated on corpus SCALE, not a clever circuit.** The single cheapest decisive de-risk is §4.

Every other capability needs a typed structure (offset / ordinal axis / listener bit / time tag) that is **layered on top of** the facts, and in regime A that layer is hand-built. So:

> **The common regime-B residual is ONE thing at the right altitude: a TYPED-RELATION ACQUISITION step — turning the brain's learned knowledge (facts + code geometry) into the typed relational structure (offset / ordinal axis / part-of / listener-state) that each reasoning operator consumes.** The operators are done; the missing piece is the *acquisition of the structure from the corpus*, not five unrelated mechanisms.

### 1.2 But the residual SPLITS into two genuinely-different sub-classes (the load-bearing distinction)

Conflating these is exactly how the project over-claimed before. The typed-structure-acquisition residual is NOT uniform — it splits by **how recoverable the structure is from what the brain already has**:

| sub-class | what acquisition requires | examples | cost class |

**The size of each, measured (not asserted):**

It is not "almost there." Whether it emerges at 10K–40K-word corpus scale on the local-normalization PPMI cortex is a genuine open bet (word2vec gets it at billions of tokens; the recent "Hierarchical Concept Geometry … Emerges from Word Co-occurrence" — arXiv 2605.23821 — shows it DOES emerge in co-occurrence geometry at scale, but that is a *different, larger* objective than the project's local-normalization stream cortex).
- **B1 is small in mechanism, and the converter ALREADY half-exists.** What B1 needs that the project lacks is *only the wiring between two validated pieces*: the corpus relational-fact extractor → the relation-learner. Verified in-repo:
  - **The extractor exists:** `_corpus_svo_extract.py` already mines corpus-attested `(subject, verb, object)` facts restricted to the brain's learned vocab, with a **provenance anti-cheat** (each fact ≥ `--min-count` occurrences, logged with a source sentence — "a fact is provably from the corpus, not invented") and an opt-in `keep_prep=True` that retains the preposition for typed obliques. This is the deep-knowledge build's Stage-1a (the owner's "extract real SVO/relational facts from `data/corpus/`" direction, `project_deep_knowledge_brain_fluency_build`). It is host-side curriculum prep (legitimate per BRAIN-BASED-ONLY: preparing the syllabus, like rendering a retinal image).
  - **The relation-learner exists:** the Tier 2.3 ordinal-map objective (`learn_positions`, Betasort biased update) learns a transitive axis from adjacent premise pairs — but those pairs are currently the hand-coded `ADJ_PAIRS`. Feed it corpus-mined comparative premises instead → same objective, learned structure.
  - **The relation-keyed co-occurrence statistic exists:** chain-of-thought's `_relation_assoc` (`rf_phasor_composer.py:737`) is already a relation-keyed co-occurrence over the fact store — the seed of "which relations exist and how strong."

### 1.3 Could the SAME ordinal/relational mechanism mine relations across capabilities? (the brief's crux — answered)

**Partly — and the part that says YES is the high-leverage one.** The Tier 2.3 ordinal-map mechanism is a *relation-axis learner*: give it ordered premise pairs, it lays items on a line. That same machinery, fed different premise types, generalises beyond order:
- **size / age / speed / quantity orderings** → a 1-D ordinal axis (the direct Tier 2.3 reuse; comparatives mine the premises).
- **bijective parallels** (capital_of, gender, past-tense) → the analogy offset is itself a *learned axis* in the factored space (Tier 2.1-A proved the operator; mining replaces the hand-`register_item` with corpus-attested pairs).
- **part-of / kinship / taxonomy** → set-membership / hierarchy, NOT a single offset. **Honest boundary (verified):** the Tier 2.1-A doc found `is_a` (many-to-one) is **not a clean additive offset** (acc 0.167 < floor) — taxonomy needs a *category-readout / hierarchy* mechanism, not a vector offset or a 1-D line. So the "one mechanism for all relations" hope is FALSE at the edges: orderings + bijective parallels share the axis-learner; many-to-one hierarchies need a separate (also-buildable) set-membership readout.

So the unifying mechanism is **not literally one operator** — it is **one acquisition PRINCIPLE** (mine typed premises from the corpus; route each relation-type to its matching learned structure: ordinal axis for orderings, factored offset for bijective parallels, set-membership for hierarchies), reusing the extractor for all of them and the ordinal/Hebbian objective for the axis cases.

## 2. REFRAME via biology — does the brain reason over learned knowledge with a UNIFYING mechanism or per-domain maps?

The biology is unusually clear and points the **same way as the residual analysis**: a **single factorisation mechanism** acquires and reasons over relational structure across many domains — but the *content/premises* are acquired from experience, and *some* relations (orderings, spatial, social rank) share the structural basis while *category membership* is a different read-out.

### 2.1 The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine — one structure-learner, many relational graphs (the decisive reframe)

TEM's central principle is **factorisation**: *separate the structural code (the transition rules of a graph) from the content code (the items), then bind them by conjunction.* The literature is explicit that this is **one learner generalising across many structures**: "separating structural codes … from sensory codes allows generalization over environments sharing the same structure," and the same factorisation "is sufficient to build a relational memory system that generalizes structural knowledge **in space and non-space** … and accounts for … **transitive inference**" (Whittington-Behrens 2020, *Cell*; verified this gate). After learning, the SAME medial-EC structural basis units show grid/band/border/object-vector properties — i.e. **one basis re-used across domains**, the items swapped in by conjunction. **This is the biological vindication of the §1.3 answer: a single structural-axis learner serves orderings/space/rank (the B1 axis cases), the items (content) supplied separately.** Park 2020 confirms the abstract case directly: humans learn a multi-item social/ordinal hierarchy **from pairwise comparisons along one axis at a time**, then infer unseen pairs at 93.6%, with map-like vmPFC/EC activity tracking ground-truth distances. The premises (pairwise comparisons) are *acquired*; the axis is *learned*; inference is a *geometric comparison* — exactly the Tier 2.3 mechanism, and exactly the B1 corpus-mining loop.

### 2.2 How the brain ACQUIRES the premises — distributional learning + relation extraction

The brain does not get its relational premises pre-curated either — it extracts them from experience. The literature gives two mechanisms that map directly onto the corpus-mining step:
- **Distributional learning** (Harris 1954; word2vec = PMI factorisation, Levy-Goldberg 2014): co-occurrence statistics build the *similarity* geometry — exactly the project's PPMI/Hebbian stream cortex (CYCLE 88–96, +0.518, generalises 0.86). This is the B2 substrate, and the recent result that **hierarchical/relational concept geometry EMERGES from word co-occurrence at LM scale** (arXiv 2605.23821) is the precise reason B2 is "gated on scale, not a circuit."
- **Pattern-based relation extraction** (Hearst 1992 onward — "X is a kind of Y", "X such as Y"; the hypernymy-from-text literature): *explicit relational statements* are minable from text by surface patterns. This is the B1 premise-mining mechanism, and the project's `_corpus_svo_extract.py` is its in-repo realisation (extend it from generic SVO to comparative/relational patterns).

### 2.3 Category membership is a DIFFERENT read-out (the honest per-domain split)

The hub-and-spoke / ATL convergence-zone account (Patterson-Lambon Ralph; catalog E.12/E.19/E.22) says **taxonomic / category** structure is a *hub representation* — modality-invariant concept neurons formed by Hebbian convergence — read out by **set-membership / similarity-to-prototype**, NOT by a 1-D axis or a vector offset. This matches the Tier 2.1-A `is_a` boundary exactly: orderings + bijective parallels live on the EC structural-axis machinery; **category membership lives on the ATL hub** and needs its own read-out. So the brain is **mostly unifying** (one structural-axis learner across orderings/space/rank) but **not fully** (hierarchy/category is a separate hub read-out). The sim should mirror this: one corpus-mining + axis-learner for B1-ordinal/parallel; a separate (cheap, already-scoped — the cross-modal convergence work, `2026-06-16-generalization-frontier-scoping.md`) set-membership read-out for taxonomy.

### 2.4 The reframe verdict

**The right hypothesis is "mine typed premises from the corpus, then learn the relational structure with the matching mechanism (EC-style ordinal axis for orderings/parallels; ATL-style set-membership for category)" — NOT "wait for analogy to fall out of raw similarity at scale" (which is the narrow B2 frontier).** The brain reasons over *learned* knowledge by factorising a structural axis from acquired content, and it acquires the premises distributionally + by pattern extraction. The sim has both halves (the extractor + the axis-learner) and the biology says wiring them is the principled move — for orderings and bijective parallels *now*, with category membership as a separate cheap read-out, and emergent-fluid-analogy as the genuine deferred bet.

## 4. VERDICT — the single cheapest decisive de-risk + anti-cheats + falsification bar

### The recommendation

**Run option (a) in its cheapest form: a CORPUS-MINED ORDINAL AXIS over the brain's learned vocabulary, scored on held-out unstated comparisons with the symbolic-distance curve.** It is the single cheapest test that decides whether regime-B reasoning over the brain's OWN learned knowledge is unlockable incrementally — because it isolates the *one* thing in question (can typed relational structure be MINED from the corpus and learned, rather than hand-curated) on the *one* capability with the strongest positive anti-cheat (the symbolic-distance effect, which a lookup / co-occurrence artifact mathematically cannot fake). It reuses two validated pieces (`_corpus_svo_extract.py` + the Tier 2.3 ordinal-map harness), needs no `sim/` edit, and runs cheap-first numpy → real spiking. A GO here is a *qualitatively stronger* claim than Tier 2.3: it shows the structure is **acquired from the corpus, not given**.

### The de-risk protocol (read-only spec; build is the separate later step)

1. **Mine the premises FROM THE CORPUS.** Extend `_corpus_svo_extract.py`'s patterns to comparatives over the brain's learned vocab (e.g. "X is bigger than Y", "X is larger than Y" for a size axis; or age/speed). Keep ONLY corpus-attested pairs (≥ `--min-count`, each logged with a source sentence — the existing provenance anti-cheat). The mined pairs are the **premise set** — the regime-A `ADJ_PAIRS` is REPLACED by corpus-attested premises.
2. **Learn the axis** via the Tier 2.3 Betasort biased-ordinal objective (`learn_positions`) over the mined premises — items are the brain's learned concept codes.
3. **Test on HELD-OUT unstated comparisons** — pairs whose order is NOT a mined premise (and never used to fit the axis) — by comparing map positions through the real spiking Wang-2002 accumulator; record correctness AND the decision margin.
4. **Compute the symbolic-distance curve** (accuracy & margin vs ordinal-rank gap).
5. Confirm the comparison **through the real spiking accumulator** (not just host arithmetic) on ≥1 seed, matching the numpy reference, before the 6-seed run.

### Anti-cheats (ALL mandatory — the burned-capability bar; any failure → STOP, write the honest NEGATIVE, do not over-claim)

- **(i) ⭐ THE SYMBOLIC-DISTANCE EFFECT — the headline control.** Held-out accuracy AND margin must **increase monotonically** with ordinal-rank distance (positive Spearman, significant across seeds). **Falsification: flat/non-monotonic curve → NO-GO** regardless of raw accuracy (the exact signature the 2026-05-14 artifact could not produce; the Tier 2.3 redemption hinged on it).
- **(ii) held-out ≫ floor, AND the order is GENUINELY from the mined premises.** Held-out unstated comparisons ≫ chance (0.5) AND ≫ a memorization baseline (stored-premise lookup = chance on unstated pairs). **PLUS the regime-B-specific control:** the mined-premise axis must beat an axis built from a **PERMUTED corpus-mining** (premises mined for a *scrambled* relation, e.g. random word pairs labelled "bigger") — proving the corpus-attested premises, not the mining apparatus, carry the order. **Falsification: held-out ≤ floor, OR permuted-mining ≈ true → NO-GO.**
- **(iii) permuted-order collapses + TRUE rank-1.** Shuffle the trained order; the TRUE corpus-derived order must uniquely produce the held-out inferences + the distance curve; permuted orders collapse to a flat chance curve (the `v16_compose_permuted_check` rank-1/N! discipline that exposed the 2026-05-14 artifact). **Falsification: permuted does not collapse / TRUE not uniquely best → NO-GO.**
- **(iv) lesion the map → collapse.** Scramble the learned positions / zero the axis → held-out drops to chance and the curve flattens. **Falsification: lesion ≈ full → NO-GO.**
- **(v) spreading-activation negative control.** Run the relation-blind co-occurrence/spreading baseline on the same corpus-mined corpus — it must FAIL the held-out unstated pairs (literature-guaranteed: associative models answer at chance on transitive inference) — confirming the corpus genuinely requires the map. **Falsification: spreading also "passes" → the corpus is not discriminating; redesign before any GO.**
- **(vi) provenance / no-invention.** Every mined premise must be corpus-attested (the existing extractor anti-cheat); assert the held-out pairs were NEVER attested as premises (no leakage). **Falsification: any inferred order traceable to a directly-attested premise → it is recall, not inference → exclude it.**
- **(vii) no-confab moat 0-FA.** An item never placed on the axis, or a comparison with an absent operand, must **abstain (None)**, zero false-accepts. **Falsification: any forced answer on an unmapped item → moat breach → NO-GO.**
- **(viii) 6-seed.** All of the above — especially the symbolic-distance correlation and the permuted-mining control — must hold across 6 seeds before any GO claim.

### The explicit falsification bar (one line)

> **GO only if: held-out unstated-comparison accuracy ≫ chance AND a significant monotone symbolic-distance curve AND the mined-premise axis beats permuted-mining AND permuted-order collapses AND lesion collapses AND the spreading baseline fails AND every premise is corpus-attested with no held-out leakage AND the moat holds 0-FA — across 6 seeds. Absence of the monotone distance curve, OR permuted-mining matching the true axis, is by itself a NO-GO regardless of raw accuracy.**

### The plain verdict — INCREMENTAL vs GENUINE-MONTHS-FRONTIER

- **Regime-B over the brain's own learned knowledge is UNLOCKABLE INCREMENTALLY for the B1 (premise-mineable) capabilities — days-to-low-weeks, reuse-by-import, no `sim/` edit expected.** The unifying mechanism is **"mine typed premises from the corpus (the existing attested-fact extractor) → learn the relation axis (the existing ordinal/Hebbian objective) → reason with the validated operator, gated on the capability's positive signature."** The de-risk above (corpus-mined ordinal axis) is *expected to GO* IF and ONLY IF the symbolic-distance effect appears AND permuted-mining collapses — those two controls are what convert "reasoning over learned knowledge" from a burned over-claim into a believable result. This single move generalises (same extractor + axis-learner) to size/age/speed orderings and bijective parallels (capital_of, gender, tense).
