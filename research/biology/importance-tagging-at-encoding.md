# What a memory is worth is marked when it is formed, by more than one signal

**Why this entry exists.** The DA tag-and-capture + sleep-replay pair (`webapp/da_tag_capture.py`,
`webapp/sleep_replay_capture.py`, both default OFF) has one importance channel: the brain's own dopamine, driven by the
spiking novelty (habituation) organ plus a host content-word count, reaching the D1 pool that makes plasticity-related
proteins. The owner ruled on 2026-09-25 that forgetting is acceptable and that what matters is that the brain keeps what
matters. The biology above says "what matters" is written by several converging channels, not one.

**What the real system runs alongside the DA tag, that the model replaced with zero.**
- Arousal: basolateral amygdala + locus coeruleus noradrenaline (McGaugh 2004; Cahill 1994 is the lesion). LC neurons
  also co-release dopamine onto the same hippocampal D1/D5 receptors (Takeuchi 2016), so an arousal channel can reach
  the existing capture edge without a new PRP route.
- Local priority: arousal amplifies the most active representation and suppresses the rest (GANE, Mather 2016). A
  key detail and its background are not marked equally.
- Expected future use: being told a memory will be needed changes whether sleep keeps it (Wilhelm 2011), and the
  instruction tag decays within a day, faster than the memory (Stickgold & Walker 2013).
- Value and reward (Kandel ch.52; Oudiette 2013) and schema fit (van Kesteren 2012).
- Behavioural tagging is relatedness-specific (Dunsmoor 2015): a later salient event rescues earlier weak memories of
  the same kind, not everything told within the hour. The model's one cell-wide PRP pool does the latter.
