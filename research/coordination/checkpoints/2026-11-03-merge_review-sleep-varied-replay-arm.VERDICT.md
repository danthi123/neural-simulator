**Defect (must be fixed before merge): the rewordings leak paraphrase-question words.**
The arm exists to answer one question: does varied replay rescue recall on reworded questions (the `PARA` read-out)? The item also says rewordings "must not reuse the wording of any PARA question". The 3-run and 2-run tests pass only because they check consecutive words in order. Comparing word sets instead shows several rewordings plant the paraphrase question's own replacement word, which the original statement and question do not contain:
- "What's my hometown?" ← "Lyon is my hometown." The question's only content word is here.
- "What's my workplace?" ← "My workplace is the Crumb bakery." The question's only content word is here.
- "Which colour do I like best?" ← "I like the colour teal best." All three content words are here; only the word order differs.
- "Which city is my grandma in?" ← "My grandma's city is Oslo." Both content words are here, in reversed order.
- Smaller cases: "What's the name of my kitty?" ← "The name of my cat is Pixel." ("name"), and "What month was my son born?" ← "March is my son's birthday month." ("month").

With these texts, a `sleep_varied` gain on `PARA` would be mixed up with simple word priming on 4 to 6 of the 12 questions. That would make the six-seed run on these texts unreadable for exactly the question it is meant to answer. It is cheap to fix now, and a full re-run later would not be.
